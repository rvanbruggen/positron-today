So, back in the 1890s, the U.S. Department of Agriculture sent out adventurous plant hunters to explore the globe! These brave souls, led by botanist David Fairchild, introduced over 51,000 new plants to America, including delicious fruits like mangoes and quinoa. Now, it wasn't all smooth sailing—these explorers faced dangers from wild animals and hostile territories. It's fascinating to think about how their efforts shaped our agriculture and food culture today!
