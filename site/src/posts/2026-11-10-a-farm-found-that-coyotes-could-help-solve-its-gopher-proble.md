John and Molly Chester once spent about $100,000 a year trapping gophers that were damaging their farm’s trees. Then they discovered that barn owls and coyotes were already keeping tens of thousands of the pests in check. Their experience shows how welcoming wildlife back can help farms thrive, even when nature brings a few surprises, like coyotes eating chickens. Now, their new free YouTube docuseries follows the joy and challenges of farming in harmony with the wild.
