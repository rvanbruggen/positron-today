A new Cato Institute study finds that immigrants have paid more in taxes than they have received in government benefits every year since 1900. Their cumulative contribution to federal, state and local budgets reached $14.6 trillion, with a surplus of $585 billion in 2023 alone. The researchers say immigrants today are more productive and generate a larger fiscal benefit than in the past, even as public spending has grown. Their findings make a positive case for modernizing immigration laws and recognizing immigrants’ contributions to a more prosperous country.
