David Silver, a billionaire and AI pioneer, has pledged to donate all profits from his company, Ineffable Intelligence, to charity. He believes that by doing so, he can make a significant impact on the world. This trend among AI founders to commit their wealth to philanthropy raises important questions about the effectiveness of such giving. As the AI industry grows, so does the potential for tech philanthropy to reshape how we address societal issues.
