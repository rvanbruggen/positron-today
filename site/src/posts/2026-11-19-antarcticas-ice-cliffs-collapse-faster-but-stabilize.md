So, a new study finds that Antarctic ice cliffs can retreat faster than scientists’ models predicted when they collapse. Researchers analyzed satellite observations of more than 15,000 cliffs collected over four years. The encouraging twist is that the glaciers eventually stabilized, rather than triggering the runaway retreat long feared by scientists. The findings suggest that cliff height alone cannot predict what happens next - and could help improve forecasts of future sea-level rise.
