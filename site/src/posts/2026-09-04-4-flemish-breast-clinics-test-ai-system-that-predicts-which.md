Four breast clinics in Flanders are testing a new AI system that helps doctors determine the best treatment for breast cancer patients. This innovative approach could lead to more targeted therapies and avoid unnecessary surgeries. The AI predicts how well a patient will respond to chemotherapy and assesses the likelihood of cancer spreading. If successful, this system could be implemented in practice within a year, enhancing patient care across Europe.
