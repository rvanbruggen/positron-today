Here’s a fascinating look at how agricultural intensification is reshaping biodiversity in Sumatra, Indonesia. Researchers assessed around 40,000 species, from soil microorganisms to vertebrates, across this exceptionally biodiverse region. They found that intensification threatens biodiversity through both species loss and changes in community composition, with effects visible at different spatial scales. This broad perspective can help guide efforts to protect tropical life as agricultural demand grows.
