What an exciting development for train lovers! After three decades, Sweden and Finland have reconnected their railway lines, allowing passengers to travel across the border by train once again. This new service, running twice daily from Oulu to Haparanda, is set to boost tourism and business in these beautiful northern regions. It's a fantastic opportunity for people to explore, work, and connect with one another, all while enjoying the stunning Arctic landscapes.
