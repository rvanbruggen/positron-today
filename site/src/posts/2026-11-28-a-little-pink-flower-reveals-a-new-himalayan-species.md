So, a small pink-flowered plant beside a stream in Arunachal Pradesh turned out to be a species new to science: Begonia siangensis. Researchers confirmed its identity by examining its leaves and flowers closely and comparing them with specimens and botanical records from across the region. The discovery, published in October 2026, adds to Arunachal Pradesh’s remarkable begonia diversity, while another recent discovery, Begonia neelamorum, also shows how local knowledge can help scientists explore nature. And here's the thing: Begonia siangensis has so far been found at just one site, so more research is needed to understand its range and how best to protect it.
