Wow, scientists have just witnessed a massive star being torn apart by a black hole in an event that's been described as cosmic snack preparation! This incredible phenomenon, observed by astronomers worldwide, released energy 400 billion times greater than our Sun, surpassing even the mightiest supernovae. The lead researcher, Daniel Perley, highlighted how this rare occurrence helps us understand black holes better and their formation processes. And here's the twist: after the explosion, a strange signal emerged that left researchers scratching their heads!
