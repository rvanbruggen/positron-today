Fermented foods like kombucha, kefir, and sourdough bread are gaining popularity, and for good reason! Recent research shows that these foods, transformed by live microorganisms, can boost both gut and mental health. Tim Spector, a British epidemiologist, emphasizes that even dead microbes can have positive effects, helping to produce antibodies and reduce inflammation. Interestingly, many study participants reported improved moods after consuming fermented foods regularly, highlighting the fascinating connection between our gut health and mental well-being.
