So, it may be time to pick up the knitting needles, try some pottery or get creative with crafts. A new study found that people who do handicrafts often report less anxiety, worry and stress than those who do them less frequently. Mats Sundbeck, a researcher at Lund University, says handcrafting is not only enjoyable and rewarding, but also good for your health. The findings offer an encouraging reason to make a little room for hands-on creativity.
