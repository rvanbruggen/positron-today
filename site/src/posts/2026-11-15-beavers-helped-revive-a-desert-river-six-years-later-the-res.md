So: in 2019, researchers brought beavers displaced from other areas to Utah’s struggling Price River, hoping their dams would create refuge for wildlife during droughts. Six years later, the river is healthier than it has been in years, fish are thriving, and locals say its revival has helped their town, Helper. The beavers are the stars, though cleanup work and better grazing rules also played an important part. I love how these natural engineers are now inspiring similar restoration projects across Utah.
