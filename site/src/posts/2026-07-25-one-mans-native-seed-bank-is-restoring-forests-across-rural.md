Anant Bhikaji Tayade is on a mission to protect India's native seeds, which are crucial for sustaining farms and forests. Growing up in Maharashtra, he witnessed the detrimental effects of losing these seeds and the balance of nature. By collaborating with tribal communities, he is reviving traditional knowledge and practices to collect and nurture these seeds. His efforts are not just about conservation; they are about restoring ecosystems and creating livelihoods for local people.
