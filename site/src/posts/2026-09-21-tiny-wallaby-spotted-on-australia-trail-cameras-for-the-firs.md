I am absolutely thrilled to share some wonderful news from the Australian Wildlife Conservancy today. They have managed to capture footage of the elusive Nabarlek wallaby, a tiny creature that had not been seen on the mainland for over 50 years. It is just incredible to think that these little guys, about the size of a loaf of bread, are still out there thriving in the Kimberley region. This discovery gives us such a massive boost of hope for the conservation of this endangered species. And here is the thing - it shows us that with the right technology and collaboration, we can still find these hidden wonders of nature.
