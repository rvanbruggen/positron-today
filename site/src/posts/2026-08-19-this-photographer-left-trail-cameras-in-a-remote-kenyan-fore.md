Wildlife photographer Will Burrard-Lucas embarked on an exciting adventure in Kenya's Maasai Mara to document the elusive black rhino. By setting up a camera trap, he uncovered a bustling wildlife corridor that revealed not just rhinos, but also elephants, leopards, and even a rare greater kudu! The findings provided crucial insights into the animal population and their behaviors in this hidden part of the reserve. It's a fantastic example of how technology can aid conservation efforts and deepen our understanding of wildlife.
