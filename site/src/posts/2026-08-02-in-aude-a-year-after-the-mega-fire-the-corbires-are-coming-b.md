So, it's been a year since the devastating mega fire in the Corbières, and the region is bouncing back beautifully! The Cathar castles are still standing strong, trails are reopened, and vineyards are being replanted. Locals are thrilled to see tourists returning, many of whom have a deep love for this stunning area. It's a heartwarming story of resilience and recovery that showcases the spirit of the community.
