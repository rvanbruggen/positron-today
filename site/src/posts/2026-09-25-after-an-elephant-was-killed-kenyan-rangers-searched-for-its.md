So: a baby elephant was left alone after his mother was tragically killed in Kenya, and the rangers didn’t stop searching for him for three whole weeks. They finally found him trying to enter local farmers’ enclosures, weak but uninjured, and rushed him to a wildlife nursery where he quickly bonded with other orphaned elephants. What’s really heartwarming is how one female elephant, Ahmed, adopted him as her special baby, showing maternal love for the first time. Now, Pardamat is thriving and never has to face the world alone again.
