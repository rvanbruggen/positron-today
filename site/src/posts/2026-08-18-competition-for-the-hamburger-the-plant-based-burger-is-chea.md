Plant-based products are often cheaper than the least expensive meat and dairy items, according to new research from ProVeg Netherlands. The study compared prices at nine Dutch supermarkets and found that over half of plant-based alternatives, like meat substitutes, are now more affordable. This trend is especially true for plant-based burgers, which are on average 31 percent cheaper than their meat counterparts. With supermarkets aiming to make plant-based options more accessible, this could encourage more people to choose healthier and more sustainable diets.
