So: in Pampore, Kashmir, a unique Nature School is changing how kids learn by taking them outdoors every Sunday. There are no fees, no walls, and no fixed syllabus - just pure, hands-on experience with wetlands and nature. Over 30 children from different backgrounds come together to explore, discuss, and connect with the environment, guided by volunteers and experts. And here's the thing - this free initiative shows that learning about nature can be fun, inclusive, and deeply meaningful for all ages.
