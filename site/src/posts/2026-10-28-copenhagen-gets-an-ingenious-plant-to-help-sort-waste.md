Copenhagen is putting Denmark’s first sorting plant for residual waste into operation, adding a clever new tool to the city’s recycling efforts. The facility is expected to recover tonnes of plastic, metal, paper, cardboard and cartons from mixed waste for recycling. And here’s the thing: residents still need to sort their rubbish at home, so those kitchen bins are not going away just yet. Still, this ingenious plant offers real hope for a simpler waste system in the future.
