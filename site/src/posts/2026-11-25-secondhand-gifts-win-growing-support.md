So, giving secondhand gifts is becoming a much more welcome part of Christmas, especially among younger shoppers. A Postnord and HUI Research survey found that 73 percent would feel comfortable receiving a pre-loved present, while half are open to buying one online. Clothes and shoes, books and media, and home furnishings are among the most popular choices. Since each item is unique, shoppers are advised to act quickly, ask about its condition and choose tracked delivery.
