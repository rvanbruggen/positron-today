Eve Ogden Schaub is on a fascinating journey to create a dress from flax she’s growing in her backyard. This project, which she documents on Instagram, challenges the fast fashion trend by embracing a slower, more meaningful approach to clothing. Schaub’s process involves everything from sowing seeds to spinning yarn, and she’s learning along the way, often with the help of friends and family. It’s not just about making a dress; it’s about questioning our consumption habits and exploring the value of creativity in a world dominated by convenience.
