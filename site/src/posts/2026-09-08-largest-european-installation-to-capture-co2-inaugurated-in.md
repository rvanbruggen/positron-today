So, in Sluiskil, the largest CO2 capture installation in Europe has just been inaugurated by the Norwegian company Yara. This facility is set to capture about 800,000 tons of CO2 annually, which is roughly 0.5 percent of the Netherlands' total emissions. The captured CO2 will be transported by boat to Norway for storage, marking a significant step in carbon capture and storage technology. It's exciting to see how innovation and cross-border collaboration can lead to real climate solutions!
