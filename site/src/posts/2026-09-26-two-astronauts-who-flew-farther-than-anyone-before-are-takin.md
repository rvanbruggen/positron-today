NASA astronauts Victor Glover and Reid Wiseman are moving into emeritus roles after their historic Artemis II mission around the Moon. This new status lets them volunteer their expertise to train and mentor current NASA teams while pursuing other opportunities. Both bring invaluable experience from their spaceflights to help prepare for Artemis III and future missions. I find it inspiring that their journey with NASA continues, sharing knowledge and pushing boundaries in human spaceflight.
