Fawzia Yawari, a young Afghan entrepreneur, defied Taliban restrictions by sneaking into an international agriculture fair in Kabul, showcasing her family's honey. Despite harsh limitations on women, many Afghan women are starting businesses to survive, with the number of licensed female entrepreneurs skyrocketing under Taliban rule. These women often work from home in traditional sectors or sell products online to avoid male intermediaries. Yet, they still face discrimination and conservative social attitudes, making their path challenging but inspiring. It’s a story of resilience and hope amid difficult circumstances.
