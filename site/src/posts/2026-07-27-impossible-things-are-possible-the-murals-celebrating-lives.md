In a heartwarming initiative, murals have been created to celebrate the stories of former rough sleepers like Lee, who now volunteers with St Mungo’s. These vibrant artworks, displayed across London, Reading, and Bristol, feature QR codes that allow people to listen to the narrated experiences of those depicted. The project aims to challenge stereotypes and highlight the resilience of individuals overcoming homelessness. With rising rough sleeping numbers, this artistic approach serves as a powerful reminder that recovery and hope are indeed possible.
