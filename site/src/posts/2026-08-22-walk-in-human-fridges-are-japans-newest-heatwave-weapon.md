So, Japan has come up with a brilliant solution to combat heatstroke during those scorching summer days! The 'Do Hiemon Box' is a personal cooling booth that delivers rapid and targeted cooling, originally designed for outdoor workplaces. Now, it’s expanding into public spaces, making it accessible to everyone. This innovative approach is not just about comfort, but also about safety in extreme weather conditions.
