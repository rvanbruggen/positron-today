So, the Mercator map, created in 1569 by Gerardus Mercator, has been a game changer for navigation. It brilliantly allows sailors to follow a straight compass bearing, but here's the catch - it distorts the size of countries, making those near the poles look much larger than they are. This has significant implications, especially for how we perceive Africa's resources and opportunities. Critics argue that maps do more than guide us; they shape our understanding of political and economic importance.
