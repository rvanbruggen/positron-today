Meet Prasad Phadke, a passionate engineer who left his corporate job to tackle the mosquito menace in India. He created the Eco BioTrap, a clever biodegradable trap that disrupts the breeding cycle of mosquitoes. After years of development, this innovative trap has shown great promise in urban areas like Dharavi, where it has been deployed to protect thousands of households. The best part? It’s environmentally friendly and doesn’t require electricity to work!
