I find Goa's approach to ending rabies absolutely fascinating! They tackle the problem on three fronts: immediate response to bites, vaccinating at least 70% of free-roaming dogs, and educating kids in schools. The response system is simple but effective—a 24/7 hotline guiding bite victims and investigating the dog. And here's the thing: precise tracking of vaccination coverage makes all the difference, turning a one-time effort into a sustainable system. This blueprint is practical and ready for any state to adopt.
