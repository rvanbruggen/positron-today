So, a remarkable discovery has been made near Cairo! Archaeologists have uncovered a tomb that dates back 4,300 years, attributed to a royal judge from the Fifth Dynasty. This exciting find was part of a long-term project involving Spanish and Egyptian teams, highlighting the rich history of the Saqqarah necropolis. It's fascinating to think about the stories this tomb could tell about life during the reign of King Djedkarê Isési.
