I love how creative the Georgia Sea Turtle Center got with Cebu, a young sea turtle injured by a boat. After severe spinal trauma left Cebu unable to swim properly, the team didn’t just give up—they crafted a custom 3D-printed harness to support Cebu during physical therapy. This clever device lets Cebu practice swimming movements independently while staying buoyant, which is a huge step forward. And here's the thing: the combination of acupuncture, stem cell therapy, and this innovative harness shows how much care and ingenuity goes into helping one little turtle heal.
