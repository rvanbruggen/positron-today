Wow, what an exciting time for space exploration! After an incredible eight-year journey, the BepiColombo spacecraft is getting ready to study Mercury, the closest planet to the sun. It successfully deployed its two science orbiters, which will work together to uncover new details about Mercury's surface and magnetic field. This mission, a collaboration between the European Space Agency and Japan's JAXA, is set to begin full science operations next April, and I can't wait to see what they discover!
