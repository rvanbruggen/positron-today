So, here's the exciting news: China's CO2 emissions have dropped by one percent in the second quarter of 2026! This decline is quite remarkable, especially considering the backdrop of geopolitical tensions and the ongoing situation in Iran. The data comes from China's national statistics bureau, analyzed by the British climate magazine Carbon Brief. It's a positive sign that shows how changes in oil consumption can impact the environment.
