Denmark's car fleet has transformed over the past decade, with nearly 700,000 electric vehicles making a real environmental difference. So here's the thing - even though we have more private cars on the roads than ever before, the overall pollution they produce has dropped dramatically compared to ten years ago. According to professor and traffic researcher Jeppe Rich from DTU Transport, this is genuinely good news for the country. The shift toward cleaner vehicles shows that we can grow our mobility without sacrificing our environment.
