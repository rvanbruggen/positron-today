So, it turns out that our understanding of fluids is getting a major upgrade! After decades of sticking with the classic Navier-Stokes equations, physicists have finally developed a new theory that incorporates the microscopic world of molecules. This fresh perspective not only redefines what it means to be a fluid but also predicts new behaviors based on the movements of tiny particles. It's fascinating how insights from studying black holes and the universe helped bring fluids into the modern era!
