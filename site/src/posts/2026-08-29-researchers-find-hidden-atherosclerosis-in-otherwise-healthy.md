Danish researchers have been on a quest to uncover hidden atherosclerosis in thousands of healthy adults, and they've found it in 1 out of every 13 individuals aged 18 to 29. This groundbreaking study, part of the React initiative, aims to improve early detection and prevention of cardiovascular diseases. The results were recently presented at a heart congress in Munich and published in a prestigious medical journal. The researchers are now looking to develop better screening programs to tackle this silent condition, which could save lives both in Denmark and globally.
