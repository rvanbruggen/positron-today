The damage in the Hoge Venen is immense, with 3,000 hectares of nature destroyed, but not everything is lost. Small zones, called 'islands of biodiversity,' have survived the fire and are crucial for future recovery. Ecologist Kris Verheyen emphasizes that these areas can help re-establish populations of rare plants and animals. This situation presents a unique opportunity to reassess and enhance conservation efforts in the region.
