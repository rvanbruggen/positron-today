What fantastic news from Uganda! The World Health Organization has officially declared the country Ebola-free after an impressive 42 days without any new cases. This achievement highlights the hard work of Ugandan authorities, health workers, and communities who came together to combat the outbreak. It's a reminder of the importance of vigilance, especially as neighboring regions still face challenges with the virus. Let's celebrate this triumph and the collaborative spirit that helped make it possible!
