So, there's this star called S301 that orbits the supermassive black hole at the center of our Milky Way, and its sky is just wild! From S301, you can see the black hole's shadow and a swirling ring of material that’s way bigger than our moon. It’s fascinating how this star, which is pretty normal in other parts of the universe, becomes extraordinary just because of its location. But here's the catch—it's also a dangerous place, with lethal radiation from the black hole, making it unlikely for any planets to survive there.
