So, here's some exciting news! Eight more African nations are joining the circular economy movement, thanks to the African Circular Economy Fund. This initiative is all about creating national roadmaps that will help these countries boost their economies while being kinder to the planet. Angola, Liberia, Madagascar, and Senegal are gearing up to develop their own strategies, while Benin, Chad, Ethiopia, and Mauritius are diving into implementation. It's a fantastic step towards sustainable growth and job creation across the continent!
