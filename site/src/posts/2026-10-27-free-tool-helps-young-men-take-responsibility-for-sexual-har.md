So: SafeBae created Vibe Check to give young men a thoughtful alternative to harmful advice on online forums and in the manosphere. The free tool uses guided scenarios and reflection exercises to help users understand consent, consider the impact of their behaviour and learn how to apologise. Since its launch, more than 94,000 people have used it, with feedback praising the skills it offers for taking responsibility and making amends. Now, SafeBae hopes to adapt the approach for young people facing sextortion, too.
