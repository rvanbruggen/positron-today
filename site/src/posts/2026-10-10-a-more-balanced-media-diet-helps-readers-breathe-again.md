For World Mental Health Day, Positive News invited readers to share how changing the stories they consume has affected their wellbeing. Many say that balancing distressing headlines with constructive journalism eases anxiety, restores perspective and helps them feel hopeful. Some describe Positive News as a daily source of comfort, while others share it with friends or use it to stay grounded for their families. And here's the thing: readers say that seeing solutions also helps turn hope into motivation to act.
