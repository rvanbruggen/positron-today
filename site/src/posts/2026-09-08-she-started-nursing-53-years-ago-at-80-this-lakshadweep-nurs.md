Hindumbi Kaurom Kakkada is an incredible 80-year-old nurse who has dedicated over 53 years to healthcare in Lakshadweep. Despite retiring two decades ago, she continues to work and has now been recognized as one of the top 10 finalists for the Aster Guardians Global Nursing Award. Her journey is remarkable, having transported patients by fishing boat and handled emergencies in challenging conditions. Hindumbi's commitment to her community and her impact on over 20,000 lives truly exemplify the spirit of nursing.
