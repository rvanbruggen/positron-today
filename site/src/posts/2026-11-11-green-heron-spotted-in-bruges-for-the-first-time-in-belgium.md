A green heron, a bird normally found only in the Americas, has been spotted in Bruges. It is the first time the species has been seen in Belgium. That makes this a particularly rare and exciting moment for local wildlife watchers. And here's the thing: a bird from across the Atlantic has turned up right here in our own backyard.
