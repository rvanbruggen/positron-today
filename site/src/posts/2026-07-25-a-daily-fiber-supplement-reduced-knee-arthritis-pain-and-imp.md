A new clinical trial shows that a daily prebiotic fiber supplement can significantly reduce pain and improve physical function in people with knee osteoarthritis. Participants taking inulin, a natural fiber, experienced stronger grip and less pain sensitivity compared to those in a physiotherapy program. This simple dietary change could offer a new way to manage knee OA, which affects millions worldwide. The study highlights the potential link between gut health and pain management, making it an exciting area for further research.
