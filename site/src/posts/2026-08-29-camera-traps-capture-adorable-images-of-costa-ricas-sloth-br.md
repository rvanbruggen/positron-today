It's amazing to see how conservationists in Costa Rica are building 'sloth crossings' to help these adorable creatures navigate roads safely. These simple rope bridges connect fragmented forest areas, allowing sloths to avoid dangers like traffic and dog attacks. Camera traps have captured thousands of photos, showing not just sloths but also other wildlife using these crossings. With over 400 bridges installed, the project has significantly reduced wildlife injuries and even helps prevent inbreeding among sloths, which is just fantastic!
