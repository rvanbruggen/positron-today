Researchers have discovered that a yeast-based dietary supplement can enhance immune cells that fight cancer in mice. By adding this supplement to the diets of obese mice, they observed improved immune responses against various cancers. This is exciting because obesity often hampers immune function, making it harder for the body to tackle tumors. The findings suggest that this yeast supplement could be a game-changer in restoring immune activity, especially for those struggling with obesity.
