So: a specially built trailer is helping researchers uncover the hidden biodiversity of Dutch cities by collecting dna traces from the air. Willem van Strien and Kris Marnette’s trials detected nearly 900 animal species and around 700 fungal species in Nijmegen, including dozens not previously recorded there. Their system draws in huge volumes of air, captures floating particles in a fine water mist and sends the collected dna to a lab for analysis. It can even reveal elusive species such as weasels and polecats, offering cities a fascinating new way to keep track of urban nature.
