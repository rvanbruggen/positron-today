In Jharkhand, a wild elephant mother was struggling with a severe foot injury, making it difficult for her to move. Instead of transporting her to a veterinary facility, which could be risky, the dedicated team brought the treatment directly to her. This innovative approach, known as ‘Haathi Sewa’, allows veterinarians to provide urgent care in the field, ensuring the safety of both the elephant and her young calf. It’s a remarkable example of how compassion and ingenuity can come together to protect these magnificent creatures.
