So, it turns out that the recent algae blooms in the Baltic Sea are not just an environmental issue, but also a stunning sight from space! These normally invisible blue-green algae have spread across the surface, creating a vibrant carpet that satellites can capture from 786 kilometers above. It's fascinating how the high temperatures have allowed these algae to thrive, even as green grasslands typically wither in the summer heat. However, this dramatic bloom, caused by years of phosphorus runoff, has raised significant concerns about water quality.
