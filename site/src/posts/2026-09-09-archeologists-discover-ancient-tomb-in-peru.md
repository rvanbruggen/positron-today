So, archeologists in Peru have made an incredible discovery – an ancient tomb that has been untouched for 600 years! They found the remains of at least 38 people along with fascinating relics, including metal weapons from the pre-Incan Chimu civilization. It's amazing to think that this site remained intact, especially since many previous finds were looted. This discovery really sheds light on a culture that thrived between 900 and 1470 AD.
