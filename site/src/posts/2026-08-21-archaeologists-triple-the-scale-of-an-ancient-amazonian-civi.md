So, here's the exciting news! Archaeologists have recently discovered hundreds of geometric earthworks in the Amazon, revealing a precolonial civilization much larger than we ever imagined. Using advanced LiDAR technology, they mapped out a staggering area that could contain between 24,000 and 30,000 of these structures. This Aquiry civilization, known for its ceremonial enclosures, may have supported a population comparable to the Late Preclassical Mayans. It's incredible to think about the hidden history beneath the jungle canopy, waiting to be uncovered!
