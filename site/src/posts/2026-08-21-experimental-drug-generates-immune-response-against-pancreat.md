Researchers at Johns Hopkins have made a groundbreaking discovery with an experimental drug that targets pancreatic cancer. This drug has shown to be safe in humans and has generated strong immune responses in individuals at high risk. In a small trial, 90% of participants developed significant immune responses against a common cancer mutation. This is just the beginning, but it opens up exciting possibilities for cancer prevention!
