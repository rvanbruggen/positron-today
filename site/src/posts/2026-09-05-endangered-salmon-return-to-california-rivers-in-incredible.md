Wow, can you believe it? A record 30,000 coho salmon have returned to central California rivers, which is ten times the average from the last decade! Scientists are absolutely thrilled, as they thought they’d never witness such a comeback. This incredible surge is a testament to the successful restoration efforts by NOAA and local partners, showing that with the right management, recovery is possible. It’s heartwarming to see these fish thriving again in rivers where they were once just a memory.
