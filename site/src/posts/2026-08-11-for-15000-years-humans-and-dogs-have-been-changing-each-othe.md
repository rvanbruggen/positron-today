Isn't it fascinating how dogs have been our companions for over 15,000 years? Archaeologists have found evidence of dogs buried alongside humans in ancient graves, highlighting this deep bond. Professor Peter Mitchell's new book dives into the complex evolution of dogs, showing how they adapted to different environments and human needs. From Arctic sled dogs to tropical hunting companions, these furry friends have truly shaped and been shaped by our lives.
