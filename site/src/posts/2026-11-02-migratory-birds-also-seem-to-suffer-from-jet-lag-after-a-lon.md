A long migration can leave birds dealing with more than tired wings: they also seem to experience jet lag. Their internal rhythms may need time to catch up after a demanding journey. It’s a fascinating reminder that migration affects birds in ways that go beyond the miles they cover. And here's the thing: even these seasoned travellers may need a little time to adjust after arrival.
