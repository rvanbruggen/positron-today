Researchers have described a tiny new egg-eating snake from forests near Ethiopia’s Bale Mountains. Less than eight inches long, the white-throated egg-eater feeds on the eggs of small birds. Its white belly scales and blue-shimmering skin between its scales help distinguish it from a similar species. And here's the thing: females can rub their scales to startle predators, but do it silently. Three more egg-eating snake species from the region are still awaiting their scientific descriptions.
