So, there's some exciting research coming out of the University of Saskatchewan suggesting that public transit might actually boost our health! It seems that when people use transit, they tend to walk more, which contributes to their physical activity. Plus, it can enhance social participation and well-being, which is just fantastic. The ongoing INTERACT study is getting fresh funding, and researchers are eager to see how changes in transit systems can positively impact our lives.
