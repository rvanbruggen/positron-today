So, the British Embassy in Santiago recently hosted an event that gathered key players to discuss the exciting potential of offshore wind energy in Chile. The studies presented by ORE Catapult revealed that Chile has fantastic conditions for this industry, but some important steps are needed to make it a reality. This includes improving regulations and ensuring that local capabilities are developed. It's all about creating a vibrant market that can attract investment and create skilled jobs, especially in regions like Biobío.
