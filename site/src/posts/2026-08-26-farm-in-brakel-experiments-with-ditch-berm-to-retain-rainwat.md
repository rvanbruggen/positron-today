It's exciting to see how a farm in Brakel is trying out innovative methods to tackle rainwater retention and erosion. By implementing a ditch-berm system, they're aiming to hold onto precious rainwater for longer periods, which is crucial for sustainable farming. This approach not only helps the crops but also benefits the surrounding environment. It's a fantastic example of how agriculture can adapt to the challenges posed by climate change.
