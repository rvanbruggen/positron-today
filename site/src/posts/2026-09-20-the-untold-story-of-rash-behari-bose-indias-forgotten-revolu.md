So, here's the thing - Rash Behari Bose was absolutely fearless, a revolutionary who challenged British rule long before Subhas Chandra Bose became famous, and yet most of us have never heard of him. The British put a massive bounty on his head and chased him across 17 different hideouts, but this guy was clever - he once disguised himself as an Odia priest and walked right past police searching for him. Beyond his daring escapes and his involvement in the 1912 attack on Viceroy Lord Hardinge, Bose played a crucial role in the Ghadar movement and helped establish the Indian Independence League and Indian National Army. And here's what really gets me - he eventually handed leadership of the INA to Subhas Chandra Bose himself, showing the kind of selfless dedication these freedom fighters had for India's independence.
