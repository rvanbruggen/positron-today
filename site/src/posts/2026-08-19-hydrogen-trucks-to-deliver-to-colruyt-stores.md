Colruyt is making strides in sustainable transport with the introduction of hydrogen trucks. These new vehicles, which complement their existing electric fleet, aim to reduce emissions significantly. With a range of up to 800 kilometers, they are perfect for long-distance deliveries. The company is targeting a 100% zero-emission fleet by 2035, showing their commitment to a greener future.
