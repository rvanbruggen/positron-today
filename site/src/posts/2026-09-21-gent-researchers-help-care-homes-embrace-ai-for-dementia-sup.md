So, I just read about some brilliant researchers at Artevelde University of Applied Sciences in Ghent who are doing something truly special. They are helping care homes figure out how to actually use artificial intelligence to support people with dementia. And here is the thing - it is not just about fancy tech, but about freeing up time for caregivers by automating admin tasks like voice-controlled reporting. Which brings me to the best part: this also means residents can rest better because sensors can monitor them without constant interruptions. I find it so inspiring to see technology being used to make life more human and compassionate.
