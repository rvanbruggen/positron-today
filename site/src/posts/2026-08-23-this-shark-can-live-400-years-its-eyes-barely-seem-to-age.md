Isn't it incredible that the Greenland shark can live for up to 400 years? Researchers have discovered that these fascinating creatures have a unique DNA repair mechanism that helps them maintain their eyesight without showing signs of aging. This study challenges previous assumptions about their vision, suggesting they are well-adapted to their dark Arctic habitat. It's amazing to think about how much we can learn from these ancient sharks about longevity and the mysteries of aging.
