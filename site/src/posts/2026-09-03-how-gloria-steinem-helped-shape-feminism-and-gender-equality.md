Gloria Steinem, a towering figure in second-wave feminism, has passed away at 92, leaving behind a legacy that reshaped gender equality. Her journey began in a time when women's rights were just starting to gain traction, and she became a powerful voice advocating for women's issues through journalism and activism. From founding Ms. magazine to championing reproductive rights, Steinem was relentless in her pursuit of equity for all women. Her experiences, including her own abortion story, fueled her passion and commitment to the feminist movement, inspiring generations to come.
