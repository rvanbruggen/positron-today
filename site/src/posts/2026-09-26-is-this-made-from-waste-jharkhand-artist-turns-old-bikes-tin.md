I love how Amrit Prakash Sah from Jharkhand sees beauty where others see waste. He collects discarded materials like old bikes and tins and transforms them into stunning public art that makes us rethink what we throw away. His Waste to Wonder Park is a fantastic example, turning over 1,000 tonnes of scrap into sculptures that inspire curiosity and promote recycling. And here’s the thing – when a child asks if something so beautiful can really come from waste, you know the art is doing its magic.
