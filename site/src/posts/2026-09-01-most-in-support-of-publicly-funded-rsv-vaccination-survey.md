A recent survey revealed that a significant majority of New Zealanders support publicly funded vaccinations for RSV, especially for infants and during pregnancy. RSV can lead to serious respiratory issues in young children, making this initiative crucial. Interestingly, support was notably higher among older respondents and those with higher education levels. Experts like Dr. Jutta Van den Boom emphasize the importance of such vaccinations to protect vulnerable children and reduce hospital admissions.
