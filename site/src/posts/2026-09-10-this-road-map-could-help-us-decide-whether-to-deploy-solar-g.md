A nonprofit in San Francisco has unveiled a detailed road map for solar geoengineering that could help us tackle climate change. This initiative, led by Reflective, aims to fill the gaps in our understanding of stratospheric aerosol injection, a method that mimics volcanic cooling effects. With a decade-long plan costing around $370 million, the hope is to responsibly accelerate research and guide funding. However, the conversation around who controls such powerful technology remains a critical and controversial topic.
