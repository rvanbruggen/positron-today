Isn't it fascinating how deep-sea isopods can survive for years without food? Researchers discovered that these incredible creatures likely acquired a gene from bacteria millions of years ago, which helps them slow their metabolism and endure long periods of starvation. This gene, found in their stomachs, is surprisingly similar to bacterial genes and plays a crucial role in their survival in the ocean depths. It's a remarkable example of evolution and adaptation that opens new doors for understanding deep-sea ecosystems!
