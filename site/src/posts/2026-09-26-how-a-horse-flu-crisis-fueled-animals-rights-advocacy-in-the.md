So: back in 1872, New York City faced a massive horse flu outbreak that left thousands of horses sick and struggling to work. This crisis spotlighted the harsh realities animals faced and stirred public concern about their treatment. Henry Bergh, founder of the ASPCA, stepped up big time to protect these vulnerable horses, inspired by his experiences abroad and a growing passion for animal welfare. And here's the thing - this outbreak helped fuel the early animal rights movement in America, showing how a health crisis can spark compassion and change.
