So, researchers have mapped chromosome-scale genomes from across the barley genus, Hordeum, to explore how its diversity evolved. They found major structural differences, signs of ancient genetic exchange and extensive changes in gene-regulating sequences. Together, these processes have shaped a remarkably varied group of plants. And here's the exciting part: this diversity could help breeders improve cultivated barley.
