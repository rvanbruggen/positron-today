The Ig Nobel Prizes celebrate the quirky side of science, showcasing research that makes us laugh and think. This year's ceremony in Zurich recognized ten unique studies, including one that scientifically defines a kiss and another that suggests wealth might lead to unethical behavior. One standout study revealed that cockroach milk is surprisingly nutritious, while another provided the best technique for blowing your nose. These projects remind us that science can be both amusing and insightful, sparking curiosity about the world around us.
