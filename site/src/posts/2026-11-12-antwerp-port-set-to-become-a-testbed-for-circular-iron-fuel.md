So, Antwerp’s port could soon become a real-world testing ground for circular iron fuel. The idea is to explore how iron can be used as a fuel and then recovered for reuse, creating a potentially circular energy cycle. That makes the port an intriguing place to test an emerging approach to cleaner industrial energy. I’m curious to see what the trials could teach us about bringing this technology closer to wider use.
