What a fantastic sight it was to see hundreds of kayakers paddling through Copenhagen harbour, proudly waving Greenlandic flags! This vibrant parade was held to celebrate the International Day of the World's Indigenous Peoples, showcasing solidarity with the people of Greenland. Organised by Uagut and ActionAid Denmark, the event drew enthusiastic crowds who gathered to cheer on the participants. Julie Rademacher, the chair of Uagut, highlighted the challenges faced by Greenlanders, making this show of support even more meaningful.
