The AfCFTA is creating exciting opportunities for Africa's textile industry by connecting cotton growers, spinners, and manufacturers across borders. This trade pact aims to build a competitive regional production network that retains more value on the continent. While there are challenges ahead, such as establishing effective rules of origin, the potential for specialization and collaboration is promising. If successful, this could transform the way textiles are produced and traded in Africa.
