So, here's the exciting part: electric vehicles might not just be for driving—they could actually help stabilize our power grid! With vehicle-to-grid technology, EVs can send energy back to the grid during peak demand, creating a win-win for drivers and utilities alike. Massachusetts is kicking off a pilot program to test this out, and it could lead to cheaper electricity for everyone. It's fascinating to think that the solution to our energy challenges might be parked right outside our homes!
