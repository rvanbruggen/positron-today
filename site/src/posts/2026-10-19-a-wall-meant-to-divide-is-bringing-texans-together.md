So: plans for a border patrol road near Big Bend’s stunning Santa Elena Canyon have sparked an unexpected show of unity. Residents have organized protests, kept watch over construction and rescued vulnerable plants, while local business owners have turned down lucrative offers from wall contractors. Democrats, Republicans and independent voices - including border sheriffs and political candidates - are joining calls to oppose the project and have a say in its planning. It’s a powerful reminder that protecting a beloved landscape can bring people across political divides together.
