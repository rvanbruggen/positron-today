Isn't it fascinating how AI is diving into the world of jazz? Researchers from the University of Cambridge have developed models that can identify the unique musical styles of legendary pianists like Chick Corea and Thelonious Monk. By analyzing over 84 hours of recordings, they’ve managed to capture those subtle nuances that make each musician special. This not only helps us appreciate their artistry but also serves as a tool for training the next generation of jazz musicians.
