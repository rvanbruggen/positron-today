In Portland, Urban Gleaners rescues surplus food and turns it into nourishing meals for local communities. Chef Bradley Cohen improvises with donated ingredients, transforming pork, fresh vegetables and a surprise tub of gochujang into a restaurant-worthy dinner. The nonprofit also delivers about 100,000 pounds of rescued food each month to around 50 schools, community centers and other locations. It’s a creative way to make good food go further and get it to people who need it.
