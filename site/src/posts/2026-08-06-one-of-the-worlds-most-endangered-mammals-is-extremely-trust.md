In the Chacabuco Valley of Chile, the rare huemul deer is making a comeback thanks to dedicated conservation efforts. Once hunted to near extinction, these trusting animals are now being protected by initiatives like the Huemul Rescue and Rehabilitation Center. The center recently treated its first patient, a young stag named Distraido, who has now been successfully released back into the wild. This marks a significant step forward in huemul conservation, ensuring that these beautiful creatures can thrive for generations to come.
