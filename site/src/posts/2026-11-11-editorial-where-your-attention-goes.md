I’ve been thinking about how a steady diet of negative news can affect our mood and make the world seem worse than it is. Mindfulness offers a simple way to notice how a headline makes us feel, while a cognitive behavioural lens can help us question thoughts that paint everything in the darkest colours. Positron.today is not a replacement for serious news, but a counterweight that gives good things a fair place in the picture. Try checking in with yourself after reading the news, then read a positive story and notice what changes.
