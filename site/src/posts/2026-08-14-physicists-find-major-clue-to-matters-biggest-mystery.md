So, physicists are getting closer to unraveling the mystery of why we have more matter than antimatter in the universe. They've discovered a crucial quantity called baryon number, which might explain this cosmic imbalance. It turns out that during the big bang, a tiny bit more baryonic matter was produced than antimatter, allowing us to exist today. This exciting research sheds light on the building blocks of matter and how they interact, revealing new insights into the universe's origins.
