Wow, what an incredible achievement! The Yayoi Kusama exhibition at Museum Ludwig in Cologne has attracted nearly 470,000 visitors, making it the most popular show in the museum's history. To accommodate the crowds, the museum even extended its hours into the early morning, which is just amazing. With around 300 works on display, including her iconic Infinity Mirror Rooms, this exhibition is a true celebration of Kusama's vibrant artistry. After this record-breaking run, it’s heading to Amsterdam, and I can't wait to see how it continues to inspire!
