A fascinating new study from Florida State University has unveiled incredible insights into the migration patterns of male green sea turtles. By tracking these turtles off the coast of Brazil, researchers found that while most migrate long distances after breeding, some prefer to stay put. This research is vital for conservation efforts, as understanding their movements helps protect their habitats. Plus, it sheds light on how changing temperatures could affect turtle populations in the future.
