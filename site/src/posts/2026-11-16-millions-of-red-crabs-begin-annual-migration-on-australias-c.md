Tens of millions of red crabs have begun their annual journey across Australia’s Christmas Island, turning roads and crossings into streams of red. After the wet-season rains, they are travelling from the forests to the coast to breed. Parks Australia prepares for the spectacle months ahead, clearing underpasses and building bridges and barriers to help keep the crabs safe from traffic. And here’s the thing: rainfall and the lunar cycle help the crabs time their journey so they can release their eggs into the ocean before dawn.
