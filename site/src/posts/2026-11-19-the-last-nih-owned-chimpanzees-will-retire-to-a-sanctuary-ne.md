So, a remarkable chapter is coming to a close: the last chimpanzees still owned by the U.S. National Institutes of Health are set to move to a sanctuary next year. Their retirement marks a major step in the shift away from using chimpanzees in biomedical research. At the sanctuary, they will have a place to live with care for their wellbeing and social needs. I find this a hopeful milestone for these intelligent animals, and a sign of how much attitudes toward research have changed.
