Ireland’s cervical cancer rate fell from 11 to 10.2 new cases per 100,000 women across the latest reported three-year periods. The HSE says the country remains on track to eliminate cervical cancer as a public health problem by 2040, meaning fewer than four new cases per 100,000 women each year. Around three-quarters of eligible women had attended screening, and almost 97% of women diagnosed between 2018 and 2022 received treatment within a year. HPV vaccination coverage is still below the WHO target, with 81.2% of girls vaccinated by age 14 in 2024. Meanwhile, screening has become available through 37 additional services, making it easier for more people to access care.
