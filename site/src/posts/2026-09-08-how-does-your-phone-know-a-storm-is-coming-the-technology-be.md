Isn't it fascinating how your phone can alert you about a storm before you even see the clouds? It all starts with an impressive network of weather radars, satellites, and sensors that gather atmospheric data from across India. Supercomputers then process this information, using advanced AI to make localized predictions about weather events. This technology not only keeps us informed but also helps us prepare for what’s coming, making our lives a little easier and safer.
