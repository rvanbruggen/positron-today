It's fantastic to see that nine out of ten new cars registered in Denmark are electric! Mads Rørvig from Mobility Denmark highlights this exciting trend, especially with 85.9 percent of the 16,165 new cars being electric. However, there's a concern about the upcoming increase in registration fees for electric vehicles, which he believes could dampen this success. Politicians are urged to reconsider this move, as making electric cars more expensive would be counterproductive to their popularity.
