India is making a bold move to bring back researchers who have settled abroad, aiming to reverse the brain drain. With generous fellowships and funding, the Prime Minister Research Chair Scheme is set to support 120 overseas researchers in strategic fields. However, some experts worry that these initiatives might not tackle the root causes of migration, like insufficient research infrastructure. Interestingly, as opportunities in India grow and US immigration becomes trickier, more researchers are considering returning home.
