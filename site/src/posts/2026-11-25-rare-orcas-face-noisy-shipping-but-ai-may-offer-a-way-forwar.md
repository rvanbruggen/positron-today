Rare orcas are under pressure from the constant noise of passing ships. That underwater din can make it harder for them to communicate and find food. So, here’s where AI could help: by analysing sounds in the sea, it may help researchers understand when and how shipping noise affects these whales. Better insight could point the way to quieter seas and stronger protection for the orcas.
