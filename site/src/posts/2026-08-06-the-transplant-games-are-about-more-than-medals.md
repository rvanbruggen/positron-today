The Transplant Games are a remarkable celebration of life and identity for athletes who have undergone organ transplants. Participants, ranging from seasoned competitors to beginners, come together to share their stories and inspire one another. It's not just about winning medals; it's about building a supportive community that fosters hope and motivation. The event highlights how far transplant recipients have come, proving that they can lead active, fulfilling lives post-transplant.
