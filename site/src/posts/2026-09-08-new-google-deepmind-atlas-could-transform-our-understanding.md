I’m really excited about the new AlphaGenome Atlas from Google DeepMind! It predicts the effects of nine billion single DNA variants, which could revolutionize genetics research. Researchers can now easily access a comprehensive map of the human genome with just a browser. This tool could significantly speed up the discovery of genetic links to diseases, making it a game-changer for scientists everywhere.
