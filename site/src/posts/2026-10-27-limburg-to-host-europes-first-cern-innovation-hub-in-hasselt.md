Limburg will become the first region in Europe to host a CERN innovation hub, based in Hasselt. The initiative is an important recognition of the region’s ambitions and its focus on innovation. It will bring CERN’s expertise closer to local researchers, companies and innovators. So, this could give fresh momentum to collaboration and help turn scientific ideas into practical opportunities.
