So: researchers have turned a Chorus fibre-optic cable in Haast Pass into a sensitive earthquake sensor, using pulses of laser light to detect tiny vibrations in the ground. Over five months in 2023, an international team monitored hundreds of small quakes and gathered new insights into the Alpine Fault. Their findings reveal a roughly 200-metre-wide band of damaged rock, with several narrower zones where the fault has been especially crushed and sheared. Understanding this complex structure can help researchers estimate how future earthquakes might rupture and support better preparation across Aotearoa.
