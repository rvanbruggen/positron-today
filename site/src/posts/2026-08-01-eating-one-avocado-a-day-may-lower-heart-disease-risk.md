So, it turns out that munching on just one avocado daily might help lower heart disease risk for adults dealing with obesity. Researchers at Penn State found that this tasty fruit is linked to a decrease in those pesky LDL particles that carry cholesterol. Even a modest 4% reduction in heart disease risk is a step in the right direction! It's fascinating how a small dietary change can make such a difference.
