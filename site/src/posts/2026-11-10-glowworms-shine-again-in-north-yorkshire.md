What a lovely sight to look forward to: glowworms may soon light up a North Yorkshire reserve where they have been absent for a century. Wildlife enthusiast Peter Cooper has raised their larvae in his garden shed, volunteering his time to help bring these rare insects back. This year, he plans to release 1,900 larvae at Nosterfield Nature Reserve, with local volunteers helping their population recover. And here's the exciting part: success will only be confirmed when the wild-born glowworms mature and shine in 2030.
