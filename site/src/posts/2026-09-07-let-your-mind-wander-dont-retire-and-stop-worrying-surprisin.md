Dr. Norman Swan shares fascinating insights on how to protect our brains from cognitive decline. Instead of fearing deterioration, he encourages us to embrace cognitive change, highlighting that our brains can improve with age. He emphasizes the importance of building neural scaffolding and suggests that activities like exercise and a healthy diet can support this process. Plus, he reminds us that it’s never too late to start taking care of our cognitive health.
