So, the artificial beaches of Maasvlakte 2 near Rotterdam are turning out to be a remarkable treasure trove of Ice Age fossils. The sand was dredged from the North Sea, once part of Doggerland, where mammoths, woolly rhinos and other animals lived before the landscape disappeared beneath the water. Volunteers and families search the shore, while palaeontologists help identify and record finds such as ancient teeth and bones. Their discoveries enrich a growing scientific database, and new fossils keep arriving as more seabed sediment is brought ashore for coastal protection.
