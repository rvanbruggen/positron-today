So, our gut bacteria may do more than simply stay put as we age. A study of more than 10,000 people scanned gut microbes for genetic variation and compared it with almost 250 traits, including age and cardiovascular fitness. The researchers identified an adaptation in a common gut bacterium that could help it withstand oxidative stress, which increases with age. Few of the mutations were associated with disease, offering a fascinating glimpse into how our microbial communities change over time.
