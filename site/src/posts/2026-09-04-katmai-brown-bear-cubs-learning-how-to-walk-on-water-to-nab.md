In the stunning Katmai National Park, brown bear #284, known as Electra, is teaching her adorable cubs how to fish for salmon. This unique skill is part of a bear culture where knowledge is passed down from mother to cub, much like in human families. Electra learned her fishing techniques from her own mother, showcasing the importance of tradition in the bear community. With Fat Bear Week around the corner, these cubs are getting ready to show off their newfound skills!
