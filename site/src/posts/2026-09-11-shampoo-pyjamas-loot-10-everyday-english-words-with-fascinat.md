Isn't it amazing how many words we use every day come from Hindi? Take 'shampoo' for instance, which traces back to 'champi' meaning head massage. As we celebrate Hindi Diwas, it's a great reminder of the rich linguistic exchange between India and the English-speaking world. From 'bungalow' to 'loot', these words tell stories of culture, trade, and history that connect us all.
