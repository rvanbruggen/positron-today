Isn't it fascinating how the English language has evolved over the centuries? Filmmaker and dialect coach Simon Roper takes us on a journey through time, showcasing the drastic changes in dialect from Old English to modern English. He even highlights The Great Vowel Shift, a pivotal moment that transformed how we pronounce words. It's amazing to think that what sounds familiar to us today was once completely different!
