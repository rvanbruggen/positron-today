Florida is grappling with harmful algal blooms, and shellfish farming might just be the solution! These hardworking clams and oysters filter out excess nutrients from the water, improving overall water quality. One oyster can filter over 50 gallons of water a day, which is pretty impressive, right? By increasing shellfish populations, Florida could not only tackle its algal bloom issues but also restore its natural oyster reefs, making it a win-win for the environment.
