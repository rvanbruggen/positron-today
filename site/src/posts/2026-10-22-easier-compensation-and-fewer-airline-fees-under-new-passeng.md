So, after nearly 13 years of negotiations, the EU has adopted updated rules to strengthen air passenger rights. Compensation remains €250 to €600 for arriving at least three hours late, but passengers will have nine months to claim, and airlines must respond within 30 days. The rules also bring practical wins, including a free personal item, clearer ticket pricing, fewer fees and more options to arrange replacement transport after a cancellation. And here's the thing: compensation rates will not increase, and free hotel accommodation during long disruptions will be capped at three nights.
