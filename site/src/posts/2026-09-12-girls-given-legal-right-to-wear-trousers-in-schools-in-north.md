This is such an exciting development for girls in Northern Ireland! They now have the legal right to wear trousers in schools, which is especially helpful during those chilly winter months. The change is part of a broader effort to ensure comfort and dignity for students, and it’s great to see young campaigners being recognized for their hard work. Schools will need to adapt their uniform policies by the next academic year, making this a significant step forward for equality in education.
