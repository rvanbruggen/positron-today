How exciting is this? Zebra sharks, once on the brink of extinction, are being rewilded for the first time ever! Thanks to a collaborative effort involving 60 aquariums across 15 countries, 82 shark pups have been released into a massive sanctuary in Indonesia. These little guys are doing great, foraging and staying safe from illegal fishing. This innovative approach could pave the way for future shark conservation efforts around the globe!
