What an inspiring journey! T K Anuradha, who was captivated by the moon landing as a child, became ISRO’s first woman satellite project director. Her innovative approach to controlling satellites has made a significant impact in the field of space technology. Leading an all-women team, she successfully manoeuvred GSAT-12 into its final orbit, showcasing the power of quiet innovations in space. This story not only highlights her achievements but also the importance of following one's dreams.
