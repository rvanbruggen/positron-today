So, it turns out that tigers, those fiercely solitary creatures we’ve always known, can actually team up! Researchers filming in Nepal captured two tiger mothers, Goma and Jugini, taking care of each other's cubs in a heartwarming display of cooperation. This adorable behavior challenges everything we thought we knew about tiger parenting. It’s a reminder that even apex predators can show vulnerability and collaboration, which is just fascinating!
