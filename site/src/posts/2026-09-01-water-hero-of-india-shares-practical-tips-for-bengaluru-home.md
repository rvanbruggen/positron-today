Bengaluru, known for its beautiful monsoons, faces a water crisis due to poor rainwater management. Subhajit Mukherjee, the Water Hero of India, emphasizes the importance of harvesting rainwater at home. By channeling rainwater into storage tanks or recharge pits, households can significantly reduce their reliance on treated water. His initiative, 'Mera Chhat, Mera Pani,' encourages everyone to make their rooftops a source of water, helping to replenish the city's groundwater and combat urbanization challenges.
