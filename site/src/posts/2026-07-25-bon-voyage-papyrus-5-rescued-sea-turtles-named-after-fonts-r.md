What an exciting week for sea turtles! Five rehabilitated Kemp’s ridley sea turtles have been released back into the ocean off Cape Cod after a successful seven-month recovery. Each turtle has been named after a font, which adds a fun twist to their journey. With the help of the New England Aquarium, these turtles are now ready to thrive in the wild, and their progress will be tracked to aid conservation efforts.
