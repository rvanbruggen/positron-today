After 16 years of careful work, the National Archives and its partners have digitized more than 180,000 pension applications from the War of 1812 - a remarkable 7.2 million pages. These fragile records preserve vivid details about veterans, their families, fellow soldiers and everyday life in the 19th century. And here's the thing: handwriting recognition makes the collection searchable by names and words across the pages, not just by the veterans who applied. The records are free to explore on Fold3, with copies also being added to the National Archives catalog.
