So, England is making incredible strides towards eliminating hepatitis C, a silent but serious disease. Since 2015, over 100,000 people have been diagnosed and treated, which is just fantastic news! While there's still a goal to reduce mortality by 65%, the country is on track to meet the World Health Organization's 2030 target. The NHS is encouraging those at risk to get tested with a free home-testing kit, which is such a great initiative to ensure everyone gets the support they need.
