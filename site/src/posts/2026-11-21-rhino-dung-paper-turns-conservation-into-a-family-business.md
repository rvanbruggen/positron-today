So, in Assam, father-daughter team Mahesh Chandra Bora and Nisha have turned an unusual idea into Elrhino Stationery, a business making paper from rhino dung. Local communities collect the dung and earn money from it, giving people living near reserves another reason to protect the animals. The plant fibers are washed out, pulped, mixed with recycled paper or cotton waste, then pressed into new sheets. Elrhino makes notebooks, bags, lampshades and more - a clever way to support livelihoods while celebrating wildlife.
