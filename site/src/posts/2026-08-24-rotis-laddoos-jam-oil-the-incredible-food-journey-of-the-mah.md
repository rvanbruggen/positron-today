Imagine a tree that gives you flowers for eating, rotis for making, laddoos for savoring, and oil for cooking! The mahua tree is a true treasure across India, with communities creatively using its flowers, fruit, and seeds. From delicious rotis in Telangana to sweet laddoos in Odisha, the versatility of mahua is simply amazing. It's not just about food; it's about culture and tradition, bringing people together through shared culinary experiences.
