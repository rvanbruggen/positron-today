It's exciting to see that nearly 40 percent of new cars in France are now electric! This surge is driven by rising fuel prices and strong government incentives that make electric vehicles more accessible. Interestingly, while Germany is also seeing growth, France is pulling ahead with a record share of fully electric cars. The focus on smaller, affordable electric models aligns perfectly with the offerings of French manufacturers like Renault and Stellantis.
