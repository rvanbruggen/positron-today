So, Groen is making room for nuclear power on the path to a fully renewable energy system. At its congress in Antwerp on Saturday, members of the Flemish green party added nuclear energy as a transition technology to the party programme. The proposal received 70% of the votes. It’s a notable update to how Groen sees the energy transition.
