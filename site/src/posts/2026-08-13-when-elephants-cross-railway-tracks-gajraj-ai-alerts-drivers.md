Isn't it amazing how technology can help protect wildlife? Indian Railways has developed an AI system called Gajraj Suraksha that detects elephants near railway tracks using vibrations picked up by fibre optic cables. This gives train drivers crucial time to slow down or stop, preventing tragic collisions. With around 81 elephants dying in train accidents recently, this innovation could save many lives and ensure safer journeys for both elephants and trains.
