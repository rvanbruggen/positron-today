In Tamil Nadu, a simple yet impactful initiative has emerged to help birds find water in urban areas. The Iyarkkaiyai Nesi Public Welfare Trust has created low-cost water troughs, costing just Rs 30 each, to combat the lack of accessible drinking sources for birds. Over the past three years, they've distributed more than 6,450 troughs to schools, government offices, and individuals, encouraging community involvement in bird conservation. It's heartwarming to see how this project not only helps our feathered friends but also brings people together to care for nature.
