NASA's Swift Observatory has made an incredible discovery of a supermassive black hole far from a galaxy's center, tearing apart a star in a rare event. This 'orphan' black hole, about 1 million times the mass of our Sun, was first detected through a bright flare in 2025. The event, known as a tidal disruption event, radiated with the brilliance of 10 billion suns at its peak. This finding not only confirms a new technique for spotting wandering black holes but also opens up exciting possibilities for future astronomical research.
