I find it fascinating how South African designer Wanda Lephoto highlights that Africa’s true fashion value isn’t just in the materials or factories, but in the rich cultural stories woven into every piece. So: these garments carry histories from family photos to traditional objects, making them unique and impossible to replicate elsewhere. Lephoto sees design as a conversation with the past, blending memory and spirituality into fashion that lasts and can be inherited. And here’s the thing - this approach challenges fast fashion by emphasizing sustainability, craftsmanship, and cultural authorship that truly shapes Africa’s identity on the global stage.
