So: Kyiv’s Namelaka has been named Europe’s most beautiful café at the Restaurant & Bar Design Awards 2026, winning in both the Café and Color categories. Its joyful interior, created by Nemov Studio, puts warm pink and five varieties of pink marble centre stage. Subtle ornament, elegant materials and a marble bar nod to historic Parisian pastry shops, while the designers say the space celebrates Ukrainian pastry culture in its own distinctive way. And here’s the thing - this café is designed to be remembered as a feeling as much as a place.
