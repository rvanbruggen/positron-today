As the temperatures in Rome rise, the Colosseum is now offering guided night tours until 11pm on four nights a week. This is such a fantastic way to experience the ancient site without the scorching heat! With up to 275 visitors allowed per tour, it’s no wonder the summer program is fully booked. The director of the Colosseum Archaeological Park mentioned that this extension provides a cooler alternative for tourists during the heatwaves.
