At Chester Zoo in England, keepers have welcomed two endangered Malay peacock-pheasant chicks, a significant boost for a species threatened by rainforest loss. When a first-time mother abandoned her egg, an experienced bronze-tailed peacock-pheasant stepped in as a foster parent, while a keeper used a rubber glove filled with warm water to help another chilled hatchling. Thankfully, the mother accepted the second chick the next morning and has raised it successfully ever since. With only about 15 Malay peacock-pheasants in European zoos, these chicks strengthen a conservation breeding program and bring hope for future generations.
