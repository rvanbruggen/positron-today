Dunedin's sea lions are truly living their best lives, and it's all thanks to a successful conservation story. Photographer Derek Morrison captures their playful nature, revealing how these magnificent creatures love to interact with their surroundings. With a population that has flourished over the last 30 years, these sea lions are reclaiming their territory and bringing joy to the local community. It's heartwarming to see how they thrive in Dunedin, making it a wonderful place for wildlife enthusiasts.
