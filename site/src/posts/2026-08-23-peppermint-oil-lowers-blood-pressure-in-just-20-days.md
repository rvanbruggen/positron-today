So, it turns out that daily peppermint oil might be a game-changer for those with mildly elevated blood pressure! Researchers found that just 100 microliters taken twice a day for 20 days led to an average drop of 8.5mmHg in systolic blood pressure. This could be a simple and cost-effective alternative to traditional medications, which often come with side effects. The study highlights the potential of peppermint oil as a natural remedy, making it an exciting option for millions around the world.
