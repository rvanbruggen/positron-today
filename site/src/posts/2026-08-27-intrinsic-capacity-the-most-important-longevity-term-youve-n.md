So, there's this fascinating concept called 'intrinsic capacity' that could change how we think about aging and longevity. It emphasizes not just the absence of disease, but the overall well-being and functional abilities of individuals as they age. Which brings me to the idea that this term might just be the key to unlocking a healthier, happier life in our later years. It's all about focusing on what we can do, rather than what we can't, and that's something to get excited about!
