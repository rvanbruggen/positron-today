Waymo’s latest data shows its driverless cars crash far less often than human drivers, with significant reductions in injury-causing incidents across five cities. The company has logged 271 million driverless miles, which is like over 300 human lifetimes behind the wheel. Now, while this is impressive, it’s still just a tiny fraction compared to the trillions of miles humans drive annually. And here’s the thing: some experts caution that the data might not capture all traffic disruptions or edge cases, but overall, Waymo’s numbers are a strong argument for safer roads.
