So, what if laundry could get clean without water or a hot dryer? These industrial machines use pressurised liquid carbon dioxide to lift away grime, then let it return to gas and leave clothes dry. They can recapture more than 90 percent of the CO2, and Tersus says its larger machines save about 300 gallons of water per load. Now, appliance makers are exploring ways to bring the technology into homes, though making it compact and affordable while preserving its environmental benefits is still a challenge.
