It's fascinating how luck can be influenced by our actions! Professor Sean Johnson emphasizes that creating more 'collisions' with people and ideas can lead to unexpected opportunities. By stepping out of our comfort zones and engaging with strangers, we increase our chances of having lucky breaks. So, whether it's attending events or simply saying 'yes' to new experiences, movement is key to changing our luck.
