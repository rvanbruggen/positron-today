In Mandvi, India, skilled boatbuilders are crafting giant dhows, keeping a remarkable shipbuilding tradition alive. These historic vessels take shape through hands-on work and knowledge passed down across generations. The photographs bring us close to the people, materials and impressive scale behind each ship. And here's the thing: this is not just about building boats, but about carrying a living piece of maritime heritage into the future.
