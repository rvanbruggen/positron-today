So, a spectacular supernova that appeared in the sky in 1181 is now being seen in remarkable detail. The Gemini telescope has photographed this cosmic remnant, giving astronomers a striking new view of an event recorded centuries ago. It’s a wonderful connection between historical observations and modern astronomy. And here’s the thing: the more clearly we can see these remains, the more we can explore the story of this ancient stellar explosion.
