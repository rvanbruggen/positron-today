A walk across his family’s farm led Jim Irvine to pottery fragments and a remarkable discovery beneath a field in Rutland, England. Satellite imagery helped reveal a Roman villa complex, including a huge mosaic depicting scenes from the Trojan War. Research published in 2025 found that one detail in the mosaic points not to Homer’s Iliad, but to a lost tragedy associated with Aeschylus. And here’s the thing: an unusually wet winter, a change of walking route, and even a swarm of bees all helped bring this ancient surprise to light.
