I’m really excited about the recent FDA approval of daraxonrasib, a new drug that could change the game for pancreatic cancer patients. This medication has shown to significantly extend survival rates, especially for those with specific gene mutations. It’s fascinating how it targets the KRAS protein, which is often stuck in the 'on' position in cancer cells. This breakthrough could mean a future where chemotherapy isn't even necessary for these patients, which is just incredible!
