It's amazing how a clear glass of water can still hide harmful substances like arsenic and fluoride. Researchers at IIT Guwahati have developed a rotating-anode electrocoagulation reactor that can remove up to 98.2% of arsenate and 91.8% of fluoride in just minutes. This innovative approach not only makes the process more efficient but also keeps costs low, making clean water more accessible. It's a fantastic step forward in tackling a serious health issue for millions in India!
