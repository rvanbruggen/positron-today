So, there's this amazing seed nursery in Friesland called Cruydt-Hoeck that's on a mission to restore Dutch biodiversity. They grow native plants and produce flower seeds, which are essential for local ecosystems. Jasper Helmantel, one of the owners, emphasizes the importance of sharing knowledge and experiences to boost biodiversity together. It's really inspiring to see how they connect with various partners to make a positive impact on nature.
