Isn't it fascinating how our ability to handle stress shifts with the seasons? During winter, many of us feel more sluggish and less social, while spring and summer can bring a burst of energy and activity. This article dives into the science behind these changes, highlighting how our internal clocks and external rhythms influence our resilience. So, whether you're a night owl or an early bird, understanding these patterns can help us better manage stress throughout the year.
