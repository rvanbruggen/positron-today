Most white dwarfs shine white, but new analysis of nearby stars reveals that some glow pink, yellow and other colours. And here's the surprising part: a few even appear green, a colour not previously seen in any star. These findings show that white dwarfs can be far more colourful than their name suggests. What a delightful reminder that the cosmos still has surprises in store.
