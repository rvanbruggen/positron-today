Ireland is at a crossroads, and the shift towards organic farming could be the bold move we need to escape the grip of volatile markets and harmful fertilizers. Despite the challenges, a quiet revolution is happening among farmers who are embracing this sustainable approach. The global organic market is booming, projected to reach €575 billion by 2034, which presents a fantastic opportunity for Ireland. By going organic, we can protect our natural resources and create a more resilient agricultural model that prioritizes quality over quantity.
