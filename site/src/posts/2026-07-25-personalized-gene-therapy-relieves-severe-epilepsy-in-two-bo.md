A groundbreaking personalized gene therapy has shown remarkable results in two boys suffering from severe epilepsy. By targeting specific neurons, the treatment not only reduced their seizures but also improved their developmental skills. One of the boys even walked independently for the first time after the therapy. This innovative approach opens new doors for treating debilitating neurological disorders, which is truly exciting!
