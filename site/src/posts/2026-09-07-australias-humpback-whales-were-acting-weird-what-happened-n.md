So, in 2009, tourism operators in Australia noticed humpback whales behaving unusually by 'fluking'—floating upside down with their tails in the air. This sparked a fascinating research effort that revealed the Whitsundays as a crucial calving ground for these majestic creatures. The collaboration between citizen scientists and researchers uncovered that this fluking was actually a breeding behavior. It's amazing how a little curiosity led to the discovery of a whale maternity ward in the Great Barrier Reef!
