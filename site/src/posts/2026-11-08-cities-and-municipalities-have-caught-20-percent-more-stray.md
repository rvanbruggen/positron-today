Cities and municipalities have caught, on average, 20 percent more stray cats since 2018. The increase highlights the ongoing importance of animal welfare and managing stray cats in local communities. The figures show a rise in captures, though they do not explain why more cats are being caught. Local authorities continue to play a valuable role in responding to the needs of stray animals.
