In Khowai, a group of 100 women, once labeled ‘kachrawala’, has transformed their community's waste management system. They’ve moved from being marginalized workers to proud owners of their cooperative, the Khowai Waste to Wealth Producer Society. This change began with a simple question about their lack of control over their work, leading to a system where they now manage waste effectively and sustainably. It's inspiring to see how these women have taken charge and created a cleaner, healthier environment for everyone.
