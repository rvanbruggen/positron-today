Imagine a whole apartment community that sends almost no waste to landfills! At Brigade Meadows in Bengaluru, around 7,000 residents have come together to manage their waste in an incredible way. They compost, recycle, and scientifically dispose of waste, turning 800 kg of wet waste into compost for farmers. This inspiring model shows how other societies in India can adopt similar practices and make a real difference in waste management.
