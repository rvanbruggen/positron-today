On June 19, the Aquarium of the Pacific welcomed nearly 500 animals rescued from trafficking, including fish, invertebrates, and sharks. This wasn't a shopping spree, but a crucial rescue mission for animals in dire need of care. The dedicated staff worked tirelessly to stabilize these creatures, ensuring they receive the same high standard of care as all other arrivals. It's a reminder of the importance of responsible wildlife consumption and the ongoing fight against animal trafficking.
