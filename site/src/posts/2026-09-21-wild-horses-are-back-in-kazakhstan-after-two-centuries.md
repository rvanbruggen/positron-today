I am absolutely thrilled to share some wonderful news from the steppes of Kazakhstan! For the first time in over 200 years, two Przewalski’s horse foals have been born on Kazakh soil, marking a massive milestone for conservation efforts. It is just incredible to see these majestic animals adapting so well to their ancestral home after being wiped out by human expansion so long ago. And here is the thing: these little ones are proof that rewilding projects can actually work when we put our minds to it. I cannot wait to see how this herd grows and thrives in the wild.
