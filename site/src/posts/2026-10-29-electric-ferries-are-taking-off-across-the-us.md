Electric hydrofoil ferries are moving from novelty toward practical public transport, lifting above the water to glide more smoothly. Vessev says its design cuts drag by about 66%, helping reduce battery size, weight and costs while making more routes feasible. Vessev operates passenger services in New Zealand, while electric hydrofoil ferries are already carrying commuters in Stockholm and Norway has ordered 20. As battery technology advances, these vessels could help cut waterfront pollution and offer a more efficient way to travel.
