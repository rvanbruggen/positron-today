It's fascinating to think about how mathematical thinking is often seen as something only a select few can grasp. This perspective can lead to the dangerous idea that math is irrelevant for the majority of people. But here's the thing: math is everywhere in our daily lives, and it can empower us all. We need to embrace it as a universal tool, not just a niche skill for the elite.
