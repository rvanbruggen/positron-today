European leaders gathered in Finnish Rovaniemi to tackle a hard truth - the Arctic is now a geopolitical hotspot that affects all of Europe, and they need to stop talking and start investing. The immediate trigger was a Russian drone attack near Poland over the weekend, but the deeper issue is clear: Arctic security means everything from icebreakers to satellites to managing new shipping routes as climate change opens the north. Leaders like Latvia's premier stressed they're in 'borrowed time' and must act smarter than simply reacting to Russian provocations. The EU is already committing serious money - €200 million for Greenland alone - with plans to present a comprehensive Arctic investment strategy to the European Commission in October.
