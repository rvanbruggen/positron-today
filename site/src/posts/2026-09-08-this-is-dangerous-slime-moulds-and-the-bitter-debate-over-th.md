So, have you ever thought about slime moulds and their surprising capabilities? Toshiyuki Nakagaki's research has sparked a fascinating debate about what intelligence really means, especially when it comes to organisms like Physarum polycephalum. This remarkable slime mould not only solves mazes but also seems to improve transportation networks, which is mind-blowing! It's incredible to think that something without a brain can exhibit such complex behaviors, challenging our definitions of intelligence.
