So, here's the exciting news: a little physical activity during midlife can really help slow down cognitive decline later in life! Researchers from UT Health San Antonio found that exercise is particularly beneficial for Hispanic adults. They studied 402 participants and discovered that cardiovascular health factors play a crucial role in how our brains age. This research could lead to meaningful changes in public health strategies, especially for high-risk populations.
