I was reading about this fascinating experiment in the Netherlands called Oosterwold, and honestly, it just blew me away. Instead of the usual sprawl we see in Ireland, they have created a space where residents are actually required to grow food on half of their land. It is such a brilliant way to blend urban living with agriculture, creating these vibrant, connected communities. I love how it brings people together, whether they are passionate foodies or just looking for a more sustainable way to live. It really makes you think about how we could rethink our own planning back home.
