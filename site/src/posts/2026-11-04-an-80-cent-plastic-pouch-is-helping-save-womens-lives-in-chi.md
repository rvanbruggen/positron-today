An 80-cent plastic pouch is helping care teams in Nigeria spot dangerous bleeding after childbirth. Used to collect and measure blood loss, this simple tool can make a serious complication easier to recognize. That early warning gives clinicians a chance to respond sooner and help protect mothers’ lives. And here’s the thing: an affordable, practical idea can make a remarkable difference.
