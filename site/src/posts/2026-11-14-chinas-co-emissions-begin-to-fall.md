China’s CO₂ emissions are beginning to fall, a welcome sign in the global effort to tackle climate change. If the decline continues, it could mark an important shift in the world’s largest emitter. The trend will be worth watching as China’s economy and energy needs evolve. And here’s the thing: a sustained drop could make a real difference to the global climate outlook.
