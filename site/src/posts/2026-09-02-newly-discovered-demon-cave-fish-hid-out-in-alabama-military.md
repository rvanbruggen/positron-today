So, scientists have stumbled upon a fascinating new species called the demon cave fish in a hidden cave in Alabama. This eyeless creature was found completely by chance during a routine survey, which is just mind-blowing! It turns out that this fish and another known species have been cohabiting the same cave system for millions of years, yet they are not closely related. This discovery highlights how much biodiversity is still out there waiting to be uncovered, even in well-explored areas.
