So, researchers have found a fascinating way to use Starlink satellites to study Earth's upper atmosphere! By applying a technique called tomography, they created a two-dimensional map of thermospheric density, which is crucial for understanding satellite motion. This innovative approach not only enhances our knowledge of atmospheric conditions but also helps improve the safety of crowded orbits. It's exciting to think about how this could lead to better space weather forecasting and safer satellite operations in the future.
