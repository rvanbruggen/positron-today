I find it fascinating how scientists are now able to ‘watermark’ proteins designed by AI, which is a clever way to mark their work and keep it safe from misuse. So: this method could really help deter the creation of bioweapons by making it easier to trace the origin of synthetic proteins. And here’s the thing, it also protects the credit of researchers who develop these proteins, which is super important in science. Now, this could open up new doors for safer biotech innovation while keeping the community honest and accountable.
