Isn't it amazing how Hyundai is teaming up with Healthy Seas to tackle the issue of ghost nets? These abandoned fishing nets are a real menace to marine life, but now they're being transformed into car floor mats! Since 2013, Healthy Seas has pulled over 1,300 tons of these nets from the ocean, and Hyundai is recycling them into new products. This partnership not only cleans our oceans but also inspires the next generation to care for our planet.
