So: physicists have found direct evidence of a hidden pattern of electron pairs in uranium ditelluride, even after its superconductivity has disappeared. These pair density waves are like a lingering trace of the superconducting state, and scientists had predicted them nearly 20 years ago. Higher-quality samples let the team detect signatures that respond to temperature and magnetic fields as expected, and persist above the superconducting transition. This discovery offers a fresh clue to how unconventional superconductors behave.
