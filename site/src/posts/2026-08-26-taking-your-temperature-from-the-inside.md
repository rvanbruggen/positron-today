So, here's the exciting news! MIT engineers have developed a tiny ingestible temperature sensor that measures core body temperature accurately. At just six by four millimeters, it's small enough to swallow without any risk of obstruction. This innovative device can continuously send temperature updates and is powered by a simple coin cell battery. Imagine the possibilities for monitoring health in various situations, from athletes to those with compromised immune systems!
