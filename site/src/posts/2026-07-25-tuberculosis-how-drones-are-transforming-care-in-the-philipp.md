So, here's the exciting part: doctors in the Philippines are using drones to combat tuberculosis, which is a major health issue there. This innovative approach is particularly crucial in remote areas where access to medical services is limited. Dr. Heidi Sampang is leading the charge, flying samples to speed up testing and improve patient outcomes. It's amazing to see how technology can make such a positive impact on healthcare and save lives in these communities.
