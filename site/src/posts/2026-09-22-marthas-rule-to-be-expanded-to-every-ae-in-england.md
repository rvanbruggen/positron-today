So, I have some truly heartening news to share about the expansion of Martha’s rule across England. This vital initiative, which allows patients and their families to request a second medical opinion if they feel their care isn't quite right, is now heading to every emergency department. It is named after young Martha Mills, and honestly, seeing how her legacy is actively saving hundreds of lives is just incredible. Now, anyone in an A&E waiting room will have that extra safety net to ensure their voice is heard when it matters most. And here's the thing - it is such a powerful reminder of how advocacy can drive real, positive change in our healthcare systems.
