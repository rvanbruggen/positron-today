So: researchers in Sri Lanka were trying to trap mice with peanut butter, but the little critters just weren't interested. They decided to get creative and used burnt coconut instead, which led to the incredible discovery of a brand new species called the Dumbara spiny mouse. It took over two decades of careful genetic analysis to confirm this tiny, elusive creature was actually unique to the Knuckles Mountain Range. And here's the thing - it really highlights just how much hidden biodiversity is still waiting for us to find. I find it absolutely fascinating that such a small animal can keep scientists guessing for so long!
