In a remarkable turnaround, Zanskar has revived a struggling geothermal power plant in New Mexico. By drilling a new well that reaches 8,000 feet, they've managed to boost production significantly, generating over twice the electricity of the old setup. This success not only breathes new life into Lightning Dock but also opens up exciting possibilities for geothermal energy across the country. It's a fantastic example of how innovation can unlock hidden potential beneath our feet.
