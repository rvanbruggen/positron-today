The Muslim World League has kicked off an exciting initiative to combat extremist views in West Africa, bringing together Muslim and Christian leaders for a cooperative effort. At a conference in Abuja, they adopted the Abuja Declaration, which outlines a joint action plan for preventing conflict and promoting peace. This includes expanding educational and media programs to foster understanding and cooperation. With the backing of Nigerian President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, this initiative aims to address the root causes of extremism and strengthen social harmony in the region.
