So: at jam sessions in Dutch asylum centers, musicians from different backgrounds turn their stories and sounds into something new. Venezuelan singer Balvina Muñoz says music gives her an essential outlet and helps her feel more confident and in control. The Arnhem band De Niemanders builds songs with refugee musicians, while Orchestre Partout brings players together across eight centers and performs on major stages. And here's the thing: these sessions are more than a social project - they create vibrant music where cultures meet.
