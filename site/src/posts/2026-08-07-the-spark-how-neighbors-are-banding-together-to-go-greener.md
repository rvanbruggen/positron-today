In Marblehead, Massachusetts, a community-driven green revolution is taking shape, led by volunteers like Lisa Wolf who help neighbors transition to energy-efficient heat pumps. These heat pump coaches provide guidance and support, making the switch from fossil fuels less daunting for homeowners. With local knowledge and connections to contractors, they empower residents to embrace sustainable energy solutions. This grassroots effort not only reduces greenhouse gas emissions but also strengthens community bonds among those passionate about climate change.
