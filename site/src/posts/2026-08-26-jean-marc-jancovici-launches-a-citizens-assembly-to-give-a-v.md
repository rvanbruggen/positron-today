Jean-Marc Jancovici, a prominent climate expert, is spearheading a citizens' assembly aimed at amplifying public voices on climate issues ahead of the 2027 presidential elections. This initiative is all about engaging citizens directly in the conversation about climate change and its impacts. Jancovici believes that by involving everyday people, we can foster a more democratic approach to tackling environmental challenges. It's an exciting step towards ensuring that the climate agenda reflects the concerns and ideas of the population.
