So, it turns out that moving your body is not just good for your health, but it can also significantly reduce serious stress! A large study found that just 30 minutes of physical activity each day can lower the risk of severe stress by about 26 percent. And here's the thing: even just two minutes of daily activity can help reduce that risk by around two percent. This research, conducted over eight years with data from over 74,000 people, really highlights the importance of staying active for our mental well-being.
