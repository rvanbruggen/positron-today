It's incredible to see how former poachers like Sunday Abiodun are now protecting the very forests they once hunted in. After realizing the impact of his actions on wildlife, he joined the Nigerian Conservation Foundation as a ranger. This unique approach of employing ex-hunters is not just innovative but also effective, as they have a deep understanding of the forest. By transforming their mindset, these individuals are becoming vital protectors of Nigeria's precious ecosystems.
