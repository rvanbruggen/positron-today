So here's the thing - flash floods are terrifyingly fast and often catch people completely off guard, as Laura Lin discovered when her Indiana home flooded in just hours. Now, scientists and weather forecasters are developing TACLS, a clever software system that combines satellite data with machine learning to spot flooding risks earlier than ever before. What makes this exciting is that those extra minutes or hours of warning could literally be the difference between life and death - as one weather official puts it, with TACLS you can actually save lives instead of issuing alerts too late. The technology works alongside existing tools like rain gauges and weather radars, but it's smart enough to learn patterns and predict dangerous conditions faster than humans alone can manage.
