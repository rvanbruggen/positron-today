When Sajitha and Mahesh decided to rebuild their 40-year-old home in Bengaluru, they focused on preserving its essence while making it more comfortable. They salvaged materials like teak wood and foundation stones, ensuring that almost nothing went to waste. The new house features a rainwater harvesting system and solar panels, which significantly reduce their utility bills. It's a beautiful example of sustainability and thoughtful design that prioritizes family comfort and environmental responsibility.
