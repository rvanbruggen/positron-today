So: NASA’s Perseverance rover has traveled 45.22 kilometers across Mars, setting a new distance record for any vehicle on another world. Guinness World Records marked the achievement with a commemorative plaque presented to the rover’s engineering twin on Earth. Before setting off again, Perseverance spent weeks investigating scientifically valuable ancient rocks, then rolled past the record when its work was done. New driving improvements and a system that helps it locate itself without GPS are making its journeys across challenging Martian terrain even more capable.
