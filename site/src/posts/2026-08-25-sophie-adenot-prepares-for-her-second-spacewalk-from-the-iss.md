So, here’s the exciting news! French astronaut Sophie Adenot is gearing up for her second spacewalk from the International Space Station. Just a week after her historic first outing, she’s set to finish tasks that were delayed due to some technical issues. She’ll be joined by her American teammate Anil Menon, making this another thrilling moment for space exploration.
