Drylands, covering 44% of the planet, are often overlooked but hold immense potential for sustainable practices and investments. With the world facing increasing drought, we can learn valuable lessons from dryland communities that have thrived under scarcity for generations. Their knowledge of diverse cropping and livestock management can guide us in adapting to changing climates. By investing in these regions, we can unlock their hidden value and create a more resilient future.
