I’m really excited about this new purple fabric that stays cooler than white cotton! Researchers from China and Australia have developed a material that not only looks great but also reflects nearly 88 percent of sunlight. This means it can keep you up to 11 degrees Fahrenheit cooler than regular purple cotton. It’s a fantastic breakthrough for those hot summer days, especially for outdoor workers who need relief from the heat.
