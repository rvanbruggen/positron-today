Isn't it fascinating that rattlesnakes have their own way of dealing with venom? Researchers found that a mix of proteins in the blood of the western diamondback rattlesnake can actually save mice from lethal doses of their own venom. This discovery could lead to new treatments for snakebites, which sadly claim tens of thousands of lives each year. It’s a brilliant example of how nature can inspire solutions to human health challenges.
