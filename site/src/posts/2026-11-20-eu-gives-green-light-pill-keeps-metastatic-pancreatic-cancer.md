The EU has given the green light to a pill for people with metastatic pancreatic cancer. In the reported results, the disease was kept under control in 90.2% of patients. That is an encouraging signal for a cancer that has spread beyond the pancreas, and it brings a new treatment option into view. And here's the thing: keeping cancer under control is not the same as curing it, but the result offers real hope.
