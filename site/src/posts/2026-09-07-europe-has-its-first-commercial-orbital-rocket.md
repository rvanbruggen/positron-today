Isar Aerospace has just made a huge leap for Europe by successfully launching its first fully commercial orbital rocket, the Spectrum. After a previous attempt ended in disaster, this launch from a Norwegian spaceport marks a new era for European space access. CEO Daniel Metzler emphasized that this achievement provides a true alternative for customers, allowing Europe to have its own sovereign access to space. This is particularly significant given the current global political climate, making 'buy European' more relevant than ever.
