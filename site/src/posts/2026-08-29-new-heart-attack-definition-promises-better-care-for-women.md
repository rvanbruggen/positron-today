Doctors have made a groundbreaking agreement on a universal definition of heart attacks, which is set to improve care for women significantly. Historically, many heart attack types affecting women were overlooked, leading to inadequate treatment. Now, with new guidelines, healthcare professionals will better recognize and address these conditions, which are often linked to childbirth and emotional stress. This change is a major step forward in ensuring that women receive the timely and effective care they deserve.
