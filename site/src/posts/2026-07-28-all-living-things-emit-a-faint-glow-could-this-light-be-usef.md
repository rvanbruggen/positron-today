So, it turns out that all living things emit a faint glow, and this discovery is sparking a lot of excitement! Researchers, led by Daniel Oblak, captured images of mice that showed a ghostly light emitted from their skin, which vanished after death. This phenomenon, known as biophotons, could potentially serve as subtle markers for health or disease, opening up a whole new field of study. Imagine if cells could communicate through light—how amazing would that be?
