It's incredible to see Syrians like Yasseen Tasabehji returning home after years away, driven by a desire to rebuild their country. After the fall of the al-Assad regime, he and his family felt a calling to contribute to their homeland, leading them to open a climbing gym in Damascus. They’re not just providing a fun activity, but also fostering community and self-esteem among young Syrians. Despite the challenges ahead, there's a palpable sense of hope and determination among returnees, eager to create a brighter future.
