So: ADHD and autism diagnoses have shot up in recent years, but this huge study from Denmark gives us a fresh perspective. It shows that kids diagnosed today look more like the general population than those diagnosed a decade ago. Which brings me to the idea that rising diagnosis numbers might partly reflect who is being identified, not just more cases. And here's the thing - this helps us understand the trend without jumping to conclusions about overdiagnosis or severity.
