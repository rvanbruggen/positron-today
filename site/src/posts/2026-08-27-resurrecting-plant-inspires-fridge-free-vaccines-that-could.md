I find it fascinating that a plant known as the resurrection fern has inspired a groundbreaking ‘fridge free’ vaccine! This new method could significantly reduce waste and improve vaccine access in low and middle-income countries. The powder-based vaccine remains stable at room temperature and can even endure extreme temperature changes. It’s amazing to think that this innovation could save lives by making immunizations more reliable and accessible.
