So, a meteorite made quite the entrance when it crashed through a New Jersey roof, and it’s got scientists buzzing with excitement! This rare CM1/2 carbonaceous chondrite offers a glimpse into the early solar system and how water influenced its chemistry. The homeowner's quick thinking helped preserve these pristine samples, making this event a remarkable find in meteorite history. It's fascinating to think about how this little rock could shed light on the origins of life itself!
