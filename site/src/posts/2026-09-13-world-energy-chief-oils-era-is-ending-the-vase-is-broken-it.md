Fatih Birol, director of the International Energy Agency and advisor to world leaders on energy policy, believes we're witnessing a historic turning point in global energy. He's confident that within just a few years, half of all cars sold globally will be electric - a fundamental shift that signals the beginning of the end for oil's dominance. So here's the thing: this transition isn't just about technology or economics, it's about a deeper reset in how the world thinks about energy. And according to Birol, there's no going back - the old era is truly broken.
