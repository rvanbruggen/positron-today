A new album called Echo in the Dark is transforming the ultrasonic calls of bats into captivating electronic music. Artists Hanna Tuulikki and Tommy Perman have blended these bat sounds with human vocals, creating a unique sound experience. The project aims to change perceptions of bats, which are often misunderstood, by inviting listeners to connect with nature through dance music. It's a delightful way to encourage curiosity and care for these essential creatures in our ecosystem.
