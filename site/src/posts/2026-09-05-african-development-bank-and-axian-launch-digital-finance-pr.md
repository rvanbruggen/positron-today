I'm really excited to share that the African Development Bank and AXIAN Group have teamed up to launch a groundbreaking programme aimed at empowering over 34,000 women-led businesses across Africa! This initiative tackles the critical issue of limited access to finance that so many women entrepreneurs face. By leveraging digital platforms like Mixx and Mvola, they're providing tailored financial products and essential training to help these businesses thrive. It's a fantastic step towards unlocking the immense potential of women entrepreneurs in Africa!
