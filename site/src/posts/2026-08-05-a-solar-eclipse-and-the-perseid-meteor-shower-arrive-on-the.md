August is shaping up to be an incredible month for skywatchers with a total solar eclipse and the Perseid meteor shower happening on the same day! On August 12, the eclipse will sweep across parts of northern Russia, Greenland, and Spain, while folks in the U.S. will catch a partial view. Later that night, the Perseids will peak, offering a spectacular show against the dark sky. And don't forget to look for Venus shining brightly in the west just after sunset in the following days!
