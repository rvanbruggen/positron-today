At a school in Gadchiroli, IAS officer Shubham Gupta tackled malnutrition by introducing an AI system to monitor school meals. This innovative approach analyzed meal quality and ensured that children received the proper nutrition they needed. Over eight months, the number of malnourished girls dropped significantly from 61 to just 20. It's a fantastic example of how technology can make a real difference in children's lives!
