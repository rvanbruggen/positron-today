It's exciting to see African manufacturers stepping up by turning locally grown cotton into high-value medical textiles. This shift not only aims to reduce reliance on imports but also taps into a growing market for healthcare products. Countries like Nigeria, Kenya, and Ethiopia are leading the charge, building hospitals and standardizing demand for essential items. With forecasts predicting significant growth in the textile market, the potential for local cotton production to thrive is brighter than ever.
