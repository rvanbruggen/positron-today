Wow, can you believe it? Italian divers have stumbled upon a Roman shipwreck off Sicily that’s over 2,000 years old! This incredible find includes hundreds of well-preserved containers that once held wine and olive oil. Culture Minister Alessandro Giuli calls it one of the most significant underwater archaeological discoveries in recent years. It’s amazing to think that someone like fisherman Giacomo De Mola could be among the first in 20 centuries to see this piece of history again!
