Researchers at Stanford Medicine have discovered a mechanism that could impact aging across the entire body. They focused on macrophages, immune cells that help clear out old and damaged cells. By blocking a specific receptor, EP2, in older mice, they found these animals became leaner and more agile, showing improvements in inflammation and overall health. While the results are promising, it's still uncertain if similar effects could be achieved in humans.
