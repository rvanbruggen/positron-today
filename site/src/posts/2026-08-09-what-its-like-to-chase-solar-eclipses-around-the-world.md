Chasing solar eclipses has become an exhilarating adventure for enthusiasts like Mark Griffiths, who drove from England to Spain to capture the upcoming total eclipse. With his high-spec cameras and a passion for celestial events, he describes this experience as an addiction, comparing it to the excitement of the World Cup for football fans. Meanwhile, Samantha and Gregory Phillips-Brown traveled from New York to Iceland, eager to witness the awe-inspiring phenomenon again after their first eclipse left them spellbound. The thrill of seeing the sun darken and the world quiet down is something they can't resist, and they are meticulously planning their trip to ensure they find the perfect viewing spot.
