The Life Education Trust is making a fascinating comparison between financial education and sex education, emphasizing the need for structured lessons in schools. With the new finance subject being introduced for primary students, it aims to ensure that all children gain essential knowledge about money, which is often left to chance discussions at home. Jo Malcolm Black highlights that a child's financial personality is largely formed by age seven, making this curriculum change crucial. By embedding financial literacy from Year 0, we can give every child a solid foundation to navigate their financial futures.
