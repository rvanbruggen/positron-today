Every year, millions trek to Kedarnath, and this time, Pradeep Sangwan wants them to take back a kilogram of waste. His #CarryMeBack campaign encourages pilgrims to pick up trash on their journey, aiming to remove 100 tonnes if just one lakh participants join in. It’s a brilliant way to turn visitors into active participants in preserving the environment. With early results showing over 1,000 kilograms collected, this initiative is a fantastic step towards a cleaner Himalayas!
