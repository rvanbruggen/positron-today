Among hundreds of results announced by OpenAI, one particularly caught mathematicians’ attention: an AI-assisted proof of the quasi-Riemann hypothesis, a weaker version of one of mathematics’ most famous open problems. The result does not settle the full Riemann hypothesis, but researchers say it could unlock many of its far-reaching consequences for understanding prime numbers. And here’s the thing: the proof appears to connect familiar mathematical ideas in a surprising new way, rather than introduce entirely new concepts. Number theorists are now working to understand how the proof fits together and what this remarkable achievement could lead to next.
