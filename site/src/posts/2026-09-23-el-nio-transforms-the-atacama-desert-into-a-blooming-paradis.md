I am absolutely fascinated by the incredible transformation happening in the Atacama Desert right now. Because of heavy rains linked to El Niño, this normally parched landscape has burst into a stunning carpet of violet and white flowers. It is truly a rare natural spectacle, with thousands of hectares coming to life after seeds lay dormant for so long. And here is the thing - nature really does find a way to surprise us when the conditions are just right. I find it so heartwarming to see such beauty emerge from one of the driest places on our planet.
