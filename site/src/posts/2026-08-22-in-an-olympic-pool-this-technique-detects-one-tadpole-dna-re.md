It's fascinating to see how cutting-edge DNA research is being used to tackle ecological challenges! In Kempen, scientists have developed a method that can detect even a single tadpole in an Olympic-sized pool. This innovative approach is aimed at combating the invasive American bullfrog, which poses a threat to local ecosystems. It's a great example of how technology can help us protect our environment and maintain biodiversity.
