The European Parliament has approved proxy voting for members who are pregnant or have recently given birth, so they no longer lose their vote when taking maternity leave. A colleague from the same political group can vote on their behalf for up to nine months, from three months before the due date until six months after birth. The change passed by 627 votes to 25, with five abstentions. So: elected representatives no longer have to choose between caring for a newborn and representing voters, and Dutch MEP Lara Wolters hopes similar arrangements will also support fathers and other forms of parenthood.
