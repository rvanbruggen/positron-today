On August 12, a total solar eclipse will allow researchers to study the Sun's corona for about two minutes, a rare opportunity since this layer is usually hidden. Scientists are eager to understand why the corona is so much hotter than the Sun's surface, and they will gather data in Spain and Iceland. This event is part of a series of total eclipses, making it even more exciting for astrophysicists. By analyzing the corona, they hope to improve predictions of space weather that can impact technology on Earth.
