Researchers at McMaster University have discovered that the hormone GDF15 does more than just curb appetite; it also protects the liver from inflammation. This finding opens up new possibilities for treating advanced fatty liver disease, which affects millions globally. Interestingly, GDF15 can reduce liver inflammation and scarring without any weight loss occurring. This shifts our understanding of the hormone's role in the body and suggests it could be a key player in defending against chronic liver injury.
