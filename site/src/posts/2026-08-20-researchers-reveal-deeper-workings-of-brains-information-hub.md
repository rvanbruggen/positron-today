I find it fascinating how our brain processes so much information simultaneously, especially when we’re driving. New research from the University of Iowa dives into the role of the frontoparietal cortex, which acts like an air traffic controller for our thoughts. It helps filter and prioritize information based on what we need at any moment, especially when facing uncertainty. This study not only sheds light on decision-making but also opens doors for understanding conditions like ADHD and schizophrenia.
