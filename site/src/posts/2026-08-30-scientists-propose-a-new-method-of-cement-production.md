Cement is essential worldwide, but its production emits over a billion tons of CO2 annually. Now, researchers from Stanford University have developed a plasma heating method that uses electricity instead of fossil fuels, significantly reducing emissions. This innovative process heats materials quickly, creating clinker almost 100 times faster than traditional methods. Plus, it promotes a circular economy by utilizing recycled cement waste, paving the way for a more sustainable future.
