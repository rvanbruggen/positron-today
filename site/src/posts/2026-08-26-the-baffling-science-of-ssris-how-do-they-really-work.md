Every year, millions rely on SSRIs, yet the exact way they work remains a mystery. Despite their widespread use, scientists are still piecing together how these medications affect our brains and bodies. Recent discussions have sparked debate about their overuse and withdrawal risks, highlighting the need for more research. As we enter a new era of understanding, the hope is that clearer answers will emerge about treating depression and the role of SSRIs.
