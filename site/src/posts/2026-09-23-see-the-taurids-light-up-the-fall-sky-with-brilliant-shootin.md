I love this time of year because the Taurid meteor showers put on a spectacular show that lasts for months! Starting in mid-September and going until mid-December, these shooting stars come from debris left by Comet 2P/Encke. The Southern Taurids peak around November 4 to 5, just after Halloween, with a great chance to see bright fireballs lighting up the night. And here's the thing - you don’t need any fancy gear, just a dark spot and patience to enjoy this celestial party.
