So, what began as a plan for handling rainwater in Ballerup has grown into something much more ambitious: a vision of future landscape art. The project reuses the familiar concrete paving tiles once seen across 1960s and 70s suburbs, arranging them in a striking diamond pattern. I love how small paths wind through the house’s exuberantly wild surroundings. And here’s the thing: it is the project’s bigger perspective that makes this transformation feel so inspiring.
