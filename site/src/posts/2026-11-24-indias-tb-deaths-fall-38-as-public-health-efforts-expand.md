India’s estimated tuberculosis death rate fell by 38% between 2015 and 2025, a bigger decline than the global average. Community-based screening, chest X-rays and rapid molecular tests are helping find cases earlier and connect people with treatment. Nutrition support, counselling and local follow-up can also make it easier for patients to complete the months-long treatment. And here’s an important distinction: fewer deaths do not mean infections fell by the same proportion.
