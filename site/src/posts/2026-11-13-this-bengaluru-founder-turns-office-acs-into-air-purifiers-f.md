When Aayush’s father moved to Delhi in 2017, doctors linked his health problems to severe air pollution. The family bought an air purifier, but Aayush realised that one device could not clean the air throughout their home. He began exploring whether office air conditioners could also filter pollutants, using the fans and filters already built into the systems. With his Bengaluru startup Clairco, he is working to make cleaner air more affordable and help 15 lakh people breathe easier.
