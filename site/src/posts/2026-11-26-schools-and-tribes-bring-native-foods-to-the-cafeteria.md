The Buffalo Youth Nation Project is bringing Indigenous foods like blue Hopi corn, fresh produce and buffalo meat to schools and community food lodges in Wyoming. The effort helps restore Indigenous food sovereignty by supporting communities’ ability to grow, choose and share ancestral foods. At school, these ingredients also spark conversations between elders and students, helping Native culture and food knowledge reach a new generation. It’s a nourishing way to support community health while strengthening connections to the land, animals and tradition.
