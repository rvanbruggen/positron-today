So: Amazon is leading a $20 million investment to help conserve water in the stressed Colorado River Basin, which supports millions of people and farmland. The new Colorado River Basin Collaborative aims to boost water conservation through projects like leak detection, irrigation efficiency, and canal lining. Amazon is already backing two projects, including leak repairs in Orange County and smart irrigation on tribal farmland using real-time sensors. And here's the thing - this corporate coalition has already raised over $33 million and hopes to reach $100 million to protect this vital water source.
