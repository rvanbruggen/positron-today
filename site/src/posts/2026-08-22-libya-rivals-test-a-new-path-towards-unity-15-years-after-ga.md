So, it's been 15 years since Gaddafi's fall, and Libya is finally seeing some movement towards unity! In a historic first, rival legislatures have agreed on a budget of 190 billion dinars, which is almost $30 billion. This could be a game changer, as it shows that despite their differences, there's a willingness to cooperate. It's a hopeful sign for a country that has faced so much turmoil since 2011.
