In an inspiring move, a Kenyan entrepreneur has developed robotic hands that convert a teacher’s voice into sign language for deaf students. With around 300,000 children in Kenya facing hearing challenges, this innovation opens up new educational avenues for those who might otherwise miss out. Founded by 22-year-old Norah Kimathi, ZeroBionic has already sold 78 units, each made from recycled plastic and boasting impressive accuracy. This is just the beginning, as they plan to enhance their technology further to support African sign language.
