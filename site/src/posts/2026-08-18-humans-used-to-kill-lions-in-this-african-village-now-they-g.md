In East Africa, a fantastic initiative called Community Camera Trapping Plus is changing the game for both lions and local communities. By encouraging villagers to monitor wildlife through trail cameras, they earn points that translate into healthcare, school supplies, and more. This system not only fosters coexistence but also promotes a positive view of wildlife, which is so important! It's amazing to see how communities are shifting from conflict to conservation, all while having a bit of fun with it.
