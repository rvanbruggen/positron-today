Researchers at Edith Cowan University have discovered that the iron-rich formations in Western Australia could be a significant source of low-emission hydrogen. This exciting finding suggests that Australia might not only secure its energy future but also become a major player in the global hydrogen export market. By stimulating hydrogen production underground, they could unlock a massive energy reserve for generations to come. It's a thrilling prospect that could transform the region's energy landscape!
