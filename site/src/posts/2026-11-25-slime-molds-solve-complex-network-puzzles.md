So: a single-celled slime mold can tackle a network-planning puzzle that has challenged mathematicians for more than 200 years. In a famous experiment, researchers arranged oat flakes to represent Tokyo and 35 nearby cities, then watched Physarum polycephalum grow and connect the food sources. Its delicate network closely resembled Tokyo’s rail system, balancing efficiency, resilience and cost. And here's the thing: it managed this remarkable feat without a brain or a complex nervous system.
