So: cocoa farmer Susana Afrifa Ankrah knows how much Ghana’s Atewa forest means to her family and community, from fresh air and rainfall to medicinal plants. Through a pioneering programme, she and other farmers are learning to grow cocoa while protecting trees and soil. Composting, organic fertilisers and shade-loving hybrid seeds can help boost harvests without clearing more forest. It’s a hopeful approach that supports local livelihoods while helping nature and communities become more resilient to climate change.
