So, it turns out that dancing salsa might be a fantastic way for young people dealing with depression to lift their spirits. Researchers from Oxford have found that engaging in this vibrant dance can significantly improve mood and overall mental health. Isn't it amazing how movement and rhythm can transform feelings? This study highlights the power of dance as a therapeutic tool, making it an exciting option for those looking for relief.
