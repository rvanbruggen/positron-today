So, have you ever noticed how solar panels are tilted? That little angle is actually super important for capturing sunlight effectively! It turns out that the position of the Sun changes throughout the day and year, and tilting the panels helps them catch more of that precious solar energy. Engineers take into account various factors like location and seasons to find the best tilt angle, making solar energy even more efficient.
