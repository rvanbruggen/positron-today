I find hydroponics fascinating because it lets plants grow without soil by delivering nutrients directly through water. Instead of soil, materials like coco peat or clay pellets support the plants while a nutrient-rich solution feeds their roots. Farmers can control everything from pH to water levels, making growth more efficient and less dependent on weather. This method also uses water smartly, which is a big win for places facing water shortages. It’s amazing how technology like this is helping farmers in India grow fresh veggies in a smarter way!
