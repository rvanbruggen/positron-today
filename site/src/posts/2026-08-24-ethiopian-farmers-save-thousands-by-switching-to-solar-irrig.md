Farmers in Ethiopia are making a remarkable shift to solar-powered irrigation, which is slashing their costs and boosting food production. With abundant sunshine and water resources, this change is a game-changer for small farmers who previously relied on costly diesel pumps. Now, they can save thousands of dollars and reduce their environmental impact at the same time. This innovative approach not only enhances agricultural productivity but also supports a healthier planet for future generations.
