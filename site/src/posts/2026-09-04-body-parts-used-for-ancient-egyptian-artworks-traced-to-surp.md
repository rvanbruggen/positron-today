So, it turns out that ancient Egyptians were quite resourceful when it came to creating their artworks! They used body parts from a variety of animals, including donkeys and antelopes, along with plant materials. This research highlights that the ingredients for their art came from a broader range of species than we ever imagined. It's fascinating to think about the creativity and resourcefulness of these ancient artists!
