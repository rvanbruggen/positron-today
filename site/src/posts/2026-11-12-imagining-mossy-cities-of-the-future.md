In 2012, artist Philip Wallisfurth began using moss to create living street art and help protect a beloved tree in Aachen. He sees moss as more than a beautiful way to brighten dull urban spaces: it can retain moisture, absorb noise, filter air and help cool the surfaces it covers. Now he imagines bringing moss to buildings such as train stations, making city spaces more welcoming while offering environmental benefits. As urban heat intensifies, this small, adaptable plant could inspire fresh ideas for greener cities.
