When his pregnant wife had to navigate Bengaluru's notoriously damaged roads for doctor visits, engineer Gaurav Sen decided to turn frustration into action. He built Pothole Reporter, a clever app that doesn't just spot potholes using a phone's camera and GPS - it actually identifies which contractor is responsible for fixing that specific stretch of road. Built in just one day without funding and at only 4 MB in size, this bootstrapped innovation transforms a decades-old problem into something accountable and actionable. The app is now heading for a Google Play launch, and the Bengaluru Development Authority is already interested in integrating it into their systems.
