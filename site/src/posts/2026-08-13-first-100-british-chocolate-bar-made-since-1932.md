Can you believe it? For the first time in nearly a century, a chocolate bar has been made entirely from British ingredients! The Reading 100 Chocolate Bar, created at the University of Reading, uses cocoa grown right in Berkshire. It’s a fantastic achievement by food security master's student Maha Khan, who highlights the challenges of cocoa production today. This bar not only celebrates local ingredients but also sheds light on the importance of sustainable cocoa farming in the face of climate change.
