So, on August 7, Colorado Parks and Wildlife shared this incredible moment of a lynx family caught on a trail camera! It's such a rare sight to see a lynx kitten with its mother in the wild, and it really highlights the efforts to bring these beautiful creatures back from the brink. Lynx are a keystone species, and their presence is vital for the ecosystem. With at least 100 lynx now estimated to be living in Colorado, it seems like conservation efforts are truly paying off!
