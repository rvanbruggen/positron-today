It's fascinating to see how technology has transformed tiger conservation in India! Once, forest guards relied on pugmarks and their keen observations to track these majestic animals. Now, with GPS, camera traps, and AI, they can monitor tiger populations more accurately and respond to threats in real-time. This shift not only enhances protection efforts but also allows for a deeper understanding of these incredible creatures and their habitats.
