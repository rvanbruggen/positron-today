So: a rare greater one-horned rhino calf was born at Chester Zoo on October 11, and CCTV captured mother Asha giving birth and the newborn’s first wobbly steps. The calf and its mother are doing well, and the team helped plan the pregnancy by tracking Asha’s hormones through non-invasive samples. This is Asha’s fourth calf with Beni, who sadly died in September, so the newborn carries on his legacy. With around 4,000 greater one-horned rhinos left in the wild, this birth also supports a conservation programme working to keep the population healthy and genetically diverse.
