So: the WHO says measles vaccination has saved an estimated 60 million lives, more than any other childhood vaccine. That remarkable figure shows how much protection routine immunisation can offer around the world. Measles is highly contagious, but vaccination helps prevent outbreaks and the severe complications the disease can cause. And here's the thing: keeping vaccination rates high remains vital so children and communities can continue to benefit.
