So: researchers at Oregon State University have grown edible potatoes in simulated lunar soil, with a little help from worm-made compost. A mix containing just 30 percent vermicompost produced crops comparable to those grown in Oregon soil. The lunar-grown potatoes showed no statistically significant increase in natural toxins called glycoalkaloids, which is encouraging news for future space farming. Next, the team plans to test different potato varieties to find the most nutritious and resilient options.
