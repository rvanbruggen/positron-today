So, there's this fascinating story about Kristoffer Aaskoven Sørensen, a 24-year-old who's dedicated his online life to building a healthy community. He’s part of a digital group with 740 other young people, mostly in their twenties, challenging the stereotypes of 'screen generation.' Now, while he spends a lot of time in front of his screen, he has a complicated relationship with social media and has even cut back on gaming. Which brings me to the idea that young people are actively creating positive spaces online rather than just mindlessly scrolling.
