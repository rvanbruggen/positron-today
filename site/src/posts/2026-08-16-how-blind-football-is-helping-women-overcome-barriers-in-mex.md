Next month, Mexico will make history by sending its first blind women's football team to an international tournament. The Chilangas FC, led by passionate coach Wendy del Rio, is made up of players who are eager to show the world their skills. Blind football, which uses a special rattle ball, allows everyone to play on equal footing, creating a unique and empowering experience. This initiative not only promotes the sport but also inspires other women with visual impairments to join in and pursue their passion.
