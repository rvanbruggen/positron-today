So, here's a fascinating story about a pygmy raccoon on Cozumel Island that turned trash into toys! A graduate student observed this little creature using a receipt to create a ball, and even taught her family to join in the fun. This behavior is a first for raccoons, showing they can pass on skills like other intelligent animals. It's a reminder of how playful and socially aware these creatures are, even in the face of environmental challenges.
