Can you believe it? White storks are back in the English Midlands after a staggering 600 years! This incredible return is part of a project to create a thriving breeding colony along the River Trent, where they hope to inspire both wildlife and people alike. The Derbyshire Wildlife Trust is leading the charge, and with the help of local charities, they’re ensuring these magnificent birds have a safe place to settle. It’s a wonderful reminder of how nature can bounce back when we give it a chance!
