Yousra Hussein’s life was turned upside down by Sudan’s civil war, forcing her to flee multiple times and start over. So: she turned to entrepreneurship, offering nail services and reviving her Golden Women initiative to empower displaced young women. The war has devastated jobs and the economy, pushing many young Sudanese into informal work and business despite huge challenges like lack of funding and skills. And here’s the thing - international programs are stepping in to help train and support these resilient entrepreneurs.
