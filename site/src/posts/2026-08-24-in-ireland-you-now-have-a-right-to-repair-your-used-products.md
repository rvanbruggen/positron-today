A new law is taking effect today that gives you the right to repair your used products! This EU regulation, now part of Irish law, aims to reduce waste by encouraging repairs instead of replacements. It also supports the growth of Ireland’s repair sector and circular economy, which is super exciting. Environment Minister Darragh O’Brien emphasizes that these changes are vital for meeting our climate goals, and I couldn't agree more!
