So, researchers have embedded enzymes that break down ester bonds into compostable polyester plastics. The material biodegraded rapidly and completely in marine environments, even where microbes are scarce. Tests covered a range of locations, from coastal waters to the deep sea. This approach could help tackle plastic pollution by enabling these plastics to break down in the ocean.
