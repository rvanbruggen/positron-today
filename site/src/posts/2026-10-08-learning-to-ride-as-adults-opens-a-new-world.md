In Biskopsgården, three women are learning to ride bicycles through a free course for adults run by Cykelfrämjandet. After six weeks of practising balance, braking and pedalling, they’re discovering how cycling can make everyday travel easier and more independent. Hina Naser was inspired by her six-year-old daughter, while Kamal Kaur is already imagining bike rides with her family. And here’s the thing: for the learners, a bicycle isn’t just transport - it’s a whole new sense of freedom.
