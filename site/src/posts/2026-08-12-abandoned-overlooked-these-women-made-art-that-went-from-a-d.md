It's truly inspiring to see how art can transform lives, especially for these 15 women with intellectual disabilities. They participated in a unique initiative called What Is Love?, where they explored their emotions and identities through various art forms. With the guidance of talented artists, they moved from being observers to creators, finding their voices in the process. This project not only allowed them to express themselves but also challenged societal perceptions of their capabilities.
