The GALA Hispanic Theater in Washington, D.C. has long been a cultural beacon for the Latino community, especially during tough times under aggressive ICE raids. Founded in 1976 to promote Hispanic culture, it remains unique by presenting plays in Spanish with English subtitles, welcoming both Latino and non-Spanish-speaking audiences. The theater has become more than just a cultural space—it’s a refuge where resilience and hope are celebrated through art. This season, GALA will stage a play reflecting the community’s quiet resistance and strength in the face of adversity.
