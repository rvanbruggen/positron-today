Isn't it amazing how a simple Google search can lead to incredible discoveries? Luke Auld-Thomas, a PhD student, stumbled upon a lost Mayan city while scrolling through search results, finding a laser survey that revealed an entire ancient metropolis. This city, named Valeriana, housed tens of thousands of people and challenges our understanding of ancient civilizations in tropical regions. It’s a reminder that sometimes, the most significant finds are just waiting for someone curious enough to look deeper.
