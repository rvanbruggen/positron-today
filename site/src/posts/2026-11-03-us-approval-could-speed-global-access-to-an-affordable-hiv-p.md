A U.S. regulatory green light for an affordable generic HIV prevention shot could help bring this long-acting option to more people around the world. The promise is especially exciting for communities where daily prevention pills can be difficult to access or take consistently. Wider availability will depend on production, regulatory steps and delivery in different countries. And here's the thing: lower-cost options could make a powerful new tool against HIV reach much further.
