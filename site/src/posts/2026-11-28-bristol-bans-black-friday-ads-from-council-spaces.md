So: Bristol has become the first city in the UK to ban Black Friday and fast-fashion adverts from council-owned spaces, including bus stops. The policy also blocks ads for fossil fuel companies, SUVs, package holidays, airlines and cruises, as part of a wider effort to curb high-carbon consumption. Private advertising spaces are unaffected, and businesses can still promote sales through their own shops and social media. I find it encouraging that more than 20 councils are now limiting some high-carbon advertising, while Bristol builds on earlier bans covering fast food, payday loans, gambling and alcohol.
