I’m really excited about this innovative startup that’s turning logging waste into wood foam to replace plastic! The University of British Columbia has developed a process that not only reduces plastic pollution but also makes use of timber waste that would otherwise go to waste. They’re already producing 400 pounds of this eco-friendly material daily at their pilot plant in East Vancouver. If successful, they plan to scale up significantly, which could make a huge difference in our packaging needs and the environment.
