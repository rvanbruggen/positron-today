In October, Peletkie in Nagaland’s Peren district turns its hillsides into a sea of pink, white and yellow cosmos, drawing thousands of visitors. The village has around 300 residents across 64 households and lies a short drive from Peren town. Organisers say eight years of planting, alongside regular cleanliness competitions between village colonies, helped create this shared floral landscape. The festival also celebrates local culture and livelihoods, introducing visitors to organic produce, traditions and Lekie Cai salt-making.
