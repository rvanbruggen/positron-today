Gabriela Nathania and her friends dove into a transformative experience in Indonesia's Gili Islands, where they bonded over their shared passion for marine conservation. Through the nonprofit Coral Catch, these women are gaining essential skills in diving and coral restoration, empowering them to take ownership of their local ecosystems. The program not only fosters environmental stewardship but also opens up new career paths for women in a region where opportunities can be limited. It's inspiring to see how this initiative is creating a dedicated workforce committed to protecting the reefs they love.
