A fascinating new study reveals how neutrinos can help us understand the Earth's mantle. By using a global network of neutrino detectors, scientists are uncovering the radioactive elements that fuel our planet's tectonic activity. The JUNO experiment in China is set to make its first geoneutrino detections this year, which is super exciting! These elusive particles may hold the key to understanding the heat driving geological processes beneath our feet.
