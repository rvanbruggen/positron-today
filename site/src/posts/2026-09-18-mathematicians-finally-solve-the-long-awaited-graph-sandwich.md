So here's the thing - back in 2004, two mathematicians came up with this beautiful idea: what if you could sandwich a complicated graph between two simpler ones to understand its properties? The trick is that graphs are everywhere - they model everything from social networks to the internet to our brains - but some types are really hard to analyze. For over two decades, researchers made progress on proving this "sandwich conjecture" but couldn't quite seal the deal, until three mathematicians in 2025 finally pushed the field's techniques far enough to complete the proof. What makes this so elegant is that proving properties about the simpler graph automatically proves them for the harder one too.
