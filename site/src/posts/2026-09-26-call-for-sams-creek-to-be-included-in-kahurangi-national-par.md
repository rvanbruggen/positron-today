So: there’s a strong push to include Sams Creek in Kahurangi National Park to protect it from mining threats. Labour and the Greens are backing this move, especially after a mining permit was recently declined. The area is precious, with stunning natural features and the famous Te Waikoropupū Springs nearby, known for some of the clearest water in the world. And here's the thing - locals and notable figures like Helen Clark are rallying behind a petition with over 11,000 signatures. It’s a perfect moment to close this long-standing gap in the park’s boundaries and keep Sams Creek safe for good.
