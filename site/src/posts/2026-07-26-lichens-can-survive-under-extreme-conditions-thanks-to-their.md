So, did you know that lichens are not just simple organisms? They are actually a fascinating combination of fungi, algae, and sometimes cyanobacteria, all working together! Ingeborg Klarenberg's research in Antarctica highlights how these resilient life forms thrive even in harsh climates. Their interconnectedness not only helps them survive but also inspires us to think about our own relationships and individuality. Plus, there's an exciting exhibition in Rotterdam showcasing their beauty and complexity!
