It's a fantastic moment for Greece as Mount Olympus has just been designated a Unesco World Heritage site! This recognition is not just a badge of honor but a vital step towards ensuring the protection of this iconic mountain. The locals are thrilled, understanding that safeguarding their natural heritage is essential for future generations. And here's the thing: this designation will also boost tourism, bringing more visitors to experience the beauty and history of Olympus.
