I find it fascinating how big housing plans can actually create beautiful, cohesive neighbourhoods rather than just scattered units. When developers think on a large scale, they can invest in nicer public spaces and better layouts, which lifts the whole community’s spirit. It’s like the best parts of Dublin, built by single landowners, show us that planning big really pays off. And here’s the thing - big plans help tackle the housing crisis by making homes more attractive and affordable, which benefits everyone.
