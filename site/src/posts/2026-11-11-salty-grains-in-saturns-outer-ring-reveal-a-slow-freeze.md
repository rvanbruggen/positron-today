Saturn’s outermost ring is fed largely by icy plumes erupting from its moon Enceladus. A new analysis finds that the ring’s varied icy and rocky grains may have formed as those plumes slowly froze. These tiny particles offer a fresh way to investigate the moon’s hidden subsurface ocean. And here's the thing: studying their composition could help scientists assess whether that ocean might be habitable.
