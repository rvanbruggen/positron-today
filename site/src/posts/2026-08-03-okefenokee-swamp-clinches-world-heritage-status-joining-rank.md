Wow, Georgia’s Okefenokee Swamp has just been recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site! This incredible swamp now stands proudly alongside the Grand Canyon and Yellowstone, showcasing the amazing biodiversity of American blackwater swamps. It’s fascinating to think that conservation efforts here date back to 1918, and now it’s a vital habitat for countless species. The swamp is not just beautiful but also plays a crucial role in carbon storage and environmental history.
