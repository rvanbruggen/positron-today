So: Serbia’s Student List is set to take its 140 seats in parliament after defeating Aleksandar Vučić’s ruling party in last month’s election, opening the way to form a government. Many incoming MPs are academics, lawyers and other professionals, and they have been preparing through regular meetings and mock parliamentary exercises alongside the wider student movement. The new cabinet and prime minister have not yet been announced, and the outgoing government will stay in place until parliament approves its successor. Observers expect obstruction at the opening session, while MPs face a 90-day deadline to approve a cabinet.
