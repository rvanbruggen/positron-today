Researchers at Dartmouth analyzed thousands of conversations to discover what makes people feel connected. They found that conversations can be too chaotic, too stuck in agreement, or just right, a pattern they call “pink noise.” This comfortable back-and-forth between shared ideas and fresh perspectives was linked to greater enjoyment, engagement, and connection, and even helped predict interest in another date. So, chemistry may depend less on having everything in common and more on finding a good conversational rhythm.
