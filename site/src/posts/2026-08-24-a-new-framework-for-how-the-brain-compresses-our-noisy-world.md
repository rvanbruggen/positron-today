Every day, our brains are busy compressing the flood of sensory information we encounter. This new framework suggests that categorization isn't just a final step but happens continuously, adapting to our body's needs. It’s fascinating to think that our perceptions are shaped by survival instincts rather than just sensory data. This fresh perspective could change how we understand brain function and categorization.
