Wow, Bulgaria has really outdone itself with this digital art festival along the Black Sea coast! From Tsarevo to Sinemorets, artists from eight countries transformed the landscape into a vibrant canvas of light and creativity. Each night, visitors were treated to stunning projections and interactive installations that turned familiar sights into something magical. It's fantastic to see how art can connect people with nature while drawing in crowds eager for contemporary experiences.
