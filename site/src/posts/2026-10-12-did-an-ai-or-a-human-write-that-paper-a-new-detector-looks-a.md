So, here's an intriguing twist: a new detector tries to tell whether a research paper was written by AI or a human by examining its ideas. Instead of focusing only on how the text sounds, it looks at the substance of what the paper says. That shifts the spotlight toward the thinking and concepts behind the writing. It offers a fresh way to explore how AI-generated research might be recognised as these tools become more common.
