Walking into the REWE supermarket in Berlin, customers are greeted by a unique sight – salad grown just above their heads! This innovative rooftop farm not only provides fresh greens but also reduces transportation emissions. It's fascinating how the store was designed with the farm in mind, creating a synergy that benefits both the environment and the community. Plus, the quick growing process means that greens are harvested and sold within weeks, making local produce more accessible than ever.
