So here's what fascinates me - scammers have become way more sophisticated than those obvious phishing emails we all laugh about. Rebecca Keithley from the FBI's Financial Crimes Section explains that modern fraud relies on three psychological tricks: fear that hijacks your brain, manufactured urgency that forces snap decisions, and demands for secrecy that cut you off from help. The good news is that once you recognize these red flags - like threats of arrest, pressure to act immediately, or requests to use gift cards and crypto - you can stop the scam in its tracks. And this matters because in 2025 alone, over one million people reported suspected online scams, losing more than $20 billion combined.
