So, it turns out that the impressive La Mojana field systems in Colombia were built not by a centralized authority, but by local family cooperatives! This discovery challenges long-held beliefs about ancient engineering in South America. These raised fields and canals allowed communities to manage water efficiently, ensuring their survival for nearly a thousand years. It's fascinating to think how these ancient families worked together to create such a monumental system, and their strategies might even help us tackle modern water management issues.
