I love this story about a group of California teens who set a Guinness World Record by recycling over 22,000 sports balls in just one day. They managed to divert 2,675 pounds of mostly tennis and pickleball balls from landfills, which is incredible! Another Bounce, their group, is not only collecting balls but also pushing for manufacturers to think about what happens to these products after use. And here's the thing - their effort is sparking real change, with local politicians even inviting them to draft legislation to support sustainable sports practices.
