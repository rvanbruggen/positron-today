Isn't it fascinating how a human, an octopus, and a coral can share a common ancestor from over 600 million years ago? Researchers at the University of Vienna have uncovered that animal genomes evolve along specific 'evolutionary highways' rather than randomly. This groundbreaking study, which analyzed thousands of genomes, reveals patterns in how chromosomes have changed over time. Understanding these evolutionary rules not only sheds light on the past but also helps us think about the future of animal biodiversity.
