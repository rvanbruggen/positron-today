Every morning, Colours Centre for Learning opens its doors to cheerful greetings and a vibrant atmosphere, creating a unique space for autistic individuals. Founded by Bela Joshi, the centre integrates therapy into daily activities, allowing students to learn and grow in a supportive environment. Bela's personal journey as a mother inspired her to create a place that emphasizes dignity and respect for every child. With a focus on individual skill development, the centre is changing the way autism support is delivered in Bengaluru.
