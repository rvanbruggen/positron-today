New data reveals that early intervention in psychosis services is making a remarkable difference for young people in Ireland. With 70% achieving recovery and many securing employment, the impact is truly inspiring. The reduction in untreated psychosis duration from 16 weeks to just 5 weeks is a game changer. Plus, the government's investment in expanding these services means even more young people will benefit from timely support.
