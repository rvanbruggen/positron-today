Belgian ambulances may soon be able to take patients to hospitals in the Netherlands. This would make cross-border hospital transport possible for patients who need care. The change could give emergency services more options when deciding where to bring someone. It’s a practical step towards smoother healthcare across the Belgian-Dutch border.
