Luke Holdsworth, born deaf, has transformed the live music experience for deaf audiences by interpreting concerts in British Sign Language. His journey began when he witnessed a sign language interpreter at a festival, inspiring him to pursue interpreting himself. Now, he brings the energy and essence of performances to life, even embodying the artists' attitudes. It's all about making music accessible and enjoyable for everyone, and Holdsworth is truly leading the way.
