I love how nature keeps surprising us! Butterfly wings aren’t just pretty—they’re like a clever trick to confuse predators. These dazzling patterns create a kind of optical illusion that makes it harder for predators to focus or predict their moves. So: it’s not just beauty, but a smart survival strategy. And here’s the thing, this old razzle-dazzle is a fascinating example of evolution’s creativity in action.
