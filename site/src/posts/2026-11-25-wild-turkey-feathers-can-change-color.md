So, wild turkeys can sport some surprisingly different feather colors, thanks to genetic changes that affect their pigments. The four recognized color phases include smoky gray, reddish erythristic, dark melanistic, and pale leucistic birds. Researchers are still tracking how common these variations are, and whether unusual plumage affects a turkey’s chances of survival or reproduction remains unknown. And if you spot a bird that looks like a brilliantly colored turkey-shaped peacock, it may be the tropical ocellated turkey of the Yucatán Peninsula.
