So, I just read about this fascinating new trial called SINGLE-AF, and it is honestly such great news for people living with atrial fibrillation. For a long time, doctors were a bit unsure about using blood thinners for patients with an intermediate stroke risk, but this study changes everything. The researchers found that newer direct oral anticoagulants reduced serious cardiovascular events by a massive 69% compared to no treatment at all. And here's the thing - they saw these amazing results without any increase in major bleeding, which is just fantastic. It really feels like this could change how we approach heart health guidelines in the future.
