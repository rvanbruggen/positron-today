I’m thrilled to share that over 3,000 endangered mountain yellow-legged frogs and tadpoles have been released back into their natural habitats in Southern California! This incredible effort, led by the San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance, aims to restore a species that has seen a staggering decline of over 98%. Watching these little amphibians hop into the waters of Bluff Lake is a sight that brings hope for their future. Conservation teams are dedicated to monitoring their progress, ensuring these frogs thrive once again in the wild.
