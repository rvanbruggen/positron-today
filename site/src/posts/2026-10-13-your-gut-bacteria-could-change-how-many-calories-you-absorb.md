So, the calories listed on food packaging don’t tell the whole story: gut microbes can help determine how much energy your body absorbs. Researchers at Arizona State University developed a model called DAMM to track digestion, nutrient absorption and the role of gut microbes. In a controlled diet study, a high-fiber eating plan was linked to 116 fewer calories absorbed per day than a typical Western-style plan. With further development, the model could help researchers understand metabolic health and support diets tailored to individuals.
