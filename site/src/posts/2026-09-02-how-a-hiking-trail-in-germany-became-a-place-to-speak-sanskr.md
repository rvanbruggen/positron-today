A group of international Sanskrit learners took to the Elbe Sandstone Mountains in Germany, transforming their hike into a lively conversation in Sanskrit. As they navigated the trails, they described the scenery and shared experiences, making the ancient language come alive in a modern context. This informal setting allowed them to practice vocabulary in real-time, turning the hike into a unique language-learning opportunity. It’s a beautiful reminder that languages thrive when used in everyday life, not just in classrooms.
