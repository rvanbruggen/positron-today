Did you know that two-thirds of an octopus's neurons are actually in its arms? It's fascinating how each arm operates independently, allowing these clever creatures to perform tasks like unscrewing jars and even throwing sand! This unique form of distributed intelligence means their movements and sensations are often guided by the arms rather than the central brain. Scientists are still uncovering the mysteries behind this remarkable anatomy and behavior, making octopuses even more intriguing.
