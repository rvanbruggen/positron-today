So, a DNA study offers a fascinating clue to how barn owls reached the Americas. It suggests that all barn owls in the Americas trace their genetic origins to the Caribbean. Their ancestors made the remarkable journey across the Atlantic from Eurasia. It’s a striking reminder of how far wildlife can travel over evolutionary time.
