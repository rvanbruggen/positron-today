So, a routine excavation for a terrace in Rebild turned into an extraordinary discovery when 18.5 kilograms of silver were unearthed. This find is now recognized as the largest silver treasure from the Viking Age ever found in Denmark, nearly three times larger than the previous record. The treasure, buried in a clay pot in the 9th century, includes around 700 items, such as silver bars, bracelets, and coins. It's fascinating to think about how this treasure not only reflects wealth but also the extensive connections Denmark had with Europe and beyond during the Viking Age.
