Wow, what an exciting discovery! A recent deep-sea expedition off Trinidad and Tobago revealed a Graneledone octopus and at least 20 suspected new species. This month-long survey, led by local marine biologists, documented 13 percent of the nation's marine territory, which is just incredible. With over 700 specimens collected, the University of the West Indies will see its deep ocean collection grow significantly, enhancing our understanding of these mysterious ecosystems.
