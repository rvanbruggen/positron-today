So, electric vans have passed a notable milestone in Denmark: more than half of newly registered vans now run on electricity. Mobility Denmark reports that 2,946 new electric vans were registered in the third quarter, an increase of more than 50 percent compared with the same period last year. Electric models made up 51.6 percent of all newly registered vans. Electric trucks are catching up more slowly, representing 14.9 percent of new truck registrations in the quarter.
