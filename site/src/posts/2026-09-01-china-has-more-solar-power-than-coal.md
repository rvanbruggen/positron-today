Wow, this is exciting! For the first time, China's installed solar power capacity has surpassed that of coal-fired plants. While coal still accounts for a significant portion of electricity production, its share has dropped below 50%. This shift is driven by government support for renewable energy and the impacts of climate change, which are creating more sunny days and stronger winds. It's a clear sign that China is rapidly moving towards a greener energy future!
