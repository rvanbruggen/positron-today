Aman Sharma, a 23-year-old from Delhi, has transformed his barren terrace into a vibrant garden with 500 native plants, attracting birds and butterflies. His passion for nature began at 13 when he started birdwatching from his balcony. During the COVID-19 lockdown, he seized the opportunity to create a green oasis in the city. This initiative not only beautifies his surroundings but also raises awareness about urban wildlife and the importance of native plants.
