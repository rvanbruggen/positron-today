During the monsoon, Maharashtra's plateaus come alive with tiny carnivorous plants like sundews and bladderworts. These fascinating plants have adapted to thrive in nutrient-poor, rocky soils by trapping insects for nourishment. It's amazing to see how the landscape transforms from barren rock to a vibrant ecosystem filled with seasonal flowers. If you're in the area, make sure to look closely before the monsoon wraps up and these unique plants disappear again!
