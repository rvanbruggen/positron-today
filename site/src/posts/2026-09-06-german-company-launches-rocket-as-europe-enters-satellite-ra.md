Isar Aerospace has made a significant leap by launching its Spectrum rocket from Norway, marking a pivotal moment for Europe in the satellite race. This 28-metre-long rocket successfully deployed five satellites into orbit, showcasing Europe's growing capabilities in space technology. It's exciting to see a privately developed rocket reach space from Europe for the first time! With plans to manufacture up to 40 launch vehicles annually, Isar Aerospace is positioning itself as a key player in the future of satellite launches.
