In rural Chad, the introduction of solar-powered Telemedan kiosks is revolutionizing access to healthcare. People like Ache Kodo can now consult doctors remotely, saving both time and money that would have been spent on travel. This initiative not only addresses the shortage of medical professionals but also overcomes the barriers posed by distance. With 37 kiosks already in operation, Telemedan is making a significant impact on the health and economic status of local communities.
