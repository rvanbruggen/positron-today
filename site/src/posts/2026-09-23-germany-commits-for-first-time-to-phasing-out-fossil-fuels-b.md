Germany has made a bold new commitment to phase out fossil fuels by 2045, marking a clear shift from previous promises of just carbon neutrality. The government plans to boost renewable energy to 80% of electricity by 2030, with major expansions in wind and solar power. There’s also a renewed focus on cutting methane emissions and potentially ending coal use even earlier than planned. Now, the challenge is to accelerate electrification in transport and heating to truly make this roadmap a reality.
