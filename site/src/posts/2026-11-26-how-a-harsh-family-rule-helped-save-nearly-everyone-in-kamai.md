So, Japan’s tsunami-tendenko rule urges everyone to flee to high ground immediately, rather than turning back to find family members. The idea can sound harsh, but it rests on a powerful promise: each person will run to safety, trusting that loved ones are doing the same. During the 2011 tsunami, students in Kamaishi acted fast, helped younger children reach higher ground and kept going when their first shelter seemed unsafe. Of the city’s 2,926 elementary and junior high students, 2,921 survived - a remarkable example of how preparation and trust can save lives.
