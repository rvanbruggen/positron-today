So, in rural Minnesota, a library can be closed to staff and still welcome readers. The East Central Regional Library’s extended-access program lets registered members enter the Wyoming branch outside staffed hours to read, borrow books and use computers. After signing up in person and learning about safety features, members can visit when work, family schedules or the weather make regular hours inconvenient. It is a practical way to make the library a more flexible place to work, learn or find a little respite.
