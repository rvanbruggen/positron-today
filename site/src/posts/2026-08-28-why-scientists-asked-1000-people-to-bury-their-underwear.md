Soil is a vital part of our ecosystem, and researchers are keen to understand its hidden biodiversity. In a fascinating project called Proof by Underpants, 1,000 volunteers buried cotton underwear to study soil activity across Switzerland. The results showed that the speed of decomposition varied significantly based on land use, revealing insights into soil health. This innovative approach not only raises awareness but also provides valuable data for improving soil management practices.
