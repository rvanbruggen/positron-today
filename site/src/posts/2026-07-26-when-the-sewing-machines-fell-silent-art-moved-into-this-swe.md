So, Borås faced a tough time when the textile industry shut down in the 1970s, leaving it a mere shadow of its former self. But here's the thing – art came to the rescue and transformed the city into a vibrant hub of creativity. This year marks the ninth edition of the art biennale, showcasing the incredible revival of Borås. It's amazing to see how art can breathe new life into a place and change perceptions.
