What a bold move by Helen Hulick back in 1938! She showed up in pants to testify in court and refused to change into a dress, which landed her in jail for a few hours. It’s incredible how she stood her ground against societal norms and a judge who just didn’t get it. Ultimately, her defiance helped pave the way for women’s rights in fashion, proving that comfort should always come first.
