So, have you ever thought about how many friends you really need? It turns out, anthropologist Robin Dunbar suggests around five close friends and about 150 less intimate connections. But here's the thing—friendship is tricky to measure, and many people struggle to form these bonds. Recent research highlights the significant benefits of friendships, like faster healing after surgeries, and reveals that loneliness can negatively impact us just like smoking. It's fascinating to explore how different people approach friendship and what that means for their well-being!
