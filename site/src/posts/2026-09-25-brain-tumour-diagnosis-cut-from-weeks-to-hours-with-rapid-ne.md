So: the NHS has started using a brilliant new test that cuts brain tumour diagnosis time from up to eight weeks down to just two hours. This means patients like Steve Palmer can get their results while still in surgery, which is amazing because it removes weeks of anxiety and speeds up treatment decisions. The test works by analyzing the tumour’s genetic code quickly using a small sequencing machine, making it almost magical according to the surgeons. And here's the thing, faster diagnosis means patients can start the right treatment sooner and surgeons can tailor their approach during operations.
