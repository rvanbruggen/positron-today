So, researchers have stumbled upon a cosmic mystery that’s as intriguing as it is mind-boggling! A photon from a gamma-ray burst, which originated two billion light-years away, somehow made it to Earth despite the odds. This photon, dubbed 'BOAT', carried an astonishing amount of energy, and scientists are now proposing that it might have dodged destruction thanks to some hypothetical particles. If this theory holds up, it could open up new avenues for understanding quantum gravity in ways we’ve never imagined!
