In Srinagar, a unique flower garden has transformed old cars, scooters, and plastic bottles into stunning displays of creativity. Opened on August 21, the Waste to Wonder Flower Garden showcases how discarded items can be repurposed alongside vibrant plants. An old jeep and even a bumper car have found new life as planters, making the garden a true feast for the eyes. It's a fantastic example of turning trash into treasure, attracting both locals and tourists alike.
