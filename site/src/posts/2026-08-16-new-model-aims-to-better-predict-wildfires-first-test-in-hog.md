So, scientists at UGent are testing an experimental model that predicts how wildfires spread during the recent blaze in Hoge Venen. The initial results are looking quite promising, even though the model isn't ready for firefighting use just yet. It still needs crucial data, like detailed maps of soil and vegetation across Belgium. This model could eventually help manage terrain better to prevent future wildfires, which is super exciting!
