Soil health is crucial, and it turns out you don't need fancy tools to assess it—just a pair of cotton underwear! In Switzerland, a fun project called 'Proof by Underpants' had volunteers bury undies and tea bags to see how they decomposed. The results showed that residential gardens had the healthiest soil, while manicured lawns lagged behind. This quirky method highlights the importance of soil health and reminds us that a thriving ecosystem is just beneath our feet.
