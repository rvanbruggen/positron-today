So: around 600 sheep made their way through Nuremberg on Sunday, heading to their winter quarters. Curious onlookers watched as the flock crossed the historic old town, turning busy streets into a charming annual spectacle. Their 10-kilometre route is the shortest way to reach their winter home west of the city. And here's the thing: grazing also helps protect the local landscape by keeping open areas from becoming overgrown.
