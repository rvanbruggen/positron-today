What might a river landscape feel like to a kestrel, an otter or a salmon? At Somerset’s River Tone, 18 volunteers explored that question by inhabiting animals’ sensory worlds, from focusing on a kestrel’s sharp eyesight to moving through the water like an otter. For volunteer Helen Lawy, seeing the landscape as a kestrel revealed how hard it can be to find prey, shelter and quiet places away from people and dogs. The project hopes this fresh perspective can uncover overlooked risks and inspire better ways to protect wildlife and the river ecosystem.
