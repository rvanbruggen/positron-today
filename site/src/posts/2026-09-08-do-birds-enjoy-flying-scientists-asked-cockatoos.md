So, a fascinating study by Jorg Massen and his team has shown that Galah Cockatoos seem to really enjoy flying! They had the freedom to choose whether to fly or stay put, and guess what? Most of them chose to soar through the sky! This suggests that flight might be rewarding for them, and their behavior after flying showed signs of excitement. It’s a delightful glimpse into the minds of these birds and how they express their natural instincts.
