So, there's this amazing project in New York called the Billion Oyster Project that's turning discarded oyster shells into new reefs! These reefs are not just pretty; they filter pollutants from the harbor and help protect the city from flooding. They've already raised 200 million oysters, filtering trillions of gallons of water. It's a win-win for the environment and the local restaurant scene, as they’re using shells from over 75 eateries. How cool is that?
