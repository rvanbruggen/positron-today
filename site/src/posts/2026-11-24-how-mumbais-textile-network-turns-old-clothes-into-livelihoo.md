So, in Mumbai, a network called TWIN is giving old clothes a new route into reuse and recycling while supporting the people who handle them. Founded by Vikram Shahi, the initiative works with bhandiwalas and waghari workers, whose experience keeps wearable textiles in circulation. It has collected around 17,500 kilograms of used textiles and reached more than 650 households. For trader Anil Datanini, a steadier supply means more reliable work and monthly earnings of around Rs 25,000 to Rs 30,000.
