Understanding quantum mechanics is like trying to grasp an alternate universe. It's fascinating how subatomic particles behave both like particles and waves, which can be mind-boggling! The famous double-slit experiment shows us that even when electrons are shot one at a time, they still create an interference pattern. This means they somehow interfere with themselves, which is just wild! So, while we can predict quantum outcomes, the deeper understanding remains elusive.
