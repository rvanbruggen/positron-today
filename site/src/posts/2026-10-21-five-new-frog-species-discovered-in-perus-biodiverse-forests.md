What a remarkable discovery: researchers have formally described five new frog species from Peru’s tropical Andes and Amazonian forests. The team identified them using a combination of physical traits, genetic data and, for two species, their calls. One of the frogs, Pristimantis aisatipoki, was named using a word from the Asháninka language and has a special connection to the Rainforest Partnership’s conservation work in Satipo. And here’s the thing: learning which species live in these forests helps make the case for protecting them.
