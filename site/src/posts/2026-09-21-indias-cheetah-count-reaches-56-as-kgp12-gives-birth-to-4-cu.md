I am absolutely thrilled to share some wonderful news from Kuno National Park! The cheetah KGP12, who was born in India herself, has just welcomed four healthy cubs into the world. It is such a resilient story because she sadly lost her first litter earlier this year, but now she is a mother once again. This brings the total cheetah population in India to 56, which is a massive milestone for the conservation programme. And here is the thing - seeing this new generation thrive really shows that the project is moving in the right direction.
