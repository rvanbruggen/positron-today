It's amazing to see black bears making a comeback in the US South after being nearly wiped out! Recently, there have been numerous sightings in unexpected places like West Tennessee, which has wildlife experts buzzing with excitement. Dan Gibbs from the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency believes these bears are reclaiming their historical range, and it's just the beginning. With the right habitats, these resilient creatures are proving they can thrive in various ecosystems, and that's something to celebrate!
