In Gateshead, a town with a rich coal mining history, a new initiative is turning old mine workings into a source of clean energy. A mine water heat network, launched in March 2023, draws warmth from flooded mines to heat homes and buildings across the area. This innovative project not only reduces carbon emissions but also revives the town's connection to its mining heritage. It's a beautiful example of how we can repurpose the past for a sustainable future.
