In Brazil, there's a fascinating trend of supercentenarians living to extraordinary ages, and it’s not what you might expect. While billionaires in Silicon Valley chase longevity through extreme measures, Brazilian centenarians thrive on a simpler lifestyle. Research shows that around 70% of these remarkable individuals are women, often with smaller physiques, which may contribute to their longevity. This rich genetic diversity in Brazil, stemming from a mix of cultures, could hold the key to understanding their incredible lifespan.
