Iliana Iotova has won Bulgaria’s presidential runoff, becoming the country’s first elected female president. Her victory marks a historic moment for women in Bulgarian politics. It also strengthens Prime Minister Rumen Radev’s political direction. That includes calls for dialogue with Moscow and opposition to military aid for Ukraine.
