So here's what I find really inspiring - Lokendra Singh, a software engineer from Hyderabad, decided to do something different when faced with India's road rage problem. Instead of getting frustrated like everyone else, he started standing at traffic signals on his days off, teaching drivers about helmets, seatbelts, lane discipline and basic safety. What began after witnessing a fatal accident has grown into something called Empowering Road Scholars, which has already reached over 10,000 people with practical driving lessons. And here's the thing - his simple goal is spot on: fewer accidents, safer roads, and more people getting home to their families safely.
