A wealth of new dates is prompting researchers to rethink the timeline of Inca history. The findings suggest that familiar accounts of when the Inca civilization developed may need a fresh look. By adding new chronological evidence, the work offers a clearer way to explore how this influential society took shape. And here's the thing: even long-held stories about the past can shift when new evidence comes to light.
