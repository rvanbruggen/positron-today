Kazakhstan has taken a remarkable step by releasing the first Amur tiger into the wild in over 70 years. This exciting initiative aims to restore the extinct Caspian tiger to Central Asia, which is just fantastic! The project is made possible through a Memorandum of Understanding signed between Kazakhstan and Russia in 2022. It’s a thrilling moment for wildlife conservation and a hopeful sign for the future of these magnificent creatures.
