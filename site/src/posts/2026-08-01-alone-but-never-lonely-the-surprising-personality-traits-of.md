It's fascinating to discover that many people actually crave solitude, especially in a world where loneliness is often highlighted. Research shows that enjoying alone time doesn't necessarily mean you're an introvert; instead, it relates more to a trait called dispositional autonomy. This means people who love solitude tend to align their actions with their values and are less influenced by external pressures. So, if you find joy in your own company, you might just be living authentically and exploring your emotions more deeply.
