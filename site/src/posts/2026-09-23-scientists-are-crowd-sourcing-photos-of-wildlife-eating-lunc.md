I find it fascinating that scientists are now crowd-sourcing photos of animals eating to understand complex food webs better. This project, called Who Eats Whom, uses the iNaturalist platform where people upload and tag wildlife photos from around the world. It’s not just about cool snapshots — this data helps track how ecosystems change, especially with climate shifts. And here’s the thing: it’s a powerful way to gather info that would take scientists ages to collect on their own. So: by watching who eats whom, we get a clearer picture of nature’s messy, beautiful connections.
