So, there's this fantastic festival called Musicorum happening all summer at the Royal Museums of Fine Arts in Brussels. It's all about making classical music more accessible, which is such a brilliant idea! With free concerts in a prestigious setting, it really invites everyone to enjoy the beauty of classical sounds without the usual intimidating atmosphere. I love how it challenges the norms of traditional concert experiences and opens up this wonderful world to a broader audience.
