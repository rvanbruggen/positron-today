Isn't it fascinating how fire and language are intertwined? Researchers suggest that the flickering light of a campfire may have pushed humans toward developing spoken language instead of relying solely on gestures. This light not only made hand signals hard to follow but also created a cozy atmosphere for storytelling, enhancing social connections. The idea that our ancestors gathered around fires to share tales is such a beautiful thought, reminding us of the importance of connection in our lives today.
