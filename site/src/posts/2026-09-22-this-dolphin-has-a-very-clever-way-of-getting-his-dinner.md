So, I just read about this fascinating dolphin named Bubbles who has developed a rather cheeky way to get his lunch. Instead of hunting normally, he actually bullies other fish until they cough up their own food - which is just wild to think about! It is the first time we have ever seen this kind of kleptoparasitism in whales or dolphins, and honestly, it shows just how incredibly smart these creatures are. And here is the thing: even though it is a bit naughty, you have to admire the sheer ingenuity of his strategy. Nature really never ceases to amaze me with these clever little survival tricks.
