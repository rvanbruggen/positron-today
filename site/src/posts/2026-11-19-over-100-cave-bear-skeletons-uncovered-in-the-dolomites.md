So: a mountain guide has discovered a remarkable cave high on a cliff in Italy’s Dolomites, containing thousands of bones from more than 100 extinct cave bears. Local authorities have secured the site to protect it from looting, and scientists will begin investigating it after the winter. The international team will bring together paleontologists, climate researchers, botanists, cave specialists and ancient-DNA experts. Their work could reveal more about how these giant, mostly plant-eating bears lived in the Alps and what led to their extinction.
