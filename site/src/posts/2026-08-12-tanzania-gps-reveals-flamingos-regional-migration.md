Tanzanian scientists have made an exciting discovery about lesser flamingos migrating across Tanzania and Kenya using GPS tracking. They found that one flamingo took a route through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, while another ventured farther north to Lake Logipi. This research is crucial for understanding the birds' seasonal movements and the wetlands they rely on. It also highlights the importance of Lake Natron as a key breeding site for these beautiful birds.
