Researchers have engineered a tobacco-related plant to produce paclitaxel, a chemotherapy drug sold as Taxol. The medicine is used to treat several types of cancer, including breast and lung tumours. This proof-of-concept study shows that plants could be harnessed to make the drug. And here's the thing: the approach could offer a more sustainable way to produce this important therapy.
