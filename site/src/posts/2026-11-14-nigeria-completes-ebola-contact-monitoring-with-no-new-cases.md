So: Nigeria has completed 21 days of monitoring for all 62 people who had contact with its first Ebola patient in more than a decade, and none developed the disease. Thirteen people reported fever or other symptoms, but each tested negative. The patient, a Swiss woman who had travelled from Uganda, recovered and was discharged from hospital. And here's the thing: monitoring is complete, but Nigeria must still wait 42 days from the patient's recovery or departure before the outbreak can officially be declared over.
