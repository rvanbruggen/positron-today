It's fascinating how we seem to crave negative news despite our natural inclination for comfort. Studies show that we react more strongly to bad news, which actually boosts engagement. This negativity bias is a reflex, not a choice, making it easier for us to consume doom and gloom. Yet, embracing a bit of discomfort can lead to personal growth and better understanding of the world around us. Positivity isn't naive; it's a conscious effort that strengthens us.
