Now, here's something exciting! Researchers in South Korea have developed contact lenses that can repair scratches on their own when exposed to UV light. This innovative hydrogel technology not only fixes small imperfections in under an hour but also offers enhanced durability and an antibacterial coating. Imagine being able to extend the life of your lenses and reduce waste—it's a game changer for millions of users. With potential for home repairs using everyday UV devices, this could revolutionize how we care for our vision.
