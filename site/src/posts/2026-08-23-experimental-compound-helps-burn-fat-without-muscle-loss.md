Researchers at UC Berkeley have discovered a new compound called TOFA that helps burn fat without losing muscle mass. Unlike GLP-1 medications that reduce appetite, TOFA increases energy expenditure by activating genes that promote fat usage for fuel. In experiments with mice, TOFA improved insulin sensitivity and reduced triglyceride levels while preserving lean muscle. This innovative approach could revolutionize treatments for obesity and diabetes, making it an exciting development in metabolic health.
