So, it turns out that nuclear power might be the key to greener cargo shipping! A South Korean consortium has received approval to explore using molten salt nuclear reactors in future vessels. This could allow ships to travel faster and carry more cargo while significantly reducing fossil fuel emissions. It's an exciting step towards transforming the shipping industry and meeting our energy needs sustainably.
