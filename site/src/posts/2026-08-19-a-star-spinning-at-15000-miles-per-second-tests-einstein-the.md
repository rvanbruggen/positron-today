This star is not just fast; it’s also the closest one ever observed to the black hole, orbiting at a distance similar to that between Earth and Saturn. Researchers believe studying S301 could unlock secrets about spacetime in extreme environments, which is just fascinating! Plus, the technology used to observe it is cutting-edge, showcasing the brilliance of modern astronomy.
