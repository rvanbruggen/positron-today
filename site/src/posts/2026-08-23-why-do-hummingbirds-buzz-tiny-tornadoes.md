Hummingbirds are fascinating little creatures, especially in Ecuador's Chocó Andino de Pichincha Biosphere Reserve, where you can see dozens of species in one afternoon. Their buzzing sound is not just charming; it comes from their unique flight mechanics and rapid wing flaps. These tiny birds have evolved to hover and dart in all directions, which is quite a feat! It's amazing to think that they consume so much nectar daily, almost like eating hundreds of hamburgers, just to fuel their incredible energy needs.
