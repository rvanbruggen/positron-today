So, how far has AI really made it into the lab? The NZZ spoke with five researchers exploring how it can transform fields from biology to history. At EPFL, Bruno Correia’s Bindcraft designs proteins that bind to other proteins, helping researchers identify promising candidates for medicines in days or weeks instead of years. The designs still need to be tested in the lab, but they could also help make gene therapies safer and let scientists test ideas much faster.
