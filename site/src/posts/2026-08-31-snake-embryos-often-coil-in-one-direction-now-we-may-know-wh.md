Isn't it fascinating how snake embryos tend to coil in one direction? Researchers have delved into this intriguing behavior and may have uncovered the underlying reasons. It turns out that the way they develop can influence their movements and orientation. This discovery not only sheds light on snake biology but also opens doors to understanding similar patterns in other species.
