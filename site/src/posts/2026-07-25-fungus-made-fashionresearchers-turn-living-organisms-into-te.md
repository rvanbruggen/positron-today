Imagine wearing clothes that are actually alive! Researchers have developed a biodegradable textile made from fungi that can even host engineered microbes to change color or provide UV protection. This living material can renew itself if damaged, which is just mind-blowing. While it’s not ready for your winter wardrobe yet, it opens up exciting possibilities for sustainable fashion and innovative uses like temporary structures.
