So, sugarcane waste can become much more than paper: its fibrous residue, bagasse, is finding fresh uses across India. Chuk, Ecoware and Dinearth turn it into plates, bowls and takeaway packaging that offer an alternative to plastic and Styrofoam. These products give an agricultural byproduct a second life and can break down under suitable conditions. A fourth venture is exploring how sugarcane residue can help produce protein through fermentation, opening up another exciting possibility.
