Did you know that lake sturgeon can live for up to 300 years? A recent study revealed these magnificent fish are among North America's oldest freshwater species, with some even outliving historical events! Researchers from Michigan State University found that by analyzing long-term data, they could better estimate the age of these ancient swimmers. This discovery not only highlights their incredible longevity but also underscores the importance of conservation efforts to protect their populations.
