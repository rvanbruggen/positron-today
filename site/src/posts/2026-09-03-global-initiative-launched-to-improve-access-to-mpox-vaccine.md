A fantastic new global initiative was launched to create a stockpile of mpox vaccines, ensuring countries can access them during outbreaks. This initiative, approved by Gavi, the Vaccine Alliance, will start operations soon and is funded by Gavi and coordinated by the International Coordinating Group. It's all about making sure that everyone, especially those in need, can get timely access to these vital vaccines. This is a huge step forward in the fight against mpox, which continues to affect many around the world.
