Finland's innovative 'Housing First' approach to homelessness is a fascinating model that prioritizes housing as a human right. Vello Senkel, a resident of a supportive housing unit, shares how having a stable home has transformed his life, allowing him to reconnect with his passions like cooking and music. The program emphasizes continuous support and community involvement, making it a holistic solution rather than just a temporary fix. It's a powerful reminder that addressing homelessness requires understanding and long-term commitment.
