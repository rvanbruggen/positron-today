The spider-tailed horned viper from Iran has a fascinating hunting strategy that involves using its tail, which resembles a spider, to attract birds. When a bird approaches, thinking it's found an easy meal, the snake strikes! This unique species was only recognized in 2006, and its tail has puzzled scientists for decades. Recent studies using advanced imaging techniques have revealed more about this remarkable mimicry, showcasing the incredible adaptations of nature.
