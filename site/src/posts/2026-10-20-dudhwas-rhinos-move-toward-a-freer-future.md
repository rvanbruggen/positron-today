So, Dudhwa’s rhino story has come a long way since five animals arrived in 1984, and the reserve now counts 53. In March 2026, four rhinos moved from a fenced rehabilitation area into grassland, bringing the number of mature rhinos living freely in the reserve to eight. The next goal is to explore whether 10 can thrive as part of a resilient population, with healthy habitats, genetic diversity and room to move all playing a vital role. And here’s the bigger lesson: lasting recovery means creating a landscape where rhinos can live and breed with less need for constant human intervention.
