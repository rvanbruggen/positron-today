Wow, what an incredible sight in Porto-Novo! Thousands came together for the third annual Benin Mask Festival, showcasing vibrant costumes and mesmerizing dances. Performers donned elaborate wooden masks, bringing ancestral traditions to life with every beat of the drums. It's a fantastic opportunity for visitors to dive deeper into Beninese culture and move beyond the usual stereotypes.
