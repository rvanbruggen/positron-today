The Bièvre, Paris' hidden second river, is making a comeback! Once buried under concrete, this beautiful waterway is being liberated to provide green spaces and combat urban heat. Local authorities are embracing eco-friendly projects to revive the river, which has a rich history tied to the city's economy and culture. With a push for biodiversity and climate adaptation, the Bièvre's revival is a hopeful sign for urban nature.
