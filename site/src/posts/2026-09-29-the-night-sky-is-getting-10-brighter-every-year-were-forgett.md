I find it fascinating that the night sky is growing 10% brighter every year, making it harder for us to experience true darkness. Living in cities like Portland, I’ve realized how much artificial light invades our nights, and it takes a trip to places like Oregon’s dark sky reserves to remember what a real starry sky looks like. The article highlights how light pollution not only dims our view of the stars but also disrupts wildlife and human health. The good news is some places are already taking action with smart lighting rules, showing us that preserving darkness is possible and worth it.
