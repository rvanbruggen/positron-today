So, here's some exciting news! A long-term study has found that statins can lower the risk of dementia by up to 15%, especially when taken earlier in life. With dementia cases expected to rise dramatically, this could be a game-changer. The research tracked over 132,000 people and showed that those who started statins early had a significantly reduced risk. It's fascinating to think that a common cholesterol medication might also help protect our brains!
