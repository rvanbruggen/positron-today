Leila Seth was a trailblazer, becoming India's first woman Chief Justice and advocating for equal rights for daughters in property matters. Her journey began almost by accident, but her impact on the legal landscape has been monumental. She was instrumental in shaping reforms after the 2012 Delhi gang rape, pushing for changes in laws regarding sexual violence. Leila's legacy continues to inspire, showing that justice is about protecting people, not punishing them.
