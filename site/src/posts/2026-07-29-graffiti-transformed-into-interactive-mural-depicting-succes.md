I love how St. Mungo’s is flipping the script on graffiti with their new interactive mural campaign called 'We Are Here.' This project showcases the inspiring stories of individuals who have successfully transitioned out of homelessness, like Lee, who now teaches creative writing. Each mural features a QR code that lets you hear their personal journeys, making it so engaging! It's a beautiful way to remind us that recovery and hope are possible, and that support plays a crucial role in these transformations.
