This article highlights an innovative solution to the childcare affordability crisis by providing free housing for educators. Eric Gil, a dedicated intern, faced housing challenges while pursuing his passion for early childhood education. The Friends Center for Children in New Haven identified this issue and created a housing program that not only supports teachers but also enhances stability for children and families. By investing in real estate, the center is able to offer significant financial benefits to its staff while fostering a nurturing environment for learning.
