So: Backpack, the gentle giant of Alaska’s Katmai National Park, has won the 12th annual Fat Bear Week! This contest celebrates the incredible brown bears that bulk up on salmon to survive winter, and Backpack took the crown with over 100,000 votes. What’s really cool is that he’s known for his calm nature, not aggression, which makes his win even more special. And here's the thing - he’s the son of a former champion, carrying on a proud family tradition in the wild.
