So, researchers have discovered a new species of ice age toad at the La Brea Tar Pits, which is only the second amphibian fossil from this era found in North America! This toad hopped into a tar pit around 45,000 years ago, and its well-preserved bones are giving scientists valuable insights into ancient climates. The unique characteristics of this toad's bones suggest it belongs to a completely new species, making it an exciting find for paleoecologist J. Alberto Cruz. This discovery not only highlights the rarity of amphibian fossils but also helps us understand the environmental conditions of the past.
