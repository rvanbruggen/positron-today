I am absolutely blown away by the story of Fariba Hashimi, an Afghan cyclist who literally had to disguise herself as a man just to escape the Taliban's regime. Now, she has managed to secure a silver medal at the Asian Games in Japan, which is just such an incredible triumph of the human spirit. It is heart-wrenching to hear about the dangers she faced, including being stoned while training, but she has turned that pain into pure athletic power. And here is the thing - she is not stopping here, as she is already looking ahead to the 2028 games in Los Angeles. It really makes me smile to see such resilience and hope shining through.
