3D printing is revolutionizing lab work in exciting ways! Researchers like Trevor Rife are creating custom tools that save time and money, making science more accessible. With 3D printers costing less than $2,500, it's easier than ever to design and share equipment. This technology not only enhances research efficiency but also allows for incredible customization, limited only by creativity. It's truly a game changer for scientists around the world!
