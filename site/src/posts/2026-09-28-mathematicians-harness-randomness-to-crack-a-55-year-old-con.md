So: I just read about this fascinating breakthrough regarding a problem that the legendary Ronald Graham posed back in 1971. It turns out that this puzzle, which might have been inspired by his love for juggling, was finally solved by a group of brilliant young mathematicians. They used the power of randomness to prove that you can always arrange certain numbers in a specific way, even in the tricky world of clock arithmetic. And here's the thing - it took four papers and a lot of collaboration to get there. Which brings me to the beauty of how these probabilistic methods can unlock such long-standing mysteries.
