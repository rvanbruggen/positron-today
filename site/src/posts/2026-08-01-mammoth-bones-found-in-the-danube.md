So, some incredible mammoth bones have been discovered near the village of Ryahovo in northern Bulgaria! A local resident spotted them, and experts from the Regional Museum of History in Ruse confirmed they belong to a woolly mammoth from the last ice age. They found a jawbone, fragments of tusks, and even a femur, all in remarkable condition. This find is particularly exciting because the bones were found together, suggesting they came from a single young mammoth.
