New Mexico is making waves with its rapid transition to clean energy, thanks to the Public Service Company of New Mexico. They’re already delivering 80% carbon-free electricity, a remarkable feat achieved in just five years! The state’s Energy Transition Act is driving this change, mandating a shift to renewable sources and creating opportunities for workers affected by coal plant closures. It’s exciting to see how this transformation not only benefits the environment but also opens new economic pathways for the community.
