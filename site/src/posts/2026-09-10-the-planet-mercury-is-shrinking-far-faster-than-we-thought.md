So, it turns out that Mercury is shrinking more than scientists previously thought! New research reveals that the planet's surface has more 'wrinkles' than we realized, which are actually signs of its contraction over billions of years. These findings give us a fresh perspective on Mercury's geological history and evolution. It's fascinating to think that understanding Mercury could even shed light on other celestial bodies like the Moon and Mars!
