The Solomon Islands have made an exciting promise to provide free universal education by 2027. This commitment was shared at the United Nations Human Rights Council, where the government also highlighted efforts to tackle transnational crime and improve human rights protections. I love how they’re focusing on making sure no child misses school due to fees, especially in rural areas. Plus, they’re working on raising the legal marriage age to 18, showing real care for young girls and women. It’s clear they’re serious but also realistic about needing international support to make it all happen.
