So, Estonia’s strong PISA results are rooted in a deep respect for education, which many see as vital both for personal opportunity and national security. In a Tallinn classroom, focused discussion and calm turn-taking offer a striking contrast with the disorder some Swedish schools face. Yet Estonian teachers and students say reading is getting harder, and teenagers are reading less than before. The challenge now is to nurture deeper reading and reflection in a world of fast-moving social media.
