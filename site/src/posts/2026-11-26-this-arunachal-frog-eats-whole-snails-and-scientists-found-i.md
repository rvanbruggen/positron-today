After five years of fieldwork in Arunachal Pradesh, researchers from the University of Delhi and Harvard University have identified five previously unknown horned frogs. One of them, Jingophrys tanogogo, is the first frog recorded in India to eat land snails, shells and all. The team spotted the shells inside its gut using 3D micro-CT imaging, revealing a feeding behaviour not previously documented in its frog family. Local tribal communities also helped researchers explore the Eastern Himalayas, where the nocturnal frogs live.
