So, here's a delightful surprise from Nepal: researchers filmed tiger Goma caring for her own cubs alongside three cubs belonging to another tigress, Jugini. Tigers are usually thought to live and raise their young alone, so this rare glimpse of shared parenting has wildlife experts rethinking what they know. The team suspects the tigresses may be cooperating to protect their cubs from males, though the reason remains a mystery. And here's the thing - moments like this show how much there is still to learn about even the most iconic animals.
