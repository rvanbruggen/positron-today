So: in Sampangiram Nagar, an old stone well was once just a dumping ground full of plastic and debris. Now, thanks to a tough cleanup led by SayTrees Environmental Trust and local support, it’s been restored to hold over 43,000 litres of water. The well, 40 feet deep, was cleared out with a mix of manual labor and machinery, turning it from forgotten to functional. And here's the thing - residents and civic authorities now maintain it, providing more than 1,000 litres daily for community use. It’s a brilliant example of how a neglected resource can be revived with care and teamwork!
