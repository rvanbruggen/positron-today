I find it fascinating that half of Polish workers aged 45 and above are open to working beyond the official retirement age. The study reveals that health, not just legal age, is a key factor in deciding how long people want to stay active professionally. It’s also interesting how attitudes differ between public and private sectors, and between men and women. And here’s the thing - this willingness to keep working could be crucial for Poland’s economic stability as experienced workers stay in the labor market longer.
