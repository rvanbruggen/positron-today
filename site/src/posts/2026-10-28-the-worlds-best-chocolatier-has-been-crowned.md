So, the new World Chocolate Master is Italian chocolatier Tommaso Grollaro. He won the title yesterday evening in Antwerp after two days of competition. China's Lyu Haoran took second place, followed by Japan's Keita Koshimizu. A sweet achievement for Grollaro on the world stage.
