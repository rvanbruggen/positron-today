Philippe Rahm is advocating for the creation of 'cactus cities' as a response to increasing droughts and heatwaves. His vision emphasizes sustainable architecture that harmonizes with the environment, aiming to create urban spaces that can thrive in challenging climates. This innovative approach not only addresses climate change but also enhances the quality of life for residents. It's an exciting time for architecture, as new ideas like Rahm's can lead to a more resilient future.
