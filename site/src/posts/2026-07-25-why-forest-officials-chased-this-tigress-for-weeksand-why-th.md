On International Tiger Day, we celebrate the inspiring journey of Zeenat, a young tigress who defied the odds after being tracked for weeks. After travelling nearly 300 kilometers, she was tranquillised and returned to Similipal, where she has now successfully raised four cubs. This marks a significant victory for Odisha's tiger supplementation programme aimed at enhancing genetic diversity. Zeenat's story has transformed from one of uncertainty to a beacon of hope for wildlife conservation.
