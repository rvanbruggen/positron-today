So, this is fascinating! Astronomers have identified a new phenomenon called a black hole star, which is the size of our solar system and shines bright red in the cosmos. Using the James Webb Space Telescope, they've observed this object, known as MoM-BH*-1, which emits an astonishing amount of energy—about 100 billion times more than any star could through nuclear fusion. It seems to be a black hole surrounded by dense gas, and its discovery could help explain some of the mysterious red spots found in the early universe. How exciting is it that we’re uncovering such incredible cosmic secrets?
