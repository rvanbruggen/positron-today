Stained glass is attracting a new generation of artisans, according to Le Monde. The craft is described as “a school of humility,” hinting at the patience and care its practice calls for. I find it lovely to see this traditional art capturing the interest of younger makers. Their enthusiasm could help keep the skills of stained-glass making alive.
