Heart Aerospace has just made a significant leap in aviation by successfully flying the X1, the largest fully electric aircraft ever. This 27-minute test flight demonstrated the feasibility of electric flying in commercial conditions, and it only cost about $5 in electricity! The potential for reducing operational costs by over 40% compared to traditional jets is incredibly exciting. With major airlines already showing interest, the future of affordable and eco-friendly air travel looks promising.
