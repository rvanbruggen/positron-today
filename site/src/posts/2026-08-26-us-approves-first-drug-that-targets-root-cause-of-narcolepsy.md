Exciting news from the pharmaceutical world! A Japanese company has just received FDA approval for Orzeyful, the first drug that treats the root cause of narcolepsy type-1. This condition affects many people, causing sudden drowsiness and muscle weakness, but now there's hope for a more effective treatment. By mimicking orexin, a crucial compound for wakefulness, this new medication could truly change lives and improve the quality of life for those affected.
