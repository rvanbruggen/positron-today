It's fascinating to learn that the brain shows remarkable stability during menopause. This period, often associated with various changes, doesn't seem to disrupt cognitive functions as much as one might expect. Researchers are uncovering that many women experience a consistent mental state, which is quite encouraging. This insight could change how we approach menopause and support women through this transition.
