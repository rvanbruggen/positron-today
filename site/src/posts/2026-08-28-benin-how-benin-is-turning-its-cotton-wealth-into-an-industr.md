Benin is making impressive strides by transforming its cotton production into a thriving industrial economy. At the Glo-Djigbé Industrial Zone, young workers are gaining valuable experience while contributing to this exciting shift. This initiative not only creates jobs but also retains the economic value of cotton that previously left the country. With a focus on processing various agricultural products, Benin is setting a remarkable example for industrialization across Africa.
