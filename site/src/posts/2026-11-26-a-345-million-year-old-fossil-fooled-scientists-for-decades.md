So, a fossil nicknamed Lizzie was long seen as one of the earliest reptiles - but powerful X-ray scans have revealed a very different story. Beneath the rock, researchers found a primitive skull, internal gills and a fish-like mouth lined with thousands of tiny teeth. Those clues suggest Westlothiana lizziae was aquatic or amphibious, not an early land-dwelling reptile. And here's the fascinating twist: features such as claws and five digits may have evolved in ancestors that were still closely tied to water.
