Archaeologists have discovered ancient ostrich eggshell engravings in southern Africa that are over 60,000 years old. These markings show an impressive level of geometric organization, suggesting that early Homo sapiens had a structured way of thinking. The research highlights the ability to create complex visual designs, indicating a significant development in human cognition. This fascinating study opens a window into the abstract thought processes of our ancestors.
