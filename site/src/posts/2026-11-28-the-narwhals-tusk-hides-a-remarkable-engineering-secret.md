So, the narwhal’s famous spiral “unicorn horn” is actually a tooth - and scientists have uncovered how its tiny building blocks help make it remarkably sturdy. Using advanced X-ray imaging, an international team found that the tusk’s microscopic components form two intertwined helices that twist in opposite directions. Collagen fibers and mineral crystals work together like reinforcement in concrete, giving the tusk both strength and flexibility. Its exact purpose remains a mystery, but this discovery offers a fascinating new look at how nature builds such an extraordinary structure.
