So: researchers are using AI to recognize an individual bird from its song and follow it over time. That could help scientists learn how birds move and behave without needing to attach tracking devices. Each bird’s distinctive vocal patterns become a natural clue to its identity. It’s a promising way to make wildlife monitoring more detailed and less intrusive.
