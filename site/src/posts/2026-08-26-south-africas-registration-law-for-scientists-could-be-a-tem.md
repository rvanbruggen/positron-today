So, South Africa is considering a law that would require scientists to register with a professional body, which could really elevate standards in the field. However, the current draft has some serious clarity issues that could confuse many in the scientific community. It’s essential to involve scientists in the conversation to ensure the law is effective and fair. And here's the thing: while the intent is good, the proposed penalties seem a bit harsh, especially without clear guidelines.
