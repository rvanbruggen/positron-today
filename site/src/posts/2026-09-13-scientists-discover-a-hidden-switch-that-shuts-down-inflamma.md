Researchers at University College London have uncovered a biological mechanism that helps the body end inflammation, which could pave the way for new treatments for chronic inflammatory diseases. Inflammation is crucial for fighting infections, but when it lingers, it can harm healthy tissue. The study highlights small fat-derived molecules called epoxy-oxylipins that act like natural brakes on the immune system. By blocking an enzyme, scientists found that these molecules can help resolve pain more quickly and reduce harmful immune cells, offering hope for safer treatments.
