I find it fascinating that CT scans are helping us understand how prehistoric crocodiles could have made the incredible journey across the Atlantic Ocean. These scans reveal details about their anatomy that suggest they were capable of long-distance travel, which is quite surprising! So: this challenges what we thought about their mobility and opens new doors for studying ancient animal behavior. And here's the thing - it makes me wonder what other secrets are hidden in fossils waiting to be uncovered.
