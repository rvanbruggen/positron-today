Isn't it fascinating how male blackbucks transform as they mature? They start off with a light fawn coat, but as they grow, they develop this striking black-and-white appearance. This change is not just about looks; it’s tied to their sexual maturity and social behavior. The dark coat and impressive horns help them compete for mates and establish dominance in their herds.
