Octopuses are renowned for their intelligence, and researchers at KU Leuven have now mapped how their large, complex brains develop. With around 500 million nerve cells, an octopus has about as many as a dog. The team found that glial cells appear before nerve cells and help guide their movement, connections and the formation of brain regions. This discovery, published in Current Biology, reveals one fascinating way evolution can produce large brains.
