So, dark matter is one of those big puzzles in physics, right? Researchers from Japan have come up with a fascinating idea to use Earth's magnetic environment as a giant detector for ultralight particles like axions and dark photons. They found that the region between the Earth's surface and the ionosphere can amplify signals, allowing them to explore frequencies that were previously ignored. What's even more exciting is that they identified several intriguing signal candidates that might hint at dark matter, although the source remains a mystery!
