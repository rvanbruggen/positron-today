Growing up with albinism, Nigerian artist Dapo Richard Oni never saw a comic-book character who looked like him. Now, he is drawing on those childhood experiences to tell a story of resilience. His work presents children with albinism as heroes, offering young readers characters they can recognise and admire. And here's the thing: Oni hopes the story will inspire a new generation.
