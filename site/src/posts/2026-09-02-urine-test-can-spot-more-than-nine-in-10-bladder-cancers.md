So, there's some exciting news in the world of bladder cancer detection! A new urine test, developed by the University of Birmingham, can identify 92% of bladder cancer cases, which is a huge leap forward. While it won't replace cystoscopies, it helps prioritize patients who need urgent attention. This could really change the game for early detection and treatment, saving lives in the process!
