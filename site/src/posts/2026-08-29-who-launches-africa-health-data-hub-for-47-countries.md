The World Health Organization has just launched an exciting new health data platform aimed at helping African governments track health trends and respond to outbreaks more effectively. Unveiled in Addis Ababa, this Regional Health Data Hub will consolidate data from various national systems, covering crucial areas like maternal health and infectious diseases. By using advanced tools like artificial intelligence, the hub will help identify gaps in services and improve health planning across the continent. This initiative is a big step towards strengthening health systems in Africa and ensuring better responses to health emergencies.
