So, researchers at PC Fruit in Sint-Truiden have made an exciting discovery! They've found that clover and herb strips can keep fruit orchards up to 20 degrees cooler than traditional grass. This year, with the heat and drought, the difference is striking – while grass is brown and dry, the clover and herbs are still vibrant and green. This not only helps with temperature but also improves soil structure, making it a win-win for fruit growers.
