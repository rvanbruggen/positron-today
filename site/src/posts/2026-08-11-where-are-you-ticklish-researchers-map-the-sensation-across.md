Isn't it fascinating how ticklishness is a universal sensation? Researchers found that people from different cultural backgrounds, like Dutch, Greek, and Chinese, all experience ticklishness in similar areas of the body. This study, published in Nature Human Behaviour, shows that ticklishness is distinct and consistent, featuring spots like the neck and armpits. It opens the door for more research into this playful sensation, which has been overlooked for too long!
