So, researchers have found that a low-fat vegan diet can help people lose weight without needing to eat less. Participants in a study enjoyed the same amount of food but reduced their calorie intake significantly, thanks to the lower energy density of plant-based foods. This means they could fill their plates with satisfying meals while still consuming fewer calories overall. It's a fascinating approach that shows how choosing the right foods can make a big difference in weight management!
