So, here's the exciting news! Researchers have discovered that a low-cost liquid called silver diamine fluoride can effectively stop cavities without the need for drilling or sedation. In a recent study, this treatment showed promising results, halting decay in over half of the affected baby teeth when applied every six months. This could be a game-changer for young children and others who struggle with traditional dental procedures. It's all about making dental care more accessible and less painful!
