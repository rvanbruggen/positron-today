So, scientists have just unveiled some truly stunning images of the sun's surface, revealing whirlpools of plasma that we've never seen before! Using the incredible Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope in Hawaii, they captured the sharpest images yet of the sun's photosphere. This breakthrough helps us understand how energy moves through the sun and can even lead to space weather that affects us here on Earth. It's like discovering a whole new world right in our own solar system!
