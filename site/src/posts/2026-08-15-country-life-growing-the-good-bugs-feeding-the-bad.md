At a unique greenhouse in New Zealand, Chris Thompson and his team are doing something quite fascinating – they’re actually nurturing pests to help grow beneficial insects. It’s all about balance, as these pests serve as food for the good bugs that help farmers. Chris’s enthusiasm shines through as he explains the intricate process of breeding both types of insects, which is crucial for sustainable farming. With a family legacy and innovative techniques, Bioforce is making waves in the agricultural world, proving that sometimes, you need the bad to grow the good.
