NASA scientists have discovered that some Earth microbes might survive in the Moon's shadowed regions, particularly near the South Pole. This finding is crucial as it highlights the challenges of contamination from astronauts when studying the Moon's ancient geology. Interestingly, some microbes, like Aspergillus niger, have shown surprising resilience in space conditions. This research not only informs lunar exploration but also prepares us for future missions to Mars.
