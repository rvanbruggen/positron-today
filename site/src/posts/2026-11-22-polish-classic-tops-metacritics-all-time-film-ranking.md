Here’s a lovely piece of news for Polish cinema: Krzysztof Kieślowski’s The Decalogue has reached the top of Metacritic’s all-time best films ranking. It earned positive reviews from every critic counted, a perfect 100 percent, a feat shared by only 14 titles on the list, while audience reactions are also strong at 77 percent. The 1988 ten-part television cycle, made with screenwriter Krzysztof Piesiewicz, explores modern moral and existential choices through stories inspired by the Ten Commandments. Now, it sits alongside giants such as The Godfather and Casablanca, while Kieślowski’s Three Colors: Red also features among the highly rated works.
