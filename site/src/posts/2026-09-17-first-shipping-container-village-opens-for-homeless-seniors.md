Phoenix is tackling a growing crisis - seniors make up about 40% of emergency shelter residents in the area, yet housing solutions have been sparse. Now Senior Bridge has opened with 40 studio units built from repurposed shipping containers, powered entirely by solar panels and batteries to keep costs down. Each resident gets paired with a case manager to help them access healthcare and permanent housing within about 90 days, with an additional 54 supportive apartments coming by end of 2026. What I find brilliant here is how the program brings everything together - housing, social services, and community support all in one place, because as the CEO says, you can't solve homelessness in isolation.
