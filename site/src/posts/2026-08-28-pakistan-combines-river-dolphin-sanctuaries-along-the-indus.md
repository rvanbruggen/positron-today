How exciting is this? Pakistan has just created a wildlife sanctuary along 123 miles of the Indus River to protect the endangered Indus River Dolphin! With only about 2,000 of these incredible creatures left, this new sanctuary connects existing protected areas, creating a whopping 249 miles of safe habitat. The WWF is teaming up with local governments to ensure these dolphins can thrive, which is fantastic news for the entire river ecosystem. It’s a big step towards preserving not just the dolphins, but the whole environment they depend on.
