So, Africa’s solar boom is increasingly being powered by smaller systems for households and businesses, rather than only by large utility-scale projects. The IEA says solar capacity on the continent has grown eightfold as imports of photovoltaic equipment have surged, largely from China. Nigeria, Algeria, Kenya and Senegal each added more than one gigawatt of solar capacity in 2025, while imports across Africa reached about 26 gigawatts. The gap between equipment imports and recorded installations points to stockpiles, uncounted systems and incomplete data - and leaves plenty of room for solar to keep growing.
