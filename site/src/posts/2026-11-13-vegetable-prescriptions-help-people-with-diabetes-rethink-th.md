So: in Cornwall, the WellFed programme gives people with type 2 diabetes or prediabetes free weekly vegetable boxes, alongside cooking and gardening sessions. More than 100 participants have taken part, and many report better blood sugar levels, weight and wellbeing. Chris Longthorp says his HbA1c fell from 50 to 42, while Johanna Evans discovered she enjoys dishes she never expected to try. Early findings are encouraging, and if the final results confirm the benefits, the programme could inspire similar projects elsewhere in the UK.
