So, here's the exciting part! A new study reveals that specific brain waves during deep sleep can actually protect against the harmful effects of a brain chemical linked to Alzheimer’s disease. Researchers found that stronger sleep spindles and oscillations during non-REM sleep are associated with less cognitive decline over time. This means that improving sleep quality could be a key factor in slowing down Alzheimer’s progression. It's a fascinating step forward in understanding how sleep can impact brain health!
