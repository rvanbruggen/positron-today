Wow, it's exciting to see that onshore windfarm applications in England have surged to their highest level in a decade! After the government lifted the previous ban, about 45 new applications were submitted just in the past year. This is a huge turnaround from the days when applications were barely trickling in. With renewable energy developers coming back, it's clear that onshore wind is becoming a vital part of England's energy future.
