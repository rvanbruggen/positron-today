Genk Cargo Connect is set to electrify its rail access to the port terminals by 2028, making it the first inland terminal in Belgium to offer a direct electric connection. This exciting investment of 1.4 million euros, largely supported by European funds, will enhance Belgium's integration into the European freight rail network. General manager Heidi Ulburghs emphasizes that this move will eliminate the need for freight trains to switch to diesel locomotives, saving both time and money. With this electrification, Genk is not only becoming greener but also more attractive for businesses in the region.
