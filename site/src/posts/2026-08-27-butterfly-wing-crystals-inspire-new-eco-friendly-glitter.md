Dolly Parton has inspired a new eco-friendly glitter that draws from the beauty of butterfly wings. Researchers at Northeastern University are developing a sustainable glitter made from natural pterins found on butterflies, which could replace toxic synthetic materials. This innovative approach not only maintains vibrant colors but is also safe for the environment and people. It's a wonderful way to honor Parton's legacy while promoting sustainability in the glitter industry.
