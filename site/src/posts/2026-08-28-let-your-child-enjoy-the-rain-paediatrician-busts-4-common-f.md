It's fascinating how the first drops of rain can spark such different reactions in families! While kids are eager to splash around, parents often worry about them getting sick. But Dr. Shravan Krishna Reddy, a paediatrician, clarifies that rain itself doesn't cause colds or weaken immunity. He encourages parents to let their children enjoy safe, supervised play in the rain, emphasizing that the real benefits come from movement and joyful experiences.
