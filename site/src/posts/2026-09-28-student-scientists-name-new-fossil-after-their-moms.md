So, I just read this absolutely heartwarming story about two student scientists who discovered a new frog-like creature from 309 million years ago. They decided to name this ancient little guy Jeanerpeton mazonensis, which is a lovely tribute to both of their mothers who happen to be named Jean. And here's the thing - it was previously stuck in a 'waste bin' of misidentified fossils, but these brilliant interns proved it was actually a unique species all along. It is just so wonderful to see young researchers making such a big mark on paleontology while honouring their families. I find that kind of dedication and heart really inspiring, don't you?
