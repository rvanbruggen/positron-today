So, London's Gingerbread City is back for its tenth edition, bringing together 50 edible architectural creations. Highlights include a carefully crafted Royal Albert Hall, sugar-river bridges and futuristic skyscrapers made with ideas like living algae and self-healing concrete. This year's “Future Forward” theme invites architects to imagine greener, more people-friendly urban living, while a collaboration with homelessness charity St Mungo's shares stories through five gingerbread structures. The exhibition opens at Space House in Holborn on 28 November and runs until 3 January 2027.
