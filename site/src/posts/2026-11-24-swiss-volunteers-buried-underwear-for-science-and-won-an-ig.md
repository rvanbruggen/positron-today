So: about 1,000 volunteers across Switzerland buried more than 2,000 pairs of cotton underwear to help scientists study soil life. Two months later, researchers found that the underwear broke down fastest in private gardens, while pairs buried in lawns lasted the longest. The project showed how land management can shape soil health, and around 240 volunteers even became co-authors. Its playful approach won the team an Ig Nobel Prize - first making people laugh, then giving them plenty to think about.
