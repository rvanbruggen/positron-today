Nightmares can be a real nightmare for those suffering from PTSD, but a new study shows promise! Researchers found that a THC medication significantly reduced the severity of nightmares in participants, with over a third reporting complete relief. This is exciting because traditional treatments often fall short. The study highlights the potential of cannabis-based therapies in addressing trauma-related sleep disturbances.
