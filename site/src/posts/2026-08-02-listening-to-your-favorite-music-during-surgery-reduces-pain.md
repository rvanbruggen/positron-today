How amazing is it that patients can choose their favorite music genre while undergoing surgery? From jazz to hard rock, this innovative approach at ErasmusMC is showing significant benefits. Research indicates that listening to music can reduce pain, stress, and even the likelihood of post-operative delirium. It's a simple yet effective way to enhance patient care, and there's growing interest from insurers to adopt this practice widely.
