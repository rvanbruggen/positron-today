I find it fascinating that the Netherlands, facing more extreme weather like droughts and floods, can learn so much from countries in Africa and Asia. The WWF and Red Cross emphasize that natural solutions and community resilience are key, not just technical fixes like dikes. In places like the Philippines and Malaysia, people rely heavily on their natural environment, which teaches us a broader way to handle climate challenges. And here's the thing: during disasters, people depend on each other, so building collective resilience is just as important as individual preparedness.
