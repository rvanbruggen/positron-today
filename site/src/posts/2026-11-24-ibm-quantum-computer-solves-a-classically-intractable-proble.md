An IBM quantum computer completed an extraordinarily complex computational task in just 15 minutes, a calculation that leading classical methods would take prohibitively long to simulate. The result meets a key test for quantum advantage: doing a task beyond the practical reach of classical computers. Crucially, researchers also developed a way to check the reliability of the results, tackling a major challenge in quantum computing. The IBM and University of Chicago team used specially structured quantum circuits, and their experiment ranks among the largest demonstrations of logical quantum computing to date.
