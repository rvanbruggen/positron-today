It's absolutely amazing to see how quickly children's lungs can recover! After the introduction of the Ultra Low Emission Zone in London, researchers found that young kids showed impressive improvements in lung growth. This study followed over 3,400 children and suggests that clean air zones can significantly reduce pollution's harmful effects on lung development. The results are a real eye-opener, showing that cleaner air can lead to healthier futures for our kids.
