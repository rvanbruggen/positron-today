So, we're on the brink of something amazing in spaceflight! Two robotic missions are set to change how we think about servicing spacecraft. One mission aims to rescue an aging NASA telescope, while the other will give satellites a new lease on life. Thanks to advances in robotics, the idea of repairing and extending the life of these machines is becoming a reality, which could help tackle the growing issue of space junk. It's an exciting time for space science and commerce!
