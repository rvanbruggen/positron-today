So, there's this exciting development from Outokumpu, a Finnish steel company, that's shaking things up in the mining world! They've created a method called selective sulfidization, which allows for extracting valuable metals from low-grade ores and even waste materials. This process is not only energy-efficient but could also lead to the establishment of a new factory in Europe by 2030. Imagine boosting the chromium content in ferrochrome from 53 to 65 percent, all while contributing to climate goals—how cool is that?
