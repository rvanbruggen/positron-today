A new oral medication called AD109 has shown promising results in treating obstructive sleep apnea, reducing breathing interruptions by 44% in a recent clinical trial. This once-nightly pill targets the neuromuscular issues that cause airway collapse during sleep, offering hope for those who struggle with CPAP. With over 40% of participants moving to a less severe category, it’s clear this could be a game changer. The FDA is already reviewing AD109, which could soon provide a much-needed alternative for patients.
