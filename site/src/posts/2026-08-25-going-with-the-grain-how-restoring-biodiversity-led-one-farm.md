Henry Astor's farm in the Cotswolds is buzzing with life as he embraces heritage wheat and biodiversity. With thousands of trees planted and vibrant wildflower meadows, he's reversing the trend of bird population decline on his land. It's fascinating to see how his journey from conventional farming to a nature-friendly approach is making a real difference. This transformation not only benefits the environment but also showcases the power of community efforts in restoring landscapes.
