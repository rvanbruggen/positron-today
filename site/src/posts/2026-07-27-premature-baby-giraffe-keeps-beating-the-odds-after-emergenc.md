A baby giraffe named Milani is defying the odds after being delivered via emergency C-section at Tanganyika Wildlife Park. Despite the challenges of her premature birth, she's now standing, laying down, and even managing to poop on her own. The dedicated team is providing round-the-clock care, ensuring she receives plasma to boost her immune system. It's a heartwarming story of resilience and teamwork, showcasing the incredible efforts to give this little one a fighting chance.
