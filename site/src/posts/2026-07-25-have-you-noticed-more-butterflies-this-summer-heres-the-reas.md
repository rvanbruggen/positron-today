So, if you've been seeing more butterflies this summer, you're absolutely right! Collie Ennis from Trinity College Dublin points out that the warm and dry weather has been perfect for these colorful insects. An early heat wave in May gave them a head start, allowing many caterpillars to pupate and emerge as butterflies. While some species are thriving, there are still challenges ahead, especially for Ireland's resident butterflies, but conservation efforts are on the rise, which is fantastic news.
