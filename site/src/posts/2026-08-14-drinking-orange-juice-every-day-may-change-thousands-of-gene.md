So, it turns out that our beloved orange juice is doing more than just refreshing us at breakfast! A recent study found that drinking 500ml of pure orange juice daily for two months can influence thousands of genes related to inflammation and blood pressure. This means that our morning ritual might actually support heart health by calming inflammation and helping blood vessels relax. It's fascinating how something so simple can have such a profound impact on our bodies!
