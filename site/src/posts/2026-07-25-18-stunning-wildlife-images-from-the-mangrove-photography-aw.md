The Mangrove Photography Awards have showcased some truly breathtaking images that highlight the beauty and importance of mangroves. This year's overall winner, Gaurav Patil, captured a haunting image of a ghost net tangled in mangroves, shedding light on a lesser-known conservation issue. With over 5,000 entries from around the world, the competition celebrates the vital role mangroves play in our ecosystem. It's a fantastic reminder of how art can raise awareness about environmental challenges and inspire action.
