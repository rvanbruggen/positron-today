So, here's a fascinating breakthrough! Researchers have found a way to transform hard-to-recycle PVC into a sustainable industrial lubricant. By stripping chlorine from the plastic and breaking it down with specific chemicals, they create a thick oil that meets high-performance needs. This innovation not only addresses the recycling challenge but also contributes to greener solutions in the lubricant market.
