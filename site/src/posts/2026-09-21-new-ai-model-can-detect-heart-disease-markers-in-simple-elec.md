So, I just read about this incredible breakthrough from the UK where researchers have developed an AI tool that can spot heart disease markers in standard ECG readings. It is absolutely brilliant because, while ECGs are common, they usually cannot detect heart disease on their own. Now, this clever model can identify signs of heart failure and valve disease in seconds, which is a total game-changer for early diagnosis. And here's the thing - it helps doctors prioritize the patients who need urgent care, potentially saving so many lives. I find it truly inspiring to see technology being used to make such a tangible difference in healthcare.
