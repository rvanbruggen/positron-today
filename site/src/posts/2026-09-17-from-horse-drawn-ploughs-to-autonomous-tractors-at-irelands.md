Ireland's 95th Ploughing Championships brought together over 350 competitors and tens of thousands of visitors at Screggan in County Offaly, showcasing the fascinating journey from traditional horse-drawn ploughs to cutting-edge autonomous machinery. The event brilliantly mixed heritage with innovation - you could see veteran farmers demonstrating the ancient Irish loy digging technique right alongside drones, robots, and precision agriculture systems. Manitou's battery-powered telehandler won Machine of the Year honours, proving that modern electric technology can match the performance of traditional diesel alternatives. And here's the thing - this massive three-day gathering, often called Europe's largest outdoor event, perfectly captures the challenge facing Irish and European farmers today: how to blend time-tested skills with new technology while managing rising costs.
