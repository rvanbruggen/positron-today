So: new research shows that the bones found at a 10,000-year-old temple complex actually connect it to a nearby village, rather than some mysterious skull cult. This is fascinating because it changes how we understand the social life of early humans in that area. Instead of a creepy ritual, it looks like a community with strong ties and shared practices. And here's the thing - it reminds me how much we can learn from bones about ancient human connections and daily life.
