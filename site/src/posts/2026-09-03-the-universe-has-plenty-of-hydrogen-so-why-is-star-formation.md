So, it turns out that while the universe is brimming with hydrogen, the formation of new stars is plummeting! Over the last 4.5 billion years, the rate of star formation has dropped to less than half, yet the amount of neutral atomic hydrogen has barely changed. This fascinating research from an international team, led by scientists from the Chinese Academy of Sciences, reveals a puzzling disconnect. Instead of running out of gas, the universe seems to be facing a new mystery about why star formation is becoming so challenging.
