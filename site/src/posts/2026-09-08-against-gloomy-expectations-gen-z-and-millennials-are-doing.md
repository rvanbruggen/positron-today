It's fascinating to see how Gen Z and millennials are actually faring better financially than older generations, despite the common stereotypes. The statistics show that their purchasing power and wealth are higher at the same age compared to baby boomers. However, experts emphasize the importance of distinguishing between objective data and subjective experiences. Mental health remains a significant concern for young people, highlighting the need for a balanced view of their financial success.
