I find it absolutely fascinating that monkeys form friendships with other species! A recent study documented over 400 interactions where primates played, groomed, and even adopted animals like dogs and pigs. This behavior not only shows their social nature but also highlights how these relationships can benefit both parties. It’s a beautiful reminder of the connections that can exist across species, which really makes you think about our own bonds with animals.
