So: after more than 35 years, a badger sett has been found again in the Flemish Ardennes, which is super exciting! This means a family of badgers is living there, with young ones spotted for the first time, showing the population is making a comeback. The exact location is kept secret to protect these shy creatures from disturbance. And here's the thing - thanks to better nature management and less conflict with foxes, badgers are spreading beyond their usual areas in Flanders.
