In Kenya, businesses and community groups are transforming discarded cotton stalks and textile offcuts into valuable products like briquettes and household textiles. This innovative approach not only reduces waste but also creates new income opportunities for farmers and women-led enterprises. Farmers like Peterson Mwadau are now selling their cotton residue instead of burning it, which is a fantastic way to boost household income. It's exciting to see how these sustainable practices can positively impact both the economy and the environment!
