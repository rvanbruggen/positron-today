So, here's the exciting news! A handwritten copy of the Declaration of Independence was recently found in Wells, Maine, dating back to July 1776. This remarkable discovery was made while staff were digging through town records, revealing a direct link to a pivotal moment in American history. The Wells Town Record Book not only contains the Declaration but also the original instructions for its dissemination, making it a treasure trove of local heritage. It's amazing to think that this piece of history has been preserved for 250 years!
