So here's the thing - Pierre Lewy, just 23 years old, decided to do something absolutely mad: cycle all the way from Brussels to Tokyo. He's been documenting this incredible journey on his Instagram account Pierrotcyclo, which has already attracted 21,000 followers who can't get enough of his adventure. Now, six months into this epic challenge, he's currently pedalling through South Korea and sharing the real stories behind this fantastic expedition. What I find really inspiring is how he's bringing people along for the ride - it's not just about the destination, it's about connecting with others through this shared adventure.
