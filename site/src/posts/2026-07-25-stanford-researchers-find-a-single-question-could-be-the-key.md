So, here's the exciting part: Stanford researchers have found that asking someone to elaborate on their opinion can be more effective than throwing facts at them. Questions like 'Could you tell me more about that?' not only make the other person feel heard but also encourage them to be more open-minded. This curiosity can bridge divides that seem impossible to cross. It’s all about creating a space for understanding, which is something we could all use a bit more of these days!
