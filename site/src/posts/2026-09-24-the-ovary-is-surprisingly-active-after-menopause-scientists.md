I was really surprised to learn that ovaries don’t just shut down after menopause like we often think. Francesca Duncan’s research shows these organs keep changing at the molecular level well into older age. Now, scientists are excited to explore what this ongoing activity means for women’s health beyond fertility. It could reshape how we think about aging and hormone production long after the reproductive years have passed.
