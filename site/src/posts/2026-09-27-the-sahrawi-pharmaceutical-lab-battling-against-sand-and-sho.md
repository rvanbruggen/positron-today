I find it fascinating how the Mohamed Embarek Fakal·la Pharmaceutical Production Laboratory thrives in the harsh Sahrawi desert, producing essential medicines despite constant sand and shortages. For over 30 years, this small team of dedicated Sahrawi technicians has crafted painkillers, antibiotics, and more, supporting their refugee community in Tindouf. Now, here’s the thing: while they only cover about 5% of the medication needs, their work is a vital emergency reserve, especially when supplies from outside are delayed. And during the COVID-19 pandemic, they even stepped up to produce hand sanitizer, showing real resilience and innovation.
