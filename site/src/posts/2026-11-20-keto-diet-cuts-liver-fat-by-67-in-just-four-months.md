So, a small clinical trial found that people following a ketogenic diet saw their liver fat fall by 67% in just four to five months. Participants on Mediterranean and low-fat diets also lost weight, but their liver-fat reductions were smaller, even though all three groups lost a similar amount of weight. About half of the participants with prediabetes who followed the keto plan no longer met the criteria for the condition by the end of the trial. These results suggest that cutting carbohydrates may bring metabolic benefits beyond weight loss, though the study involved just 42 people.
