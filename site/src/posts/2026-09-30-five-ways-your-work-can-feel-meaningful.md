I find it fascinating how work can feel meaningful in so many different ways. It’s not just about self-growth or saving the world, but also about the simple joy of the task itself or the connection it brings with others. Work can help us develop ourselves, support our loved ones, and even contribute to a greater cause. And here’s the thing - some people even find a spiritual calling in their work, which adds a whole new layer of meaning.
