I find it fascinating that ancient Etruscan tombs may have been designed as immersive experiences, not just places to bury the dead. Researchers combined AI, acoustic analysis and visual reconstructions to study two painted tombs from the fifth century BC. Their work suggests that echoing sounds, flickering torchlight and ritual scents shaped how visitors experienced the scenes around them. So these remarkable spaces may also have helped families honour their ancestors and sustain their legacy.
