A stunning 11,000-year-old stone sculpture depicting a human figure riding a leopard has been discovered at Karahan Tepe, near Göbekli Tepe in Turkey. This remarkable find, part of ongoing excavations, is reshaping our understanding of early Neolithic societies. The sculpture, which stands 70 centimeters tall, was found in a unique architectural context, suggesting complex social practices. Archaeologists believe this discovery opens new avenues for exploring humanity's earliest history.
