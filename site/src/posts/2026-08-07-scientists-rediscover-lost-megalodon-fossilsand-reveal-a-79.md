It's amazing to think that lost fossils can be found again, right? A recent study has unearthed vertebrae from the legendary Megalodon, revealing that this giant shark could have reached lengths of up to 79 feet! These fossils, thought to be lost for decades, were rediscovered on a museum shelf, which is just fantastic. This discovery not only sheds light on the size of these ancient predators but also highlights the importance of preserving our scientific heritage.
