A new solar-powered model train has returned to a section of Lebanon’s historic railway. The project hopes to bring new life to the country’s long-dormant rail network. It also aims to encourage tourism and renew public interest in the railway’s history. So, a small train is helping spark big hopes for the future of rail in Lebanon.
