Researchers have made a fascinating discovery about Ötzi the Iceman, revealing a mix of ancient and modern microorganisms living on his body. They found bacteria from his original gut flora and cold-adapted yeasts that have survived since he was frozen in a glacier. This unique microbial community offers a rare glimpse into the past, showing how Ötzi's body is not just a relic but a dynamic biological system. It's exciting to think that these findings could have implications for industrial processes in the future!
