So, Francis of Assisi began life as a well-dressed merchant’s son who loved parties and even went to war, but imprisonment left him questioning everything. He turned away from wealth and status, caring for people with leprosy and choosing a life of radical simplicity. As a wandering preacher, he championed human equality and friendship with the natural world, animals included. And here’s the thing: a rediscovered medieval manuscript offers fresh details about how closely he sought to live in harmony with nature.
