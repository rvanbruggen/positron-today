Construction has started on Green Turtle, a massive battery storage facility in Dilsen-Stokkem that will become Belgium's largest. The project, developed by GIGA Storage in collaboration with Tesla, will feature 640 Tesla Megapack 3 batteries with a capacity to power 385,000 households - roughly equivalent to the entire Limburg province. The 700 million euro investment will store renewable energy during periods of excess production and release it during peak demand, helping to stabilize electricity prices across the grid. The facility is expected to be fully operational by mid-2028, transforming a former industrial site while compensating for its carbon footprint within just six months.
