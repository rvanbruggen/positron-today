I love hearing stories like this where dedicated conservation efforts truly pay off. A female eastern lowland gorilla named Lulingu, who was rescued from the illegal wildlife trade as a tiny baby, has just given birth in Virunga National Park. So: after years of rehabilitation at a sanctuary, she was returned to the wild and integrated with a local silverback's family. And here's the thing - this little infant isn't just an adorable newcomer, but a massive genetic boost for an isolated population. Which brings me to the best part, because both mother and baby are doing wonderfully well out in the wild.
