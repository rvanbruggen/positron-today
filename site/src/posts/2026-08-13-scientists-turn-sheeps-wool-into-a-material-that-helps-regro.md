I find it fascinating that scientists have discovered a way to use sheep's wool for bone repair! They focused on keratin, a natural protein from wool, which showed promising results in animal tests by supporting bone regeneration. This new material not only outperformed traditional collagen but also offers a sustainable solution since wool is often discarded as waste. It's exciting to think about the potential impact this could have on regenerative medicine and how it could change the way we approach bone healing.
