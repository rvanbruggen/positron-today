So, researchers have discovered that arginine, an amino acid, plays a crucial role in supporting our immune system. When levels of arginine are low, it can hinder the production of MHC-1 proteins, which are essential for alerting the immune system to threats like cancer cells and viruses. Interestingly, a moderate amount of arginine could potentially restore this important immune function. This exciting finding opens up possibilities for using arginine supplements alongside existing therapies to enhance cancer and viral infection treatments.
