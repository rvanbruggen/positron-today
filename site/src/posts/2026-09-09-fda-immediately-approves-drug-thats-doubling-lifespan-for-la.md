This is incredible news for pancreatic cancer patients! A new drug called daraxonrasib has just been approved by the FDA, and it’s showing remarkable results by doubling the lifespan of patients who have already undergone chemotherapy. In clinical trials, patients went from living an average of 7 months to over 13 months. This breakthrough not only offers hope to those battling pancreatic cancer but also opens doors for research into other cancers that rely on the same protein.
