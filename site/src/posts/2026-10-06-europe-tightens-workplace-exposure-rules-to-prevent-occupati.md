Europe is tightening its standards for exposure to substances linked to occupational cancers. The goal is simple and important: people should not become ill just by going to work. Stricter limits are intended to strengthen prevention and better protect workers’ health. Now, employers will need to pay even closer attention to the risks people face on the job.
