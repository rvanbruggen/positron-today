The Diocese of Antwerp is facing a critical priest shortage that's reshaping Catholic pastoral life across Flanders - with only 70 priests under 70 years old remaining, many traveling between parishes and no clergy left in hospitals, schools, or most prisons. Bishop Johan Bonny has decided to take an unprecedented step: he's pushing Rome to allow him to ordain married men in his diocese, framing it as a practical necessity rather than an ideological stance. He's already met with two cardinals in Rome to present the situation with hard numbers, making clear that relying on foreign priests who don't speak Dutch simply isn't sustainable. Now the Vatican holds the key - Pope Francis and his advisors will decide whether this historic shift for the Belgian Church can move forward, with Bonny banking on pragmatism winning the day.
