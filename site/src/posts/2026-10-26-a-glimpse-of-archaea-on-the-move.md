So, how did cells first gain the ability to crawl? Live imaging of archaea related to early eukaryotes is helping researchers explore this intriguing question. Radler and colleagues’ study offers fresh insight into how crawling movement may have evolved. It fills an important gap in our understanding of cellular evolution.
