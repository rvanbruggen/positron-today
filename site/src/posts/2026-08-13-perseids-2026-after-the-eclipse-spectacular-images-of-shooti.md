So, after the solar eclipse on August 12, astronomy enthusiasts had another reason to look up at the sky! The Perseids reached their peak during the night of August 12-13, providing one of the summer's most beautiful celestial displays. In Turkey, the spectacle was captured perfectly, with long, bright trails visible to the naked eye. And here's the thing: this meteor shower remains active until August 24, so there's still time to catch a glimpse of these shooting stars!
