How exciting is this? Dublin City Council is rolling out a fantastic initiative to provide free sunscreen at 12 parks across the city! This is such a great way to promote sun safety and help reduce the risk of skin cancer, especially since so many people in Ireland have fair skin. The Lord Mayor of Dublin, Daryl Barron, expressed his delight at this move, emphasizing the importance of protecting our skin while enjoying the sunny weather. It's a simple yet effective step that encourages everyone to take care of themselves without worrying about the cost.
