Music has an incredible ability to alleviate stress, and recent research shows it goes beyond just lifting our spirits. It actively changes our brain's neural pathways and helps regulate our physiological responses to stress. Unlike nature sounds, music takes us on a structured emotional journey, building tension and eventually resolving it. This study highlights how music can effectively calm our heart rates and lower cortisol levels, making it a unique tool for stress relief.
