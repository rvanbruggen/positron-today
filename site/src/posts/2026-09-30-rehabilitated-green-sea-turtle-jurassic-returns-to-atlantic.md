So: Jurassic, a hefty green sea turtle weighing 215 pounds, has made a triumphant return to the Atlantic Ocean after months of care in Florida. Found stranded and struggling with abnormal buoyancy, he was treated successfully at the Loggerhead Marinelife Center. And here's the thing - he was released with a satellite tracker so researchers can follow his ocean adventures. Dozens of people cheered as he was carried back to the sea, marking a hopeful new chapter for this remarkable turtle.
