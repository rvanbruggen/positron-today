Octopuses are fascinating creatures with brains in their arms and skin that can see, making them seem almost alien. Recently, researchers discovered that California two-spot octopuses can use mirrors as tools, a behavior previously seen only in some vertebrates. This finding suggests that octopuses have developed this ability independently, a great example of convergent evolution. It’s incredible to think these invertebrates can interact with their reflections to solve problems, showing a surprising level of intelligence.
