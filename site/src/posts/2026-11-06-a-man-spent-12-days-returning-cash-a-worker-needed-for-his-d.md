So: when Pedro Moro found about $219 in a public plaza in southern Brazil, he set out to find its owner. The cash turned out to be the monthly salary of construction worker Matheus Reginaldo Celestino, who had withdrawn it to provide for his daughter. Moro shared appeals online, checked with local businesses for security footage and kept searching until Celestino saw a post and got in touch. After a quick check confirmed the money was his, Moro returned it, and Celestino was grateful to have it back.
