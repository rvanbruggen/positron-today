Scientists have engineered synthetic DNA combined with semiconductors to create a revolutionary memory device that uses significantly less power than traditional storage methods. This new technology, called a memristor, can store and process information simultaneously, mimicking how our brains work. By utilizing the incredible storage capacity of DNA, researchers have developed a system that could lead to more efficient and powerful memory devices. It's an exciting step forward, especially as the demand for advanced technology continues to grow.
