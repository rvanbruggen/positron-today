So here's something really encouraging - the Netherlands is taking a bold step by removing locks from dementia wards, treating residents with dignity rather than restriction. This shift reflects a growing understanding that people living with dementia deserve autonomy and respect, not confinement. The approach prioritizes freedom and personal agency even when cognitive abilities are changing, which is quite a powerful statement about how we should care for our most vulnerable. It's not about being reckless - it's about recognizing that freedom matters for everyone, and that includes people dealing with dementia.
