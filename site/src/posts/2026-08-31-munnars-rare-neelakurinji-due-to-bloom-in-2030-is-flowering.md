So, it turns out that Munnar's neelakurinji is surprising us by blooming four years early! This beautiful flower, known for its unique life cycle, is currently brightening the hills around Chokramudi and Mattupetty. While many were expecting the next big bloom in 2030, this different population is on its own 12-year cycle. Isn’t it fascinating how nature has its own schedule? It's a perfect time for visitors to explore these stunning landscapes!
