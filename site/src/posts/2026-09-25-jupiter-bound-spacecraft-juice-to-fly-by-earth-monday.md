So: the European Space Agency’s Juice spacecraft is making a quick flyby of Earth this Monday, zooming just under 5,400 miles above us. Now, this isn’t just a casual visit—it’s using Earth’s gravity to slingshot itself further into the outer solar system on its way to Jupiter. Juice will reach Jupiter in 2031 and then explore its icy moons with 35 flybys, searching for hidden oceans and studying magnetic fields. And here’s the thing: while passing Earth at an impressive speed of about 24,600 miles per hour, Juice will also snap some cool pictures and gather data about our planet and its magnetic field. It’s a clever pit stop that keeps the mission on track and makes the most of the journey!
