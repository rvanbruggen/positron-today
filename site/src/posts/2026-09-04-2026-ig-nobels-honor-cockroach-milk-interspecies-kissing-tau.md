The 2026 Ig Nobel Prizes have been awarded, celebrating the quirkiest and most amusing research out there! One standout winner studied cockroach milk, revealing it's over three times as energy-dense as regular dairy. Other fun awards went to scientists defining 'kissing' and a team that bravely provoked venomous snakes to understand their behavior. It's a reminder that even the silliest science can lead to meaningful insights, making us laugh and think at the same time.
