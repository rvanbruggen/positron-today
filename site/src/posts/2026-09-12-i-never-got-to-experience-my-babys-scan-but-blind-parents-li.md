It's amazing to see how technology is changing the experience for blind parents during pregnancy. With tactile 3D models of ultrasound scans, parents like Reece Finnegan-Knight can finally feel connected to their unborn babies in a way that wasn't possible before. This innovation not only enhances their experience but also empowers them to engage fully without relying on others' descriptions. It's a heartwarming reminder of how advancements can make a real difference in people's lives.
