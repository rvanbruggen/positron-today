So, scientists have made a fascinating discovery about Earth's core thanks to nuclear tests conducted 30 years ago in French Polynesia. They've found a vast anomaly in the liquid core, suggesting that changes within our planet can happen much faster than we previously thought. This is exciting because it challenges our understanding of geological processes. The research also indicates that this anomaly could be linked to the mixing of materials that drives Earth's magnetic field.
