So, it turns out that Venus's clouds, which look pale yellow to us, might actually be hiding something much darker! Researchers have been trying to figure out the mysterious 'unknown absorber' in these clouds for ages. By using advanced modeling and observations, they’ve estimated how this material interacts with light, revealing that it could be surprisingly effective at absorbing ultraviolet light. This discovery opens up exciting possibilities about the chemical makeup of Venus's atmosphere and what it might mean for our understanding of the planet.
