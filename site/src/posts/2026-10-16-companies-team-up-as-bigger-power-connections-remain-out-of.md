So, as grid capacity runs short, companies are finding creative ways to share the electricity connections they already have. Transdev, which needs to charge its growing fleet of electric buses, now uses spare capacity at a flower auction, a strawberry farm and other businesses when their electricity demand is lower. This teamwork is helping more companies get access to power, and the growth of the connection waiting list slowed in the first half of 2026. The trade-off is a more complex bus operation, with scattered charging sites sometimes leading to extra journeys without passengers.
