So, a researcher from the University of Otago has made an exciting discovery in New Zealand's lakes! They lured a fish that might be a new species using Marmite, and it's got a see-through head and huge eyes. This find opens up a whole new world of possibilities about the ecosystem down there, which is just fascinating. Dr. Roe Stuart is thrilled and even has a fun name in mind if it's confirmed as a new species!
