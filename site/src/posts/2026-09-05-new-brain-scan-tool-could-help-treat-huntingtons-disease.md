It helps researchers estimate cellular damage in living patients, which is crucial for tracking treatment effectiveness. The study showed clear differences in brain structure between those with Huntington's and healthy individuals, which is fascinating. This could lead to better understanding and new therapies for a condition that currently has no cure.
