So, here's a fantastic story about two friends, Aniket and Jay, who are changing the game for farmers! They launched IFE Store to help farmers sell directly to consumers, cutting out those pesky middlemen who took a huge 30% commission. This initiative not only boosts farmers' profits but also empowers them with better information on where to sell their organic products. It's a win-win situation that brings hope and sustainability to the farming community!
