A Harvard-led team has built a tiny photonic device that produces about 120 times more ultraviolet light than comparable earlier devices. Instead of guiding UV light through a chip, the device creates it from red light inside thin-film lithium niobate. The researchers’ sidewall poling technique tackles a longstanding manufacturing challenge and makes light conversion more effective. This could pave the way for smaller quantum computers, ultra-precise atomic clocks and other advanced technologies.
