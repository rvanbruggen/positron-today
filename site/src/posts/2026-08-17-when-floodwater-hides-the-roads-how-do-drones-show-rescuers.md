When floods hit, knowing where the water is and who needs help can be a challenge for rescuers. Drones have become a game changer, providing aerial views that help identify accessible routes and stranded individuals. Recently, in Gujarat, drones were deployed to deliver essential supplies and map the flooded areas. This innovative technology not only aids in immediate rescue efforts but also helps in planning for future floods by creating detailed terrain maps.
