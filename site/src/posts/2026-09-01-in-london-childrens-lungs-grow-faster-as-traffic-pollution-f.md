It's fantastic news that a recent study shows children's lungs in London are developing faster thanks to reduced traffic pollution! The research compared kids living in the ultra low-emissions zone with those in nearby Luton, revealing a significant improvement in lung capacity. As nitrogen dioxide levels dropped, London children saw an increase of 233 milliliters of air breathed out per year, bridging the gap with their suburban peers. This is a hopeful sign for future health, as fewer children are experiencing impaired lung function.
