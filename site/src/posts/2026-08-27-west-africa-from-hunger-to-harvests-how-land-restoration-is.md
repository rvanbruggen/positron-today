It's amazing to see how land restoration is turning the tide in the Sahel! Communities like Doungoulou in Chad are now capturing seasonal rains with dikes, which helps regenerate the land and boost food security. Since 2018, over 4 million people have benefited from these initiatives, showcasing the power of collaboration. With ongoing efforts, WFP aims to restore even more land, proving that we can tackle hunger and rebuild livelihoods together.
