So: for the very first time, olive ridley sea turtles have laid eggs on Southern California beaches, a surprising and exciting event! These turtles usually nest far south in places like Costa Rica, but a couple of brave females made their nests right here on the West Coast. When storms threatened the nests, wildlife officials acted fast to rescue the eggs and move them to a safe place for incubation. It’s a hopeful sign that these adaptable creatures might be shifting their habits as the climate changes, showing resilience in the face of new challenges.
