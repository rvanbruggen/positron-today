I find it fascinating that scientists have discovered a new genus of beetles called Luffy, inspired by the stretchy abilities of a beloved manga character! These beetles have long, slender features that remind researchers of Luffy's rubbery body. They've identified two species so far, each with unique traits and names that pay homage to the character's adventures. This discovery not only adds to our understanding of beetle evolution but also shows how science can connect with popular culture in such a fun way.
