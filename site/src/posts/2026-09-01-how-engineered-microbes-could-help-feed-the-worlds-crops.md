Fertilizer is essential for feeding the world, but its production is energy-intensive and emits a lot of greenhouse gases. This is where engineered microbes come into play, potentially reducing the need for chemical fertilizers by providing nitrogen directly to plants. A startup called Switch Bioworks is exploring a novel approach that allows these microbes to thrive before they switch to nitrogen production mode. Early trials show promise, with treated corn plants appearing healthier, which could be a game-changer for farmers facing rising costs.
