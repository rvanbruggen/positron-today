I’m really excited about this new discovery at Göbekli Tepe! Archaeologists have uncovered houses that date back 11,000 years, right beneath the surface. This area, which has been carefully excavated, reveals rectangular structures that hint at a more residential function. It’s fascinating to think that Göbekli Tepe was not just a ritual site but also a place where people lived and thrived.
