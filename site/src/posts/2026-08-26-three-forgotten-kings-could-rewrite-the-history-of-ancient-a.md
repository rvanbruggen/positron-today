So, it turns out that the Assyrian King List might not be the complete story we thought it was! Recent research has uncovered three rulers who were previously overlooked, suggesting a much more complex political landscape in ancient Assyria. One of these kings, intriguingly named Tiglath-pileser, even tried to usurp his nephew during a solar eclipse! This exciting discovery opens up new questions about how history was recorded and the motives behind it.
