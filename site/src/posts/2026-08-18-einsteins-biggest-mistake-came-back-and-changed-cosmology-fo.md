Isn't it fascinating how Einstein's so-called 'greatest blunder' turned out to be a game changer for cosmology? When he first applied his equations to the universe, he added a cosmological constant to keep things stable, but later discoveries showed the universe was actually expanding. Fast forward to the late 90s, and astronomers found that this expansion was accelerating, leading to the revival of Einstein's idea. It’s incredible how a concept thought to be discarded has reshaped our understanding of the cosmos!
