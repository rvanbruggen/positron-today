On the shores of Lake Albert, local fishers are sounding the alarm about pollution threatening their livelihoods. Plastic waste and agricultural runoff are endangering fish breeding grounds, which could jeopardize food security for future generations. Community leaders are urging everyone to take action and stop dumping waste into the lake. It's a call for collective responsibility to protect this vital resource that sustains so many lives.
