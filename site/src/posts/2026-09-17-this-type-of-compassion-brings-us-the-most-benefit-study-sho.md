So here's something I find really fascinating - we've always known that helping others makes us feel happier, and that includes caring for animals too. But a new study about compassion reveals something quite striking: one type of compassion matters more than we might expect. And it turns out that how we care about and treat ourselves has a much bigger impact on our mental health than we realized. Which brings me to this insight - self-compassion isn't selfish at all, it's actually foundational to our wellbeing.
