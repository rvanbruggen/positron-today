Imagine a microchip the size of a grain of rice that can create a stunning 'rainbow' of light! This innovation from Loughborough University could revolutionize 6G communications by generating precise light frequencies that convert into high-frequency signals. With millimeter waves offering more bandwidth, we can send and receive data faster and clearer than ever before. While there are still challenges to tackle, this breakthrough is a significant step toward a data-hungry future.
