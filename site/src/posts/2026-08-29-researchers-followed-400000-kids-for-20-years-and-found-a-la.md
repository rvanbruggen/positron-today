So, this fascinating study shows that kids who attended public school pre-K outperformed their peers in family-based childcare throughout high school. With a whopping 400,000 students tracked over 20 years, the research highlights the importance of having well-trained teachers in early education. It turns out that familiarity with classroom routines helps kids adjust better when they start elementary school. This could really change how we think about early childhood education!
