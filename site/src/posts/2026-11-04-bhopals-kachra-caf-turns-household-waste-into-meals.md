In Bhopal, Anjita Sabhlok’s Kachra Café lets residents exchange household waste, such as plastic bottles and old newspapers, for food and everyday essentials. Launched with her NGO Mannat and the Bhopal Municipal Corporation, the initiative gives waste a clear value and makes responsible sorting practical. In just eight months, the café sent more than 45 tonnes of waste for recycling. Now, this simple exchange is helping residents turn an everyday pile of rubbish into a meal while inspiring other cities to rethink waste.
