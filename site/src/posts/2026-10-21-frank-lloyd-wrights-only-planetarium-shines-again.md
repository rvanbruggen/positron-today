A unique planetarium designed by Frank Lloyd Wright at Florida Southern College has been brought back to life after a five-year restoration. Closed since 2004, the landmark is expected to welcome visitors again soon. The work renewed the exterior and roof, restored Wright’s original seating and Cherokee Red floor, and upgraded the projection equipment. It is part of a wider programme to preserve the architect’s remarkable campus, whose buildings were all designed by Wright.
