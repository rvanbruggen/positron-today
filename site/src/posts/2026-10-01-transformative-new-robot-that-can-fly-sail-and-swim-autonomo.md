I’m really excited about ALBATROSS, a robot inspired by nature but designed to do what no animal can. It can fly from the sky, slow its fall by spinning its wings, land on water, and then transform into a sailboat with a fish-like rudder to navigate autonomously. The clever part is how it uses the same parts for multiple functions, keeping it simple and efficient. This robot could revolutionize how we monitor oceans during emergencies, making data collection faster and safer.
