How amazing is this? In the Amazon, eight rope bridges have been installed, allowing wildlife to cross safely without the threat of roadkill. Over 15 months, these bridges facilitated 15,000 crossings, showing just how effective they can be for biodiversity. Biologist Fernanda Abra is leading this incredible initiative, which is now being recognized as a national standard in Brazil. It's a fantastic step forward in conservation and a win for both wildlife and local communities!
