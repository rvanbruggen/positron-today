Archaeologists have uncovered a remarkable prehistoric hammer made from elephant bone, dating back nearly 500,000 years. This fascinating find, the oldest known elephant bone tool in Europe, highlights the intelligence and craftsmanship of early human ancestors. Researchers believe it was likely used by Neanderthals or Homo heidelbergensis to maintain and sharpen stone tools. The discovery not only showcases their resourcefulness but also their understanding of the value of rare materials in tool-making.
