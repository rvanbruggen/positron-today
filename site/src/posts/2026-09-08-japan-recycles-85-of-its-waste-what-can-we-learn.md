Japan has turned waste segregation into a daily routine, with specific rules for burnable, non-burnable, and recyclable items. It's fascinating how even large items have their own collection system, and if you miss your day, you simply wait for the next one. Now, India is facing its own waste management challenges, but there's been significant progress, with urban waste processing jumping from 16% in 2014 to over 81% today. This shows that building better waste segregation habits at home could be the key to success.
