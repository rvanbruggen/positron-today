This summer, a mysterious island in Canada's Williston Lake made quite the splash by appearing, disappearing, and then reappearing over 30 kilometers away! Initially thought to be an illusion, satellite images confirmed its existence, covering an area as large as two football fields. The island likely broke off due to record-high water levels and strong winds, creating a fascinating ecological phenomenon. It's a reminder of nature's surprises and how floating islands can form in large reservoirs like this one.
