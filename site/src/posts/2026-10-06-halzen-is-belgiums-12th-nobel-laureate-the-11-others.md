Halzen has become Belgium’s 12th Nobel laureate, adding a new chapter to the country’s history of Nobel recognition. The milestone also offers a chance to look back at the 11 Belgians who received the honour before him. Together, their achievements reflect Belgium’s long-standing place in the world of Nobel Prize winners. It’s a joyful moment for Belgian science and a celebration of those who paved the way.
