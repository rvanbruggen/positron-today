So: a quick finger-prick blood test, combined with an ECG when available, could help GPs decide which patients with chest pain need urgent hospital care. In a study of 917 people, the approach correctly identified 98.1 percent of heart attacks, compared with around two-thirds using clinical judgement alone. It also helped rule out emergencies, sparing many patients an unnecessary referral. The tests complement, rather than replace, a doctor’s experience and overall assessment.
