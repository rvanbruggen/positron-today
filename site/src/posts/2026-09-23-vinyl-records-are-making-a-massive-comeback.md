I find it absolutely fascinating to see how vinyl records are making such a huge comeback, with sales in the US hitting over a billion dollars. It is not just a global trend either, because here in Poland, we are seeing a massive 46 percent jump in interest for these classic black discs. And here is the thing - even with prices climbing higher, people are just loving the tactile experience of owning music again. It really feels like we are moving into a more mature era for the music industry, where physical media is truly cherished. Which brings me to the best part: the production bottlenecks are finally clearing up, so more of us can get our hands on these gems.
