I’m so excited to share that Colorado has just opened the nation’s first nature library! It’s a fantastic place where you can borrow outdoor gear like bird-watching kits and snowshoes, plus enjoy a mud pit and a kinship garden. This library is all about connecting the community with nature, offering hands-on experiences for everyone. With over 550 items available, it’s designed to inspire a love for the outdoors in a fun and accessible way.
