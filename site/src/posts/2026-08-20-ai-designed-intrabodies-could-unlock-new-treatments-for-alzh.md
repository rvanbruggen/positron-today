Researchers at the University of Essex have made a groundbreaking discovery in the fight against neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's and Parkinson's. They've developed tiny antibody fragments, known as intrabodies, that can be produced inside human cells and target disease-related proteins. This innovative approach, powered by artificial intelligence, could revolutionize how we study and treat these conditions. The potential for new therapeutic strategies is exciting, especially since millions of existing antibodies could be repurposed for this purpose.
