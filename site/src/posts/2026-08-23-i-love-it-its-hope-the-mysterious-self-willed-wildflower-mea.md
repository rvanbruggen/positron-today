So, there's this amazing wildflower meadow in north Norfolk that just transformed itself without any human help! It started with just one plant 21 years ago, and now it boasts over 150 species, including beautiful orchids and hoverflies. Carl Sayer, who studied this incredible change, emphasizes that sometimes we just need to let nature do its thing instead of relying on commercial seeds. It’s a heartwarming reminder that nature knows best, and it’s inspiring to see how a little patience can lead to such vibrant biodiversity.
