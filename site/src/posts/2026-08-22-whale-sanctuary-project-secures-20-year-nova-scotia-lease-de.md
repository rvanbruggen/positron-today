What an exciting development for marine life! The Whale Sanctuary Project has just secured a 20-year lease for a stunning site in Nova Scotia, aiming to create a refuge for retired whales from marine parks. This sanctuary will be a game-changer, offering a natural ocean environment that's over 100 times larger than typical tanks. While there’s some local opposition, the project leaders are committed to engaging with the community and ensuring a peaceful coexistence.
