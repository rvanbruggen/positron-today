It's incredible how a group of homesick engineers from Silicon Valley turned their longing for home into a mission to rebuild schools in India. After witnessing the devastation of a school in Karnataka, they founded One School At A Time, which has now rebuilt over 150 schools! Their dedication to providing quality education for rural children is truly inspiring. Just imagine the joy of students returning to a safe and dignified classroom after such hardship!
