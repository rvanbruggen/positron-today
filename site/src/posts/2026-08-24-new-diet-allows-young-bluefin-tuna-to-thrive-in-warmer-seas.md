It's fascinating to see how young bluefin tuna are adapting to climate change with a new diet! Recent research shows that these fish larvae are now eating more diverse prey, which helps them grow faster and survive better. This flexibility is crucial as ocean temperatures rise, and it gives hope for the future of this endangered species. The shift in their diet from copepods to protein-rich mantle fish could be a game changer for their survival.
