So, Kenya’s first Ebola case was followed by a swift, coordinated response, and the country discharged its last patient just 37 days later. No community transmission has been recorded, and Kenya could be declared Ebola-free on 23 December if no new cases emerge. The response built on training and virtual networks established before the outbreak, helping health workers investigate alerts and trace contacts. The article argues that sustained investment in these networks can strengthen local expertise and help communities across Africa prepare for future health emergencies.
