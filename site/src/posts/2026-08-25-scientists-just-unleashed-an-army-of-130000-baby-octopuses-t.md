How exciting is this? Researchers have released 130,000 baby octopuses into the Adriatic Sea to tackle the invasive blue crab population that’s been causing havoc in the Mediterranean. These little paralarvae are just a few millimeters long now, but when they grow up, they could eat up to four blue crabs a day! This project, called Octo-Blu, aims to bolster the native octopus population and help restore balance in the ecosystem. It’s a fascinating blend of science and nature working together to combat a real problem!
