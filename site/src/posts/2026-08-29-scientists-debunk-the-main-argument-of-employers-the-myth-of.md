So, researchers have found that the negative effects of remote work are just a myth! Their study shows that remote workers report higher levels of mental well-being compared to their hybrid and in-office counterparts. Interestingly, remote employees also have a lower turnover rate, suggesting they feel more connected to their companies. This is such exciting news for the future of work!
