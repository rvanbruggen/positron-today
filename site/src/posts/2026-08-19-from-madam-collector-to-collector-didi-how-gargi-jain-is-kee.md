Gargi Jain, the district collector of Chhota Udepur, has transformed the way tribal girls experience education by visiting their classrooms and engaging with them personally. Instead of the formal title of 'Madam Collector', the girls affectionately call her 'Collector Didi', which reflects a deeper connection. Her initiative, 'Collector in Classroom', encourages open conversations about dreams, fears, and life skills, making school a more welcoming place. The results speak for themselves, with a significant drop in dropout rates among girls, showcasing the power of trust and mentorship.
