So, researchers have uncovered some fascinating insights about Gondwana, a supercontinent that was much larger than we previously thought. By analyzing over 25,000 volcanic rocks, they found that Gondwana might have covered around 80 percent of the Earth's land area 500 million years ago! This new understanding suggests that Gondwana played a significant role in the Cambrian explosion of life. It's exciting to think about what other hidden parts of our planet's history might still be waiting to be discovered.
