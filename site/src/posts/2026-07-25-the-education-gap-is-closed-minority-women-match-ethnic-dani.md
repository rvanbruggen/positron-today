It's fantastic to see that young people from non-Western backgrounds are completing their education like never before! This shift is a significant win for Denmark, as experts highlight the positive impact on integration. For years, the education gap symbolized the challenges in Danish society, but now, things are changing for the better. This progress shows that when opportunities are available, everyone can thrive together.
