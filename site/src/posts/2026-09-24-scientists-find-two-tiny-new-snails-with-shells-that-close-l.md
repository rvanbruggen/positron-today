I find it fascinating that even in Europe, new species can still be discovered! Two tiny snails, Cochlostoma komarnica and Cochlostoma dobrido, were found in Montenegro's rugged mountains. These snails have unique features like eyes on the sides of their heads and shells that close like doors. The discovery highlights the rich biodiversity of the Balkans, but there's a real concern as Komarnica Canyon, home to one of these snails, faces threats from a proposed hydropower dam.
