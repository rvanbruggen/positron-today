It's amazing how one person's vision can spark a community's action! Carl Wilson, a dedicated truck driver from Idaho, saw potential in a neglected bridge and dreamed of turning it into a wildlife crossing. After rallying support and passing the torch to civil engineer Kirsten Voorhees, the project finally came to life, ensuring safer passage for deer and elk. This initiative not only honors Wilson's legacy but also highlights the importance of community collaboration in protecting wildlife.
