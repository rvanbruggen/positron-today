I am absolutely thrilled to see how Michigan is tackling poverty head-on with their new Rx Kids program. It is such a brilliant idea to provide expectant mothers with direct cash, no strings attached, to help them focus on their health rather than just survival. So: they are essentially treating poverty like a medical condition that needs support, which is just genius. And here is the thing, it is already making a massive difference for thousands of families across the state. It really shows what we can achieve when we lead with trust and dignity.
