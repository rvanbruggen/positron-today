It's such exciting news that ocelots, those beautiful and elusive wild cats, are using wildlife crossings in Texas! After years of habitat loss, these crossings are a vital lifeline for their dwindling population. The first sighting of an ocelot using the underpass was a huge win for conservation efforts. With more funding now available, Texas is set to build even more crossings, which is fantastic for both the ocelots and the environment.
