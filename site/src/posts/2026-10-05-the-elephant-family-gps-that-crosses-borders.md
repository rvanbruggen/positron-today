I was completely fascinated reading about how elephant herds travel across the border between India and Nepal without any maps or technology. The oldest matriarch leads the way entirely by memory, guiding young calves through forests and across rushing rivers. And here's the thing - this is actual culture being passed down through generations, just like our grandmothers' unwritten recipes. Conservationists are working alongside local communities to keep these ancient forested corridors connected and safe. It really warms my heart to see such magnificent animals and humans finding thoughtful ways to share this living landscape together.
