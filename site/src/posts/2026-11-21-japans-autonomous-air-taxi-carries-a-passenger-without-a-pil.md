Now, here's a striking glimpse of what urban transport could look like: a pilotless electric air taxi has carried a passenger in Japan. On 20 November, the Chinese-built EH216-S took off vertically in Yamanashi Prefecture and flew a pre-programmed 380-metre route with one passenger on board. A later uncrewed flight covered 1,800 metres near the Kamanashi River, and the aircraft can reach speeds of up to 120 km/h and travel about 30 kilometres on one charge. The demonstration points to the promise of short trips above congested roads, while regulation, safety and infrastructure remain important steps before commercial flights can begin.
