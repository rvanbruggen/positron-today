So, here's the exciting news! New research from Emory University suggests that taking higher doses of vitamin D could lead to better cognitive performance, especially for adults at risk of dementia. Participants who took at least 5,000 IU of vitamin D daily scored over 13% higher on cognitive tests compared to those who didn't. This study highlights a crucial window for intervention in older adults experiencing mild cognitive impairment and sleep disturbances, showing that vitamin D could play a vital role in supporting brain health.
