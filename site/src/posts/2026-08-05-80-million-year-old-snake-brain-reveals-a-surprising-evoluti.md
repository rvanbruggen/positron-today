So, researchers have uncovered fascinating insights into snake evolution by studying an 80-million-year-old brain. It turns out that early snakes didn't just adapt to one environment but explored various habitats, leading to a rich diversity in their forms and functions. This study highlights how different lineages of snakes developed unique adaptations for burrowing, terrestrial, and marine lifestyles. It's amazing to think that the brain can tell us so much more than just the skeleton alone!
