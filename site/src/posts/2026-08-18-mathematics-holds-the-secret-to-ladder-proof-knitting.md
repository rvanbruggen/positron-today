So, it turns out that knitting isn't just about yarn and needles — there's some fascinating math behind it! Researchers have found that knot theory can help us understand which knitting patterns are less likely to unravel. This means we can design fabrics that resist those pesky defects that cause laddering. Isn't it amazing how mathematics can enhance our everyday crafts?
