A recent ranking published in 'Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems' compares 20 popular protein sources based on nutritional value, price, and environmental impact. Tofu emerged as the clear winner with 36 points, while animal products like cheddar and beef scored significantly lower. This ranking aims to help consumers make more informed choices about their protein sources. It's fascinating to see how plant-based options can be both healthier and more sustainable!
