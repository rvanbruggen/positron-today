So, at Kenya’s Reteti sanctuary, keeper Dorothy Lowakutuk helped save a newborn elephant named Nemparwai by patiently earning her trust. Reteti is Africa’s first Indigenous-owned elephant sanctuary, showing how wildlife protection can thrive when local communities lead. Lowakutuk’s journey also challenges expectations for Samburu women: she chose a career of her own and now helps other women build independence and a stronger voice. And here’s the thing - caring for elephants and supporting the people who share their landscape can go hand in hand.
