It's fascinating to think that beneath the flat Dutch landscape, there were once massive volcanoes! A geologist's unexpected discovery in the Wadden Sea revealed a dormant volcano hidden under layers of sediment. This article dives into the geological history of the region, explaining how tectonic shifts millions of years ago allowed magma to rise and form these ancient giants. It's a reminder that even in places we think are plain, there's a rich and dynamic history waiting to be uncovered.
