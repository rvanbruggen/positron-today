So here's what's interesting - as A.I. continues to develop at breakneck speed, a lot of the anxiety seems to center on the technology itself, but experts are pushing back on that perspective. They're saying we should really be focusing on the people making decisions about how A.I. gets built and deployed - because ultimately, that's where the real responsibility lies. And here's the thing: the humans in charge have far more agency in shaping outcomes than we often give them credit for. Which brings me to the broader point - understanding who controls these systems and holding them accountable might just be more productive than fearing the A.I. itself.
