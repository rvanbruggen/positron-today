Isn't it fascinating how two sisters turned their ancestral land into a cool, breezy home without relying on air conditioning? Renu and Rekha, inspired by their roots, worked with architect Amrutha Kishor to create Kadangotu House, which utilizes natural airflow and traditional materials. The design features high ceilings and perforated walls that promote cross-ventilation, making the indoor climate comfortable even in Kerala's humidity. After a 21-day stay, Renu was amazed to find she didn't need AC at all – just fans were enough!
