Ganesh Chaturthi is such an exciting festival for kids, and this year, it can also be a fantastic opportunity to teach them about sustainability! By involving children in making their own Ganpati idols and decorations from recycled materials, they learn that celebrations can be eco-friendly. It's all about giving them small responsibilities that show how their choices can protect nature. Plus, these activities turn into fun crafts that spark their creativity while caring for the environment.
