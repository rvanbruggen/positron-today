So, mosquitoes carrying virus-fighting bacteria may offer a powerful new way to protect children from dengue. Researchers found that releasing these mosquitoes was linked to a sharp drop in dengue cases among children. The bacteria make it harder for dengue viruses to thrive inside mosquitoes, helping curb the spread of infection. It’s an encouraging approach that could strengthen public health efforts in places where dengue is a serious threat.
