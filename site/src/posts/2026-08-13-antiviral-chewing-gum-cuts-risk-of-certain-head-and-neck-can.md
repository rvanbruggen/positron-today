So, researchers at the University of Pennsylvania have discovered that antiviral chewing gum can significantly reduce the levels of harmful microbes linked to oral cancer. This is exciting because head and neck cancers can be quite aggressive, and improving treatment options is crucial. The gum, made from lablab beans, showed a remarkable 93% reduction in HPV levels in saliva samples! This could pave the way for more effective and affordable therapies, which is fantastic news for those at risk.
