Isn't it fascinating that cosmic gold has actually made its way to Earth? Scientists have discovered that collisions between neutron stars can create enough gold to form dozens of Earth-sized planets! This groundbreaking insight came from observing the gravitational wave event GW170817 back in 2017. While it's unlikely that more gold will arrive on our planet, the process behind its creation is simply mind-blowing.
