Archaeologists in central Turkey have uncovered a fascinating collection of uniquely etched obsidian arrowheads that date back to the Neolithic era. Despite having catalogued 54 of these artifacts, the exact purpose of their intricate markings remains a mystery. Researchers believe that the markings might have been intentional, possibly serving as signatures for hunters, but the evidence is still inconclusive. The excitement around these finds continues to grow as teams like Alice Vinet's uncover more about the lives of these ancient artisans.
