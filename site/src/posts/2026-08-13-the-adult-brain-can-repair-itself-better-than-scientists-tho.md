I find it fascinating that the adult brain has more repair capabilities than we ever imagined! Researchers at the University of Zurich discovered that specialized astrocytes can actually send new cell nuclei to damaged areas, helping to rebuild the brain's support network. This challenges the old belief that once astrocytes are lost, they can't be replaced. The study opens up exciting possibilities for future treatments of brain injuries and autoimmune diseases.
