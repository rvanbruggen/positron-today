It's fascinating how some stars can survive close encounters with supermassive black holes, isn't it? These stars, after being partially torn apart, return for more interactions, creating bursts of light each time. Researchers have discovered that the spin of the star before its first encounter plays a crucial role in how it behaves during these events. This new understanding could help explain why some stars fade more than others after multiple passes, which is just mind-blowing!
