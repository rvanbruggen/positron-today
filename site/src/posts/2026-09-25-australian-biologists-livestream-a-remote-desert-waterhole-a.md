So: deep in Watarrka National Park, a remote desert waterhole is being livestreamed by Australian biologists, and it’s fascinating! The camera captures dingoes, wallabies, frogs, and even a wedge-tailed eagle stopping by for a drink, all without disturbing them. This project, dreamed up by ecologist Jennifer Davis, uses solar power and satellite internet to keep watch 24/7. And here's the thing - it helps scientists observe how climate change might affect this precious water source and its visitors over time.
