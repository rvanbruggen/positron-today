I find it fascinating how singing is slowly disappearing from schools, even though we need it now more than ever. Ulrika Lind from the Royal Academy of Music highlights how singing fosters community, inclusion, and participation among children and youth. So: a Nordic network for singing in schools has handed over a memorandum to the Nordic Council of Ministers to push for more singing in education. It’s such a positive step to bring back this joyful and unifying activity into classrooms!
