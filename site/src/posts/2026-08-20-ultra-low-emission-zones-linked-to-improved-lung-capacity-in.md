A recent study has shown that children’s lung capacity in London improved significantly after the introduction of ultra low emission zones. Researchers found that the lung function of over 3,000 children aged six to nine was restored to healthy levels after reducing exposure to pollution. This evidence supports the wider implementation of clean air zones as a vital public health measure. It’s exciting to see such positive results, especially since they suggest that cleaner air can lead to better lung growth in kids.
