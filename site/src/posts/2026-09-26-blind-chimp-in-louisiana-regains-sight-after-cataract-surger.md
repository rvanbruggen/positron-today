Pierre, a 54-year-old chimpanzee living at Chimp Haven in Louisiana, has regained his sight after a successful cataract surgery. The procedure took about an hour, but the preparation and recovery involved months of training to help Pierre accept eye care. His newfound vision has allowed him to move more confidently, enjoy activities, and reconnect with his chimpanzee friends sooner than expected. It’s heartwarming to see how this surgery has improved Pierre’s quality of life and social interactions.
