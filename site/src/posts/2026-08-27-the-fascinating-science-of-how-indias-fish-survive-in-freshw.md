Isn't it amazing how fish adapt to their environments? In India, species like the rohu thrive in freshwater, while the bangda navigates the salty seas. They have developed unique ways to manage their internal salt and water balance, which is crucial for survival. This balancing act, called osmoregulation, showcases the incredible diversity of fish habitats across the country.
