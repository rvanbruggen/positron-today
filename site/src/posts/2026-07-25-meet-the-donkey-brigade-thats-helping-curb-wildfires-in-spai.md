So, there's this amazing initiative in Spain where a herd of rescue donkeys is actually helping to prevent wildfires! The Tivissa Donkeys Firefighters charity not only rescues these animals but also uses them to graze on flammable undergrowth, which is a clever way to manage fire risks. It's fascinating how these donkeys, like Roberto and Eleanor, are playing a vital role in protecting the Catalan countryside. And here's the thing – this approach not only gives the donkeys a second chance at life but also contributes to a sustainable solution for wildfire prevention.
