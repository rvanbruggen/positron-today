So, it turns out that these little scraps of papyrus, affectionately dubbed 'cornflakes', are treasure troves of ancient knowledge! This summer, a group of researchers at the University of Michigan dove into the world of papyrus conservation, preserving over 150 fragments in just two weeks. It's fascinating how they combined their skills, with conservators and papyrologists working hand in hand to unlock the mysteries of these texts. The collaboration not only enhances preservation but also enriches the understanding of the ancient writings they handle.
