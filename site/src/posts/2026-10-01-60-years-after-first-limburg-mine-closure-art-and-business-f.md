So: 60 years ago, the Zwartberg coal mine closed as the first of Limburg’s seven mines, marking the end of an era for thousands of miners. Now, the site looks almost unrecognizable, transformed into a vibrant hub of art, nature, and business with Labiomista as the shining star attracting 35,000 visitors annually. The transition from heavy industry to culture and commerce is a powerful story of resilience and renewal. And here's the thing - this place honors its rich mining heritage while embracing a bright, creative future.
