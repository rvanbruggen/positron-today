It's fascinating to see how women's participation in the workforce can transform entire societies. In many regions, outdated beliefs about female roles still hold sway, preventing women from pursuing jobs even when opportunities are available. The World Bank estimates that breaking down these barriers could significantly boost national incomes. Plus, when women earn, they gain more power at home, leading to fairer family dynamics.
