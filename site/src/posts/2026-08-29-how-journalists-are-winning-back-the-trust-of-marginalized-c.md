Trust in journalism has taken a hit, especially among marginalized communities like Black people, who have often been misrepresented. But here's the exciting part: some journalists are finding ways to rebuild that trust. By actively engaging with these communities and practicing principles of care and respect, they are making strides. It's a two-way street, where both journalists and community members play a role in fostering understanding and connection.
