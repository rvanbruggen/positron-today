So, researchers at Kindred Companion Sciences have made a fantastic breakthrough by using CRISPR gene editing to create hypoallergenic dogs! They successfully modified two beagles, Bailey and Alfie, to eliminate a major protein responsible for allergic reactions in humans. This means allergy sufferers can finally enjoy the company of dogs without the sneezing and itching. However, experts note that further research is needed before this method can be widely applied.
