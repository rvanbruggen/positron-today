The Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory has been a game-changer in astrophysics for nearly 22 years, detecting gamma-ray bursts that signal the birth of black holes. Its innovative design allowed it to quickly locate these fleeting explosions, revolutionizing our understanding of the universe. Swift detected its first burst just weeks after launch, and since then, it has provided crucial data that has deepened our knowledge of these powerful cosmic events. As it approaches its end, we celebrate the incredible legacy of Swift and its contributions to science.
