Scientists in Alaska are exploring how an entire Arctic ecosystem might adapt to the climate crisis. They're studying the interactions between species like white-crowned sparrows and Arctic grayling, hoping to understand how these relationships can help them survive. The project aims to show that evolution isn't just about individual species but also about how they can thrive together. It's a fascinating approach that could change our understanding of ecosystems worldwide.
