At the Dutch Defence clothing centre in Soesterberg, new recruits collect their kit while former personnel return gear they no longer need. Defence receives about 5,500 pallet crates of used uniforms each year, and newer items in good condition can be issued again. Older combat clothing now goes through a growing recycling network, where its fibres are turned into wall panels, insulation and even socks. Some running shoes are also broken down and made into new pairs for recruits - a practical second life that makes military supplies more circular.
