I just love this story about a long-lost painting by Clara Peeters that was rediscovered in Norway! Cynthia Osiecki stumbled upon this still life while researching Peeters, who was a trailblazer in the genre. The painting, featuring dead birds and grapes, had been misattributed for years, but Osiecki's keen eye and research led to its reattribution. It's set to be a highlight in an upcoming exhibition celebrating female artists, showcasing how art history is finally giving credit where it's due.
