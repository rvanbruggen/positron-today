So, have you ever thought about how cities can actually absorb rainwater instead of just draining it away? The ‘sponge city’ concept is all about creating spaces that allow rain to seep into the ground, which can really help with flooding issues. By using permeable pavements, rain gardens, and restoring wetlands, cities can manage water more effectively. This innovative approach not only prevents flooding but also helps recharge groundwater levels, making urban areas more resilient.
