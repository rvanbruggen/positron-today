So, India’s 454.61 million tonnes of sugarcane in 2024-25 offer much more than sweetness. The crop gives us sugar, jaggery and refreshing juice, while its leaves can feed cattle. Even the fibrous bagasse left after crushing the stalks can be turned into compostable tableware, paper and electricity. It’s a great example of how making use of every part of a familiar plant can benefit industries and the environment.
