Researchers at Georgia State University have developed an exciting oral antiviral that can prevent the spread of a measles-like virus in ferrets. This innovative treatment not only blocks airborne transmission but also reduces symptoms when given before or after exposure. The findings highlight the potential of GHP-88310, a drug that could enhance traditional vaccination strategies against measles. With measles outbreaks on the rise in North America, this breakthrough could be a game-changer in controlling the virus.
