So: a robotic submarine has found evidence of explosive volcanic eruptions about 20,000 feet beneath the surface in the Mariana Trench. The rocks are the deepest pyroclastic deposits ever documented in the ocean, and their discovery surprised the researchers. Using the veteran Jason/Medea robot, the team explored a stretch of the trench near Challenger Deep over seven dives. I find it fascinating that these ancient rocks may even help explain why Challenger Deep is so extraordinarily deep.
