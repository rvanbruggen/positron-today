Heart Aerospace's X1 has officially completed its maiden flight, marking a significant milestone in the world of electric aviation. This impressive aircraft, with a wingspan of 106 feet, flew for 27 minutes on battery power alone, showcasing the potential of battery-electric technology. While we might not be booking flights on electric planes just yet, the future looks promising with the upcoming hybrid-electric ES-30 model. Major airlines are already interested, drawn by the prospect of lower costs and a greener alternative to traditional jet fuel.
