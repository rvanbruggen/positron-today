So, there’s encouraging news for green turtles: they have been reclassified from endangered to least concern in global conservation rankings. The change reflects decades of protection efforts. In Malaysia’s Turtle Islands, nesting numbers have tripled since the 1970s. And here’s the thing: hunting, fishing nets and pollution remain threats, so continued protection matters.
