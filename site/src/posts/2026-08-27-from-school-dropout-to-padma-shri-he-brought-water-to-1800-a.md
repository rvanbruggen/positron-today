At just 17, Laxman Singh took a bold step to revive a dried-up pond in Laporiya, Rajasthan, during a severe drought. His innovative Chauka system has since transformed the village's water landscape, benefiting local farmers and their families. With the community rallying around him, they restored the bund, which now irrigates 1,800 acres of farmland. This inspiring journey not only earned him the Padma Shri but also revitalized the spirit of the village.
