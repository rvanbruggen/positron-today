So: I was reading about how universities across Africa are teaming up to tackle the future of work. They are focusing heavily on artificial intelligence, which is just so crucial for everyone today. And here's the thing - they are not just talking about theory, but actually pushing for stronger links with industry to solve real-world problems. It makes me feel quite optimistic about how these graduates will be ready to drive change.
