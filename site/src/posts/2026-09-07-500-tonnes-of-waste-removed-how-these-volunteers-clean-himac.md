Every year, the beauty of Himachal Pradesh's high-altitude trails is overshadowed by the waste left behind by pilgrims and trekkers. The Himalayn Tribe Foundation, founded by RP Negi Yullam, is tackling this issue head-on by involving the very people who travel these sacred routes in cleanup efforts. Their grassroots approach not only removes waste but also fosters a personal connection to conservation among participants. It's inspiring to see how awareness and responsibility can grow when travelers take an active role in preserving the environment.
