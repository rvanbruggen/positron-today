So, Terence Tao’s new book introduces six big ideas that help us make sense of the world: numbers, algebra, geometry, probability, analysis and dynamics. With clear, equation-light explanations, he shows how these concepts connect abstract maths to everything from uncertainty and infinity to the changing patterns in Conway’s Game of Life. Along the way, stories of pioneering mathematicians reveal how mathematical thinking has shaped human history and solved practical problems. Tao also reflects on AI, celebrating its remarkable power while reminding us to think carefully about the trust and fears it can inspire.
