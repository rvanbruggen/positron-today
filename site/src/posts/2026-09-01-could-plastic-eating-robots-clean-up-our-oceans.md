So, there's this exciting initiative where autonomous plastic-eating robots are being deployed in cities around the world to tackle ocean pollution. The Ocean Cleanup, a Dutch nonprofit, aims to remove 90 percent of floating plastic by 2040, and they're making great strides with innovative technology. These robots are not just sci-fi fantasies; they're actively cleaning our waterways, monitored by drones and GPS. It's a huge step forward in addressing one of our planet's most pressing environmental issues!
