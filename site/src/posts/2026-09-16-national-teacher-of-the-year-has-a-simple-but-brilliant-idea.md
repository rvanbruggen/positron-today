Leon Smith, the 2026 National Teacher of the Year, has spent 25 years at the same Pennsylvania high school and noticed something alarming - applications to education programs dropped 70% over a decade. His brilliant solution? Simply ask young people if they've ever thought about teaching, a question most teenagers rarely hear. He's also working to bring more diversity into the profession and giving students real experience mentoring younger kids, because most young people only hear negative things about teaching in the media. Smith believes that when young people, especially students of color, see themselves reflected in educators, it opens up a whole new world of possibility.
