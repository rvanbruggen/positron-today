Isn't it amazing? Researchers have discovered that sunlight can create pairs of entangled photons, which are crucial for photonic quantum computing. This breakthrough, led by teams from the University of Ottawa and the Max Planck Institute, shows that we can generate quantum entanglement without the need for expensive lasers. By focusing sunlight onto a special crystal, they achieved a remarkable 94 percent fidelity in entanglement. This could make quantum technology much more accessible, allowing it to be used almost anywhere under the sun!
