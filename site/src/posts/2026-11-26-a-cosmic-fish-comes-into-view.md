So: about 6,800 light-years away, a stellar explosion left behind a ghostly cloud now known as the Betta Fish Nebula. A powerful Dark Energy Camera in Chile has captured its glowing swirls of gas, dust, and stellar debris in remarkable detail. The supernova happened roughly 7,000 to 10,000 years ago, and a tiny, ultra-dense neutron star remains at its heart. And here's the thing: that stellar remnant might have become a black hole instead, which could have swallowed the nebula’s luminous clouds.
