On August 8, the Coast Guard in Florida rescued a pregnant loggerhead sea turtle named Roxanne, who was tangled in fishing line. They took her to The Turtle Hospital, the first facility dedicated to sea turtle rehabilitation. Fortunately, her flipper appeared salvageable, and she was carrying 100 eggs! Just a day later, Roxanne was deemed healthy enough to be released back into the ocean, reminding us all of the importance of protecting our marine friends.
