After encountering internships that required a bachelor’s degree or charged fees, 21-year-old Punjab student Nikhil Kumar wanted to make practical training easier to manage. With support from his faculty mentor and Vibha’s Programme Management Unit, he developed Place Intern from an existing college project. The portal brings student progress reports, faculty visits and internship records together in one place. It is designed to help colleges track students during semester-long industry placements.
