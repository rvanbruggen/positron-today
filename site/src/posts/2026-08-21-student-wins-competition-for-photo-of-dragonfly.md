A student has just won a scientific photography competition with a stunning image of a dragonfly, which the jury says highlights the diversity of wetlands, one of the most threatened habitats in the world. The award-winning photo features an orange-tailed dragonfly taken by Indian student Sritam Kumar Sethy, who aptly named it 'Window to Wetlands'. It's fantastic to see young talents bringing attention to such important environmental issues through their art. This recognition not only celebrates the beauty of nature but also raises awareness about the need to protect these vital ecosystems.
