So, a partial solar eclipse is coming up for some lucky Canadians, and it’s followed by the stunning Perseid meteor shower! The eclipse will be best seen in Newfoundland and Labrador, where they’ll get the clearest view. Just remember, proper eye protection is a must when gazing at the sun, even if it’s just partially covered. And if you’re patient, the meteor shower promises to be a fantastic spectacle, especially if you find a dark spot away from city lights.
