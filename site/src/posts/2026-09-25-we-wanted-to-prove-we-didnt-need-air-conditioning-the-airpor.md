At Roland Garros airport on Réunion island, the arrivals terminal is cooled entirely by natural ventilation, defying tropical heat without air conditioning. This innovative design uses a unique roof structure and large ceiling fans to create a refreshing breeze that lowers the perceived temperature by several degrees. The project began in 2016 with the goal of reducing carbon emissions and has since saved the airport €150,000 annually in energy costs. It’s a brilliant example of blending traditional local knowledge with modern architecture to create sustainable comfort.
