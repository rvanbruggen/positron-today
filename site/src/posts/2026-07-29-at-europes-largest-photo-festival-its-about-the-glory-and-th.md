So, the 57th edition of Les Rencontres de la Photographie in Arles is showcasing the concept of glory through stunning photography. Artists like Anne-Lise Broyer draw connections between the past and present, highlighting how images can transform our understanding of identity. The festival features over fifty exhibitions, with a significant focus on African photographers and the rich history of independence movements. It’s a vibrant celebration of how photography captures the essence of cultural narratives, making it a must-see event this summer.
