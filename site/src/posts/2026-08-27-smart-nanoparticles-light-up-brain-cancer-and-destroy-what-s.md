Researchers have developed a groundbreaking nanoparticle platform that helps surgeons visualize and treat glioblastoma more effectively. This innovative system not only illuminates tiny tumor clusters during surgery but also targets residual cancer cells post-operation. By using the same near-infrared light for both imaging and therapy, it enhances the chances of reducing tumor recurrence. While results in mouse models are promising, further research is needed to confirm its effectiveness in humans.
