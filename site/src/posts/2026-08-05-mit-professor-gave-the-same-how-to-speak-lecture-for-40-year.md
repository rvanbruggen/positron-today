Patrick Winston, a beloved MIT professor, captivated audiences for over 40 years with his annual lecture on public speaking. He emphasized the importance of a strong opening, repetition, and using props to engage listeners. Winston also shared insights on inspiring people and the value of humor in concluding a talk. His wisdom continues to resonate, reminding us that effective communication is key to success.
