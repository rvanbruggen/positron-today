So, scientists have made an exciting discovery in Germany's Hohle Fels cave – two tiny bird figurines carved from mammoth ivory that are around 40,000 years old! These delicate pieces, found just a few feet apart, showcase the incredible artistry of our ancient ancestors. One figurine might represent a brooding bird, while the other could be in flight or landing. It's fascinating to think about how these carvings connect us to the natural world and the birds that filled the skies back then.
