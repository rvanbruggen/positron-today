Along the Odet River in Brittany, volunteers are stepping up to save fish as rivers dry up due to extreme heat. Fisher Mathieu Le Bouter leads a team that catches trout and young salmon, transferring them to oxygenated tanks before releasing them downstream. The situation is dire, with many rivers at historical lows and water levels dropping alarmingly. This urgent effort highlights the impact of climate change on aquatic life and the need for immediate action.
