Northshore Apparel is making waves in Savelugu by creating formal jobs and connecting local farmers to the garment industry. Kausara Alhassan, a recent graduate, finally found work after years of searching, and she's thrilled about the skills and income she's gaining. With over 2,300 employees, mostly women, Northshore is set to expand significantly, aiming for 10,000 jobs in the future. This initiative could transform the job landscape in northern Ghana, keeping young people in their communities and boosting the local economy.
