The Otago Peninsula is becoming a paradise for birdwatchers after being declared possum-free. This achievement is the result of over 15 years of hard work by the community, and the positive changes are already visible. Dunedin's mayor, Sophie Barker, recalls the pesky possums from her childhood, but now she's thrilled to see more birds returning, including the beautiful tui. It's a fantastic example of how collaboration can restore biodiversity and bring joy to the local environment.
