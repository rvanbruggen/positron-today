So: three products made by Keurslagerij Van Hoof in Mol have received the official Flemish regional product label: Kempens smout, Kempense black pudding and beef fat. The family has been making them for three generations, meeting the label’s requirement of at least 25 years of tradition. The recognition celebrates local food heritage, from smout once used like butter to the black pudding’s finer texture. Owner Guido Van Hoof hopes it will introduce more people to these authentic products and help keep the butcher’s craft alive.
