So, here's the exciting news! A study found that a combination of omega-3 and low-dose aspirin works almost as well as antibiotics for treating severe gum disease. Conducted by researchers from Brazil and Harvard, this could be a game-changer in reducing antibiotic use, which is super important given the rise of bacterial resistance. After a year, nearly 58% of patients on the supplement regimen met the clinical goals, just shy of those on antibiotics. This opens up new possibilities for treating periodontitis more safely and effectively!
