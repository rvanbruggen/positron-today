Isn't it fascinating how coffee, a daily ritual for millions, might be linked to better health? New research from Finland shows that regular coffee drinkers tend to have less body fat and more muscle, along with interesting hormone differences between men and women. The study suggests that coffee consumption could influence metabolic health through hormonal pathways, although it doesn't prove a direct cause. This opens up exciting possibilities for future research on how coffee affects our bodies!
