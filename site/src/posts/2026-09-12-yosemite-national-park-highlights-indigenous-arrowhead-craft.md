It's fascinating to see how Indigenous arrowhead crafting has stood the test of time, remaining largely unchanged for thousands of years. The process, known as knapping, involves selecting the right stones like obsidian, which has a rich history of trade among tribes. These artisans skillfully shape the stones into various tools, adapting their designs over time for better efficiency. Yosemite National Park even celebrates this ancient tradition with its annual Knap-In event, showcasing the artistry and cultural significance behind these remarkable tools.
