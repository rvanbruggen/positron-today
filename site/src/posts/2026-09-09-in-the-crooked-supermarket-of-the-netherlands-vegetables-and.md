Isn't it fascinating how crooked cucumbers can find a home? In the Kromste Supermarkt, a pop-up initiative, they rescue perfectly good produce that doesn't meet supermarket standards. This week, they aim to save 100,000 kilos of food that would otherwise go to waste. Customers are excited about the surprise boxes filled with these 'wonky' fruits and veggies, and it’s a great reminder of how we can all reduce food waste together.
