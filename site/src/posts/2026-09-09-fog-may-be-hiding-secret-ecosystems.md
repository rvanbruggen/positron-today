Fog is not just a mystical phenomenon; it might actually be a thriving habitat for microbes! Scientists are discovering that fog can support microbial life, with bacteria found to be metabolizing compounds like formaldehyde. This research opens up a whole new field of fog microbiology, showing that these tiny droplets create unique microhabitats. It's fascinating to think about how something as common as fog can play such a crucial role in our ecosystem.
