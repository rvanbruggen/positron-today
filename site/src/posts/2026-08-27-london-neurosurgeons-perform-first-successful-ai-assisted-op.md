How exciting is this? Neurosurgeons in London have just performed the world’s first successful AI-assisted operation to remove a brain tumour, saving a man's sight! The surgery, which took place at the National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery, used AI to help identify critical structures in real time. The patient, Rhys Hibbert, was able to see clearly right after waking up and even returned to work within a week. This groundbreaking procedure showcases the incredible potential of AI in enhancing surgical outcomes.
