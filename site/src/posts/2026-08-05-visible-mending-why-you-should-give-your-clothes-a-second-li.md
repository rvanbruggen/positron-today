So, have you ever thought about giving your clothes a second life? Instead of tossing them out, visible mending is a fantastic way to show off those repairs and reduce textile waste. It’s all about embracing the beauty of imperfections, just like the Japanese art of kintsugi. Plus, with the rise of social media, more people are proudly sharing their mended pieces, making it a trendy movement that’s here to stay!
