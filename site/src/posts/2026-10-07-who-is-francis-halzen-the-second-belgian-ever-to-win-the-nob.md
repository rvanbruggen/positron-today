Francis Halzen has become only the second Belgian ever to receive the Nobel Prize in Physics, a remarkable milestone for Belgian science. The Belgian-born physicist is known for his work on neutrinos, elusive particles that can reveal distant cosmic events. He helped lead the creation of IceCube, a vast neutrino detector embedded in the Antarctic ice. His story shows how curiosity about the universe can lead to discoveries on an extraordinary scale.
