So, the future of exoskeletons is looking bright! Daniel Ferris, a biomechanical engineer, believes these devices will soon be part of our daily lives, helping everyone from athletes to the elderly. Researchers are shifting their focus from just rehabilitation to enhancing movement, which is super exciting. However, there are still challenges to tackle, like making them smarter and more accessible for everyone. It's a fascinating journey, and I can't wait to see how it unfolds!
