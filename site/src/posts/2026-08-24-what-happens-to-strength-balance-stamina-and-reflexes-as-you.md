Aging brings some surprising changes to our bodies, especially when it comes to strength and stamina. Research shows that even if you maintain muscle mass, you can still lose strength, particularly in your legs. It's fascinating to learn that while your maximum heart rate declines with age, the level you start from can make a huge difference in your physical abilities later on. The good news is that resistance training can help improve strength and function, even for those in their 70s and beyond.
