So, scientific publishing is really at a crossroads right now. With more submissions and complex manuscripts, getting timely reviews is becoming quite the challenge. Interestingly, while AI tools could help reviewers, many journals still ban their use. This blanket approach might not be the best solution, as it raises concerns that are tough to monitor.
