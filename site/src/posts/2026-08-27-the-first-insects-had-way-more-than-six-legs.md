So, it turns out that the first insects were way more diverse than we thought! A team of paleontologists discovered that some ancient critters had way more than six legs, suggesting they were missing links between modern insects and their ancestors. This exciting find, based on fossils from places like Texas and Scotland, changes our understanding of insect evolution. It’s fascinating to think about how these creatures adapted to life on land while still holding onto some aquatic traits.
