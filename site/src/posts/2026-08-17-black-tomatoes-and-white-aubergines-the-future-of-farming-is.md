So, there's this amazing farm in Italy where twin brothers are shaking up traditional farming! They’re using biomimicry and agroecological tech to grow resilient crops like black tomatoes and white aubergines, which you won’t find in regular supermarkets. It’s all about working with nature instead of against it, especially after they thrived during a severe drought. Their approach is not just sustainable but also community-focused, proving that farming can be both innovative and delicious!
