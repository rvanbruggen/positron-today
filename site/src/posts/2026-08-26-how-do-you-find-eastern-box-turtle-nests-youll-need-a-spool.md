So, a team of researchers in Virginia has been on a fascinating quest to track Eastern box turtle nests using a method that feels like a scene from a Greek myth! They’ve been using GPS and telemetry to follow these turtles and even employing portable X-ray machines to check if they’re carrying eggs. Once they confirm a turtle is gravid, they attach a spool of thread to her shell, leading them back to her nesting site. It’s a clever and engaging way to gather data that could help protect these amazing creatures!
