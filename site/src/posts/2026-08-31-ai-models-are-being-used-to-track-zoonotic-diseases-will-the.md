In Uganda's Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, a fascinating initiative is underway to protect endangered mountain gorillas from zoonotic diseases. Thanks to a collaboration with AI, researchers are shifting from reactive measures to proactive disease prevention. By analyzing data from livestock and gorilla monitoring, they aim to predict and prevent potential outbreaks. This innovative approach could be a game-changer in managing zoonotic diseases and safeguarding both wildlife and human health.
