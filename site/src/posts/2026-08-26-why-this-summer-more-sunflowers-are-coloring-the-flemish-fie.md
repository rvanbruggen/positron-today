So, it turns out that this summer, we're seeing a delightful increase in sunflowers across the Flemish countryside! Farmers are embracing these vibrant flowers not just for their beauty but also for their benefits to the environment. Sunflowers are fantastic for attracting pollinators and improving soil health, which is a win-win situation. It's heartwarming to see nature and agriculture working hand in hand, creating a stunning landscape that brings joy to everyone.
