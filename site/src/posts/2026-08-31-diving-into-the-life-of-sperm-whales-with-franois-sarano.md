François Sarano, a passionate oceanographer, has dedicated his life to understanding sperm whales, those gentle giants of the sea. He believes that to truly know them, one must dive into their world, which he does regularly. His recent observations reveal surprising hunting behaviors among male sperm whales, challenging previous assumptions about their solitary nature. With each dive, he uncovers more about these magnificent creatures and advocates for their protection.
