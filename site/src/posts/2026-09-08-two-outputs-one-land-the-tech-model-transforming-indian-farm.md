I find it fascinating how Project Chittoor is revolutionizing farming by generating clean energy alongside crops. Each block produces around 300 kilowatts, which adds up to a whopping 6 megawatts across 600 acres! This innovative approach not only supports India's clean energy goals but also ensures that farming continues to thrive. It's a brilliant example of how technology can harmonize with agriculture to feed a growing population.
