So, after more than 40 years of searching, physicists have found evidence of a particle decay that happens only about once in every 10 billion beauty particles. The upgraded LHCb experiment at CERN measured the signal at 3.2 sigma, enough to count as evidence but not yet as a confirmed discovery. This exceptionally rare decay gives scientists a new way to test the Standard Model and look for clues to unknown particles or forces. And here's the thing: more data could help turn this milestone into an even deeper glimpse of how nature works.
