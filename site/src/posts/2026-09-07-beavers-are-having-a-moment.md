So, beavers are finally getting the recognition they deserve! Once seen as pests, these furry little engineers are now being celebrated for their vital role in restoring ecosystems, especially in Oregon. With salmon populations dwindling, researchers discovered that beaver ponds significantly boost salmon productivity. It’s amazing how nature can help heal itself when we give it a chance, and beavers are leading the charge!
