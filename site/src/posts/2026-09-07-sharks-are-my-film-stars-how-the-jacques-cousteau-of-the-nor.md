The North Sea might look grey and dull, but Peter van Rodijnen's dedication to diving has uncovered its vibrant marine life. Inspired by Jacques Cousteau, he and director Mark Verkerk have created a documentary that showcases the hidden wonders of this often-misunderstood ecosystem. Despite challenges like poor visibility and rough seas, their film reveals a world teeming with colorful creatures and complex interactions. It's a celebration of the North Sea's beauty and the importance of sustainable practices in preserving it.
