Getting vaccinated against shingles is not just about avoiding that painful rash—new research suggests it might also lower your heart disease risk! The study found that those who received the Shingrix vaccine had fewer heart problems compared to those who got an older vaccine. While the exact reasons are still a bit of a mystery, it seems Shingrix could boost your immune system in a way that protects your heart. This could mean significant health benefits for many people, especially as we age.
