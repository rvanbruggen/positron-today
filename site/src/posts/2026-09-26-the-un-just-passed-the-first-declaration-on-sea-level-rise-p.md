So: the United Nations just made a huge step by passing the first-ever declaration on sea level rise, and it’s a big win for island nations. These countries, like the Bahamas and Tuvalu, face losing their land to the ocean, but now they’ll keep their sovereignty and control over maritime zones even if their islands disappear. And here's the thing - the US didn’t block this resolution, which is rare these days! This agreement could really boost global efforts to support vulnerable island states and protect their rights in a warming world.
