Imagine a hospital that travels right to your doorstep! That's exactly what Rudra, the 'hospital on wheels', is doing for railway employees in Maharashtra. Launched in January 2025, this innovative mobile unit has already helped over 4,300 people by bringing essential healthcare closer to home. With its practical setup and focus on early detection, Rudra is changing how families approach their health, making it easier to seek care without the long travel times.
