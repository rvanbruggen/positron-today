Social prescription is gaining traction as a way to enhance patient health and well-being. It focuses on connecting individuals with community resources, which can lead to a significant reduction in feelings of loneliness and pain. Patients report feeling better when they engage with others and participate in social activities. This approach highlights the importance of social connections in the healing process.
