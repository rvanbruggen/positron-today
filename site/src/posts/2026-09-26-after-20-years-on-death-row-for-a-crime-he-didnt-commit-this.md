Charles Fain spent nearly 20 years on death row in Idaho for a crime he didn’t commit, until DNA evidence set him free in 2001. Since then, he’s been a passionate advocate for others wrongfully convicted, joining forces with new organizations like The Innocence Center to continue this vital work. The Idaho Innocence Project may have closed, but the fight for justice is far from over, with dedicated experts and exonerees stepping up. It’s inspiring to see how one man’s freedom has sparked hope and action for many more.
