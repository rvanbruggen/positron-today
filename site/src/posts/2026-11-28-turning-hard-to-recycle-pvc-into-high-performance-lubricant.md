So: discarded PVC from pipes, window frames and credit cards could become a valuable ingredient in synthetic engine oil. Researchers at Virginia Tech developed a chemical process that converts PVC into polyalphaolefin, a high-performance lubricant material. The approach could help tackle two challenges at once: stubborn plastic waste and the environmental costs of making industrial lubricants. And here's the thing - turning a difficult-to-recycle plastic into something useful for engines is a promising example of upcycling.
