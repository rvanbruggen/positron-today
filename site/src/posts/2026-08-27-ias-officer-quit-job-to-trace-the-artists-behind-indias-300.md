Isn't it fascinating how art can connect us to our history? B. N. Goswamy, a former IAS officer, dedicated his life to uncovering the stories of the artists behind India's stunning miniature paintings. His research revealed how families moved between royal courts, shaping artistic styles over centuries. By following clues and even traveling to historical sites, he brought forgotten names back to life, enriching our understanding of Indian art.
