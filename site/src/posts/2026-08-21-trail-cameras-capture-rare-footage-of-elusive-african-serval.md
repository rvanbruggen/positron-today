So, researchers in Tanzania have made an exciting discovery with trail cameras capturing footage of serval kittens! These unique cats, often described as looking like 'stretched out cheetahs', are known for their incredible hunting skills. What's even more thrilling is that they recorded a rare color morph of the serval for the first time in the country. This study not only sheds light on these elusive creatures but also highlights the importance of conservation efforts in the region.
