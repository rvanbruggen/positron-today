Jay Van Bavel’s study of 11 climate messages tested on 59,440 people in 63 countries found that the most frightening message was shared most, but was least effective at prompting action. A calm message stating that human activity is warming Earth and that 99% of climate scientists agree performed better on every outcome except sharing. Van Bavel also found that each extra negative word in 22,743 Upworthy headline tests brought 2.3% more clicks, helping explain why feeds amplify alarm while hope and action can fade. Rik argues that Positron’s positive stories do not replace serious news, but offer a steady reason to believe progress is possible and keep people able to act.
