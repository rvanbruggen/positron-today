So: China is opening the door for students in professional clinical medicine master’s and PhD programmes to graduate with a practical achievement instead of a thesis. These projects could tackle real-world medical challenges, such as analysing a rare clinical case, and follow a similar pathway already available to engineering students. One early graduate developed AI software to help doctors diagnose a rare spinal condition and choose treatments. The next big task is creating clear, profession-specific standards to make sure these achievements are assessed fairly and rigorously.
