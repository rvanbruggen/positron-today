Here’s a surprising finding: remote work brought colleagues closer, rather than pulling them apart. A researcher followed a professional-services team through 2024, conducting 275 interviews and around 330 hours of observation. Video calls and team chats gave coworkers a glimpse of one another’s family lives and everyday interruptions, helping them open up and build psychological safety. That trust can make it easier to ask for help, admit mistakes and share ideas - good news for people and workplaces alike.
