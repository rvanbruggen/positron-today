So, Japan’s H3 rocket has lifted off from Tanegashima carrying the Martian Moons eXploration probe, or MMX. Its destination is Phobos, one of Mars’s moons, where the mission aims to collect samples and bring them back to Earth for the first time. Scientists hope those precious materials will reveal more about how the solar system formed. And here's the fascinating part: they could also offer clues to the origins of life on Earth.
