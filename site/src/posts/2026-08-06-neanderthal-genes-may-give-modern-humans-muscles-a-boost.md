Isn't it fascinating that a bit of Neanderthal DNA can actually enhance our muscles? A recent study reveals that those with Neanderthal versions of a growth hormone receptor tend to have more muscle mass—271 grams more, to be exact! This genetic trait is particularly common in South and East Asia, showing just how interconnected our histories are. So, while some might have this genetic advantage, it’s still important to hit the gym and train hard!
