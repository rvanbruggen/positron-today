This article dives into the fascinating idea that scientific breakthroughs often come from the tools we create rather than just brilliant ideas. Philosopher Alexander Krauss argues that innovations like the electron microscope have historically driven discovery by expanding our ability to observe and measure. He suggests that we should see ourselves as Homo methodologicus, a species defined by our tool-making capabilities. This perspective is especially relevant today as we explore the role of AI in enhancing scientific research.
