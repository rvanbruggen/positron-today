So: Gonzo, the blue Muppet with the long nose, has puzzled fans and scientists alike for decades. Despite his fuzzy feathers and bird-like traits, experts say he’s not quite any known animal, landing somewhere back in dinosaur times. His origins trace back to a 1970 Christmas special where he was a different character called Snarl. And here's the thing: Gonzo’s species remains a mystery, perfectly fitting his quirky personality as a lovable 'whatever.'
