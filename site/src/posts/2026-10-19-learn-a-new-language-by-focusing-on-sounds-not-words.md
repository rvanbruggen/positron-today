Want to speak a new language more naturally? A study of 67 Japanese participants found that people with a strong ear for languages tend to notice unfamiliar speech as sounds, rather than immediately interpreting it as words. This may help them get around sound patterns shaped by their native language, and learn from less exposure. The researchers say language teaching could do more to build listening skills tailored to each learner, instead of relying mainly on more practice.
