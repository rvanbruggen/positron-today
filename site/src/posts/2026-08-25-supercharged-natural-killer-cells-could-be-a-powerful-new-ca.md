Researchers at Stanford Medicine have developed a groundbreaking strategy to enhance natural killer cells, enabling them to infiltrate solid tumors more effectively. These modified cells not only attack cancer cells but also work well with antibody treatments to slow tumor growth. What's exciting is that they can be produced in large batches, making them potentially available as an off-the-shelf therapy. This could revolutionize cancer treatment by making cell therapy more accessible to a wider range of patients.
