So, a coordinated humanitarian truce has helped more than 750 people leave Oleshky, where residents faced severe food shortages as winter approached. Russian and Ukrainian officials worked together to arrange a safe corridor, and evacuees travelled with their pets through Crimea and Belarus to reach Kyiv. Aid groups and local residents welcomed them at the border with practical support and warm refreshments. Officials hope this cooperation can open the way for further evacuations and aid for people still in the city.
