Sonika Bhasin believes that sustainability should be a part of everyday life for her children, not just something they read about in books. Her son has been composting since he was two, learning about nature through hands-on experience. Instead of fearing creatures like maggots, he understands their role in the ecosystem. This natural learning process shows how children can grasp complex ideas about nature simply by being involved.
