So, Spain is turning to next-generation wind turbines to make better use of its wind resources while easing the visual impact on the landscape. More powerful modern turbines can produce more electricity, potentially allowing wind farms to rely on fewer machines. That means renewable energy and open scenery may be able to coexist more comfortably. It is an encouraging example of how technology can help make the energy transition fit more naturally into its surroundings.
