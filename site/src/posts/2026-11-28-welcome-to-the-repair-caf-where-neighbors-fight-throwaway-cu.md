So, across the US, repair cafés are bringing neighbors together to fix everything from broken chairs to glitchy electronics. Volunteers share their skills for free, while owners get to see how their belongings work and give them a second life. Organizers say these welcoming meetups can help reduce waste and emissions, while also challenging the idea that replacing things is easier than repairing them. And here's the thing: fixing a treasured item can bring people together as much as it keeps that item out of the trash.
