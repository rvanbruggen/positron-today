It's fascinating to see how the Pacific is experiencing a flurry of hurricanes while the Atlantic is unusually calm this season. Experts attribute this to a powerful El Niño, which creates warmer waters in the Pacific and less favorable conditions for hurricanes in the Atlantic. Interestingly, Hawaii has had more storms than usual, with several hurricanes making their presence felt. This stark contrast in hurricane activity is a reminder of how dynamic our climate can be.
