It's genuinely encouraging to see that fewer Belgians are taking their first steps into online gambling - this suggests that awareness and prevention efforts might actually be working. The trend shows a shift in how people approach betting habits, which is a positive sign for public health and responsible gaming initiatives. So what's driving this change? It could be better regulation, improved information campaigns, or simply people becoming more conscious about the risks involved. Either way, fewer new gamblers entering the market means fewer potential struggles with addiction and financial difficulties down the line.
