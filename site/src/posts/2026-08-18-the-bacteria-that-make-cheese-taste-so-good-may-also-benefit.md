So, researchers have discovered that the bacteria responsible for the unique flavors in three British artisan cheeses might also be good for our gut health. They found that these cheeses, made in Oxfordshire, are teeming with helpful microbes that could support our digestive systems. Interestingly, the aging process enhances the complexity of flavors while also protecting these beneficial bacteria. Plus, the rinds of these cheeses might even act as prebiotics, giving our gut flora a boost!
