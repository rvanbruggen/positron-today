So, imagine this—a man in Denmark was just trying to improve his backyard when he stumbled upon a treasure trove of Viking silver! In just half an hour of digging, he uncovered over 700 objects, including bracelets, coins, and even an amulet shaped like Thor’s hammer. This hoard is nearly three times larger than the previous record in Denmark, showcasing the importance of silver in the Viking Age economy. It’s a fascinating glimpse into history that tells us so much about trade and daily life back then.
