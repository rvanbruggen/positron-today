It's fantastic news that over 70,000 social and affordable homes are set to be built across England in the next decade! With nearly £10 billion allocated, this initiative aims to tackle the pressing housing crisis. Most of these new homes will be for social rent, making them much more accessible for families in need. This is just the beginning of a larger plan to create 300,000 homes, and I can't wait to see how this unfolds!
