Direct recordings from inside the human brain have helped resolve a century-old puzzle about Wernicke’s area. Long associated with processing spoken words, this region also appears to contribute to a related cognitive task. The findings show that signals from Wernicke’s area can help decode the speech people intend to produce, including for people with paralysis of the vocal tract. This insight deepens our understanding of speech and could help guide efforts to restore communication.
