Imagine living next to a lake that was so polluted you had to buy water for daily needs. That was the reality for residents of Chitlapakkam until they decided to take matters into their own hands. Over 2,000 people came together to clean and restore the lake, turning it from a sewage dump into a vibrant community space. Their seven-year journey shows the power of collective action and determination in creating positive change.
