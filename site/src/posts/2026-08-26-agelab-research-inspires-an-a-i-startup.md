So, Don Yansen, an MIT alumnus, stumbled upon a brilliant idea while studying at the MIT AgeLab. He realized that many older adults struggle with modern technology, which sparked his motivation to create a solution. Enter Kiwi Health, a startup he founded that offers a voice-controlled wristband designed specifically for seniors. This innovative device allows users to set reminders, make calls, and share health data without the hassle of screens, all while keeping caregivers informed. Yansen's mission is to empower seniors to live their best lives, and I find that truly inspiring!
