A Chinese experiment in space has chilled atoms to an astonishingly low temperature, setting a new record. Researchers used the weightless conditions of orbit to explore how ultra-cold atoms behave. This achievement opens fresh opportunities to investigate quantum physics and make highly precise measurements. And here's the thing: space is becoming an exciting laboratory for discoveries that are difficult to make on Earth.
