So: Denmark now hosts 11 wolf packs, two pairs, and two lone males according to a fresh count after this year's breeding season. This update comes from Aarhus University and the Natural History Museum in Aarhus, showing a lively wolf population. And here's the thing - the number of pups is a minimum estimate, so we might see those numbers rise as more observations come in. It’s fascinating to see these majestic animals thriving and expanding their presence in Denmark!
