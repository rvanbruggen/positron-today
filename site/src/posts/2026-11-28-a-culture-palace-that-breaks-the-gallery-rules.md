A former Brussels car garage is being reinvented as Kanal, a vast new cultural space with room for much more than art on gallery walls. The vision brings together a bakery, a playground and a speaker’s corner, making everyday life part of the experience. So: this is a culture palace designed to welcome people in, invite them to linger and spark unexpected encounters. It’s a fresh, generous take on what an art institution can be.
