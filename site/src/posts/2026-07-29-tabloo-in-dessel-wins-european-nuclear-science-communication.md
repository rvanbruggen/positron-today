How exciting is this? Tabloo, the interactive center in Dessel focused on radioactivity and radioactive waste, has won the prestigious Nuclear Science Communication Award! This award recognizes initiatives that make complex nuclear topics accessible to the public. What’s even better is that the prize money will support the Girls Leading in Science project, encouraging young girls to explore careers in science and technology. It’s a fantastic way to inspire the next generation while addressing important societal issues.
