So: for the first time, there’s a declining trend in the number of pleural cancer diagnoses, which is quite encouraging! This type of cancer, often linked to asbestos exposure, has been a serious health concern for years. Now, the data suggests that prevention efforts and regulations might be paying off. And here’s the thing - it gives hope that continued awareness and safety measures can further reduce these cases in the future.
