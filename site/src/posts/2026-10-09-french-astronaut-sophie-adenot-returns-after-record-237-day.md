French astronaut Sophie Adenot is back on Earth after spending 237 days, 5 hours and 18 minutes in space. That makes her the European Space Agency astronaut with the longest single stay in space so far. Her capsule landed off the coast of California with three crewmates after a mission of nearly eight months aboard the International Space Station. During the mission, Adenot completed three spacewalks, and fellow ESA graduate Raphaël Liégeois is expected to head to space no earlier than 2027.
