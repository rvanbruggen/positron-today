So, how do you create a doughnut shape from a flat piece of paper? Mathematician Richard Schwartz has found that it takes at least 24 folds to achieve this, resulting in a fascinating structure made of triangles. He even consulted AI during his research, which led to some surprising discoveries! This work not only builds on past mathematical findings but also opens up new avenues for origami enthusiasts and mathematicians alike.
