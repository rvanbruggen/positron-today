James Reed McGuire and Crystal Fritz transformed their suburban lawn into a vibrant native ecosystem, despite starting with little gardening experience. They learned from their early mistakes, gradually identifying local plants and creating a beautiful space that attracts birds and pollinators. Their efforts have not only beautified their home but also inspired neighbors to consider similar changes. Now, their garden is recognized as a Certified Wildlife Habitat, showcasing the positive impact of rewilding.
