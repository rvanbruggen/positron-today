So I find this really encouraging - a massive analysis of nearly 10,000 patients across 139 clinical trials shows that simple, drug-free treatments can be incredibly effective for knee osteoarthritis pain. Knee braces, water-based therapy, and regular exercise came out on top, which means people don't have to rely on medications that can carry cardiovascular and stomach risks. Now here's the thing - high-tech solutions like ultrasound actually ranked near the bottom, suggesting that sometimes the most straightforward approaches are genuinely the best. The researchers are clear that these accessible, affordable options could reshape how we treat this condition and give patients real alternatives.
