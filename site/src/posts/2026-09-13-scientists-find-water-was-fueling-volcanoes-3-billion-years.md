It's fascinating to think that water was influencing volcanic activity on Earth more than three billion years ago! A team of geologists led by Dr. Eric Vandenburg found evidence in ancient rocks from Western Australia that suggests water traveled deep beneath the surface, contributing to magma formation. This discovery hints that the young Earth was more dynamic than we previously thought, with processes like 'dripduction' moving water into the mantle. These findings could reshape our understanding of how Earth's continents developed and how vital resources like water were recycled early on.
