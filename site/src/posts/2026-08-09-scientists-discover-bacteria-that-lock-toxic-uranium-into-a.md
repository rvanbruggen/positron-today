So, researchers have made an exciting discovery about bacteria that can transform toxic uranium into a stable compound! By using glycerol as a food source, these bacteria can effectively reduce the amount of uranium dissolved in water, which is a game changer for environmental cleanup. After just 130 days, they found that only about five percent of the uranium remained in the water samples. This groundbreaking work could pave the way for innovative solutions to tackle uranium contamination in the environment.
