So, the “Golden Girls” idea is inspiring women to imagine retirement with friends rather than facing it alone. In New Mexico, women in their 50s to early 70s are joining retreats to explore the practical and emotional questions of shared living. Chip Conley’s Modern Elder Academy encourages people to see midlife and aging as opportunities for growth, purpose, and connection. Living together can offer companionship while preserving independence, though it takes honest communication, clear boundaries, and the right housemates.
