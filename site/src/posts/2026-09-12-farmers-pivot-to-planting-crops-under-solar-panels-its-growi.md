It's fascinating to see how Egyptian farmers are adapting to climate challenges by using solar panels to create shade for their crops. This innovative technique, known as agrivoltaics, not only generates clean energy but also helps reduce water loss and heat stress on plants. Early results from pilot projects are promising, with crops like watermelon thriving under the panels. It's a wonderful example of how ancient agricultural wisdom is being combined with modern technology to create a more resilient future.
