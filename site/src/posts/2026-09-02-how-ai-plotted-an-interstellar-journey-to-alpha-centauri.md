A nonprofit called the Fermi Explorer Mission is gearing up to launch a spacecraft to Alpha Centauri by 2029, which is super exciting! This mission is unique because it will use a trajectory discovered by an AI system, making it a groundbreaking approach. The spacecraft aims to carry a kilogram of cargo, including messages and a copy of the Golden Record, just like the Voyager probes did. It's a massive challenge, but the team is determined to make it happen, even if it takes 80,000 years to reach our nearest star!
