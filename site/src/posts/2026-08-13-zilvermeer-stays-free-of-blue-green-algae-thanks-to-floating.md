So, the Zilvermeer in Mol is having a fantastic summer without any blue-green algae, which is quite a feat! This is largely thanks to floating plant islands that naturally improve water quality. Director Igor Geubbelmans highlights that these islands act like a natural purification system, absorbing nutrients that algae thrive on. It's amazing to see how a sustainable approach can make such a difference, especially during these hot months.
