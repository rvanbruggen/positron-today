So: a new snow leopard cub named Sarka has just been born at the Bronx Zoo, and she's already exploring her snowy habitat with her mother. These elusive big cats, often called 'ghosts of the mountains,' are truly special, and Sarka’s arrival is a big win for conservation. The Bronx Zoo has been a pioneer in protecting snow leopards for over a century, and this cub adds to their impressive legacy. Which brings me to the exciting fact that the zoo has now welcomed over 80 snow leopard births since 1903, helping secure the future of this vulnerable species.
