While studying fireflies in Thailand, researchers discovered that crickets seemed to chirp in sync with the fireflies' flashing. This fascinating observation led to the realization that both species produced signals at a similar tempo of about 2.4 hertz. Interestingly, a broader survey revealed that many animals, from fish to birds, also communicate within a similar tempo range. This raises intriguing questions about why certain tempos are preferred across such diverse species.
