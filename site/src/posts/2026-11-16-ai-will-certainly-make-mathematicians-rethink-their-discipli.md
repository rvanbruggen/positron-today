So, an AI solved a mathematical puzzle in just 88 hours, after researchers had wrestled with it for 90 years. At the University of Copenhagen’s Department of Mathematical Sciences, the news sparked surprise, awe, pounding hearts and optimism rather than distress. Third-year bachelor’s student Johannes Danbjørg shares his reaction to the breakthrough. And here’s the thing: AI may soon prompt mathematicians to rethink what their discipline can be.
