So, it turns out that Belgium spends nearly 14 billion euros each year on fossil fuel subsidies, which really hinders our climate transition. Experts are suggesting reforms that could save the country over six billion euros by 2030. This is exciting news because it means we can tackle climate change while also improving our budget! And here's the thing: these changes could lead to a healthier environment and a more sustainable future for everyone.
