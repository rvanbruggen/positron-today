OpenAI has made a remarkable announcement, stating that its AI model has solved a century-old math problem known as the Navier-Stokes equation. This equation is vital for predicting fluid dynamics and has real-world applications like weather modeling and aircraft design. The solution was achieved in just 88 hours using over 10,000 AI agents, generating a staggering 130 billion tokens. This breakthrough not only showcases the power of AI but also opens doors to tackling other complex questions in the future.
