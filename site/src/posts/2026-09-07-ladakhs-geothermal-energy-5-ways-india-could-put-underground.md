At an impressive altitude, Puga Valley in Ladakh is setting the stage for India's first geothermal power project. This initiative, featuring two deep wells, aims to harness the earth's heat for sustainable energy solutions. Beyond just generating electricity, geothermal energy could revolutionize heating homes and greenhouses, especially in cold regions. It’s exciting to think about how this natural resource can also help reduce food waste and support local industries.
