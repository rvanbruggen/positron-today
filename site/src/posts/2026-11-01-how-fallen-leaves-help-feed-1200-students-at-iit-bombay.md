So, here’s a clever idea from IIT Bombay: turn the campus’s garden waste into cooking fuel. Every day, a startup called Infixen transforms around two tonnes of fallen leaves into pellets that help cook meals for students and staff. At Hostel 16, this has cut LPG use by 60%, reducing the number of cylinders needed from 10 to just 4. It’s a great example of how a campus can turn waste into a practical source of cleaner energy.
