So, researchers have found that GLP-1 weight-loss drugs like semaglutide can actually extend lifespan in older mice by about 12 percent! This is super exciting because it not only helps with weight loss but also improves memory and blood sugar control. The study suggests that this medication mimics the effects of calorie restriction without the struggle of dieting. It's fascinating to think that a single drug could have such a wide range of benefits, potentially changing how we approach aging and health.
