I find it absolutely fascinating how ranchers in the Great Plains are using fire as a tool to protect their land. Instead of fearing wildfires, families like the Coffeys are conducting controlled burns to revitalize their grasslands and remove invasive trees. It is such a clever way to manage risk - by burning early, they remove the fuel that would otherwise feed a catastrophic blaze later in the season. And here is the thing: this practice actually helps the cattle by encouraging healthier plant growth. It is a brilliant example of working with nature rather than against it.
