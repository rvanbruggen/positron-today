So: more than 5,000 light-years away, astronomers have found a white dwarf spinning once every eight minutes and sending out mysterious radio pulses. Its companion is a Sun-like star, making this the first known system of its kind to produce these signals. By tracking the stars through more than 100 hours of radio observations, researchers could locate the pulses close to the white dwarf. And here's the thing: the observations also revealed powerful magnetic activity, including what may be a giant loop of plasma on the companion star.
