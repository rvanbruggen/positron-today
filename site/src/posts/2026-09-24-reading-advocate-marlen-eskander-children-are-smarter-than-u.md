Marlen Eskander, founder of the Reading Promotion Institute, shares her passion for making literature accessible to children and young people. She believes children are smarter than adults but need practice and support to develop reading skills. Eskander’s approach includes interactive storytelling that invites children to shape the narrative, helping them interpret texts deeply. She highlights reading as a vital democratic skill and hopes future discussions will focus on the magic of storytelling, not just its utility.
