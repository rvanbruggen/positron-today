So, researchers have uncovered the incredible engineering skills of the ancient city of Liangzhu, which dates back around 5,000 years. This fascinating site in eastern China features mounds, dams, and city walls, all built on a challenging waterlogged landscape. It's amazing how these early inhabitants managed to reshape their environment to create a stable living area. Which brings me to the idea that this discovery highlights not just their ingenuity but also the organization required to build such a complex city.
