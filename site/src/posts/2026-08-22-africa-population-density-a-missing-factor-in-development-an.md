Joe Studwell's insights on Africa's development highlight that low population density has historically hindered progress. With sub-Saharan Africa now experiencing rapid demographic growth, the conditions for economic transformation are finally aligning. This shift is crucial as density fosters markets, infrastructure, and specialization, which are essential for modern economies. As urbanization accelerates, Africa is on the brink of a significant change that could reshape its future.
