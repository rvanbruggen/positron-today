At Medanta Hospital in Gurugram, surgeons Nitin Kumar Rajput and Lalit Malik performed a complex heart bypass using five robotic arms controlled from two consoles. The procedure was carried out through tiny incisions while the patient’s heart continued beating, without a heart-lung machine. It is the world’s first reported dual-console, five-arm robotic beating-heart TECAB procedure. The team used SSi Mantra, a surgical robot developed in India, and the patient’s recovery has been encouraging.
