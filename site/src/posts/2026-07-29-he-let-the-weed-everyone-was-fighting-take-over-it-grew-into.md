In a bold move, botanist Hugh Wilson decided to let gorse, a weed many wanted to eradicate, take over a barren hillside in New Zealand. Instead of fighting it, he recognized its role as a nurse crop that would eventually support native forest regeneration. Over time, the gorse created the perfect environment for native seedlings to thrive, ultimately leading to its own demise as the trees grew tall and shaded it out. Today, Hinewai Reserve is a thriving ecosystem, showcasing the power of nature when we simply let it be.
