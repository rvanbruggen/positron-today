Researchers have made exciting progress in unraveling one of art history's biggest mysteries regarding Claude Monet's famous painting 'Impression, Sunrise'. By using astronomical analysis, archival documents, and weather data, they determined that Monet likely began painting at 7:35 AM on November 13, 1872. This work is not just a masterpiece of Impressionism but also a fascinating puzzle that combines science and art. It's amazing to think about how much effort went into pinpointing the exact moment when this iconic piece came to life!
