So: Liberia will hold the rotating presidency of the UN Security Council in December, its first turn in the role in 65 years. President Joseph Boakai is set to chair a high-level debate on maritime security on December 3. Liberia plans to draw on its journey from civil conflict to reconciliation and recovery as it contributes to international peace and security. And here's the thing: the government sees this presidency as a shared national achievement built through decades of diplomacy and resilience.
