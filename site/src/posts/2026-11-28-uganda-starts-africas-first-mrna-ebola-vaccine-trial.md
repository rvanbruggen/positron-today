So, Uganda has begun Africa’s first clinical trial of an mRNA vaccine targeting the Bundibugyo strain of Ebola, which is behind an outbreak in neighbouring Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC). The state-run Uganda Virus Research Institute and Moderna will assess the vaccine’s safety and immune response in volunteers aged one and older, with 224 people expected to take part. It is the vaccine’s second human trial, following an earlier study in Canada, and will be the first to test it in children. Meanwhile, a separate candidate vaccine is entering trials in the DRC, and Gavi has secured a commitment for 1.5 million doses that could help speed up deployment if the vaccine is approved.
