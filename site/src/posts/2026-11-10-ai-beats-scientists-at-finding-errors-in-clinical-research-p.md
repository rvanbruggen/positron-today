So, here's an intriguing result: AI outperformed scientists at finding errors in clinical research papers. That could give researchers a useful extra way to check studies before their findings shape medical decisions. The technology may help flag problems, while people still bring the expertise needed to judge what they mean. And here's the thing - better checks can help make clinical research more trustworthy.
