So, researchers have made an exciting discovery about gliomas, which are tough tumors in the central nervous system. They've found that drugs mimicking GABAergic neurons can actually slow down these aggressive tumors in mice. Isn't that fascinating? This could open up new avenues for treatment and give hope to those affected by this challenging condition.
