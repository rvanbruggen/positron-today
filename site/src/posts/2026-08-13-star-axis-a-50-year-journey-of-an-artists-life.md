So, 'Star Axis' is this incredible artistic installation that has been in the making for over 50 years by American artist Charles Ross. At 88 years old, Ross has dedicated his life to creating this monumental observatory in New Mexico, blending art and science in a truly unique way. The structure features a tunnel with 147 steps leading to an opening for observing the North Star's orbit, which spans an astonishing 26,000 years! It's amazing how this project connects us to the cosmos and offers a profound experience of time and space.
