What fantastic news for the eastern imperial eagle, Serbia's national bird! This year, conservation efforts have led to a record 29 breeding pairs, with 16 successfully hatching 25 chicks. It's a remarkable turnaround since only one breeding pair was known in 2017. The growing interest in these majestic birds is a real reason to celebrate, and it shows how dedicated efforts can make a difference.
