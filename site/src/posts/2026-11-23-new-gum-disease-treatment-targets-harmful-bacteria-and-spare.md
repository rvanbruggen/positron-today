Researchers at Nagoya University have developed a promising treatment that targets the gum-damaging bacterium P. gingivalis while sparing beneficial microbes. The approach pairs antibodies from egg yolks with a light-sensitive dye, then uses near-infrared light to kill the targeted bacteria. Early tests in human cell cultures and mice suggest it can act selectively without harming healthy gum cells. So, this technology could offer a more precise way to tackle periodontitis, though further research is needed.
