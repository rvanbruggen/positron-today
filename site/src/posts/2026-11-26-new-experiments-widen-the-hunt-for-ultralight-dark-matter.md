So, despite decades of searching, dark matter remains unseen, and scientists still know very little about most of the universe’s contents. As familiar searches for WIMPs have come up short, researchers are turning to ultralight particles that could behave more like vast waves than ordinary particles. Clever new experiments use everything from quantum clouds of atoms and tiny levitating magnets to nuclear clocks and Earth’s magnetic field. And here’s the exciting part: one of these fresh approaches could reveal clues to an entirely new realm of physics.
