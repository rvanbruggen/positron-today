When the monsoon hits Alappuzha, it brings not just rain but also a surge in mosquito breeding. Residents of Vadackal took matters into their own hands, reducing breeding sites from 64.2% to just 8.6% in two years! They organized themselves into committees, checked homes for stagnant water, and even tackled garbage that was creating breeding pools. This community-driven effort shows how grassroots action can lead to significant health improvements, and it’s just so inspiring to see people come together for a common cause.
