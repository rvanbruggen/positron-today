Chetan Soorenji from Mumbai faced a water scarcity issue as his borewell went deeper and the water quality declined. He had a brilliant idea to harvest rainwater and redirect it into his borewell, along with creating a percolation pit. To top it off, he even connected his air conditioner to contribute more water during the hot summer months. This innovative approach not only improved his water supply but also inspired his neighbors to consider similar solutions.
