Rewilding is a fascinating approach that could significantly reduce the risk of wildfires in Britain. Experts suggest that restoring ecosystems can create landscapes that are wetter and more fire-resistant, which is crucial since most wildfires are human-caused. It's not just about preventing fires but also about rebuilding the land to better withstand them. By focusing on natural processes, we can transform our moors and uplands into resilient environments that thrive even in the face of climate change.
