Mary Gray is a remarkable figure who combines her expertise in statistics and law to tackle human-rights abuses and discrimination. Even in her 80s, she continues to make a significant impact, training investigators for the International Criminal Court and advocating for equality in academia. Her journey began with a keen observation of gender discrimination in retirement benefits, which led her to earn a law degree and fight for justice. Gray's legacy includes founding the Association for Women in Mathematics and receiving recognition for her lifelong commitment to social justice.
