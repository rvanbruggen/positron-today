So, I just read this incredible story about how technology is literally saving lives in Mozambique. When the Incomáti River burst its banks, a group of newly trained drone pilots stepped up to help with rescue efforts. They used their new skills to map flooded areas and guide rescue teams through dangerous waters in real time. And here's the thing - because of their quick thinking and these drones, not a single life was lost during the worst flood in decades. It is just so inspiring to see how training and innovation can make such a massive difference on the ground.
