So, how much time did Spinosaurus really spend in the water? A new Yale study combined fossil measurements, CT scans and machine learning to investigate how ancient animals adapted to aquatic life. Its findings suggest that Spinosaurus spent most of its life underwater, adding fresh evidence to a long-running scientific debate. The approach also helps researchers make more rigorous estimates of how extinct animals lived when fossil clues point in different directions.
