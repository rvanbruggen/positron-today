This Independence Day, we're shining a light on everyday heroes who embody true patriotism through civic action. Take Dadarao Bilhore, who, after losing his son to a pothole, took it upon himself to fill over 1,500 potholes in Mumbai. Or Anand Malligavad, who revived a dried-up lake in just 45 days, proving that love for one's community can spark real change. These stories remind us that patriotism isn't just about flags and anthems—it's about caring for our shared spaces and taking action.
