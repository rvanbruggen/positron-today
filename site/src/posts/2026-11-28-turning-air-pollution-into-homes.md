Indian architect Tejas Sidnal is turning recovered carbon waste into carbon tiles through his startup, Carbon Craft Design. These eco-friendly tiles can be used in homes and buildings as an alternative to traditional construction materials. Instead of leaving carbon waste unused, the idea gives it a practical second life. And here's the thing: this kind of innovation could help make the future of construction greener.
