Isn't it fascinating how a mother's age can influence her offspring's traits and behaviors? Researchers are diving deep into this phenomenon, known as maternal age effects, which appear across many species, including humans. The exciting twist is that these effects might not stem from DNA changes but rather from epigenetic processes that modify gene expression. This research could unlock new insights into how we understand maternal influences on future generations.
