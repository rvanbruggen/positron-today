Samantha Cooper, a former oil executive, has made a remarkable shift from powering the oil industry to fighting against it. After two decades in the business, she realized the toll it was taking on her life and the planet. Through mindfulness and volunteering, she discovered a new purpose in advocating for climate action. Now, as the director of Business Declares, she inspires corporations to tackle the pressing ecological and social crises we face.
