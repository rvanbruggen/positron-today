So, those piles of hair swept up in salons across France, Belgium, and Luxembourg are getting a remarkable second life. French company Capillum turns the clippings into biodegradable mats that help keep hungry deer away from young trees. As the hair slowly breaks down, it also returns nitrogen and other nutrients to the soil. It’s a clever way to reduce waste while giving forests a helping hand.
