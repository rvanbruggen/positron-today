I find it fascinating how the brain uses electrical waves to process information so quickly and flexibly. Recent research shows these waves are not just simple signals but complex patterns like sources, sinks, and spirals that relate to different behaviors. This means the brain might be reorganizing itself in real time to keep up with what we do every second. And here's the thing - these discoveries are shifting how we understand brain activity from just noise to a major way the cortex processes information.
