Isn't it fascinating how avocado trees have this incredible ability to switch their flowers' sex every day? They alternate between male and female phases, which helps them with their unique reproductive strategy. A recent study identified a specific gene that enables this remarkable behavior, revealing how these trees coordinate their flowering patterns. It’s a beautiful reminder of the complexity of nature and how much we still have to learn about it!
