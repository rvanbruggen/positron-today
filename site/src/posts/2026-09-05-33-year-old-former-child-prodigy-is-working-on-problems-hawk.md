Sabrina Gonzalez Pasterski is a remarkable theoretical physicist who was once dubbed the 'Next Einstein' for her incredible achievements at a young age. She soloed a plane she built herself at just 16 and graduated from MIT with a perfect GPA. Now, at 33, she's tackling the monumental challenge of unifying quantum mechanics and general relativity, a problem that has stumped the greatest minds. Her groundbreaking work on gravitational waves has already caught the attention of legends like Stephen Hawking, and she continues to push the boundaries of physics.
