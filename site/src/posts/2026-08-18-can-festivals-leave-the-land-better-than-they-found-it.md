Festival season is buzzing with exciting initiatives that go beyond just recycling. Events like UK Youth Day are encouraging attendees to bring plastic waste instead of tickets, turning trash into art and community action. It's all about empowering young people to shape their future while celebrating sustainability. This shift towards lasting environmental solutions is inspiring, as festivals start to focus on what remains after the music fades.
