So here's something truly special - Chinese swimmer Yu Zidi just claimed gold in the 200m butterfly at the Asian Games in Japan at just 13 years old, and she did it in style with a time of 2:05.08. Now, what makes this even more impressive is that she absolutely crushed the competition, beating her closest rival by over three seconds. And here's the thing - she's already showing the kind of dominance that hints at an extraordinary future for world swimming. With the Los Angeles Olympics just two years away, Yu is clearly one to watch as she continues to develop her talent at such a young age.
