So: an Italian-led international team has made black hole calculations more precise, modelling what happens as two black holes orbit and spiral toward a merger. These calculations help researchers interpret gravitational waves and reconstruct the cosmic events that produced them. Led by Giacomo Brunello, the team’s work is presented in two papers published in Physical Review Letters. And here’s the thing: methods first developed for particle physics could help reveal the properties of black holes and put Einstein’s theory to an even sharper test.
