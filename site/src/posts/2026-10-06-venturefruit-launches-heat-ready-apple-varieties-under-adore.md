So: VentureFruit has launched ADORE, an umbrella brand for apple varieties bred to cope with hotter growing conditions. The first range includes HOT03A1 and a second variety that ripens four to six weeks earlier, giving growers options to suit their climate and harvest season. The apples are being tested for yield, quality and appeal to consumers, with trials in Europe and Asia and planting material also being tested for New Zealand. Around 120 hectares are already licensed in Catalonia, and HOT03A1 could reach European consumers in the next few years.
