On the unique prison island of Gorgona in Italy, inmates are finding hope and purpose through winemaking. They work diligently in the vineyards, cultivating grapes that will produce a highly valued wine, even though they can't taste it themselves. One inmate, Piero, shares his pride in the meticulous care of the vines against the stunning backdrop of the Tyrrhenian Sea. This initiative not only aids in their rehabilitation but also offers a glimpse of a brighter future beyond the prison walls.
