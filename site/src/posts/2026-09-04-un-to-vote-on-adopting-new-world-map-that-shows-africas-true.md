The UN is gearing up for a significant vote on a resolution that could replace the traditional Mercator map with one that accurately represents Africa's size. Togo's foreign affairs minister highlighted the scientific basis for this change, supported by the African Union and other nations. This shift could reshape educational materials and technology, challenging long-held biases in global perceptions. If successful, it would mark a step towards decolonizing how we view the world, especially Africa's vital role.
