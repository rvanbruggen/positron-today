I love how the neem tree is truly a one-plant wonder with over 100 uses! From twigs that serve as natural toothbrushes to seeds pressed for oil used in soaps and pest control, neem is woven into daily life across India. Its leaves protect stored grains from insects, and even the leftover neem cake enriches the soil. And here's the thing - every part of the tree, including bark and flowers, has a special role, making neem a remarkable gift from nature.
