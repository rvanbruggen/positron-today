Imagine a rich chorus of crickets and grasshoppers echoing through ancient forests 165 million years ago! A team of biologists has reconstructed these sounds based on the wing structures of nine extinct insect species. Their findings reveal a surprising variety of sounds, including ultrasonic calls from one species, challenging previous beliefs about insect sound production. This research not only sheds light on the past but also enhances our understanding of the evolution of communication in the animal kingdom.
