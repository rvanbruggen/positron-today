So: a remarkable piece of ring armour has been found in the wreck of a warship belonging to Danish king Johan, which sank in 1495. The discovery offers a tangible link to a dramatic moment in maritime history. Ring armour, made from interlinked metal rings, gives researchers a fresh clue to the equipment used aboard the ship. And here's the thing: finds like this can help bring the people and stories behind a centuries-old wreck into sharper focus.
