So, after flying around the Moon as Artemis 2 pilot, Victor Glover says the bigger goal is getting humans to Mars, not simply beating China to the lunar finish line. The historic mission made him one of the people who have traveled farthest from Earth, and the first Black astronaut to journey to the Moon. Now retired from active astronaut duties, he plans to return to the California university where he studied and met his wife, while continuing to support NASA as an astronaut emeritus. He also reflects on the moving connection with his family during quarantine and will share his experiences at the Starmus Festival in La Palma.
