So, a fascinating new study reveals that changing your breathing rhythm can actually influence your decision-making! Researchers found that longer exhalations increased heart rate variability and made the brain more responsive to rewards, leading to bolder choices. This is quite a shift from the traditional view that decision-making is solely a brain process. It turns out our bodily state plays a crucial role, and by simply breathing differently, we can enhance our ability to take risks.
