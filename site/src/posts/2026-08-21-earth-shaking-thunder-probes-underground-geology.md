Isn't it fascinating how nature can help us understand the Earth better? Researchers have discovered that thunderquakes, those vibrations caused by thunder, can actually map hidden geological structures. This is especially useful in areas where seismic activity is low. So, the next time you hear a big crack of thunder, just think about the secrets it might be revealing beneath our feet!
