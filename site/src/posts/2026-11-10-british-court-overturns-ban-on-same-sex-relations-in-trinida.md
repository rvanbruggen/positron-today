A British court has overturned a law banning sex between people of the same sex in Trinidad and Tobago. The case was brought by Jason Jones, who argued that the ban violated his human rights. The Privy Council in London ruled in his favour. So, this decision marks an important step for LGBTQ+ rights in the Caribbean nation.
