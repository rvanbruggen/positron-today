I love how these Arunachal villages have turned waste sorting into a community ritual every Sunday! Families separate their trash into 22 different types right at home, making recycling way more effective. With around 6,000 people involved across 27 villages, they’ve recovered nearly 90 tonnes of waste so far. And here's the thing - this system isn’t just about cleaning up; it’s about everyone pitching in, even paying a small fee to keep things running smoothly.
