Here’s a lovely surprise from Harvard researchers: listeners enjoyed familiar stories more than exciting new ones. In experiments, people could follow a story better when they already knew the context, while unfamiliar details sometimes left them confused. The researchers call this the “novelty penalty” - storytellers may expect fresh tales to impress, but shared experiences help people connect. So, I’d keep stories simple and relatable, and trust that common ground makes a conversation more engaging.
