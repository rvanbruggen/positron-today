So, Gävle Symphony Orchestra is bringing the music of little-known Swedish composer Melcher Melchers to new listeners. Its sparkling world premiere recording features both of his piano concertos. Chief conductor Christian Reif draws out the music's shadowy shades as well as its radiance. DN critic Johanna Paulsson names it the month's best classical album.
