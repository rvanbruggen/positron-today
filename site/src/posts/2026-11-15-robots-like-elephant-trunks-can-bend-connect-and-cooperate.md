So: researchers at Colorado State University have developed flexible, tube-shaped robots that move much like an elephant’s trunk. Their tensegrity structure uses tensioned cables to hold rigid rods in place, allowing each robot to bend and change shape without traditional joints. Mechanical grippers at both ends help the robots handle objects and connect securely, so teams can carry heavier loads or cross gaps together. Tested in the lab and real-world settings, the machines could one day support manufacturing, space exploration and search-and-rescue missions.
