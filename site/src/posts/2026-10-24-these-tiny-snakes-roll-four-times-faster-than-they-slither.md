So, these tiny Southeast Asian snakes have a surprising escape trick: they roll along at speeds about four times faster than their usual slithering. Researchers found they alternate between rolling onto each side and sweeping a curved part of the body down beside the head to push themselves forward. Even at high speed, they can follow a narrow track while keeping their heads relatively steady and pointed ahead. It’s a remarkable example of precise body control creating an unexpected way to move.
