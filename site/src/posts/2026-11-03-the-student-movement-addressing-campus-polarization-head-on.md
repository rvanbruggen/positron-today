After political tensions and Charlie Kirk’s assassination sharpened campus divisions, students at the University of Michigan showed how respectful conversation can make room for very different views. Their meeting was hosted by BridgeUSA, a student movement that helps peers explain their beliefs, listen closely and disagree without needing to reach consensus. Founded at UC Berkeley in 2017, BridgeUSA has grown to 146 chapters, and its national summit trains students to lead and moderate these conversations. From Columbia to campuses nationwide, the group is creating spaces for thoughtful dialogue during moments of conflict - a hopeful reminder that listening can be a courageous act.
