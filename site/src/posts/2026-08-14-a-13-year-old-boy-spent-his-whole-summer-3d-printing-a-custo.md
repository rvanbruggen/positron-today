Isn't it amazing when a young person's creativity meets technology? Zac Waters, a 13-year-old, dedicated his summer to 3D printing a custom wheelchair for a baby in need after being inspired by a YouTube video. He spent around 300 hours designing and assembling this life-changing device, showcasing how one person's effort can make a significant impact. Organizations like MakeGood are making it possible for anyone to create assistive technology, and Zac is a shining example of this spirit.
