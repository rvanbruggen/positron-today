Marie Tharp was a groundbreaking cartographer whose work fundamentally transformed our understanding of the Earth's geology. She created the first detailed maps of the ocean floor, revealing the existence of mid-ocean ridges and plate tectonics. Her contributions were initially overlooked in a male-dominated field, but today she is celebrated for her pioneering spirit and scientific insight. It's fascinating to see how her maps not only changed science but also inspired future generations of geoscientists.
