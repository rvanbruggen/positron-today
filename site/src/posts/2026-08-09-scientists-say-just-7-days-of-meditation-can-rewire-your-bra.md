So, here's the exciting news: just a week of meditation can lead to some amazing changes in your brain and body! Researchers at UC San Diego found that a seven-day retreat not only improved brain activity but also boosted immune signaling and metabolism. It's fascinating to think that these practices can enhance neuroplasticity, allowing our brains to adapt and form new connections. This study really highlights how powerful meditation can be, going beyond relaxation to fundamentally change our biology.
