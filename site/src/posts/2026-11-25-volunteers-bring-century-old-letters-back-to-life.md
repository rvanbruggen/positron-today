At Bowling Green State University, AmeriCorps volunteers are transcribing handwritten letters, diaries, and journals through a project called “Cursive for a Cause.” Many documents were written by women, and preserving them helps keep personal stories, recipes, and everyday records from being forgotten. The typed versions also become searchable digital resources, making the archives easier for researchers and the public to explore. I love how this hands-on effort connects generations - and shows that cursive writing is far from dead.
