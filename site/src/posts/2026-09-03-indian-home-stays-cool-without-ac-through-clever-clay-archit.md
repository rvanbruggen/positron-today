In the sweltering heat of Kerala, Elemental Architects have designed a family home that stays cool without air conditioning. Using clever features like terracotta roofs and perforated walls, the house harnesses natural airflow to create a comfortable living space. The unique materials, including local laterite, help regulate temperature and moisture, making it an eco-friendly solution. It's amazing to see how traditional techniques are being revived to tackle modern challenges!
