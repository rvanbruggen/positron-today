So, doctors at Franciscan Health have reported the first bone marrow transplant using cells from a deceased organ donor to treat severe aplastic anemia. The 40-year-old patient had lived with the disease for a decade, and her condition had worsened despite multiple treatments. Six months after the transplant, the donor marrow had taken hold, her blood counts had recovered, and she no longer needed transfusions. This promising result could help widen access to potentially curative transplants for people with serious bone marrow disorders.
