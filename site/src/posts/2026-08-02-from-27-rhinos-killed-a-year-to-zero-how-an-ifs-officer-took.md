It's incredible to think that just a few years ago, Assam was losing 27 rhinos a year to poaching. Thanks to the tireless efforts of dedicated individuals like IFS officer Sandeep Kumar, the state celebrated zero rhino poaching for the first time in 2022! This achievement is a testament to the power of teamwork, technology, and community involvement in conservation. With ongoing efforts, including special task forces and local intelligence networks, the future looks bright for these magnificent creatures.
