So here's the thing - artists are discovering something really beautiful about connecting with people living with dementia through creative practice. Touch, movement, and imagination become a language that transcends the communication challenges these individuals face, creating moments of genuine connection and joy. The approach shows that even when memory fades, the capacity to engage with art, music, and physical expression remains remarkably alive. It's a reminder that creativity isn't just about making something - it's about reaching people in the most human ways possible.
