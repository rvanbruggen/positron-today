So: California is close to finishing the world’s largest wildlife crossing, a vegetated bridge over the 101 Freeway in Agoura Hills. About 210 feet long and up to 174 feet wide, it will reconnect habitats in the Santa Monica Mountains and help wildlife, especially mountain lions, move safely between them. That could reduce dangerous road encounters and help prevent isolated animal populations from losing genetic diversity. The crossing is scheduled to be completed on December 2, although flooding from wet winter weather could slow the final work.
