So, it turns out Irish teenagers are absolutely smashing it when it comes to reading, ranking first in the EU and seventh globally! They also scored third in science and fourth in maths according to the latest PISA study. This is such a fantastic achievement, especially considering the increasing number of countries participating in this assessment. The Minister for Education is thrilled, highlighting the importance of fostering a love for reading and maintaining strong educational standards.
