For centuries, indigo was a prized commodity in India, but it came at a terrible cost to farmers. The East India Company's coercive practices forced peasants into debt and exploitation under the tinkathia system. However, in 1859, a wave of resistance emerged, culminating in the Champaran Satyagraha led by Gandhi in 1917. This pivotal moment transformed indigo from a symbol of oppression into a powerful catalyst for India's freedom movement.
