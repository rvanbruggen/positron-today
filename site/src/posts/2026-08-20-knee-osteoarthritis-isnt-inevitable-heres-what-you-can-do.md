Knee osteoarthritis is a common issue, especially as we age, but it's not a given. There are effective strategies to manage pain and maintain mobility, like healthy eating and regular exercise. Interestingly, even small lifestyle changes can have a big impact, such as losing weight to relieve pressure on the knees. It's all about taking proactive steps to improve your quality of life and reduce the risk of developing this condition.
