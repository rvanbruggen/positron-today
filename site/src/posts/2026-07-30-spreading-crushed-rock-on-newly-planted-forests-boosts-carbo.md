I find it fascinating that a sprinkle of crushed basalt rock can really help newly planted forests thrive! Scientists from Imperial College London have discovered that this technique boosts carbon absorption and enhances soil nutrients. With billions of trees being planted worldwide, ensuring their survival is crucial for tackling CO2 emissions. Early results show a 27% increase in carbon storage potential, which is just amazing!
