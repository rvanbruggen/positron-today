This article dives into the fascinating history of how the Enigma was cracked, focusing on the previously unknown correspondence of mathematician Marian Rejewski. It all started with a chance encounter at a customs office in Warsaw that sparked a major decryption operation. Rejewski's letters reveal his efforts to document Poland's crucial role in breaking German codes, which had been overlooked in British publications. It's a captivating glimpse into the past that highlights the importance of preserving such historical documents.
