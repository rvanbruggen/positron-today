The Riemann hypothesis, a tantalizing mystery in mathematics, suggests that prime numbers follow a hidden pattern. Now, a team in China has used a quantum computer to explore this hypothesis in a groundbreaking way. By encoding the Riemann zeta function into a physical system, they’ve made this abstract concept more tangible. This innovative approach could open new doors for understanding prime numbers and the potential of quantum computing in tackling complex mathematical problems.
