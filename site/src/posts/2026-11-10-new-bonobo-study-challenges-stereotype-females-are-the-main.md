A new study of bonobos challenges the familiar idea that males are the main hunters among great apes. Its findings point instead to females playing the leading role in hunting. That offers a fresh perspective on bonobo society and the roles different sexes can take. It’s a welcome reminder that animal behaviour can be more varied than stereotypes suggest.
