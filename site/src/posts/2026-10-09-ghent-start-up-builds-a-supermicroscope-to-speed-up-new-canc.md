A start-up in Ghent is building a “supermicroscope” to help speed up the development of new cancer treatments. The project brings advanced microscopy into the effort to find better ways to tackle the disease. So, researchers could gain a useful new tool as they study potential treatments. It’s an exciting example of technology supporting progress in cancer research.
