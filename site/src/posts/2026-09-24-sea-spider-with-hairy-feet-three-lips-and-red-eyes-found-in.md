So: marine biologists at the University of British Columbia have uncovered two fascinating new species of sea spiders in Canada’s Salish Sea. One sports hairy feet and striking red eyes, while the other struggles to groom itself, becoming a tiny ecosystem for parasites. These creatures are truly weird wonders, with traits like breathing through their skin and males caring for eggs on their legs. And here's the thing - these discoveries highlight how little we know about marine life in this area, especially as climate change threatens their fragile habitats.
