So, researchers have discovered a fascinating way to enhance the interaction between light and sound by freezing the core of optical fibers. By cooling the liquid inside to -196 °C, they created a solid that still guides light and sound waves effectively. This innovative approach leads to a staggering increase in interaction strength, over 1,000 times that of standard fibers. It's a groundbreaking step towards more efficient photonic computing and opens up exciting possibilities for future technologies.
