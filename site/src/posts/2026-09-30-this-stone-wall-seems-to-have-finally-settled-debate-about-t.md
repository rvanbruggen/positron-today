So: archaeologists in Paris have uncovered a stone wall that might just solve the mystery of the city’s origins. This wall, dating back to the time of Julius Caesar’s conquest, was found on the Île de la Cité, the historic heart of Paris. Alongside it, they discovered charred remains and artifacts pointing to the ancient Gallic settlement of Lutetia. The mayor of Paris called the find dizzying, as it connects the modern city directly to its deep past. And here's the thing - this discovery could finally confirm where Lutetia truly stood, settling centuries of debate.
