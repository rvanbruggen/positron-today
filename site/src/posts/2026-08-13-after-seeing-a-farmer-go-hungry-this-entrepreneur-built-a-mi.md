Sathya Raghu Mokkapati was deeply moved by the sight of a farmer eating mud due to failed crops, which inspired him to create a solution for smallholder farmers. In 2015, he co-founded Kheyti and developed the Greenhouse-in-a-Box, a compact greenhouse that protects crops from extreme weather and saves a staggering 98% water. This innovative approach not only helps farmers grow better but also provides them with guidance on crop selection and cultivation techniques. It's a fantastic example of how technology can empower farmers and make agriculture more sustainable in the face of climate challenges.
