How cool is this? Scientists have captured video of a fish, the Scalicus engyceros, walking backwards along the seafloor in the South China Sea! This armored searobin uses its unique fins to stroll, which is a first for fish locomotion. The study reveals not just this quirky behavior but also highlights the fish's adaptations for hunting in the deep sea. It’s amazing how technology is helping us uncover the secrets of our oceans!
