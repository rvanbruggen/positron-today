So, meet Miss Abyss, a cheerful illustrated dumbo octopus on a mission to rally support for protecting the deep sea. Real dumbo octopuses live on the ocean floor, sometimes nearly 13,000 feet below the surface. The Center for American Progress is inviting people to share photos of Miss Abyss in places that represent their home, and to sign a petition opposing deep-sea mining. And here’s the thing: mining could harm poorly understood ocean ecosystems, while the campaign gives people a fun way to speak up for their protection.
