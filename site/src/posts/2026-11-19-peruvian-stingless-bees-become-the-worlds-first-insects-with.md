In Peru, stingless bees have become the first insects in the world to receive legal rights. It is a remarkable step in how laws can recognise and protect the natural world. These small pollinators play an important role in plant life and biodiversity, despite often receiving less attention than larger animals. Now, their new status could help strengthen efforts to protect them and the habitats they depend on.
