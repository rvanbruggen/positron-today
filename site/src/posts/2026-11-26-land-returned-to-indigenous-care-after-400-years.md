So, more than 400 years after the Pilgrims arrived on Cape Cod, The Nature Conservancy has transferred about 126 acres of marsh and habitat in Eastham, Massachusetts, to the Indigenous-led Native Land Conservancy. The land joins a nearby 20.4-acre parcel, creating a larger protected area overlooking First Encounter Beach, where colonists and Nauset warriors clashed in 1620. The Conservancy plans to care for the land while supporting traditional activities, restoring native plants and protecting wildlife such as diamondback terrapins. It’s a meaningful step in the growing land-back movement, helping Indigenous communities reconnect with ancestral places and share their histories on their own terms.
