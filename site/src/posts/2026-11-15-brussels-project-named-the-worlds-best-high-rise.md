So, Brussels has a new reason to celebrate: Zin has won the 2026/27 International High-Rise Award. The prize is considered one of the most important international honours for tall-building architecture, and Zin stood out among more than 1,000 towers completed worldwide over the past two years. Delivered in 2024, the 129-metre-high project brings together offices, homes, a hotel, sports facilities and restaurants across about 115,500 square metres. And here’s the thing: it also offers public spaces, including a rooftop garden and a greenhouse.
