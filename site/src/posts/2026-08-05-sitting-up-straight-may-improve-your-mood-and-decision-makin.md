So, it turns out that how we sit can actually influence our mood and decision-making! A study from McGill University found that participants who sat upright felt more positive and took smarter risks compared to those who slouched. This is fascinating because it suggests that our physical posture can subtly shape our emotions and behaviors. It’s a reminder that even small changes in our environment, like how we set up our desks, might have a bigger impact than we think.
