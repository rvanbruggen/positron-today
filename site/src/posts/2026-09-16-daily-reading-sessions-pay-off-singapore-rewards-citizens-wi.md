So Singapore has come up with a pretty clever idea - they're actually paying people to read more. After completing 50 daily reading sessions, citizens get rewarded with 1 Singaporese dollar, which might not sound like much but the real prize is building a reading habit. I think what makes this so interesting is that it tackles two birds with one stone: encouraging literacy while making it genuinely engaging for people of all ages. And here's the thing - it shows how creative incentives can shift behavior in positive ways, turning something as simple as picking up a book into a shared community achievement.
