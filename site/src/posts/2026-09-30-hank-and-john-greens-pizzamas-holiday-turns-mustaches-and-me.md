So: Pizzamas started as a quirky fan celebration around John Green’s mustache and a fun piece of fan art called Pizza John. Over the years, it’s grown into a two-week internet holiday where Hank and John upload videos daily and sell limited-edition merch. And here's the thing - all profits from these sales go to support maternal healthcare through Partners in Health. It’s amazing how a simple meme turned into a joyful community event that raises over $100,000 annually!
