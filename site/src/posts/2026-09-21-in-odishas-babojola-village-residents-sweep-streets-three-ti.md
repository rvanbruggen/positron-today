In Babojola, a tribal village in Odisha's Mayurbhanj district, cleanliness isn't something that happens once a year - it's woven into daily life. For over 15 years, residents have swept the streets three times daily, banned plastic, and enforced rules against littering for both locals and visitors alike. The whole movement started with one dedicated school teacher, Madhusudan Marandi, who inspired the community to take ownership of their surroundings and formalize their efforts through a village club. What's remarkable is how these practices have transformed from a campaign into an everyday norm, showing that real change happens when infrastructure meets genuine community commitment.
