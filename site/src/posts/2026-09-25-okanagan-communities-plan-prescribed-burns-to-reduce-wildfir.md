After a tough wildfire season, Kelowna is stepping up with planned prescribed burns in places like Knox Mountain to lower wildfire risks. I love that residents are on board, seeing this proactive approach as a smart move. The fire department depends on perfect weather conditions to safely carry out these burns, which help reduce fuel for potential wildfires. And here's the thing - these burns also serve as training for firefighters across B.C., building skills for the future. It’s a thoughtful way to protect the community and prepare for what’s ahead.
