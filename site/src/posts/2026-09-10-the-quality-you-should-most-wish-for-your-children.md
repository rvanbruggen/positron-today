Have you ever thought about what quality you'd want for your kids? Deborah Cobb-Clark suggests that an internal locus of control is the key. This means believing they can shape their own lives rather than leaving it to fate. Studies show that this mindset not only boosts their motivation but also positively impacts their parenting. It's fascinating how this sense of agency can ripple through generations!
