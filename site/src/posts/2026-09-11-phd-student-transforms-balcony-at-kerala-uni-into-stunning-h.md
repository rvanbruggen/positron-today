A PhD student at Kerala University has turned a 50-square-foot balcony into a vibrant healing garden filled with plants and soothing elements. Reshma PR, who studies indoor air-purifying plants, designed this green space to combat indoor pollutants like formaldehyde. Guided by her professor, she chose plants based on a NASA study, ensuring they are both effective and eco-friendly. This delightful garden not only beautifies the space but also promotes relaxation and rejuvenation for anyone who visits.
