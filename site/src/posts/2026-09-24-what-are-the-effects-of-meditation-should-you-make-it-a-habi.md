Meditation is more than just a trendy practice - it helps you take control of your thoughts and feelings by training your mind to observe without judgment. Experts explain that mindfulness meditation can reduce stress, anxiety, and depression, with lasting benefits when practiced regularly. But here’s the thing: meditation isn’t always easy or suitable for everyone, especially those with a history of mental health issues, who should practice under guidance. So, if you’re curious, starting meditation thoughtfully can open a path to greater emotional balance and self-awareness.
