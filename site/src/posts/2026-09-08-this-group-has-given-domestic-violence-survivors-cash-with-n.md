FreeFrom, founded by Sonya Passi, has been transforming the lives of domestic violence survivors through direct cash transfers for a decade. With a mission to end gender-based violence, they provide up to $1,500 to help survivors regain their economic freedom. The results are impressive, with 94% of participants reporting improved social well-being and over half making progress in various life areas. This innovative approach proves that when you trust survivors, they can truly thrive.
