So, a new study shows that just reaching out to friends can really boost your mental health! Researchers found that even a quick chat or a compliment can make a big difference in how we feel. Interestingly, they discovered that the ideal time for a catch-up is about 84 minutes, which seems just right for covering all the good stuff. But here's the thing: even just a few minutes a day can help us feel connected and supported, especially after the isolation many of us felt during the pandemic.
