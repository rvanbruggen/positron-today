Isn't it amazing how necessity drives innovation? Nitin, facing a water shortage in his family's car wash business, embarked on a two-year quest to create a waterless solution. After experimenting with nearly 600 formulas, he developed a plant-based cleaner that uses just 100 ml instead of the usual 25 litres. Now, with 214 franchises across 22 states, his venture not only saves water but also inspires others to think differently about everyday tasks.
