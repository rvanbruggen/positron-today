Greece is making a passionate bid to have Mount Olympus recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site, and it’s about time! This majestic mountain, steeped in mythology and history, has been a national park since 2021 and is already a biosphere reserve. Local officials believe that this designation would not only protect the site but also celebrate its significance for all of humanity. With the World Heritage Committee discussing the nomination soon, there’s a buzz of excitement about what this could mean for the future of Olympus.
