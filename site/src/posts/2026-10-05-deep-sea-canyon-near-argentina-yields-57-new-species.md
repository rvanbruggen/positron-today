I always get thrilled by how much wonder is still hiding in our oceans! Scientists exploring the Mar del Plata Submarine Canyon off Argentina have uncovered a whopping 57 new species, including a sea cucumber playfully nicknamed Loki. Now, this canyon is twice as deep as the Grand Canyon, sitting right where warm and cold currents crash together into a biodiversity hotspot. And here's the thing - the team only examined a tiny fraction of their collected samples to find all these new creatures. Which brings me to the best part: international collaboration is speeding up how we map and protect these fascinating, mysterious deep-sea worlds.
