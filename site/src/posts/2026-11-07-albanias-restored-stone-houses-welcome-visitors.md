I love how Albania’s restored kullas bring the country’s history to life, offering stays in traditional stone tower houses once built for protection and shelter. The new nine-day Tower Trail crosses remote villages, forests, high plains and river valleys in the north-east. Along the way, travellers can meet local families, enjoy home-grown food and discover traditions that are fading as rural communities grow smaller. It’s a welcoming adventure that also gives families a chance to earn income and keep their heritage alive.
