I find it fascinating how dogs like Lucky, a German Shepherd, are trained to detect invasive pests such as spotted lanternfly eggs. At the Penn Vet Working Dog Center, pups start their training young, learning to associate specific scents with rewards. These dogs have been helping humans for decades, sniffing out termites, bed bugs, and now agricultural pests with impressive accuracy. And here's the thing - their incredible noses might soon help fight the return of the devastating New World screwworm. It’s amazing to see how canine skills are being used in such innovative ways!
