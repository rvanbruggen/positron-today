So, there's this amazing story about two sisters in Portugal who are turning their neighbors' plastic waste into cash! Since the launch of the Volta scheme, they've been collecting empty bottles and making plans for exciting trips abroad. Their mom helps them with the logistics, and together they're aiming to collect a whopping 120,000 bottles by next June. It's not just about the money, though; they're also promoting recycling and getting their community involved!
