So here's the thing - human memory is far more unreliable than we'd like to believe, and that's a serious problem when it comes to criminal justice. Over 70 percent of wrongful convictions based on DNA evidence involved mistaken eyewitness identification, which shows just how malleable our memories really are. But new research is bringing nuance to this picture, suggesting that eyewitness testimony isn't completely useless - it just needs to be handled carefully, like any other forensic evidence. Police lineups, leading questions, and even small details like the wording investigators use can all contaminate a witness's memory without them even realizing it's happened.
