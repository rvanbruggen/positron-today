A new observation captures a giant star swallowing its neighbour, offering fresh insight into what happens when stars merge. In this Nature Podcast episode, Benjamin Thompson and Nick Petrić Howe explore the finding and the science behind stellar mergers. The episode also looks at how quantum technology could help pinpoint someone’s location securely. Along the way, it shares research highlights on ocean microplastics and rattlesnake personalities.
