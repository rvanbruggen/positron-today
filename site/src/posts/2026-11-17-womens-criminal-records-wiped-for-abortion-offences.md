Women convicted of abortion offences relating to their own pregnancies in England and Wales will have their criminal records cleared under a historic legal change. The move follows cases such as Nicola Packer, who was acquitted after a five-year investigation and trial following a stillbirth in 2020. Her friend said the arrest and prosecution caused profound trauma, and campaigners have welcomed the change as an important step towards justice. Affected women do not need to take any action themselves.
