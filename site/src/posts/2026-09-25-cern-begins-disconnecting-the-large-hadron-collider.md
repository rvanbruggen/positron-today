So: CERN has started the big job of disconnecting parts of the Large Hadron Collider to prepare for a new era. They’re removing 28 superconducting magnets, including the crucial inner triplets that focus particle beams for collisions. These magnets will be replaced with much stronger ones using niobium-tin coils, boosting magnetic fields by about 40%. This upgrade will especially benefit the ATLAS and CMS experiments, increasing collision rates and giving scientists more data to explore. It’s a thrilling moment marking the handover from old to new technology after nearly two decades!
