So, Health Canada has just given the green light to a new drug called elinzanetant for breast cancer patients suffering from hot flashes and night sweats. This is such exciting news because it directly targets the symptoms that many women face during their cancer treatment. Previously, options were quite limited since hormone replacement therapy isn't suitable for these patients. Now, with this approval, oncologists can offer a more effective solution to help women manage their symptoms and stay on track with their cancer therapies.
