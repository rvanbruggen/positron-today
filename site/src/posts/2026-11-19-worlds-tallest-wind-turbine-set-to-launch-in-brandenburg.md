So: a 365-metre wind turbine in Schipkau, Brandenburg, is set to officially start operating, just three metres shorter than Berlin’s TV Tower. Built by Dresden-based Gicon at a cost of €25 million, the 3.8-megawatt turbine will first be tested in a small wind farm. Gicon says its tower could generate twice as much energy as a conventional turbine, thanks to stronger and steadier winds at greater heights. If the project succeeds, the company hopes to bring 100 such turbines to Germany, potentially adding a new layer of clean energy to existing wind farms without using more land.
