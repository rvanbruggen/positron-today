Imagine tiny robots that are so small they can operate in the microbial world - it's like something out of a sci-fi movie! These innovative machines can hunt down and collect bacteria, making them a game changer for scientists. Powered by light, they use photon recoil to move and steer with incredible precision. This means they can effectively clean microscopic environments, gathering bacteria and depositing them exactly where needed.
