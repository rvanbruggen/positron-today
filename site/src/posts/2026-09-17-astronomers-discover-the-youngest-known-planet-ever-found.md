So here's what got me excited - astronomers have just confirmed a planet called Elias 2-24 b that's less than 1 million years old, which is absolutely mind-blowing. This world is still surrounded by the gas and dust from which it formed, giving scientists a rare window into planet formation happening in real time. The discovery challenges our current planet-formation models because we thought the previous youngest planets were around 5 million years old, and now this newcomer is five times younger. What makes this even more special is that it was found using archived NASA observations with the Keck Observatory's coronagraph in Hawaii - a technique that blocks the star's bright light to reveal faint objects hiding in dusty disks. This discovery helps us understand what our own solar system may have looked like billions of years ago.
