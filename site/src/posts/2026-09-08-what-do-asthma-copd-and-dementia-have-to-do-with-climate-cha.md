A new UN report highlights the undeniable link between global warming and public health. It emphasizes that climate change isn't just about extreme weather but also affects our health, increasing the prevalence of diseases like asthma and COPD. Danish lung professor Ole Hilberg finds the report fascinating and agrees with its conclusions. This connection between our environment and well-being is crucial for understanding the broader impacts of climate change.
