Tiny houses are making their way into several municipalities, offering a climate-friendly and affordable lifestyle. Helga König-Jacobsen, the chairperson of Tiny Varigheden, showcases a new village with 16 tiny houses that promote simplicity. They feature a shared vegetable garden, outdoor kitchen, and even a pizza oven! It's a fantastic way to live in harmony with nature and foster community spirit.
