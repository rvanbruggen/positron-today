Long before Egyptians learned to smelt iron from ore, they shaped rare meteoric iron that had fallen from space. Nine beads found near the Nile, dating to around 3,200 BCE, are the oldest known objects made from this material. Their value shines through in the jewelry they adorned, and in the spectacular meteoric iron dagger buried with Tutankhamun. Researchers also think prized meteoric iron rings found in ancient Greek tombs may have been imported from Egypt.
