I find it fascinating how Azerbaijan honors its fallen soldiers from the 2020 conflict while embracing a hopeful future of peace with Armenia. The peace agreement has sparked new economic opportunities, including the reopening of rail freight routes and digital transit cooperation. This is not just about diplomacy but real, tangible progress on the ground, with goods and data flowing between the two countries. It’s like watching a fresh chapter unfold in a region long marked by conflict, and I’m genuinely excited about what’s next.
