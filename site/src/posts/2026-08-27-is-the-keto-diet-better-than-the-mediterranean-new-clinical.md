A new clinical trial has found that while all three popular diets can help with weight loss, the keto diet shows remarkable health benefits compared to the Mediterranean and plant-focused diets. Participants on the keto diet experienced significant improvements in liver health, including reduced liver fat and better insulin sensitivity. Interestingly, half of those on the keto diet even achieved remission of prediabetes. This study highlights the importance of reducing sugar intake for better metabolic health, which is a game changer for those looking to improve their well-being.
