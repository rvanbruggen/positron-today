Imagine this: cockroaches, yes, those little critters, could actually help save lives in disaster zones! Researchers from the University of Queensland and UNSW have developed cyborg cockroaches that can navigate through rubble and even administer first aid. Equipped with cameras and injection mechanisms, these bugs are designed to assist human rescuers by providing crucial information and medical support. It's a fascinating leap in bio-robotics that could change how we respond to emergencies!
