So, here's the fascinating scoop: tobacco hornworm caterpillars can sense danger without having ears! Researchers are diving deep into how these little creatures hear using sensitive hairs on their bodies. They even studied them in an anechoic chamber, the quietest place on Earth, to understand their reactions to vibrations and sounds. This could not only unravel a mystery of nature but also inspire new microphone technology. Isn’t it amazing how much we can learn from these tiny beings?
