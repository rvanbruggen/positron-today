So, what if a small change to the way you walk could ease knee arthritis pain? In a year-long, placebo-controlled trial, people who learned a personalized foot position reported significantly less pain than those who received the placebo treatment. Researchers also saw encouraging signs that the adjustment may slow cartilage deterioration by reducing pressure on vulnerable parts of the knee. The best foot angle differs from person to person, making personalized guidance key.
