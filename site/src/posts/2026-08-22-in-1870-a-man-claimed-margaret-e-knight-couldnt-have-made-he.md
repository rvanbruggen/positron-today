Margaret E. Knight faced a tough challenge when a man claimed she couldn't have invented her own 'Bag Machine.' This was back in 1870, a time when women weren't seen as mechanical innovators. But Knight didn't back down—she gathered evidence of her work and proved her capabilities. In a remarkable turn of events, the U.S. Patent Office sided with her, granting her the patent for her invention.
