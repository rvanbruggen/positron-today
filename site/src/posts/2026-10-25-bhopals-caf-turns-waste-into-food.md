In Bhopal, the Kachra Café lets people exchange plastic and other waste for food and essentials. So, materials that might otherwise be litter can find their way into recycling instead. The café makes waste sorting feel accessible and shows how small everyday choices can support a cleaner community. It’s a clever reminder that what we call rubbish can still have value when handled responsibly.
