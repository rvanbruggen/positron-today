So: sheepdog auctions in the English village of Skipton are drawing buyers from around the world, all looking for a talented partner to work their flocks. A record was set in 2021, when a one-year-old dog sold for the equivalent of 370,000 kronor, and online bidding is making it easier to buy dogs internationally. Buyers say drones cannot replace skilled dogs, which can cover rough ground quickly and keep sheep moving. For Samantha Furman, excitement got the better of her budget: she paid the equivalent of 320,000 kronor for a dog, with transport expected to bring the total to 400,000 kronor.
