Many animal populations are shrinking, but tigers and tuna are bucking that trend. Their numbers are moving in a more encouraging direction, offering a rare bright spot in a worrying picture. This contrast shows that declines are not inevitable. It is a hopeful reminder of the importance of protecting wildlife and its habitats.
