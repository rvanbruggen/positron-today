So, a fascinating new study from Finland shows that just one sauna session can really boost your immune system! After spending 30 minutes in the heat, participants had more white blood cells circulating in their bloodstream, which are crucial for fighting off diseases. It's like giving your immune system a little workout! While the effects were temporary, this could help explain why regular sauna use is linked to better health.
