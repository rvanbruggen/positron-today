So this retired gentleman in Bengaluru decided he wanted to live completely off-grid - and he actually did it. His home runs entirely on solar power, harvests its own rainwater, and he's even growing his own food with fruit trees like papaya and pomegranate alongside vegetables like spinach, beans, and carrots. Now here's the thing - his electricity bill has dropped to zero, which means he's not just living sustainably, he's living smarter. It's the kind of retirement project that shows you don't need to compromise between comfort and environmental responsibility.
