So, three Danish films have made the shortlist for the European Film Awards, bringing them a step closer to a nomination. They are the feature films 'Kvinde ukendt' by May el-Touky and 'Gæsten' by Mads Mengel, plus Sine Plambech’s documentary 'Rescue'. 'Kvinde ukendt' follows Marie as she tries to hide her past relationship with a German soldier after the Second World War. The film was also chosen last month as Denmark’s Oscar submission, making this a lovely moment for Danish cinema.
