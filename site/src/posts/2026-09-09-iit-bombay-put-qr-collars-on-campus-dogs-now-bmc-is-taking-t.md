IIT Bombay has come up with a fantastic solution for its campus dogs by fitting them with QR collars that provide vital information about their health and history. This innovative idea not only helps identify and care for the dogs but also ensures their safety in a bustling environment. Following this success, the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation is now implementing the same system across Mumbai, which is just brilliant! It’s a wonderful example of how technology can enhance animal welfare and community responsibility.
