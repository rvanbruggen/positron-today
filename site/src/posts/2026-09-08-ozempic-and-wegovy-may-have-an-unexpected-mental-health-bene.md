So, it turns out that GLP-1 medications like Ozempic and Wegovy might do more than just help with diabetes and weight loss—they could also benefit mental health, especially for those with bipolar disorder. Researchers from Griffith University found that people taking semaglutide had significantly lower rates of psychiatric hospitalization. This is quite exciting, as it suggests that these medications could help stabilize mood and reduce relapse risks. However, not all GLP-1 drugs showed the same promise, so further research is definitely needed.
