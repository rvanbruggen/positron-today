So, researchers have discovered that a stress hormone plays a crucial role in brain repair after injury. When brain damage occurs, a specific group of cells called oligodendrocyte progenitor cells springs into action, and they even produce corticotropin-releasing hormone (CRH). This hormone helps regulate the timing of myelin repair, which is vital for healthy brain function. It's fascinating how this process not only aids recovery but also influences brain development throughout life.
