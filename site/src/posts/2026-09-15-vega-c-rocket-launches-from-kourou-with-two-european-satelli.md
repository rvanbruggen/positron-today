So, Europe's Vega-C rocket has successfully lifted off from the Kourou space center in French Guiana, carrying two important European satellites into orbit. This is a really significant moment for European space capabilities - it shows we're maintaining our independent access to space, which matters more than ever these days. The mission demonstrates the reliability and strength of the Vega-C program as a key launcher for medium-sized payloads. And here's the thing - these kinds of successful launches keep Europe competitive in the growing commercial space sector.
