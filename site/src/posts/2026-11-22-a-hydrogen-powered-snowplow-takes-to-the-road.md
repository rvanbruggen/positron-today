So, this winter a hydrogen fuel-cell snowplow will be tested in real winter road operations around Borlänge, Sweden. The project brings together the Swedish Transport Administration, Peab, Maserfrakt and Scania, and marks the first time a fuel-cell snowplow is put to work in these conditions. Scania says it could clear snow for a full day on one tank, which takes just 12 to 15 minutes to refill, and drivers will benefit from a quieter working environment. Hydrogen vehicles still face high costs and limited refuelling infrastructure, but this practical trial will help show where they can make a difference.
