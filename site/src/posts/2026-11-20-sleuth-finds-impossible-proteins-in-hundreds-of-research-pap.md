So: a scientific sleuth has found 230 reports of proteins appearing in cell lines that lack the genes needed to produce them, across 221 research papers. The most common examples involve caspase-3 in MCF-7 cells, BAX in Jurkat cells and STAT3 in PC-3 cells. These patterns could reflect mistaken cell identities, contamination or antibodies that bind to the wrong proteins, while some papers may have been produced by paper mills. Journals are already correcting or retracting affected studies, and researchers say there may be many more to uncover.
