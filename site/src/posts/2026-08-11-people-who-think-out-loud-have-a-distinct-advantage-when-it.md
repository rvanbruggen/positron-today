So, it turns out that talking to yourself out loud might actually make you smarter when it comes to decision-making! Research shows that self-talk helps clarify thoughts and allows you to evaluate them from a fresh perspective. It’s like giving your brain a little lecture, which can lead to better focus and problem-solving skills. So if you’ve been judged for your quirky habit, just remember that you might be onto something great!
