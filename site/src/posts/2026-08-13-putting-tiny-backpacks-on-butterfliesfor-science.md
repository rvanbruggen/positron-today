How do you track the incredible journey of monarch butterflies? By giving them tiny solar-powered backpacks! This innovative project aims to monitor their migration and habitat needs, helping scientists understand how to protect these beautiful creatures. It's fascinating to see how technology can help us learn more about nature, and even citizen scientists can join in the fun with a special app. This project is truly a game-changer for butterfly conservation!
