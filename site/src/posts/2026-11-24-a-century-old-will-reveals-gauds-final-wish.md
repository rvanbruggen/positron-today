A century after Antoni Gaudí’s death, a handwritten document setting out his will has become available to researchers and the public in Barcelona. Gaudí left his possessions, including his Park Güell home and modest bank savings, to the Sagrada Familia so its construction could continue. The 12-page document on display is a historical copy, not the original will, which Gaudí drew up in 1911. It also reveals that he entrusted three close friends with communicating his wishes, offering a fascinating glimpse into his life and the legal customs of his time.
