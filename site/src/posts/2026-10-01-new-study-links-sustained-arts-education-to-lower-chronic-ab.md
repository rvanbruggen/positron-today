I find it fascinating that students who stick with arts education, especially music, tend to show up more consistently at school. This new study from Rhode Island highlights a clear link between sustained arts participation and lower chronic absenteeism rates. Now, it’s not just about showing up but engaging actively, as Danielle Trial Lucini’s music class demonstrates with enthusiasm and dedication. And here’s the thing - while the study can’t prove arts classes cause better attendance, it shines a light on something educators have long believed: arts keep students connected and motivated.
