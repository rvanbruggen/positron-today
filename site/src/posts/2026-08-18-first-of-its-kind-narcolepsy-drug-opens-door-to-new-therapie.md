I’m really excited about the recent approval of the first drug targeting the root cause of narcolepsy, called oveporexton, or Orzeyful. This breakthrough is a game changer for millions who struggle with extreme sleepiness during the day. It’s not just about alleviating symptoms; this drug could lead to new therapies for various sleep disorders. Patients like Tyler Chapman are already experiencing life-changing improvements, and researchers are buzzing with anticipation for what’s next.
