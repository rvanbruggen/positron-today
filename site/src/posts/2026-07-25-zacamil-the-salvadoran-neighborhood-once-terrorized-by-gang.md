Zacamil, a neighborhood in San Salvador, is undergoing an incredible transformation from a site of gang violence to an open-air museum. Artists have been painting murals and creating light installations, bringing new life and hope to the community. Residents like Esmeralda Quintanilla are finding joy in the changes, as the artwork redefines their home. Although the scars of the past remain, this project is a step towards healing and urban renewal.
