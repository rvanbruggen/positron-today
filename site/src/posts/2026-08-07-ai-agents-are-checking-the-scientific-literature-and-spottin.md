It's fascinating to see how AI is stepping in to check the scientific literature and uncovering errors that have been around for decades. A recent study showed that an AI model found inaccuracies in boiling points listed in a long-trusted chemistry database. Researchers like Sebastian Pios are using these tools to not only verify data but also to spot mistakes in older papers. This could really change how we approach scientific research and ensure that the foundations of knowledge are solid.
