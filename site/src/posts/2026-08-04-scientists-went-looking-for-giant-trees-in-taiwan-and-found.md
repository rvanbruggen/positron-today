Taiwan is hiding a remarkable natural world, especially with its giant trees that can reach over 80 meters tall! Since 2014, the 'Taiwan tree seekers' have been on an adventurous quest to find and document these incredible specimens. Their hard work paid off in 2023 when they discovered an 84.1-meter-tall Taiwania fir, now the tallest tree in Taiwan and East Asia. This journey not only highlights the beauty of Taiwan's forests but also showcases the collaboration of scientists and local communities in preserving this unique environment.
