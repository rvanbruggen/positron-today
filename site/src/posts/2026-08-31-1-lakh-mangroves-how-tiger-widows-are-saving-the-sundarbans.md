In a remarkable turn of events, women known as 'tiger widows' are taking action to restore the Sundarbans by planting 1 lakh mangrove saplings. These women, who lost their husbands to tiger attacks, are not just grieving but are actively contributing to the ecosystem that supports their communities. Mangroves play a crucial role in protecting villages from cyclones and sustaining local wildlife, making their efforts vital. This inspiring story highlights how, even in the face of loss, communities can unite to restore nature and create a brighter future.
