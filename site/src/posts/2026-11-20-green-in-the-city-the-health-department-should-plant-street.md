Urban greenery does more than cool streets: it can also support mental well-being. Researcher Aletta Bonn explains how street trees may help prevent depression and encourage people to feel more connected. The conversation makes a striking case for treating trees as part of public health, not just urban decoration. As she puts it, the health department should be planting street trees.
