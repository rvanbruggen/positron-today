In Brussels and Wallonia, some families have chosen to live without a car, opting for bikes, walking, and public transport instead. This lifestyle is reshaping what normality looks like for their children. For the Tillieux family, mornings are filled with playful debates over who gets to sit where on their cargo bike, rather than the usual car-related squabbles. It's a refreshing take on family life that shows how embracing alternative modes of transport can lead to a more connected and active community.
