Anne-Marie Poirier, who has lost her ability to speak because of ALS, can now join family conversations using an AI app that recreates a voice resembling her own. With Vocality, she enters a few keywords and the app turns them into complete spoken sentences. The Montreal-developed tool can also listen to conversations and suggest replies, making communication easier for people who find typing difficult. Poirier says hearing her voice again brings her energy and helps her family feel closer.
