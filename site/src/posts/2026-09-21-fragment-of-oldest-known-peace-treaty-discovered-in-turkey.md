So: I was absolutely fascinated to read about this new discovery in Turkey. Archaeologists have found a fresh fragment of the Kadesh Peace Treaty, which dates all the way back to 1259 BCE. It is just incredible to think that this ancient clay tablet details how kingdoms handled refugees over 3,000 years ago. And here's the thing - it really shows that even in those distant times, leaders were trying to find common ground. Which brings me to the beautiful reminder that protecting human dignity is a responsibility we have carried for millennia.
