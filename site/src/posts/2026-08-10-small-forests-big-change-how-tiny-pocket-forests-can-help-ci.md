Isn't it amazing how small changes can lead to big impacts? Pocket Forests is transforming urban spaces by planting dense native woodlands that not only cool our streets but also boost biodiversity. Take Ballymun, for example, where a once barren lawn has become a thriving pocket forest, fostering community and nature alike. It's a reminder that the best time to plant trees was years ago, but the second-best time is right now!
