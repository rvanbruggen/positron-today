Doug Tallamy, an enthusiastic entomologist, believes that we can tackle the biodiversity crisis right in our own backyards. He transformed his property in Pennsylvania by replacing invasive plants with native species, leading to a flourishing ecosystem. This idea has sparked the creation of Homegrown National Park, encouraging homeowners to cultivate native habitats. With over 52,000 participants already involved, Tallamy emphasizes that these local efforts can create a habitat larger than 10 Yellowstones, connecting wildlife across private lands.
