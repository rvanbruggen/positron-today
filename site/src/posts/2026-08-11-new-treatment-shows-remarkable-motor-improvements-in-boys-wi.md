I’m really excited about this breakthrough! Researchers at Binghamton University have found that an early treatment with Vamorolone can significantly improve motor skills in young boys with Duchenne muscular dystrophy. In just 12 weeks, these boys showed remarkable progress, moving from an average score of 5 to around 8 on the motor skills scale. This could change the way we approach treatment for this condition, making a real difference in the lives of these children.
