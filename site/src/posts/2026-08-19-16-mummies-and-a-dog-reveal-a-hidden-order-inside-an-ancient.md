Researchers have uncovered fascinating insights into an ancient Egyptian tomb, revealing a complex burial order. Inside tomb TT 209, they found 16 mummies and a mummified dog, showcasing how burial practices evolved over centuries. The study highlights a spatial logic in how bodies were arranged, indicating a deep respect for funerary traditions. It's amazing to think about the connections between the individuals and even the dog, reflecting the rich cultural practices of the time.
