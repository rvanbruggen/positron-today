Major cities around the world are experiencing a significant shift away from car dependency toward more sustainable and efficient forms of transportation. So Paris, New York, and Tokyo - three global powerhouses - are all seeing dramatic increases in metro usage, cycling, and walking as primary modes of getting around. And here's the thing: this transformation reflects both changing urban priorities and a growing recognition that cities work better when they're designed for people, not just vehicles. The trend shows how cities can dramatically improve quality of life, reduce congestion, and cut emissions all at the same time.
