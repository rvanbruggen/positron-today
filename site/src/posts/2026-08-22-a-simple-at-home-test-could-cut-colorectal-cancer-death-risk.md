So, researchers from Sweden have found that participating in colorectal cancer screening can lower the risk of dying from the disease by a whopping 43 percent! This exciting news comes from a study that tracked over 376,000 people for up to 14 years. The screening involves a simple at-home stool test that checks for hidden blood, and if detected, further testing is offered. It's amazing to see how a straightforward test can significantly impact lives and encourage more people to participate in screening programs.
