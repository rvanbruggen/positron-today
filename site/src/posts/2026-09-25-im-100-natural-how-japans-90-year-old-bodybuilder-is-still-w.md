Toshisuke Kanazawa, a 90-year-old Japanese bodybuilder, continues to defy age by winning national titles with his impressive natural physique. He trains six days a week, adapts his workouts to suit his body, and follows a strict, clean diet that excludes meat and alcohol. Kanazawa’s dedication started in his youth, overcoming family doubts and setbacks to become a multiple-time champion. His story is a powerful reminder that age is just a number when passion and discipline lead the way.
