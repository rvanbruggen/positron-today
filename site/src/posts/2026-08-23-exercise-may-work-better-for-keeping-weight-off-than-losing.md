So, here's the scoop: while exercise might not be the magic bullet for losing weight, it plays a crucial role in keeping those pounds off once you've shed them. Research shows that even though physical activity has a modest impact on initial weight loss, higher levels of exercise are strongly linked to maintaining that loss. Plus, the health benefits of working out are fantastic, from better cholesterol to improved insulin sensitivity. So, don't give up on your workouts – they’re essential for long-term health and weight management!
