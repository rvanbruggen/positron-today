So, observations offer evidence that supermassive black holes help regulate star formation in their host galaxies. Their jets of plasma affect the gas cloud surrounding a galaxy, just as researchers expected. This influence can help slow the formation of new stars. It’s a fascinating glimpse of how activity at a galaxy’s centre can shape its wider surroundings.
