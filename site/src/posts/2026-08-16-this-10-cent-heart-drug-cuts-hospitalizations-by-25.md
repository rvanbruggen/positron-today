A new study reveals that a low dose of digoxin can significantly reduce hospitalizations for heart failure by 25%. This affordable medication, costing less than ten cents a day, could change treatment guidelines and help many more patients. Researchers found that those taking digoxin experienced fewer complications compared to those who stopped the drug. It's exciting to think about how this could make a real difference in the lives of people struggling with heart failure.
