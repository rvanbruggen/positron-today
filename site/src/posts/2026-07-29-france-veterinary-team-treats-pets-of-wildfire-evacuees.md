A dedicated veterinary team in Bordeaux is doing amazing work by caring for the pets of families evacuated due to wildfires in Gironde. They’ve set up a special area at the Bordeaux Exhibition Centre to provide food and medical support for these animals. It’s heartwarming to see how volunteers and professionals come together to help pets, from dogs to rabbits, during such a tough time. Evacuees have expressed how reassuring it is to have their furry friends close by while they wait to return home.
