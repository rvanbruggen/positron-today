A fascinating study from Ontario reveals how common medications can sometimes lead to a cascade of additional prescriptions for older adults. This happens when a side effect from one drug is misinterpreted as a new health issue, resulting in unnecessary treatments. Dr. Paula Rochon and her team identified 24 potentially inappropriate prescribing cascades that highlight the importance of communication between healthcare providers and patients. It's a reminder to stay informed about our medications and their effects!
