So: Ireland’s hot school meals scheme is under review because many kids aren’t finishing their meals and there are concerns about quality. Now, the idea of adding cold lunch options every day is gaining traction, which could boost fruit and vegetable intake among children. This change might also support local farmers by creating a steady demand for fresh produce. And here’s the thing: parents overwhelmingly want this choice, showing a real appetite for healthier, more varied school lunches.
