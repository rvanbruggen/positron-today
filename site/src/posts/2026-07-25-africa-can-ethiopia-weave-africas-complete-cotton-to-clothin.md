Ethiopia is on an exciting journey to create a full cotton-to-clothing value chain, linking farmers, researchers, and manufacturers. This initiative aims to boost industrialization, generate jobs, and enhance regional trade. Farmers like Yossef Tewelde see cotton as a vital source of income, but challenges remain in accessing quality seeds and stable markets. With the right support, Ethiopia could transform into a leading textile hub in Africa, showcasing its immense potential.
