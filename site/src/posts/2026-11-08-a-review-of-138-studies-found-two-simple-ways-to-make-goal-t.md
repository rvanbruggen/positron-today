Following through on a goal can be tricky, but a review of 138 studies involving nearly 20,000 people found two simple practices that may help. Keeping a written record of your progress and sharing updates with someone else were both linked to greater success. That could be as easy as ticking off workouts on a calendar or sending a weekly note to a friend. The findings don’t prove that both practices work best for everyone, but they offer a practical, low-fuss place to start.
