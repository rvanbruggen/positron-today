This incredible telescope will help us understand dark matter and dark energy, which make up most of the universe but remain a mystery. With its wide field of view, it’s expected to discover around 200,000 new planets and provide insights into how galaxies evolve over time. It’s like having a new pair of glasses to see the cosmos more clearly, and who knows what surprising discoveries await us!
