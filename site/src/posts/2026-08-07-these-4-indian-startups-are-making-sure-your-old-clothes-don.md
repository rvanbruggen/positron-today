So, textile waste is a huge issue in India, but four innovative startups are stepping up to tackle it! They’re finding creative ways to give old clothes a second life, from doorstep pickups to recycling initiatives. One startup, NoKasa, sorts garments for reuse or responsible disposal, while MuddleArt tracks fabric scraps for better management. It's inspiring to see how these companies are making a positive impact on the environment!
