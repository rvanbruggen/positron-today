Scrolling through social media can leave us feeling more agitated than when we started, and research shows this isn't just a personal experience. Algorithms on platforms like TikTok and Instagram often prioritize emotionally charged content, especially negative posts, which tend to spread faster. Interestingly, while negative content can dominate certain discussions, studies indicate that positive posts are still more common overall. This suggests that the issue lies in the design choices of these platforms, which reward our worst impulses in specific contexts. Understanding this can help us navigate our feeds with a bit more awareness.
