So: Norway has just created a huge new marine protected area called Andfjorden, covering nearly 260,000 football pitches! This spot is bursting with life, from coral reefs to kelp forests, and is home to many fish, whales, and seabirds. The government is serious about protecting this treasure while balancing fishing and other activities, thanks to great cooperation with local communities. And here's the thing - this new area boosts Norway’s marine protection from 4.8% to 6%, securing nature’s wonders for future generations.
