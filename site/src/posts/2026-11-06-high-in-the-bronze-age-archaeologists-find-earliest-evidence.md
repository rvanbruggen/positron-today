Could people have been getting stoned in the Bronze Age? Archaeologists have uncovered what may be the earliest evidence yet of cannabis use, pushing the story of the plant’s relationship with humans deep into prehistory. The discovery offers a fascinating glimpse into how ancient communities may have used cannabis, though the exact purpose remains an open question. And here's the thing: a plant we often associate with modern life has a much older human history than many might expect.
