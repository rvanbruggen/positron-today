Have you ever tried writing your name with your non-dominant hand? It can be quite a challenge! A new study suggests that our preferred hand isn't inherently more skilled; it becomes more adept simply because we use it more. The researchers found that practice and the use of tools play a significant role in developing motor skills, showing that handedness is more about preference than innate ability. It's fascinating to think that with enough training, even our non-dominant hand can improve significantly!
