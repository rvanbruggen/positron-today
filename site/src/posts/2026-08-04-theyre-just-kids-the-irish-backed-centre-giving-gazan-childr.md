Ionad Hind Rajab is a heartwarming initiative in Gaza, providing a safe haven for displaced children to learn and play. Founded by Irish artist Adam Doyle, also known as Spicebag, the centre offers education, meals, and psychosocial support to help kids cope with the trauma of war. With plans to expand, it currently serves 93 children, giving them a break from the harsh realities around them. This project is a beautiful reminder of the resilience of childhood amidst adversity.
