It's incredible to see the Salim Ali Birdwatchers Network, a group of young women in Kashmir, come together to revive their local wetland and attract nearly 1 lakh migratory birds! They’re not just cleaning up the environment, but also educating their community about the importance of biodiversity. Alimah, a 13-year-old member, is even writing a book on migratory birds to share with schools. This initiative shows how empowered young women can lead the charge in conservation efforts, making a real difference in their ecosystem.
