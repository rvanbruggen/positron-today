So, it turns out that our understanding of the yin-yang symbol might be completely off! Instead of representing balance between good and evil, it actually symbolizes the natural flow of change. Tang, a YouTuber, explains that yin and yang are not opposing forces but rather different aspects of the same cycle. This fresh perspective encourages us to embrace the motion of life instead of striving for a perfect balance.
