What if a surgeon could use GPS-like guidance while operating on the brain or spine? Bengaluru founders Arpit and Shweta Paliwal created easyNav™, a homegrown system that maps a patient’s anatomy from CT scans and tracks surgical instruments in real time. Designed to work with the equipment Indian hospitals already have, it aims to make advanced surgical technology more accessible beyond major cities. HRS Navigation says its systems are now used in more than 200 hospitals and have supported over 20,000 surgeries.
