I find it fascinating that scientists at the University of Otago are working with a remarkable sediment core from Antarctica! This core, which took three attempts to retrieve, holds clues about how the climate has changed over time. It’s like reading a history book written in layers of rock and mud. By studying this sediment, researchers hope to predict future changes in our environment as the climate warms.
