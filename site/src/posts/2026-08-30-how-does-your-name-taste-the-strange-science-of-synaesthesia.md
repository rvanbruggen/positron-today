Constantina Makridakis, a jewellery designer, thought everyone experienced this until she discovered it was a special ability. The article highlights how synaesthesia varies among individuals, with some perceiving colors in music or shapes in numbers. It's a delightful exploration of how our brains can connect senses in unexpected ways, revealing the beauty of human perception.
