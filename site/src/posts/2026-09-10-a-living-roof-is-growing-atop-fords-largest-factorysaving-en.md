How cool is this? Ford has installed a massive 454,000-square-foot living roof on its Rogue Factory in Michigan, which not only saves energy but also boosts local biodiversity. This green space is home to wild plants and even 20,000 honeybees, making it a haven for birds and insects alike. It keeps the building comfortable year-round and helps manage rainwater, solving past flooding issues. It’s a fantastic example of how industry can harmonize with nature!
