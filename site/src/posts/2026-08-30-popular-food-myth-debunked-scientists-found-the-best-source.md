So, researchers have recently published a fascinating ranking comparing 20 popular protein sources in terms of nutrition, cost, and environmental impact. It turns out that plant-based products, especially tofu and chia seeds, are leading the way with the lowest emissions and resource use. This study not only helps consumers make informed choices but also highlights the significant differences between animal and plant proteins. It's a real eye-opener for anyone interested in sustainable eating!
