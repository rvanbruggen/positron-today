This exciting new trial is set to explore whether an experimental Alzheimer’s drug can prevent the onset of dementia in people who currently show no symptoms. Healthy middle-aged individuals at higher risk will be screened, and those with a specific biomarker will have the chance to participate. The PrevenTRON study aims to shift the focus from treating Alzheimer’s to preventing it altogether, which is a game changer! With the potential to help millions, this research could redefine how we approach this condition.
