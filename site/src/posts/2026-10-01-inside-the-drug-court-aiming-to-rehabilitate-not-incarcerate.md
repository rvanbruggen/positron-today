So: in Auckland, there's a unique court called Te Whare Whakapiki Wairua that focuses on healing rather than punishment for drug-related crimes. Instead of the usual prison sentence, people like Shannan Hill get a chance to break the cycle of addiction and crime through support and treatment. The court opens with karakia and waiata, creating a respectful and hopeful atmosphere. Judge Lisa Tremewan highlights how this approach not only reduces reoffending but also improves family and community health. And here's the thing - despite its success, there are only three such courts in New Zealand, with no current plans to expand.
