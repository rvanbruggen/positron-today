A groundbreaking cancer vaccine developed by Moderna and Merck has shown promising results in reducing melanoma recurrence. This personalized mRNA vaccine, which utilizes the same technology as COVID-19 shots, is the first of its kind to succeed in a late-stage trial. The study, involving around 1,100 participants, demonstrated that those receiving the vaccine alongside immunotherapy had better outcomes than those on immunotherapy alone. This could pave the way for more tailored cancer treatments and even inspire advancements in treating rare genetic conditions.
