It's fascinating how some scientists turn their personal health struggles into a driving force for research. Take Francesca Granata, for example—after years of suffering from a rare genetic disorder, she not only found her diagnosis but also dedicated her career to studying it. This personal connection fuels her passion for finding better treatments, not just for herself but for others facing similar challenges. It's a beautiful blend of science and empathy that showcases the incredible resilience of the human spirit.
