So, a virtual power plant is like a team of your household devices working together to save energy during peak times. Utilities can control things like your smart thermostat or EV charger to help balance the grid, and in return, you get discounts on your bills. It’s fascinating to think that even small adjustments at home can add up to significant savings and help avoid costly upgrades for utilities. If you're curious about joining a VPP, just check your utility's website or your device's app to see if you're eligible!
