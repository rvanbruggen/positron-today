So, it's fascinating to see how some Indian rice varieties are stepping up to the challenge of extreme rainfall! Farmers are turning to indigenous crops that have evolved to thrive in tough conditions, like flooding. For instance, the Kajejaya rice from Karnataka can survive weeks of submersion, which is pretty impressive. This approach not only helps in adapting to climate change but also preserves agricultural diversity, giving farmers more options to rely on. It's a brilliant blend of tradition and innovation!
