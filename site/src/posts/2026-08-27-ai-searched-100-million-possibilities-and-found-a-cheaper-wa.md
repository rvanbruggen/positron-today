Researchers at Washington State University have made a groundbreaking leap by using AI to find a faster and cheaper method to 3D print a high-performance metal alloy. This alloy, known as GRCop-42, is crucial for aerospace applications but has been challenging to print due to its high energy requirements. By analyzing data from previous failed attempts, the AI efficiently narrowed down over 100 million configurations to find feasible printing settings. This innovation not only democratizes access to this alloy but could also revolutionize other scientific fields, like drug discovery.
