Inflation often makes shoppers brace for higher bills, but this headline points to a more encouraging trend: the shopping basket is getting cheaper. That offers some welcome relief in an area people notice every week. It is a reminder that price movements can feel different from one everyday purchase to another. So, even amid the talk of inflation, there is a positive sign at the checkout.
