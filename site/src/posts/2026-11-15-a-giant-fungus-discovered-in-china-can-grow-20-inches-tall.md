So, researchers in China’s Yunnan Province have discovered a remarkable new fungus, named Dianjunus rex, whose hand-like fruiting bodies can grow up to 20 inches tall and weigh nearly five pounds. It belongs to an entirely new fungal family, and DNA analysis suggests it split from other species around 65 million years ago. The team found it growing near the roots of dying trees, though it may also live inside healthy trees before feeding on dead plant matter. Researchers think its towering fruiting bodies could help spread spores through the dense forest, and the discovery hints at just how much fungal life remains unknown.
