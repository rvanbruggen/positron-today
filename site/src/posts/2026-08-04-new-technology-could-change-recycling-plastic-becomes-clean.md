So, researchers have developed a groundbreaking chemical process that transforms a mix of common plastics into high-purity hydrogen fuel. This method, which operates at much lower temperatures than traditional gasification, not only simplifies recycling but also captures carbon in a solid form, preventing CO2 emissions. It's fascinating to see how this alkaline thermal treatment can process PET, PE, and PP plastics all in one reactor without the need for sorting. This innovative approach addresses two pressing global issues: the growing plastic waste crisis and the urgent need for clean hydrogen to decarbonize energy.
