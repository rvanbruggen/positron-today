Scientists in New Zealand are diving into local waters to discover marine organisms that could revolutionize skincare products like sunscreens and anti-aging creams. The Cawthron Institute and Callaghan Innovation are leading this exciting research, which involves collecting samples from pristine environments. They're using cutting-edge technology to identify valuable compounds that could be commercially viable. It's fascinating to think about the untapped potential of the ocean and how it could benefit our skincare routines!
