A new six-legged soft robot from the University of California San Diego can race at more than 15 feet per second, making it the fastest soft robot built so far. It runs without electronics and uses a clever air-powered engine that sends pressurized air through its chambers twice. The 3D-printed robot keeps moving across gravel, grass, concrete and sand, and it can even operate underwater. For now, it needs a tethered air hose, but future versions could use portable power and bring this nimble design to challenging environments.
