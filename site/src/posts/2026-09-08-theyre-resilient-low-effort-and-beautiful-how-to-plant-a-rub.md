So, did you know you can create a garden without traditional soil? Innovative gardeners are turning to leftover building materials like concrete and gravel to cultivate stunning, drought-resistant gardens. This approach not only beautifies spaces but also supports biodiversity, which is crucial as our climate changes. It’s exciting to see how this trend is gaining traction, with experts like Errol Reuben Fernandes leading the way at places like the Horniman Museum.
