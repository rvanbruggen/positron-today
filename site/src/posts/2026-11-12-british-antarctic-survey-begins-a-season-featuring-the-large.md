The British Antarctic Survey is beginning a new season with a landmark effort: the largest penguin census ever. The count will help build a clearer picture of penguin populations across Antarctica. That knowledge can support research into how these remarkable birds are faring in a changing environment. And here's the thing - every careful count adds valuable insight into the region's wildlife.
