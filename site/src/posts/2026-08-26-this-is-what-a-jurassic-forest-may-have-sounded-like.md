Imagine listening to the sounds of a Jurassic forest! Researchers have reconstructed what these ancient sounds might have been like using fossilized insect wings and modern technology. They discovered that some insects could have communicated using ultrasonic calls long before bats existed. It's fascinating to think about how these sounds helped insects thrive in their environment, possibly even evading predators. This study opens up a whole new way of understanding the evolution of communication in the animal kingdom.
