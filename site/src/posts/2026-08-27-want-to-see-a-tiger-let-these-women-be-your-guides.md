It's fantastic to see women breaking into the male-dominated world of safari guiding in India! These trailblazers, like Shahnaz Suleman Baig, are not just leading tours but also changing perceptions about women's roles in wildlife conservation. With programs like ProNat, more women are gaining the skills and confidence to thrive in this field. It's truly inspiring to witness how these women are making their mark and proving that passion for nature knows no gender.
