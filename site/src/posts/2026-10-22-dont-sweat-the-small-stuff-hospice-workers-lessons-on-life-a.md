Hospice workers say end-of-life care is not about giving up, but about comfort, dignity and helping people spend their final days as they wish. They encourage open conversations about death so loved ones can understand and honour someone’s choices. Their work is also a reminder not to postpone meaningful dreams, and to focus on what we can control. Even in grief, they say, death can hold moments of beauty, love and connection.
