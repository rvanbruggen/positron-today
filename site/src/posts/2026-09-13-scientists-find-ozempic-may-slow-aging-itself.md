A fascinating study has revealed that semaglutide, found in Ozempic, may do more than just help with weight loss and blood sugar. Researchers discovered it could actually reduce aging effects and extend lifespan in older mice. This opens up the exciting possibility that GLP-1 drugs might influence the aging process itself, which could lead to a range of health benefits. However, more research is needed to see if these effects translate to humans, but the potential is certainly thrilling!
