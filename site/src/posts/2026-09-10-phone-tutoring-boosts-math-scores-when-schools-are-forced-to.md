So, it turns out that regular phone calls with tutors can really help kids keep learning during school closures. This new evidence shows that students can actually boost their math scores when they have that extra support. Which brings me to the idea that education can adapt in surprising ways, even when faced with challenges. It's a fantastic reminder of how important it is to stay connected and keep learning, no matter the circumstances.
