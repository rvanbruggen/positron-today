So, a recent clinical trial has shown that a ketogenic diet can significantly reduce liver fat by 67% in people with obesity and prediabetes. This is quite impressive, especially when compared to other diets like the Mediterranean and low-fat diets, which only saw reductions of 45%. What's fascinating is that about half of the participants on the keto diet no longer met the criteria for prediabetes after just a few months. This study really highlights the potential of low-carb, high-fat diets for improving metabolic health beyond just weight loss.
