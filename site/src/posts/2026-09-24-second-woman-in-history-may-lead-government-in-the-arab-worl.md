The liberal-centrist Authenticity and Modernity Party (PAM) won the parliamentary elections in Morocco, led by Fatima Ezzahra El Mansuri. She has the chance to become the second woman ever to lead a government in the Arab world, following Tunisia's Najla Bouden. PAM was founded in 2008 by an advisor to King Mohammed VI and promotes social modernization and a secular state. While the king retains significant powers, if he appoints El Mansuri as prime minister, it would be a historic moment for Morocco and the region.
