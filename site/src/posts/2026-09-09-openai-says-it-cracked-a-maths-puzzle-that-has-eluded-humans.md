Wow, this is exciting! OpenAI has announced that it cracked the Navier-Stokes problem, a notorious maths puzzle that has baffled experts for nearly 90 years, in just about 88 hours. This breakthrough, achieved with a new AI model, could change how we understand fluid dynamics, which is crucial for everything from weather forecasting to aircraft design. Mark Chen from OpenAI believes this milestone could pave the way for solving even more complex questions in the future. It's a thrilling time for AI and mathematics!
