On Wednesday evening, millions across Europe will witness a stunning solar eclipse, with a total eclipse visible in parts of Spain and Portugal. This is a rare event, the first total eclipse in mainland Europe in two decades, and it’s a fantastic opportunity for stargazers! Experts advise everyone to use special eclipse glasses to view the phenomenon safely, as looking directly at the sun can cause serious eye damage. So, whether you're in Spain or just catching a partial view in the UK, make sure to find a good spot and enjoy this celestial spectacle!
