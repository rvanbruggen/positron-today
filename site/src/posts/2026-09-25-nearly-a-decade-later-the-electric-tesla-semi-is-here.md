So: after nearly ten years of anticipation, Tesla has officially launched the electric Semi truck, targeting large fleet managers who care about cost and performance. The long-range model offers about 500 miles per charge, which is impressive compared to competitors. And here's the thing - despite challenges like high upfront costs and limited charging infrastructure, Tesla is pushing hard with plans for more megawatt chargers and big orders from major companies like Microsoft and PepsiCo. It’s exciting to see electric trucks gaining real momentum, especially as diesel prices soar and climate concerns grow.
