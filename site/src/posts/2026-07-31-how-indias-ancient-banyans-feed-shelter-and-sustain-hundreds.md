Isn't it fascinating how ancient banyan trees in India are like living ecosystems? These majestic giants not only provide shade but also support a vast array of wildlife, from birds to insects. A recent study highlights their importance as keystone species, reinforcing the idea that protecting them helps sustain entire ecosystems. With some banyans dating back nearly 700 years, they truly are silent witnesses to history and biodiversity.
