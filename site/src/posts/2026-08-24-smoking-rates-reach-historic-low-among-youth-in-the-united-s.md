It's fantastic to see that smoking rates among U.S. youth have hit a historic low of just 1.4%! This is a huge drop from the 36% of high school students who smoked back in 1997. Vaping is also down to 5.2%, the lowest since its peak in 2019, which is a positive trend. While nicotine pouches are gaining popularity, overall, it seems like young people are making healthier choices when it comes to tobacco.
