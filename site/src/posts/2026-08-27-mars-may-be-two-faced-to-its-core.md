Mars is such a fascinating planet, isn't it? With its two distinct hemispheres, one smooth and low, the other rugged and high, it really makes you wonder about its history. A new study suggests that this difference goes deeper than we thought, reaching all the way to its core! Researchers found that the southern hemisphere's mantle is significantly hotter than the northern one, which could explain some of Mars's geological mysteries. It's incredible how much we can learn about our neighboring planet through innovative techniques like tidal tomography.
