NASA is gearing up for an exciting future with the Gateway space station, which will be the first human outpost around the Moon. This innovative hub will support astronauts in their missions and help pave the way for future trips to Mars. Managing lunar traffic will be a complex task, requiring precise calculations and new strategies to keep multiple spacecraft safely loitering near Gateway. It's a fascinating challenge that combines the wonders of space travel with the intricacies of air traffic management.
