So, on November 27, the United Nations will mark its first international day against child marriage. The initiative brings global attention to a practice that can limit children’s safety, choices and future opportunities. It also offers governments, communities and advocates a moment to renew their efforts to protect children and support their rights. And here's the thing: making the issue visible is an important step toward a safer, fairer future for every child.
