So, flowers are not just pretty things we enjoy; they have a fascinating history that has shaped ecosystems! David George Haskell's new book dives into how flowers, like magnolias, transformed the planet through their unique structures and reproductive strategies. It's amazing to think that flowers are both beautiful and complex, actively engaging with pollinators in clever ways. And here's the thing: orchids, which evolved later, took a completely different route, specializing in relationships with specific insects. This whole floral journey is a testament to nature's creativity and resilience!
