Good news for Belgian commuters: train punctuality is improving overall. Now, ULB doctoral student Gaspard Merten has created a tool that lets passengers check average delays for their own train. It complements SNCB and Infrabel’s existing figures by focusing on the traveller’s perspective, rather than the train or route. And here’s the thing: cancelled trains remain numerous, so having more personal, useful information can make a real difference.
