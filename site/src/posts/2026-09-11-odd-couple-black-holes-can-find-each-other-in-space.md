Isn't it fascinating that black holes can form these odd couples in space? Researchers have discovered that many of these pairs are actually the result of hierarchical mergers, where one black hole finds another after a previous merger. This new understanding helps us learn more about how galaxies evolve and the physics of supernovae. It's like a cosmic love story unfolding in the universe, and it’s exciting to think about what else we might uncover!
