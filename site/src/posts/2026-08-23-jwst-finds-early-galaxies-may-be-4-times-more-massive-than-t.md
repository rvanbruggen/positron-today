Astronomers using the James Webb Space Telescope have made a fascinating discovery about early galaxies. They found that these ancient galaxies contain many more small stars than previously thought, making them significantly more massive. This challenges existing theories about galaxy formation and evolution, as some galaxies may be four times more massive than earlier estimates. It's like uncovering a hidden world within the cosmos, and it opens up new questions about how galaxies developed in the early universe.
