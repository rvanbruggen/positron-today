Charmiben treats her cows like family, and now she has Sarlaben, an AI assistant that speaks her language and connects her to Amul’s deep dairy knowledge. So: when her cows aren’t feeling right, she gets tailored advice that’s just for them. It’s amazing to see AI helping farmers care better for their animals, making life easier and more personal.
