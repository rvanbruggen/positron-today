Isn't it amazing how a little creativity can make such a big difference? In Taiwan, volunteers have built tiny bamboo bridges to help migratory crabs safely cross roads during their journey to the beach. This compassionate effort has led to a doubling of the crab population, with 10,000 crabs observed migrating last year. It's a beautiful example of how communities can come together to protect their local environment and wildlife.
