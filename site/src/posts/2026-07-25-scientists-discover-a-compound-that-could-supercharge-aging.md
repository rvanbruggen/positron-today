So, researchers have found a molecule that could really boost muscle repair as we age! This compound, known as LASSS, enhances the signaling of a protein called HGF, which is crucial for muscle recovery. It turns out that LASSS not only protects HGF from damage but also strengthens its ability to bind to receptors. This exciting discovery could pave the way for new treatments to combat muscle deterioration in older adults.
