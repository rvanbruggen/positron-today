So: Danish homeowners are keeping up with their mortgage payments at a record rate. In the second quarter of 2026, just 0.1% of scheduled payments remained unpaid more than three and a half months after their due date - the lowest level ever recorded. That works out to 10 øre unpaid for every 100 kroner owed. Finans Danmark says homeowners’ generally strong finances and careful lending assessments help keep arrears so low.
