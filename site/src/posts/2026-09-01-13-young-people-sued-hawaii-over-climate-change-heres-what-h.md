So, a group of 13 young people in Hawaii took a bold step by suing the state’s Department of Transportation over climate change issues. This lawsuit led to the creation of the Hawai‘i Youth Transportation Council, where these passionate youths now share their ideas on public transit and bike lanes. The settlement requires the DOT to decarbonize transportation by 2045, which is a huge win for the environment! It’s inspiring to see young voices making a real difference in their community.
