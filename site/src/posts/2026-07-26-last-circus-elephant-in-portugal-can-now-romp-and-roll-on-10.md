What a heartwarming story! Julie, the last circus elephant in Portugal, has finally found her forever home at the Pangea Elephant Sanctuary after 40 years of performing. She made the move from her circus grounds to this beautiful 1,000-acre sanctuary, where she can now enjoy a peaceful retirement. It's amazing to think that she will be joined by another elephant soon, giving her a chance to socialize and thrive in a natural environment. This sanctuary is truly a beacon of hope for elephants in need!
