Isn't it fascinating? Researchers have discovered two unknown human ancestors lurking in our DNA, adding to the rich tapestry of our genetic history. This exciting finding shows that Homo sapiens interbred with various archaic humans, not just Neanderthals and Denisovans. The study reveals that one ghost ancestor contributed about 1% of our genome, dating back over 50,000 years. It's a reminder of how interconnected our evolutionary past really is!
