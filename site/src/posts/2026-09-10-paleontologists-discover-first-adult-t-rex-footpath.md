This is such an exciting discovery! Paleontologists have found the first known trackway made by an adult T. rex in North Dakota, consisting of four footprints over a 23-foot-long path. Each footprint is about three feet long, showcasing the massive size of this apex predator. What's fascinating is that the dinosaur was likely strolling at a leisurely pace, similar to a human power-walking. This find not only adds to our understanding of T. rex behavior but also highlights the rarity of such fossilized tracks.
