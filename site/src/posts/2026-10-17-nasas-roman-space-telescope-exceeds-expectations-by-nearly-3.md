So, NASA’s Roman Space Telescope is already delivering image quality nearly 30% better than scientists predicted. After testing its detectors, filters, calibration system and other components, engineers fine-tuned the telescope’s optics to bring them into near-perfect alignment. That sharper vision will support Roman’s sweeping surveys and help scientists investigate dark matter, dark energy and other cosmic mysteries. It’s exciting to see such a powerful observatory begin its mission with performance already exceeding expectations.
