I find it fascinating that a pill originally designed for rheumatoid arthritis can help regrow hair in people with severe alopecia areata. So: after just 24 weeks of treatment, patients already notice a clear improvement, which is really encouraging. Now, this breakthrough offers hope for many who have struggled with hair loss for a long time. And here’s the thing - it’s not just about hair, it’s about restoring confidence and joy in everyday life.
