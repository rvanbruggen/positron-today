Farah Pandith brings young people together with former extremists who share honest stories of what drew them in and how they found a way out. After decades studying extremism and visiting more than 100 countries, she has learned that prevention starts with listening closely and understanding people’s search for belonging, recognition and certainty. She believes local communities must help shape solutions, because trust cannot be built from a distance. Now, she is putting those lessons to work to make compassion a practical force for connection.
