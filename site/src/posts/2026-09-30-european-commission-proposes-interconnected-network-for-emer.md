So: the European Commission wants to create a secure communication system that connects emergency services across Europe. This means police, firefighters, medical teams, and civil protection can share data, photos, and locations instantly, even across borders. Currently, their systems don’t talk to each other well and can fail at borders, which is a big problem in crises. The plan is to upgrade to high-speed tech like 5G and use satellites as backups, aiming for rollout starting in 2030. And here's the thing - it’s all about saving lives by making sure Europe’s first responders act as one.
