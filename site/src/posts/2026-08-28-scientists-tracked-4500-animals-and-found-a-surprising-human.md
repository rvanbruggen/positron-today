So, researchers have uncovered some fascinating insights about how wildlife responds to human presence. By tracking over 4,500 animals and combining that data with cellphone location info, they found that animals react differently based on both species and the level of landscape alteration. This is a big deal because it means we need to consider not just how we change habitats, but also how our mere presence affects wildlife. It's an exciting step towards better conservation strategies that take all these factors into account!
