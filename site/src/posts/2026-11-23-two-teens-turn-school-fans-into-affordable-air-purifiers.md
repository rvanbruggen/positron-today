So: Delhi teenagers Krish Aggarwal and Samarth Meattle have created Project Vayu, an affordable filter that turns ordinary classroom fans into air purifiers. The balloon-shaped filter fits onto table or wall fans in under a minute and costs about Rs 140, making it a practical option for schools with limited budgets. Its charged fibres capture fine particles, and reported tests found PM2.5 levels fell by over 90% in a small room within an hour, with results varying by room size and conditions. By making existing equipment work harder, the young innovators hope to help more children breathe cleaner air at school.
