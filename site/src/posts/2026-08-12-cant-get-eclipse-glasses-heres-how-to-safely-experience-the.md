So, if you missed out on eclipse glasses, don't worry! There are safe ways to enjoy the solar eclipse without risking your eyesight. You can visit one of the six public observatories in Flanders to use telescopes with solar filters. And if you're feeling creative, try making a pinhole projector to safely view the eclipse's projection on a surface.
