What an inspiring story from Turkiye! A dedicated group of conservationists and fishermen, with support from an NGO, has transformed 300 miles of coastline into a thriving marine paradise. They faced challenges like illegal fishing and dwindling marine life, but with a $3 million grant, they created no-fishing zones and trained local marine rangers. This effort has led to a remarkable tripling of protected seascapes, showcasing the power of community engagement in conservation.
