There's a fascinating debate happening about whether nations should prioritize economic growth or focus on sustainability for future generations. Recent reports emphasize the need for wealth rebalancing and managing economies with a broader set of indicators beyond just GDP. It's all about ensuring that today's actions don't compromise the well-being of tomorrow's citizens. This perspective on intergenerational justice is crucial, as it challenges us to think about what we owe to the future.
