Central European University is open to expanding its programmes in Budapest after Hungary’s new government pledged to restore academic freedom. The university moved its main activities to Vienna in 2019 following pressure from Viktor Orbán’s government, but it still has 312 staff in Budapest. Rector Carsten Q Schneider says any expansion must protect the student experience, and the university has no plans for a wholesale return from Vienna. He sees a stronger Budapest presence as a powerful symbol of resilience and academic freedom, while stressing that the decision rests with the university’s board.
