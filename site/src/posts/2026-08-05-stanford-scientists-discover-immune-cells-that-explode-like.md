Stanford researchers have made an exciting discovery of a new type of immune cell that literally explodes to destroy nearby cells. These cells, called ruptoblasts, were found in planarian flatworms, which are fascinating creatures known for their regenerative abilities. The researchers observed that when these cells respond to a specific hormone, they trigger a rapid and complete self-destruction process, leaving no trace behind. This explosive mechanism could provide valuable insights for developing new medical treatments.
