So, it's fascinating how NASA's food safety innovations have reshaped US food inspection! Back in the early 1900s, the government reacted to shocking meatpacking conditions by establishing the Food Safety and Inspection Service. But as the demand for beef skyrocketed, the old methods just couldn't keep up, especially with the rise of E. coli outbreaks. NASA's approach to food safety, particularly the HACCP system, has been a game changer, focusing on preventing issues before they start rather than just testing after the fact.
