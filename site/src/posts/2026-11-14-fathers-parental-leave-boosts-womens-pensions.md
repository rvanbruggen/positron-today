New figures show that Denmark’s reserved parental leave for fathers, introduced in 2022, is already shifting the financial balance between women and men. By taking more leave, fathers can help reduce the pension gap women often face after having children. Julia Vollertsen and Peter Fredsbo had agreed to divide leave as equally as possible even before the policy came in. Peter noticed a change in attitudes between the leave they took for their first child in 2020 and their second.
