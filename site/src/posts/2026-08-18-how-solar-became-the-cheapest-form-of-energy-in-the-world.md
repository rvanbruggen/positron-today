It's amazing to think that just over four decades ago, solar energy was a novelty, but now it's the cheapest form of electricity on the planet! President Carter's vision back in 1979 has really come to fruition, showing us how far we've come. With technological advances and increased efficiency, solar panels are now not only affordable but also incredibly effective. Last year alone, solar power generation surged, proving it's not just a trend but a vital part of our energy future.
