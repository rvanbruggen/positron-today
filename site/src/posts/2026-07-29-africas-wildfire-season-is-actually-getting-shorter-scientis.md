So, it turns out that Africa’s wildfire season is getting shorter thanks to a changing climate! Researchers found that increased rainfall is delaying the dry season, which means less land is burning during those peak months. This is fascinating because it suggests that while the wet season isn't longer, rain is falling later, keeping the land moist for a bit longer. This could really help in managing fire risks across the continent, making it a positive shift for the environment.
