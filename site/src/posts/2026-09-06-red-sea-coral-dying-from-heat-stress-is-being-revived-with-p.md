It's fantastic to hear that scientists have successfully revived Red Sea coral suffering from heat stress using probiotics for the first time in open waters! This innovative approach not only helps the corals themselves but also restores their vital microbiomes. The results showed significant improvements in coral health, even during a natural marine heatwave. This research opens exciting new possibilities for coral conservation and highlights the importance of understanding how to enhance coral resilience.
