So, it turns out that the monthly fuel budget for gasoline cars has skyrocketed recently! With a jump of 25 euros, drivers are now spending around 137 euros a month, which is nearly three times what electric car owners pay for charging. This is largely due to rising oil prices linked to geopolitical tensions. Meanwhile, electric vehicle costs have remained stable at about 46 euros, making them a much more economical choice these days.
