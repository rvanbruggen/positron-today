So: a game about Kinshasa’s famously lively traffic has become an online sensation, with more than 400,000 downloads since its release this month. In Moto Wewa, players take on the role of a motorcycle-taxi driver, weaving through the city, picking up passengers and negotiating fares along a 16-kilometre route. Congolese developer Johnny Andedi built the game from local experiences, so players can recognise their own city and its everyday rhythms. And here's the thing: it’s also a heartfelt tribute to the drivers, and a celebration of the stories African creators can tell for themselves.
