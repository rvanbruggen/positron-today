I’m really excited about this breakthrough! An international team has found evidence of a moonlike body orbiting a brown dwarf 73 light-years away, which is the first of its kind outside our solar system. This discovery challenges previous assumptions, as it shows that moons can exist around these massive celestial objects. The findings, published in Nature, suggest this exomoon has a mass at least 0.9 times that of Jupiter, making it a significant find in the field of astronomy.
