Every monsoon, India’s grasslands come alive with the incredible lesser florican, the world’s smallest bustard. Male floricans put on a spectacular aerial display, leaping nearly two meters high to impress females and mark their territory. This breathtaking performance happens in small territories across the grasslands, where they can repeat their dance hundreds of times a day. The lesser florican thrives in shared landscapes, using fields and pastures alongside native grasses, making their breeding grounds a unique blend of nature and agriculture.
