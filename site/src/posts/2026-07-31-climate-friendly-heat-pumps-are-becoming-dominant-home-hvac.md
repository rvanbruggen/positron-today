The rise of heat pumps in the U.S. is truly exciting, as these innovative HVAC systems are set to replace traditional heating methods. Last year, 46% of new homes were built with heat pumps, showing a clear shift in consumer preference. They not only reduce greenhouse gas emissions but also utilize renewable energy, making them a win-win for the environment. With sales doubling over the past 15 years, it's clear that heat pumps are becoming the go-to choice for efficient home heating and cooling.
