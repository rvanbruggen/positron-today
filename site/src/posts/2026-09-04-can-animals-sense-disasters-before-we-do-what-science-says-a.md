It's fascinating to think that animals might sense disasters before we do! In India, people have observed ants moving their eggs to higher ground before heavy rains, and this behavior is part of a rich folklore. But science reveals that animals are picking up on physical signals like vibrations and changes in pressure, rather than predicting events. This intriguing connection between animal behavior and environmental changes opens up a whole new perspective on how we understand nature.
