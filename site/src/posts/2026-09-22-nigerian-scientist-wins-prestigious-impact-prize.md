I am so thrilled to share that Edu Inam, a brilliant chemistry professor from Nigeria, has just won the inaugural Schlumberger Foundation Impact Prize. She created a fantastic platform called LabEquipment.ng to help researchers find functional scientific gear across the country, which is just such a smart solution to a real problem. It is basically a searchable database that connects students and scientists with the tools they need, saving everyone a lot of frustration. And here's the thing - this prize is going to help her expand the platform even further, making the whole research ecosystem in Nigeria much more connected. I love seeing innovation like this that makes science more accessible for everyone.
