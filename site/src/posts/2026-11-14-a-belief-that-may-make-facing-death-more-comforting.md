So, a 2026 study suggests that believing you’ll be remembered after death may offer more comfort than believing in an afterlife. Among Israeli adults, this sense of symbolic immortality was associated with fewer mental health difficulties and lower levels of substance use and behavioral addictions. Belief in an afterlife, by contrast, showed only modest links with some outcomes. The researchers point to meaningful connections, self-esteem, and feeling part of something bigger as ways to build a legacy that can ease worries about mortality.
