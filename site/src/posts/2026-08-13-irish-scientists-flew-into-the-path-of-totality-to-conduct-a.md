A team of Irish scientists took to the skies during a solar eclipse to observe this rare phenomenon from a unique vantage point. They flew three hours west of Ireland in a specially equipped plane, capturing crucial data about the Sun's corona. Astrophysicist Dr Laura Hayes expressed her excitement, noting that despite initial weather concerns, they enjoyed a clear view just before totality. The mission was a significant success, providing valuable insights into solar dynamics and the magnetic field.
