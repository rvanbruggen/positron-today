At 17, Arnav Maharshi is building AI-powered rehabilitation tools after a life-changing accident left him paralysed at 14. Following a coma and critical injuries, he spent months in intensive physiotherapy and occupational therapy, gradually rebuilding his independence and confidence. During his recovery journey, he noticed that progress measurements relied too much on subjective feedback rather than concrete data. So he decided to create technology that could track rehabilitation improvements precisely - turning his personal struggle into a tool to help countless others facing similar challenges.
