More and more people are seeking refuge from the hustle and bustle of cities, drawn to places where the only light comes from distant stars. Astrotourism, a unique blend of travel, science, and nature, is experiencing a remarkable renaissance. This trend is fueled by a desire to escape artificial light, the need for digital detox, and a thirst for educational exploration. Observing the night sky not only reduces stress but also fosters connections among people, creating communities united by their awe of the cosmos.
