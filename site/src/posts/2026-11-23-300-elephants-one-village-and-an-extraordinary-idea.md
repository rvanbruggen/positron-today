In Hatikhuli, Assam, elephants once faced fear and hostility as they crossed village farms. So, 90-year-old conservationist Pradip Kumar Bhuyan, Binod Dulu Bora and local residents found a more hopeful approach: feed, don’t fight. Villagers now plant food for elephants, map their migration routes and work to create safer corridors. More than 300 elephants pass through each year, showing how people and wildlife can share the same landscape.
