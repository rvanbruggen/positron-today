Madhya Pradesh is not just about big cats; it’s home to 772 Indian peninsular wolves, the highest number in India! This fascinating species thrives in the state's open landscapes, which provide the perfect habitat for them. Interestingly, these wolves coexist with tigers in some areas, creating a unique dynamic in wildlife conservation. Researchers are actively studying how these two apex predators share their space, which is a thrilling development for wildlife enthusiasts.
