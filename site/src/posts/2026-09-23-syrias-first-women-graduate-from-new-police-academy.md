So: around 150 women have just graduated from Syria's brand-new police academy after six months of intense training. This is a big step for Syria as it rebuilds its security forces and boosts women's roles in policing. The graduates learned everything from military skills to law and fieldwork, which is pretty impressive. And here's the thing, one of them, May Aklajneh, has dreamed of this since she was a kid. Now, these women are set to join police units all over the country, which feels like a fresh, hopeful start.
