Wow, what exciting news! The Port of Churchill is back in action, resuming grain shipments for the first time in six years. A ship is currently being loaded with 30,000 tonnes of wheat durum, headed for Europe. This marks a significant moment for Churchill, which is being recognized again as a vital Arctic gateway. Plus, there are plans for year-round shipping, which could really transform the region!
