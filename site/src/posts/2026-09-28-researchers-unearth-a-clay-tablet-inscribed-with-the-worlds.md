So, I just read about this incredible discovery in Turkiye where archaeologists found a fragment of the Peace of Kadesh. It is actually the world's oldest peace treaty, dating back to around 1,300 BCE after a massive chariot battle between the Egyptians and the Hittites. And here's the thing, the language on the tablet is just beautiful, pledging eternal friendship and non-aggression between these two ancient civilizations. It really makes you think about how long humans have been striving for peace, doesn't it? I find it absolutely fascinating that we are still uncovering these pieces of our shared history today.
