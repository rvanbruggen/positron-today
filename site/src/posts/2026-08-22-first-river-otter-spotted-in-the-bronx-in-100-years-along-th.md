Can you believe it? A river otter has been spotted in the Bronx for the first time in a century! This exciting discovery along the Bronx River shows just how far conservation efforts have come in revitalizing this iconic waterway. Local scientist RJ Hawkins captured the moment on a motion-activated camera, and it’s a testament to the hard work of many dedicated individuals. It’s a real win for both wildlife and the community, reminding us that nature can bounce back when we invest in it.
