So, after four years of dedicated work, Giotto's stunning frescoes depicting the Stories of Saint Francis have been beautifully restored at Santa Croce in Florence. This project not only revitalizes the artwork but also enhances our understanding of Giotto's innovative techniques and expressive style. It’s fascinating to see how the restoration clarifies the differences among the various painters who contributed under Giotto's guidance. And here's the thing: this restoration coincides with the 800th anniversary of Saint Francis's death, making it a truly special moment for art lovers and history enthusiasts alike.
