Audrey Tang challenges Silicon Valley’s vision of a future where AI takes over every human task. She imagines a diverse garden of technologies that help people work together while keeping communities in control. Her ideas include open-source AI that runs on personal devices, citizen assemblies that help shape AI policy, and social media designed to build connection. So: technology can strengthen cooperation instead of driving people apart.
