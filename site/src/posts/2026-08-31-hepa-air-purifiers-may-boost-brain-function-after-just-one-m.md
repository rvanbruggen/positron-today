So, it turns out that using a HEPA air purifier for just a month can actually improve brain function in adults over 40! This fascinating study highlights how these purifiers, which filter out harmful particulate matter, can lead to a 12% faster performance in mental flexibility and executive function tests. Now, while that might sound small, it’s comparable to the cognitive benefits gained from regular exercise. It’s a great reminder that even minor changes in our environment can have a positive impact on our mental health.
