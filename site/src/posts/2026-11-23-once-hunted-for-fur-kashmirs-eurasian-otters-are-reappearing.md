After years of being rarely seen, Eurasian otters are appearing again in Kashmir’s waterways. A new study brings together recent videos and field evidence, including otter droppings, tracks and sightings along several rivers. The findings build on earlier research in Hirpora Wildlife Sanctuary and point to the species’ presence across parts of the valley. It’s encouraging news for freshwater ecosystems, though researchers say the evidence does not yet confirm a population recovery or show how many otters live there.
