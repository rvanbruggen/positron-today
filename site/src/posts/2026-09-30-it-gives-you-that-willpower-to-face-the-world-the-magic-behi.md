Leith Freeman recently received her fourth prosthetic eye after losing one to melanoma 14 years ago. The process of creating a prosthetic eye is detailed and artistic, involving careful painting to match the healthy eye perfectly. Dr Keith Pine explains that prosthetic eyes are more than aesthetics—they help people regain confidence and ease social interactions by reducing awkward stares. The journey includes support from medical professionals and loved ones, highlighting the emotional and psychological importance of these devices.
