New wildlife crossings are set to make it easier for otters to move through Flanders. The passages take account of a curious habit: otters often prefer to travel on dry ground. By offering a safer route alongside waterways, they can help otters move between habitats and avoid dangerous crossings. It is a practical boost for the region’s otter population and local biodiversity.
