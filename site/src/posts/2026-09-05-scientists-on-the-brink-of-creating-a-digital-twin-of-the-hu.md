Researchers from the University of Warwick are exploring the creation of a synthetic copy of the human brain, which could offer tremendous benefits across various fields. This digital twin could facilitate ethical neuroscience experiments and help in personalizing medical treatments for patients. Additionally, it could enhance AI systems by improving their perception and adaptability. However, the challenge lies in accurately mapping the complex connections of the human brain, which far exceeds simpler models like that of the C. elegans worm.
