So, Kolkata-born physicist Ashoke Sen has made history as the first Indian to receive the Oskar Klein Medal, honouring his influential work in theoretical physics. His pioneering research in string theory and black hole physics has helped deepen our understanding of the universe. Sen also received the $3 million Fundamental Physics Prize in 2012, yet he still cycles to work. And here's the thing: his story shows how curiosity, dedication and a love of ideas can matter more than outward displays of success.
