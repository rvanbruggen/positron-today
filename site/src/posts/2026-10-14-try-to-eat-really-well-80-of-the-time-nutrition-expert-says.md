So, here's some welcome good news: nutrition expert Christopher Gardner says healthy eating is simpler than the noisy online debate makes it seem. Drawing on decades of research, he explains that scientists often agree on practical advice, even when every detail has not yet been studied. His new book, Food Sense, aims to help people make realistic, science-backed choices without fearmongering or miracle-food claims. And here's the thing - aiming to eat really well most of the time can be a more helpful approach than chasing perfection.
