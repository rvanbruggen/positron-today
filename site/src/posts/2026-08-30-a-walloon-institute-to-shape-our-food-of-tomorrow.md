So, there's some exciting news from Wallonia! The Wallonia Institute of Food Science and Technology has just been launched, bringing together researchers to innovate our food systems. With a focus on sustainability and health, this new virtual institute unites 60 senior researchers from five Belgian universities and several research centers. It's all about creating a greener and healthier plate for the future, which is just fantastic!
