Before we’re even born, our brains are being built through a fascinating process involving radial glia, these incredible stem cells that play a crucial role in forming our cerebral cortex. Recent studies have shown that these cells not only respond to nutrients but also to signals from other brain regions, shaping how they decide which types of cells to produce. It turns out that metabolism is a key player in this decision-making process, influencing brain development in surprising ways. This research opens up exciting possibilities for understanding brain disorders and how our environment impacts brain health.
