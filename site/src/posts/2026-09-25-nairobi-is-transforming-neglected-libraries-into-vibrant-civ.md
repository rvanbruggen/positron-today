I love how Nairobi’s old libraries are getting a fresh start as lively civic hubs where people can work, learn, and connect. What used to be dusty, forgotten spaces like Eastlands Library are now buzzing with activity, offering free internet, meals, and community programs. Book Bunk, the nonprofit behind this, is not just fixing buildings but reshaping access to knowledge and culture for everyone. And here’s the thing: these libraries are becoming essential places for career building, creativity, and inclusion in Nairobi.
