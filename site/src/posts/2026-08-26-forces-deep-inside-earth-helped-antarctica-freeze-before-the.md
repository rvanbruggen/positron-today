Scientists have uncovered an exciting explanation for why Antarctica became icy long before the Arctic did. Their research reveals that the gradual uplift of East Antarctica created the perfect conditions for ice to form, even when the planet was warmer. This fascinating process began millions of years ago as tectonic forces shaped the landscape. It's amazing to think that the East Antarctic Ice Sheet, the largest on Earth, owes its existence to these geological changes!
