You know how our minds can spiral into worst-case scenarios? It’s like a little switch flips, and suddenly we’re imagining the sky is falling! But here’s the good news: there’s a simple trick to help us regain control. By writing down our anxious thoughts, we can challenge them and replace them with more realistic ones. It’s all about taking a step back and realizing that those thoughts don’t have to dictate our reality.
