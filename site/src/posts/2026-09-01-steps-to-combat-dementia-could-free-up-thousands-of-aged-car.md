So, here's the exciting news: tackling dementia now could really make a difference in the future by freeing up thousands of aged care beds! A recent report highlights the urgent need for reform in the aged care system, especially as the population ages. The author, Dr. Etu Ma'u, emphasizes that nearly half of the residential beds are occupied by people with dementia, which is a significant driver of demand. By focusing on prevention and healthier living, we could see a substantial reduction in dementia cases and, in turn, alleviate pressure on the aged care sector.
