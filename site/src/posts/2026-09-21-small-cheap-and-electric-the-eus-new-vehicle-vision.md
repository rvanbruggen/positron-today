So, the EU is working on something really interesting - a whole new category of vehicles that are smaller, cheaper, and electric. Manufacturers across Europe are already developing different proposals to meet these new requirements and expectations for what cars will look like on our roads in the future. And here's the thing - it's not just about making cars more affordable, it's about reimagining what urban mobility could be. This shift could genuinely change how Europeans think about getting around their cities and towns.
