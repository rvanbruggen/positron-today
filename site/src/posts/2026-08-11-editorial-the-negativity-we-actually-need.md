Recently, a friend pointed out that my project might be doing more harm than good by filtering out negativity. I totally get his point, especially regarding climate change and other pressing issues that deserve more attention. But here's the twist: I see Positron as essential for cutting through the noise of unimportant bad news that overshadows the truly significant stories. The data shows that while political drama and celebrity gossip dominate, crucial topics like climate and AI are often drowned out, which is exactly why Positron needs to exist.
