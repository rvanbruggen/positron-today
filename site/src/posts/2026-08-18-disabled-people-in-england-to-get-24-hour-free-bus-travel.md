Starting in April, disabled individuals in England will enjoy the freedom of 24-hour free bus travel, which is such fantastic news! Previously, they could only use their bus passes during limited hours on weekdays, making it tough to get to work or attend events. This change, benefiting around one million people, is all about breaking down barriers and making life easier. Prime Minister Andy Burnham is leading the charge, and it's a huge win for the disabled community!
