At Mumbai’s Museum of Solutions, children are learning about AI by experimenting, asking questions and even breaking things to see how they work. Through the AI Explorers programme, they build digital basics, train machine-learning models and take on coding challenges. Between July 2025 and June 2026, the initiative reached 9,087 children, trained 80 teachers and partnered with schools and community groups across six Maharashtra districts. And here’s the thing: it’s making sure a child’s chance to explore new technology isn’t determined by their circumstances.
