So, Japanese researchers are planning to build a 12-meter telescope in Antarctica, and it could potentially detect up to 10 million distant galaxies hidden by dust. This telescope, called the Antarctic 12-m Terahertz Telescope, will help us understand the early evolution of the universe. With its wide-angle cameras, it aims to spot galaxies that existed less than a billion years after the Big Bang. It's exciting to think about how this project could reshape our understanding of cosmic history!
