Singapore is taking an innovative approach to fight dengue by releasing 10 million specially bred male mosquitoes each week. These aren't just any mosquitoes—they carry Wolbachia, a bacterium that prevents their eggs from hatching when they mate with wild females. This strategy has already led to a remarkable 90% reduction in Aedes mosquito populations in some areas. With dengue cases on the rise in places like India, could this unique method be a game-changer in controlling the disease?
