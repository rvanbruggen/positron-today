NASA's latest mission to rescue the Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory has faced a setback, as the probe designed to save it has been canceled due to technical issues. This satellite, launched in 2004, has been crucial for observing cosmic events like supernovas and black holes. Now, as it continues to descend, the Swift team is determined to maximize its observations before it reenters the atmosphere, possibly by the end of this year. It's a bittersweet moment, but there's still excitement about what data it might collect in its final days.
