New discoveries at the Hohle Fels cave in Germany are shedding light on the artistic abilities of ancient humans. Two tiny bird carvings, dating back 40,000 years, have been found, crafted from ivory and showcasing remarkable skill. Excavation leader Nicholas Conard discusses how these artifacts might reveal insights into the culture and success of our ancestors. It's fascinating to think about how art played a role in their survival and social connections.
