Wow, researchers have uncovered hundreds of ancient earthworks in the Amazon that date back to the Aquiry civilization! Using advanced remote sensing technology, they found 396 new structures, suggesting there could be up to 30,000 of these fascinating designs hidden in the region. These geoglyphs, which are massive geometric shapes, might have been used for ceremonial purposes, but their exact role is still a mystery. This discovery could change how we view the population and history of the Amazon before European colonization.
