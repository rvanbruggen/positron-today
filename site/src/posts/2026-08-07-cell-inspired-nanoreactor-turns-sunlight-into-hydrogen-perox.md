Researchers have developed a fascinating nanoreactor that mimics the functions of living cells to produce hydrogen peroxide using sunlight. This innovative design features a dynamic redox pair and a compartmentalized structure, enhancing the efficiency of chemical reactions. Under visible light, it achieved impressive photosynthesis rates, showcasing the potential of nanocell engineering. This breakthrough opens exciting avenues for artificial photosynthesis and sustainable energy solutions.
