So, a team at Virginia Tech has found a brilliant way to turn PVC, one of the toughest plastics to recycle, into a valuable engine lubricant! This innovative method could tackle two big environmental issues: reducing plastic waste and creating sustainable lubricants. By breaking down PVC, which is often discarded, they’ve developed a process that produces a thick oil perfect for engines. It’s exciting to see how science can help us recycle better and meet the growing demand for eco-friendly products.
