A midwife is speaking out against the harmful beauty standards that social media perpetuates around women's bodies, particularly vulva ideals that bear little resemblance to reality. She's pushing back on the pressure women feel to conform to narrow, unrealistic expectations - which can cause real distress and body image issues. So here's the thing: by normalizing the natural diversity of women's bodies, we can help people feel more confident and comfortable in their own skin. Her message is simple but powerful - there's no single 'ideal' body, and that's perfectly healthy and beautiful.
