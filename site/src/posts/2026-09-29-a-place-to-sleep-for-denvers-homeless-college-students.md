So: Denver just opened its first student residence for homeless college students through Depaul USA’s Dax Program, and I find that incredibly inspiring. This program offers a safe place to live for up to two years, helping students focus on their studies without worrying about where to sleep. The building, a charming 1890s brick duplex, can house up to 15 students and partners with local universities to expand its reach. And here's the thing - the program also provides meals, workshops, and support services, showing real care for these students’ independence and success.
