So, bispecific antibodies are really making waves in the cancer treatment world! These innovative drugs can latch onto two different proteins at once, which is quite a game changer. In just five years, the number of approved bispecifics has skyrocketed from 3 to over 20, generating a whopping $18 billion in sales. Researchers are even exploring multispecific antibodies that target three or more proteins, which could lead to even more effective treatments. It's an exciting time for cancer research, and I can't wait to see where this leads us!
