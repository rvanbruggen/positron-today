So, on September 3, 1967, Sweden made a monumental switch from left to right-hand driving, a change that involved 2 million drivers! This event, known as Dagen H, was not just a logistical feat but also a fascinating study in human behavior and resistance to change. Despite initial fears and a previous referendum against the switch, the Swedish parliament pushed ahead, leading to a well-planned transition. With catchy songs and widespread advertising, the country embraced this new direction, proving that change, while daunting, can be achieved with creativity and community spirit.
