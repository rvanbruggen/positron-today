So, researchers have finally identified the Milpe robber frog, a species that was hiding in plain sight for over a hundred years! This frog is not rare at all; in fact, it thrives across 16 million acres in the Chocó forests of Ecuador and Colombia. What's fascinating is its loud mating call, which sounds just like a smoke detector — how quirky is that? This discovery really emphasizes how important it is to correctly identify species and connect them with their natural populations.
