So, I was just reading about this fascinating new study from Stockholm University, and it really changes how we look at history. It turns out that when archaeologists find adults and children buried together in medieval graves, they aren't always related by blood. DNA analysis shows that social or religious factors were often the real reason for these shared resting places. And here's the thing - it also reveals that children were treated with the same gender-based burial customs as adults, which is just incredible to think about. It really shows how science can open up a whole new window into the lives of people from the past.
