Laureen Meroueh is shaking up the steel industry with her innovative furnace that cuts emissions by at least half and reduces costs by 25%. This new method simplifies the steelmaking process by using natural gas instead of coal, which is a huge win for the environment. With plans to expand production significantly by 2030, Hertha Metals could be a game changer in making steel production more sustainable. Meroueh's journey from a young engineering enthusiast to a startup founder is truly inspiring and shows how passion can lead to impactful change.
