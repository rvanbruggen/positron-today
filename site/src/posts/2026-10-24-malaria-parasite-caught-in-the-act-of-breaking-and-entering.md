Researchers have captured Plasmodium, the parasite that causes malaria, as it slips into a red blood cell. Using detailed imaging, they revealed the temporary molecular junction the parasite builds to open a tiny passage through the cell membrane. And here's the thing: the parasite reshapes the membrane so the opening disappears behind it without a trace. This discovery could help guide future malaria treatments and research into other parasitic diseases.
