So, scientists have discovered that the thunder we hear from lightning can actually create seismic waves, which they call 'thunderquakes.' These waves can help map the underground structures, similar to how X-rays work for our bodies. Using fiber-optic cables, researchers turned them into thousands of vibration sensors to capture these waves. This innovative approach could revolutionize how we monitor subsurface conditions without any drilling involved.
