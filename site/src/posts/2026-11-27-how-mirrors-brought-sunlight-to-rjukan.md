So, Rjukan sits deep in a Norwegian valley where the surrounding mountains block direct sunlight from September to March. Since 2013, three computer-controlled mirrors on a nearby hillside have reflected sunlight onto the town square, giving residents a bright spot to gather. The system was rebuilt in 2023 with a reliable power line and remote controls, which can now direct the light to other places around town too. It’s a clever bit of engineering that has brightened daily life and brought curious visitors to this small community.
