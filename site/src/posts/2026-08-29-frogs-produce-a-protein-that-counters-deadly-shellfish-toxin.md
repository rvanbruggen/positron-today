Isn't it fascinating that the American bullfrog can produce a protein that acts as an antidote to a deadly shellfish toxin? Researchers at UC San Francisco discovered that this protein, called saxiphilin, can neutralize saxitoxin, which causes paralytic shellfish poisoning. This breakthrough could have significant implications for public health, especially for communities that rely on shellfish harvesting. The study shows that this natural protein can effectively prevent and even reverse poisoning, which is just incredible!
