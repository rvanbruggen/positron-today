Scientists are making exciting progress towards a universal vaccine for Streptococcus pneumoniae, the bacterium responsible for pneumonia and meningitis. With over 100 subtypes of this bacteria, current vaccines often miss the mark, leaving many vulnerable. The new approach focuses on targeting shared proteins, which could provide broader protection. This development is a hopeful step forward in the fight against these serious infections.
