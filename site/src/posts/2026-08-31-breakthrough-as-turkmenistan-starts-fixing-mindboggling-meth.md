Turkmenistan is making strides by plugging massive methane leaks from its aging oil and gas facilities, which is a fantastic breakthrough! Methane is a powerful greenhouse gas, and stopping these leaks can have a quick and significant impact on global warming. The UN has praised this initial effort, noting that while eight leaks have been fixed, there's still much work to be done. It's exciting to see how satellite data is helping to drive real action in addressing these environmental issues.
