Hungary is taking steps to revise its LGBT+ laws that were enacted during the Orban government's tenure - which brings me to say this is a significant moment for LGBTQ+ rights in the country. The government appears ready to reconsider some of the more restrictive policies that had been put in place over the years. So this shift suggests a growing recognition that these laws may need updating to better reflect contemporary values and protect vulnerable communities. It's encouraging to see a nation working toward more inclusive legislation.
