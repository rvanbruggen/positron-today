New research reveals that the so-called American cheetah, Miracinonyx trumani, was actually a close relative of the puma, not a cheetah at all. This fascinating study shows how these animals adapted to diverse environments, including Arctic regions where they relied on fish for survival. The findings also extend their known range significantly, highlighting their role as versatile predators in various habitats. It's amazing to think how evolution can lead to similar traits in unrelated species due to environmental pressures.
