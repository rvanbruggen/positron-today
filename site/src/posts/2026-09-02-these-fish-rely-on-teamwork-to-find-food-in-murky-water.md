Three-spined sticklebacks are fascinating little fish that adapt their swimming behavior to find food in murky waters. When the water gets cloudy, they take longer to reach their meals, but those in larger groups respond more quickly overall. It's incredible to see how these fish increase their cooperation and coordination when visibility is low. This study highlights their ability to thrive even in challenging conditions, which is just a testament to the wonders of nature.
