Eilish Rothney, a nature reserve manager, found herself sharing her office with a wasp nest and decided to embrace the situation instead of evicting them. Over time, she’s grown fond of her buzzing colleagues, appreciating the intricate beauty of their lives. Rothney encourages a more tolerant view of wasps, emphasizing their role in nature rather than labeling them as pests. As autumn approaches, she reflects on the bittersweet departure of the wasps, feeling a sense of motherly affection towards them.
