So, researchers have taken a deep dive into the genome of the phage ΦX174, and wow, the results are fascinating! By systematically mutating nearly every nucleotide, they discovered that many mutations were harmful, but the reasons behind some of them remain a mystery. Even advanced AI struggled to predict these effects, highlighting the need for more experimental data. This study not only challenges our understanding of a well-studied virus but also pushes the boundaries of what we know about AI in biology.
