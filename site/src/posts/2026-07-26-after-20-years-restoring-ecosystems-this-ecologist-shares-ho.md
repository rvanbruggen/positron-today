Sohail Madan's journey from Silicon Valley engineer to wildlife conservationist is truly inspiring. He believes that every balcony and backyard can play a role in restoring nature, which is so important given the alarming decline in bird and insect populations. His work with the WildTales Foundation focuses on bringing back native plants and creating habitats for butterflies and birds. It's a reminder that we can all contribute to a healthier environment, no matter how small our space may be.
