Swedish hop growers face challenges, but the Jönsson family remains enthusiastic despite a tough season and low demand. Hampus Olofsson, a brewer, finds it exciting to brew with Swedish hops, praising their unique aroma and taste. The Jönsson family harvests three hop varieties, with cascade performing best despite heat and disease issues. Although Swedish hops are not yet widely used, their local origin and quality offer a fresh, authentic option for brewers eager to support small-scale producers.
