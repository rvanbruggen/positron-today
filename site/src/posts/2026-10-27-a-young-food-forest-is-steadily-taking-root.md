Near Arnhem, the Young Food Forest Farmers are turning former grassland into a food forest designed to produce fruit, nuts and vegetables for years to come. Around 20,000 trees have already been planted, with 5,000 more set to go in this winter, though it will take time before much of the harvest arrives. This summer brought some tense moments, and the project is a reminder that food forests need care and attention rather than simply being left to grow. Across the Netherlands, food forests are gaining ground, with new networks and knowledge-sharing helping the movement develop.
