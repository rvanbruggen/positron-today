In the village of Hatikhuli Ronghang in Assam, farmers faced a daunting challenge with wild elephants raiding their crops. Instead of viewing these magnificent creatures as enemies, the community decided to embrace a new approach. They began growing fruits and Napier grass specifically for the elephants, transforming fear into friendship. This innovative idea, known as Hati Bondhu, has not only protected crops but also fostered a harmonious relationship between humans and elephants.
