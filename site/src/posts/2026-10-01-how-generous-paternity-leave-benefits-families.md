So, generous leave for new fathers can make a meaningful difference well beyond those first few weeks at home. Research links paternity leave with better maternal mental health, stronger father-child bonds, improved child health, and longer breastfeeding. It can also help share caregiving more fairly, easing the mental load on mothers and supporting a healthier family balance. And here's the thing: paid leave can benefit a family’s finances, too, while giving parents precious time to bond and find their feet.
