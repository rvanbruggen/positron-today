In the village of Kulak, the sun has transformed daily life by providing 24/7 electricity through solar panels. This shift not only powers homes but also supports local agriculture, allowing farmers to store their harvests and manage their resources better. It's a fantastic example of how energy transitions can be sustainable when local communities are empowered to maintain their systems. This approach highlights the importance of decentralised solutions in the global climate conversation, especially for rural areas facing energy crises.
