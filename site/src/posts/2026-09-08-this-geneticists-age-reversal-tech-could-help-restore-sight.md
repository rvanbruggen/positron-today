Yuancheng (Ryan) Lu is on a fascinating journey to tackle aging and vision loss. His groundbreaking work in gene therapy has shown promising results in restoring sight in mice, and now it's moving into human trials. The excitement around his treatment, known as ER-100, is palpable, with many believing it could change the future of medicine. Lu's innovative approach to reprogramming genes is not just about reversing age—it's about giving hope to those facing vision loss.
