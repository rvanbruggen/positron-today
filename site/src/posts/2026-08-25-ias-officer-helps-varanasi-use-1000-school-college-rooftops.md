It's amazing how one IAS officer, Himanshu Nagpal, is transforming Varanasi's approach to groundwater management! By utilizing over 1,000 school and college rooftops for rainwater harvesting, he’s not only addressing water scarcity but also engaging local companies to fund these systems. This innovative solution is a win-win, as it helps both educational institutions and businesses. Plus, the revival of the Nad river is a fantastic bonus, improving health in nearby communities. Talk about making a difference!
