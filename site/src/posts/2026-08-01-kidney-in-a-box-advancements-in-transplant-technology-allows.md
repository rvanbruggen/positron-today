A groundbreaking technology is transforming kidney transportation between donors and recipients. The XVIVO Kidney Assist Transport, recently approved in Canada, is being used for the first time at the University of Alberta Hospital in Edmonton. This innovative device introduces oxygen into the kidney's circulation, significantly improving recovery outcomes. Patients like David Cliplef are already experiencing faster recoveries, allowing them to return to their normal lives much sooner than before.
