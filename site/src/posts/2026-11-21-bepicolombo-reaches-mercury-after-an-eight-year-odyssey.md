After an eight-year journey, BepiColombo has reached orbit around Mercury, becoming only the second mission to do so. In the coming weeks, its European and Japanese probes will separate and move into distinct orbits. The Japanese MIO will study Mercury’s magnetic field and its interaction with the solar wind, while the European MPO will examine the planet’s surface from a lower orbit. Along the way, the mission also tested its pioneering X-ray camera by observing the Crab Nebula.
