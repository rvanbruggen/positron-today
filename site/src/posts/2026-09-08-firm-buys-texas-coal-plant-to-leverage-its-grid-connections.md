I find it fascinating that Panamint is investing $1.7 billion to build the Big Rooter solar project right next to the Twin Oaks coal plant in Texas. This move aims to utilize the existing grid infrastructure to deliver a whopping 1.2 gigawatts of solar power to millions of Texans. It’s part of a larger trend of transforming old industrial sites into renewable energy hubs, which is just brilliant! While some may raise eyebrows about the coal plant's future, it’s exciting to see such innovative approaches to energy production.
