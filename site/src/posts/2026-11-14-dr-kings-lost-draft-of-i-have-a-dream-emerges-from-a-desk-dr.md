So: a handwritten draft of Martin Luther King Jr.’s iconic “I Have a Dream” speech has resurfaced after more than 60 years. Xernona Clayton discovered the seven yellow pages in her late husband’s desk drawer, and their many handwritten edits offer a rare glimpse of King shaping his words. The draft ends with “Free at last,” though it does not include the famous “I have a dream” passage, which King improvised at the Lincoln Memorial. Christie’s will auction the historic manuscript for charity, with public displays planned in Atlanta, Chicago, and New York.
