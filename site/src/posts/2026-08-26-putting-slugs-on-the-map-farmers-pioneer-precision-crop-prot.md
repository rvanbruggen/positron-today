So, slugs have been a real headache for farmers, but a new mapping tool is changing the game! This innovative tool helps pinpoint where these pesky molluscs are likely to strike, allowing farmers to use pesticides more efficiently. Thanks to a network of 'slug sleuth' farmers, the project has already led to a 50% reduction in slug pellet usage in pilot tests. It's exciting to see how this could save farmers millions while also respecting their invaluable knowledge of the land.
