For the first time since the Iran war began, senior leaders from countries facing regional conflicts met in Baku for the CICA summit, hosted by Azerbaijan. President Ilham Aliyev brought together Israeli President Isaac Herzog, Iranian deputy foreign minister Kazem Gharibabadi and other Middle Eastern and Asian representatives, including China’s Wang Yi. The forum aims to build dialogue and trust, with regional security, transport links, digital cooperation and sustainable growth on the agenda. Herzog also met Aliyev and Bahrain’s foreign minister, highlighting ties with Azerbaijan and Bahrain and the value of cooperation for regional stability.
