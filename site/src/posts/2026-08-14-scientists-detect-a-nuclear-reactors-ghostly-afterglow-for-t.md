It's fascinating to think that even after a nuclear reactor is turned off, there's still activity happening inside! Scientists from the Double Chooz collaboration have successfully measured the lingering emission of antineutrinos, which are incredibly elusive particles. This breakthrough could revolutionize how we monitor nuclear reactors, enhancing safety and oversight. Their findings provide a new way to gather information about reactors even when they're not operational, which is just so exciting!
