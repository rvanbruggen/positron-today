Air quality in Europe is on the rise, with significant drops in emissions from major pollutants. The latest report from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service shows that industrial and road transport emissions have decreased impressively over the past decade. For instance, sulphur oxides from industry have plummeted by 59%, while nitrogen oxides have dropped by 39%. This positive trend highlights the effectiveness of air quality policies and technological advancements in creating a cleaner environment.
