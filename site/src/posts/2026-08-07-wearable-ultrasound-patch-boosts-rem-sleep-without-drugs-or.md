Researchers at the University of Texas at Austin have developed a soft wearable patch called NEUSLeeP that enhances REM sleep without the need for medication or surgery. This innovative device uses gentle ultrasound stimulation while monitoring brain activity, allowing it to target deep brain regions involved in sleep. In a study, participants experienced REM sleep 43 minutes sooner and stayed in that stage for 16 minutes longer, with minimal side effects reported. The potential benefits for emotional health and stress adaptation are exciting, paving the way for future trials in treating sleep disorders.
