So, a cleverly layered fabric called Plant Armor more than tripled strawberry yields in field trials. Draped over the plants, it keeps insects out while still letting sunlight, rain, and air through. The cover also provides gentle warmth that can help plants reach the fruiting stage sooner, without harming their health. And here's the thing: it could help growers use fewer pesticides and less water.
