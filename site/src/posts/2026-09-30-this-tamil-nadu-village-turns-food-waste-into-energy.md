I find it fascinating how the village of Kanjirangal in Tamil Nadu is turning food waste into something truly valuable. They convert biodegradable waste into biogas and electricity, which powers streetlights and battery-powered vehicles. And here's the thing - the leftover material becomes nutrient-rich manure, closing the loop beautifully. It’s a simple yet powerful example of how waste can have a second life, inspiring us to rethink our own cities’ waste.
