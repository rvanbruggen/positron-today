Now, a federal judge has stepped in and ordered the US government to bring him back, release him, and respect his rights. This is a huge deal because it challenges ICE’s policy of sending migrants to third countries without proper notice or due process. And here's the thing - Sánchez could become the first person returned after these controversial deportations to Africa. It’s a hopeful sign that justice can prevail even in tough immigration battles.
