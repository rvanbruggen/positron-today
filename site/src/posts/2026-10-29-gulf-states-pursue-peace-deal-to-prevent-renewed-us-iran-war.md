So, Gulf states are working on a multi-part plan to prevent a renewed US-Iran war, with Iran offering to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within four days of sanctions being lifted. The discussions also focus on reducing Houthi forces in Yemen and addressing Saudi demands, with Iran showing signs of a willingness to help mediate. The US is looking for Iran to make a further concession, potentially allowing UN weapons inspectors back into the country and access to bombed nuclear sites. Alongside these steps, regional leaders are promoting a broader security pact that could bring Iran and the Gulf states closer and support lasting peace.
