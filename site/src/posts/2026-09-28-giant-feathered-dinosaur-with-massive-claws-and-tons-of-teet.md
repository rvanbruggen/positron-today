So: have you ever heard of the therizinosaurus? These creatures are just fascinating, looking like a strange mix between an ostrich and a grizzly bear, but with claws that could reach over three feet long! Now, paleontologists have just discovered a new, even bigger species called Kayrasaurus changliensis in China. And here's the thing - this dinosaur was built for biting, sporting a unique jaw full of tightly packed teeth that sets it apart from its cousins. It is just incredible to think about these massive, feathered giants roaming the Earth millions of years ago.
