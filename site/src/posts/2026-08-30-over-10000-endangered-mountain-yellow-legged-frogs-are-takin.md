It's fantastic news for the mountain yellow-legged frogs in California! After years of decline, conservationists have reintroduced over 10,000 of these endangered amphibians back into their mountain habitats. This effort is part of a long-term mission to restore their populations, which have faced threats like disease and habitat loss. With dedicated monitoring and support, these frogs are getting a second chance to thrive in the wild.
