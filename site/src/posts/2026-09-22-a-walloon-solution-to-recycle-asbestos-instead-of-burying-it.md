I am absolutely thrilled to share some fantastic news coming out of Ghlin today. A local company called SBMI has developed a brilliant process that neutralizes the dangers of asbestos while turning it into a useful raw material for cement production. So: instead of just burying this hazardous waste, we are now looking at a circular economy solution that actually helps the environment. It is truly inspiring to see such innovation right here in our own backyard. And here's the thing - this kind of breakthrough gives me so much hope for a cleaner future.
