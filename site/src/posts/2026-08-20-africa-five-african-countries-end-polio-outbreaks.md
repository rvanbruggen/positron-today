It's fantastic news that five African countries have successfully stopped the spread of polio! This milestone is a testament to the hard work of health workers and strong national leadership. The World Health Organization confirmed that transmission has been interrupted in Burundi, Ghana, Guinea-Bissau, Republic of Congo, and Uganda. While this is a huge achievement, we must remain vigilant to prevent future outbreaks and ensure that all children are protected.
