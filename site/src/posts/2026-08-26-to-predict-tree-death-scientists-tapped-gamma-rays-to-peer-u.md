In a fascinating twist, researchers have found that gamma rays can help predict tree mortality by revealing soil depth. After a severe drought in Western Australia, they discovered that areas with shallow soils were more prone to tree die-offs. This innovative method could be a game-changer for understanding forest health, as it allows scientists to see below the surface without invasive techniques. It’s exciting to think about how this could apply to forests worldwide, potentially transforming our approach to conservation.
