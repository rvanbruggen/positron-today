I find it fascinating that the largest rewilding site in England is taking shape in Northumberland! The Restore company has acquired the 7,500-acre Emblehope Moor with a vision to revive its natural beauty and biodiversity. They’re not just focused on profits; after paying back investors, they plan to hand the land over to the local community. This project could set a new standard for land management, blending capitalist and community interests in a way that really excites me.
