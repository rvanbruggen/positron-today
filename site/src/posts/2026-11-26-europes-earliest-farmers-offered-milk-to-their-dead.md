What a fascinating glimpse into the beliefs of Europe’s earliest farming communities. Archaeological evidence suggests they placed milk with their dead as an offering. The practice hints that dairy foods may have held meaning beyond everyday nourishment. And here's the thing: even a simple vessel can reveal how people cared for the dead and imagined life beyond it.
