Narwhals are fascinating creatures, especially known for their long, spiral tusks that can reach up to 10 feet! Scientists have recently discovered that these tusks grow in a unique way, with a left-handed helix on the outside and a right-handed one inside. This unusual structure keeps the tusk straight, unlike other tusked animals. Researchers are now eager to explore more about how these tusks can reveal the narwhals' life history, which is crucial since observing them in the wild is quite challenging.
