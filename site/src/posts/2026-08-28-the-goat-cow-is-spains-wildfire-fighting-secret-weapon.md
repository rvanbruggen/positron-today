In Galicia, wildfires have become a serious threat, but a community in Vincios is turning to an unexpected ally: the Cachena, or 'goat-cow.' These hardy animals are being reintroduced to graze on flammable vegetation, helping to reduce the risk of fires. Since their introduction in 2023, the community has seen no serious fires, which is quite promising! This innovative approach is gaining traction in Spain as a sustainable way to manage wildfire risks.
