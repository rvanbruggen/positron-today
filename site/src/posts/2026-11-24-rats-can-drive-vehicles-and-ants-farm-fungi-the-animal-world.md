So, zoologist and science communicator Joanna Bagniewska has turned the medieval bestiary into a lively, science-based guide to animals, ecology and evolution. Her book explores remarkable behaviours, from ants farming fungi underground to the astonishing abilities of other creatures. And here’s the thing: scientists have described fewer than 1.5 million animal species, while estimates suggest millions more may still be undiscovered. Bagniewska also makes the case that every species deserves protection, whether people find it beautiful or unsettling.
