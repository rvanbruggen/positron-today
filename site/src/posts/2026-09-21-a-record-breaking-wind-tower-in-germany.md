I am absolutely thrilled to see this new development in Schipkau, Germany, where a record-breaking wind tower is currently under construction. It is a telescopic lattice tower that reaches incredible heights, allowing it to capture much stronger and more stable winds than ever before. And here's the thing - this design is a total game changer because it can deliver more than double the energy of a conventional turbine. It is so exciting to see a former coal region transforming into a hub for such cutting-edge renewable technology. Which brings me to the best part: this project is part of a hybrid system that will help stabilize our energy supply for the future.
