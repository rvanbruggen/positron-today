So, here's the thing: a 1960s ex-council house in south London is staying cool without air conditioning, even during heatwaves! Marion Baeli, the homeowner and architect, has transformed her home into a Passivhaus, which keeps the temperature stable and reduces energy consumption. With clever insulation and airtight design, her house is 10C cooler than the outside on a scorching day. It's amazing to see how retrofitting can lead to such significant savings and comfort, proving that you can beat the heat sustainably.
