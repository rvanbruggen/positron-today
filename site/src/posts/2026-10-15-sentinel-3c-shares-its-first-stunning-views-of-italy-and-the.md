So, just two weeks after launch, ESA’s Sentinel-3C satellite has sent back its first colorful views of Earth, including Italy, the Canadian Arctic and the Caribbean. Its instruments also measured the height of Greenland’s ice sheet and the sea surface. The satellite will help monitor oceans, ice, land and the atmosphere, supporting work on everything from marine heatwaves to wildfires and drought. Sentinel-3C is still being prepared for service, but these first images are already a promising start.
