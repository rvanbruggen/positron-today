What an inspiring story! Paola García, a passionate escaramuza rider, is leading the charge for gender equality in charrería, Mexico's national sport. Since founding Más Mujeres Charreando, she's been empowering women to break free from traditional roles and claim their space. The recent recognition of the first all-female charro team by the Mexican Federation is a huge milestone, opening doors for women in a sport long dominated by men. It's a beautiful reminder that progress is possible, even in the face of deep-rooted traditions.
