So, this winter, a new artificial breeding wall for sand martins is being built along the Lommelse Sahara. Athletes participating in paid events at national park Bosland are helping to fund this fantastic project. The wall aims to provide a safe nesting place for these birds, whose natural habitats are dwindling. It's a great initiative that not only supports wildlife but also raises awareness among visitors about their impact on nature.
