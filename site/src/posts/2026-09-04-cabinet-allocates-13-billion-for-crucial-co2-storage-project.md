So, the Belgian government is setting aside 1.3 billion euros for the Aramis CO2 storage project, which could be one of Europe's largest decarbonization initiatives. Now, this project has faced some challenges, especially with rising costs and uncertainty from industrial partners. The aim is to transport CO2 from major factories to empty gas fields in the North Sea, potentially storing over 400 megatons of CO2. With this funding, the government hopes to lower transport costs and encourage more industries to participate.
