So: Zottegem is launching a fantastic project where every 12-year-old in the city will get free CPR lessons at school. This is part of the Hartveilig Zottegem initiative, aiming to teach both kids and adults how to save lives. The lessons start now with children, and by 2027, free courses will also be available for all residents. And here's the thing - quick CPR can dramatically increase survival chances during a cardiac arrest, so this is really important stuff!
