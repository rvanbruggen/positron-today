Researchers have discovered a promising new therapy for autism spectrum disorder by targeting a specific brain receptor. This innovative approach restores NMDA receptor function, which is crucial for cognitive processes like learning and memory. Remarkably, the treatment showed benefits even in adult mice, suggesting that brain dysfunction can still be addressed later in life. The results are exciting, as they indicate a potential new direction for treating neurodevelopmental disorders.
