So, what really makes a city safe for women? The NARI 2025 index reveals that community trust and active policing can create a safer environment, as seen in cities like Kohima and Aizawl. It's fascinating how social connections often outperform physical infrastructure in ensuring safety. Plus, the Northeast showcases a model worth emulating, where women's voices are integral to local governance. This is a hopeful reminder that safety is not just about laws but about people looking out for each other.
