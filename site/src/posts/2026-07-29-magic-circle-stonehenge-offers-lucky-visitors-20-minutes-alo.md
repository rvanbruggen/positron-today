So, here's the exciting news! Throughout August, visitors to Stonehenge have a chance to win 20 minutes of solitude among the ancient stones, which is quite rare. English Heritage is running a draw for those who buy advance tickets, allowing one lucky person each day to experience the monument without the usual crowds. Imagine standing there, soaking in the history and the peaceful atmosphere, while the sounds of nature surround you. It’s a unique opportunity to connect with this iconic site in a way that few get to experience!
