I am so excited about The Whale, a new museum opening in Norway on June 4, 2027! It’s designed to feel like you’re inside a massive whale, using immersive art and storytelling to explore everything about these magnificent creatures. Located in Andenes, a prime spot for whale watching, the museum will feature life-size sculptures and an incredible soundscape room where you can hear whale calls. Plus, it will highlight the important relationship between humans and whales, making it a must-visit for nature lovers!
