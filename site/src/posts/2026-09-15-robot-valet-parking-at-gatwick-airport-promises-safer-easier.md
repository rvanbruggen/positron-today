So here's what's happening at Gatwick - they've got these French-made robots from Stanley Robotics that can park your car with incredible precision, right down to the centimetre. The system can handle about 95% of vehicles, though there are some size and weight restrictions to keep in mind. Beyond just being clever tech, passengers really appreciate the security aspect - the whole area's got fencing, floodlighting, and 24-hour patrols, plus number plate recognition tracking everything. The service costs a bit more than standard parking, somewhere between £5 to £10 extra, and Gatwick's already working on relaxing those initial restrictions around minimum stays and operating hours.
