Wow, this is big news! New Mexico has just banned all new uranium mining projects on over 13 million acres of state land. Commissioner Stephanie Garcia Richard made this decision to protect the environment and public health, emphasizing that the risks of contamination far outweigh any financial benefits. It's a bold move, especially with the recent push for uranium extraction in the state, and it shows a commitment to cleaning up abandoned sites instead. I really hope this sets a precedent for other states to follow!
