So: climate activists and wetland charity WWT have installed floating gardens along Bristol Harbour, creating a wildlife corridor through the city. Built from recycled, recyclable, and biodegradable materials, the platforms are planted with more than 20 native species that offer shelter for birds and pollinators. Beneath the water, plant roots can provide refuge for marine life, help clean the water, and regulate its temperature. And here's the thing: a baby gull already used a ramp to climb to safety, showing how quickly these welcoming habitats can make a difference.
