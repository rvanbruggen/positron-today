In Assam, India is launching its first 'vulture restaurant' to help critically endangered vultures find safe food. These feeding stations are crucial as natural food sources diminish due to various threats like poisoning and habitat loss. Dr. Sachin P Ranade explains that while diclofenac was banned, vultures still face many challenges today. This initiative aims to support their recovery and ensure these vital scavengers can thrive once more.
