So, here's the exciting news! An American company has successfully printed nerve implants using bio-ink from a Ghent-based firm aboard the International Space Station. This groundbreaking achievement allows cells to grow together, marking a significant step for medicine in space. With the weightlessness of space making 3D printing easier, these implants could pave the way for future medical applications in upcoming space missions. It's like something out of science fiction, but the potential benefits are very real!
