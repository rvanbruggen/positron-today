How exciting is this? Scientists in London have created what they claim is the world's first fully biodegradable party balloon! Unlike standard balloons that can take years to break down and harm the environment, this new balloon, called Bioloon, biodegrades in just nine months. It's not only eco-friendly but also just as affordable and easier to inflate. This innovation could really change the way we celebrate while protecting our beautiful marine wildlife.
