A year ago, a significant fire devastated thirty hectares in a nature reserve in Maasmechelen. Since then, nature has shown an incredible ability to bounce back. In Mechelse Heide, the area is now teeming with new growth, including heather and resilient trees. It's fascinating to see how life can flourish again, even after such destruction.
