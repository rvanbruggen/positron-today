This summer, Europe faced unprecedented heat waves and droughts, leading to the hottest June on record. While wind and thermal power struggled, solar energy thrived, producing 17% more power than usual. This surge in solar generation helped meet the rising demand for air conditioning during the extreme heat. As scientists study the causes of these heat events, this summer serves as a crucial lesson for Europe's energy future.
