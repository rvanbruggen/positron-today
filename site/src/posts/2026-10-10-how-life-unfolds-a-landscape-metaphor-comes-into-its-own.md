So, how do genes guide an embryo as it grows into a body made of many different cell types? Biologist C.H. Waddington imagined cells as a ball rolling through a landscape of branching valleys, with each path leading to a different cell fate. Now, researchers are using data from thousands of cells to map real cell-state landscapes, finding that development is more dynamic than a simple genetic readout. And here's the fascinating part: cells interact not only with one another, but also help shape the landscape that guides their journey.
