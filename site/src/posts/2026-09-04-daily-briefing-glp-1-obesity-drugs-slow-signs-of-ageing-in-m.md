So, it turns out that GLP-1 medications, like semaglutide, might do more than just help with weight loss. A recent study showed that older female mice on this drug lived longer and showed fewer signs of ageing, such as inflammation. And here's the thing – these mice also performed better on cognitive tasks compared to those on a calorie-restricted diet. This suggests that the benefits of GLP-1 drugs go beyond just cutting calories, which is pretty exciting!
