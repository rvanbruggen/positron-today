Imagine wearing glasses that let you see the hidden world of infrared light! A team of engineers has developed eyewear that captures infrared photons and converts them into visible light, allowing users to perceive wavelengths previously invisible to the human eye. This breakthrough could revolutionize everything from augmented reality to navigation in low-visibility environments. It's an exciting step towards enhancing our natural abilities and exploring new frontiers in vision technology.
