So: two Dutch GPs have adapted an anesthetic technique that can make IUD insertion much more comfortable. It uses three small injections in the cervix, followed by a one-minute wait before the device is placed. Among 57 patients, the average pain score was just 2 out of 10, and everyone said they would choose the method again. The doctors’ findings were published in September, adding fresh evidence to the discussion about pain relief during IUD insertion.
