Almost 98 percent of new cars sold in Norway are fully electric, the highest share in the world. Long-running tax advantages, higher costs for petrol and diesel cars, and a nationwide network of fast chargers have made the switch attractive to drivers. In northern Sweden, by contrast, concerns about winter range and high purchase prices still hold many people back. The comparison shows how steady policies and practical financial incentives can help accelerate the transition.
