Here’s a remarkable fact: DNA can suffer thousands of injuries in a single cell every day, yet its instructions usually stay intact. Cells constantly spot and repair damage, from small flaws to broken strands, helping keep life’s genetic information stable. These natural repair tools also make gene editing possible, as researchers rely on them to add, remove, or change genetic material. So: the same elegant system that protects cells is helping scientists explore new ways to study and treat genetic disease.
