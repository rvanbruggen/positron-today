Millions of people across India are celebrating Diwali, the festival of lights, with glowing oil lamps and colourful displays in homes, temples and streets. The celebrations welcome the triumph of light over darkness and bring families together for prayers, sweets and festive gatherings. This year’s festivities come as air pollution is a serious concern, especially in Delhi, where levels have reached a decade high for Diwali. Certified green firecrackers are permitted for a limited time, offering a way to celebrate with fewer emissions, though questions remain about their impact.
