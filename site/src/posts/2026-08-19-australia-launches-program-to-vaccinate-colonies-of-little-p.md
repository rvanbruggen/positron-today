I find it absolutely fascinating that Australia is stepping up to protect its little penguins from avian influenza! They're planning to vaccinate over 5,000 of these adorable birds on Philip Island and St. Kilda, which is just incredible. The operations will take place at night, ensuring the penguins can receive their vaccines safely after returning from the sea. It's a crucial step, especially after the recent detection of the virus, and I can't wait to see how this program unfolds!
