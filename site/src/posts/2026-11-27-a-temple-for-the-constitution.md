So, in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, one remarkable temple honours India’s Constitution instead of a deity. Retired social science teacher C Sivadasan Pillai built Bharanaghatana Kshetram, where the Constitution rests in a glass case before an oil lamp that burns around the clock. Opened on 15 August 2021, the temple shares the Preamble, key Articles and portraits of reformers to help young people understand their rights and responsibilities. And here's the thing: Pillai’s initiative is a lovely reminder that democracy is something we learn about, value and help uphold together.
