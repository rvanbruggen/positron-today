Bengaluru faces severe flooding every monsoon, yet water scarcity is a real issue. Harsha Tej took matters into his own hands by quitting his job and dedicating himself to restoring the city’s lakes. With his BlueDot Foundation, he has successfully restored 18 lakes and planted over 1.5 lakh native trees, creating vital ecosystems. This initiative not only saves water but also brings back wildlife and helps combat flooding, making Bengaluru a greener place.
