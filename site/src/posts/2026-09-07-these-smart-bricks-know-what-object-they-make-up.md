Isn't it fascinating how cells, without brains, manage to organize themselves into complex forms? This new research shows that smart bricks, equipped with their own neural networks, can identify what object they belong to by communicating with each other. They achieved an impressive accuracy of 85 to 100 percent in recognizing shapes, even when some bricks were missing! Imagine a future where machines can build and repair themselves—it's not just a dream anymore.
