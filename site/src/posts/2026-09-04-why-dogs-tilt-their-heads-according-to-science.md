So, have you ever noticed how dogs tilt their heads when you talk to them? It's not just for cuteness—there's some intriguing science behind it! Researchers suggest that this adorable behavior might help dogs hear and process our words better. Some dogs even tilt their heads more when they recognize certain words, which is just fascinating. This episode of Ask Us Anything dives into all these theories and more!
