Isn't it fascinating how a forest has managed to thrive right in the middle of a bustling city? The Guarani people, with a rich history dating back to the 1500s, have faced numerous challenges in their fight for recognition and territory. Recently, the Lula administration has taken steps to revive their ancestral rights, but political hurdles still loom large. It's a story of resilience and hope, showing that nature and culture can endure even in the most unexpected places.
