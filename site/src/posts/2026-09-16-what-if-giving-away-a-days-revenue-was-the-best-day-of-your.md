BGC Group turns the tragedy of 9/11 into something meaningful each year through their Charity Day, where celebrities help close trades knowing that 100% of commission revenue goes to charities worldwide. This year alone, they raised a staggering US$16 million, and since 2005 they've donated a total of US$250 million globally - money that fundamentally transforms lives for organisations supporting everything from arts programmes to rare disease research. For charities like HvH Arts, BGC's annual donation makes up a third of their entire budget, allowing them to triple the children they help since 2019. So here's the thing: watching young people like 12-year-old Carmela, who lives with muscular dystrophy, benefit directly from this generosity shows how one day of radical commitment to giving can ripple out and change everything.
