How exciting is this? An international team of researchers has found and described seven new species of frogs from Madagascar! These fascinating creatures, known as Rhombophryne or diamond frogs, have been hiding right under our feet. With their unique features and vibrant markings, they add to the rich biodiversity of the island. This discovery highlights the importance of understanding and protecting these amazing amphibians as their habitats face increasing threats.
