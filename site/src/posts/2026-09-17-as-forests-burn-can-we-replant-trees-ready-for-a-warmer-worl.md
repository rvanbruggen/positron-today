So here's the thing - as climate change intensifies wildfires and threatens our forests, scientists are asking a crucial question about how we replant. The challenge is picking tree species that won't just survive today's climate, but will thrive in the warmer conditions we're heading toward. Now, this isn't just about grabbing any seedling and hoping for the best - it requires careful thinking about which trees have the resilience to adapt. And that's where the real opportunity lies: by selecting the right species now, we can rebuild forests that are genuinely prepared for the future.
