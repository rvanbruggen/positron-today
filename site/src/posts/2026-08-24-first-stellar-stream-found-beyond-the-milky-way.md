Astronomers have made an exciting discovery of a faint stellar stream outside the Milky Way, shedding light on dark matter. This ancient cluster of stars has been gradually breaking apart, creating a delicate ribbon across space. By analyzing this stream, researchers can better understand the gravitational effects of dark matter on galaxies. This breakthrough could help unravel the mysteries of dark matter distribution throughout the universe.
