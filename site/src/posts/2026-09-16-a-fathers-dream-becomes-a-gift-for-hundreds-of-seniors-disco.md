Saakshi Maheshwari lost her father in 2023 and realized that waiting for the right time to travel and chase dreams is a luxury we don't always have. Inspired by his passion for both helping others and exploring the world, she launched The Original Travel Company with her husband Rajat to take senior citizens on journeys across India and beyond. Since June 2023, they've travelled with approximately 350 seniors on over 40 domestic and international trips, proving that retirement is the perfect time to start living those postponed adventures. Her father's unfulfilled dreams have transformed into a beautiful mission - turning what could have been her greatest loss into a gift that's changing how hundreds of older people see their own futures.
