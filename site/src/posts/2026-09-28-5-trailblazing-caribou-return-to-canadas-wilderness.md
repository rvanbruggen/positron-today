I am absolutely thrilled to share some wonderful news from Jasper National Park, where five special caribou have just been released back into the wild. This group, consisting of one bull and four calves, is the very first to successfully graduate from North America's first conservation breeding program. It is just so heartening to see them exploring the Tonquin Valley and even joining up with existing wild herds. And here's the thing - this milestone is a massive step forward for the recovery of this vulnerable species. I really cannot wait to see how these trailblazers continue to adapt to their natural home.
