So, the US Library of Congress has opened a permanent exhibition tracing the Americas’ rich history, from Indigenous civilizations to the present. Its remarkable collection includes 3,000-year-old jade, Taíno carvings, the 1531 Huexotzinco Codex and the 1507 map that first gave the continent the name “America.” Around 80% of the objects come from a donation of 4,000 items, while a $10 million gift helped bring the new gallery to life. And here’s the thing: the exhibition brings Indigenous voices and European accounts together, inviting visitors to explore both the continent’s cultural heritage and the stories told about its conquest.
