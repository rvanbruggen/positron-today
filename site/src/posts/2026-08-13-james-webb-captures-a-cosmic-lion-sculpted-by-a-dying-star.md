The James Webb Space Telescope has unveiled stunning infrared images of the Lion Nebula, revealing intricate details of this cosmic marvel. Unlike the earlier observations by Hubble, Webb's high-resolution instruments highlight features like dense dust and ionized gas. At the heart of this nebula lies a white dwarf, the remnant of a dying star, which continues to shape its surroundings. It's fascinating to think that this cosmic lion is constantly evolving, with its beauty set to change over the next 10,000 years.
