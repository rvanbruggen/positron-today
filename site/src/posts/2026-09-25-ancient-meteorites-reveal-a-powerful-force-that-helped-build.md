About 4.6 billion years ago, our solar system began as a huge cloud of gas and dust that eventually formed the sun and planets. For a long time, gravity was thought to be the main player in this transformation. Now, new research from MIT shows magnetism also played a crucial role, with ancient magnetic fields found in some of the oldest meteorite grains. These findings suggest a strong magnetic field helped shape the early solar nebula, influencing how material moved as the sun was forming. It’s fascinating to see magnetism stepping into the spotlight alongside gravity in the story of our cosmic origins.
