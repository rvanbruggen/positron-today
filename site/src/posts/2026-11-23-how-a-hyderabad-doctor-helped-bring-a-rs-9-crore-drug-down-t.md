A medicine that could cost up to Rs 9 crore a year was out of reach for seven children with Allan-Herndon-Dudley syndrome, a rare condition affecting brain development and movement. Hyderabad paediatrician Dr Sivaranjani Santosh found that tiratricol could help, but one family had to pay Rs 12–13 lakh for just a month’s supply from Germany. So she reached out to pharmaceutical companies and, when that stalled, shared her appeal publicly to find a more affordable option. With help from Access to Medicines India and the Pharmaceutical Accountability Foundation, the estimated annual cost came down to around Rs 2.5 lakh per child - a remarkable step toward making treatment accessible.
