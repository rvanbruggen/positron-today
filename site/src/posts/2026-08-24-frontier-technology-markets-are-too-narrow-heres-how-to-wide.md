It's fascinating how public money can shape the markets around frontier technologies! Governments have a crucial role in deciding which firms thrive and which technologies get adopted. By making informed choices, they can ensure that markets remain open and competitive instead of being dominated by a few players. This is especially important in sectors like space and AI, where today's decisions can lock in tomorrow's options.
