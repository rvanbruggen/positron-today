A group of parents in Limburg has taken matters into their own hands by organizing a carpool system for their children attending special education schools. The initiative came about because many kids were spending far too much time commuting - sometimes up to four hours daily on school buses - which left them exhausted and struggling to focus on their learning. What started as a practical solution has become a real community effort, showing how parents can work together when existing transport systems fall short. So it's a heartwarming example of people stepping up to improve their children's school experience.
