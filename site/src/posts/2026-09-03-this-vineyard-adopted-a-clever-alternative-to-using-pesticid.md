So, here's a fascinating story from South Africa! The Vergenoegd Löw Wine Estate has swapped out harmful pesticides for a fleet of 1,000 Indian Runner ducks, which not only munch on pests but also enrich the soil with their droppings. This eco-friendly approach not only protects beneficial insects but also leads to better wine quality. It's amazing to see how nature can inspire sustainable farming practices that benefit both the environment and the farmers.
