So, scientists are diving deep into the mysteries of the East African Rift System, and guess what? They found that a massive upwelling of hot mantle material, known as the African Superplume, is causing some unexpected movements in the Earth's crust. This is not just your typical continental rifting; there's a fascinating twist with deformation happening parallel to the rift itself! It's like watching Silly Putty stretch and crack in different ways depending on how you pull it. How cool is that?
