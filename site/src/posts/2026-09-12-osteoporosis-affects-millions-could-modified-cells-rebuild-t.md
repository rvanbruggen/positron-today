Osteoporosis is a serious condition that leads to fragile bones, but a new study offers hope with a cell therapy that might rebuild them. In a small trial, older women who received their own modified bone-marrow cells saw a dramatic decrease in fractures. This one-time treatment could potentially change the lives of millions suffering from osteoporosis. While the results are promising, more research is needed to fully understand the therapy's effects and track the cells in the body.
