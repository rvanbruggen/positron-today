Today marks a monumental day in mathematics as AI has reportedly cracked one of the six open 'million-dollar' problems. This breakthrough reveals that the Navier-Stokes equations, which describe fluid motion, might be fundamentally flawed. Mathematician Tristan Buckmaster likens this moment to the famous Deep Blue victory over Garry Kasparov in chess, suggesting a significant shift in our understanding of machine intelligence. It's fascinating to think about how this could reshape the future of math and science as we know it.
