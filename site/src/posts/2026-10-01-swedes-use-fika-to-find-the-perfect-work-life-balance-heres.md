I love how the Swedes have turned a simple coffee break into a powerful tool against burnout called fika. It’s not just about coffee but about pausing, connecting, and truly being present with others or yourself. This tradition dates back centuries and is deeply embedded in their work culture, encouraging genuine social interaction and a break from multitasking. So: if you want to refresh your focus and avoid burnout, maybe it’s time to fika!
