So, a fascinating new study shows that listening to birdsong in a restored urban forest can significantly lower stress and boost your mood! Researchers found that participants felt the most relaxed and positive when surrounded by nature and the sounds of birds. Interestingly, the combination of a natural setting and birdsong had the greatest impact, more than any other environment tested. This suggests that creating green spaces in cities could really enhance our mental well-being.
