So, have you heard about the ‘nonna maxxing’ trend? It’s all about Gen Z embracing the wholesome lifestyle of Italian grandmothers, focusing on real food, family meals, and intentional movement. This trend encourages young people to slow down, enjoy fresh ingredients, and engage in tactile hobbies, which is such a refreshing change! Plus, the health benefits are fantastic, from better nutrition to reduced stress levels. It’s a beautiful way to reconnect with old-world wisdom in our fast-paced lives.
