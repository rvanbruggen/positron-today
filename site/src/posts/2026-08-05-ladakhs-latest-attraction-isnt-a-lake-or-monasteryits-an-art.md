So, have you heard about the sā Ladakh Biennale? It's not your typical art exhibition—it's spread across the stunning landscapes of Ladakh, transforming the region into an open-air gallery until August 2026. Artists from all over are showcasing their works in harmony with the breathtaking environment, making the journey between Leh and Kargil a unique experience. This biennale is all about connecting art with the land and local communities, inviting visitors to engage with the culture in a whole new way.
