So: Prestige University’s new campus in Indore, India, turns traditional stepwells into an imaginative design for learning and gathering. Its striking terraces contain 463 platforms for meeting, studying and relaxing, while courtyards and open corridors help keep the building cool without air conditioning. The five-level complex also features a rooftop auditorium with space for 9,000 people. Designed by Sanjay Puri Architects, the campus has earned international recognition, including the World Building of the Year title and a Dezeen Awards 2026 audience award.
