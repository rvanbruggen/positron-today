So, it turns out that fertilization isn't just about a mad dash to the egg—it's more like a well-coordinated team effort! Researchers have discovered that in many arthropod species, sperm can actually work together, forming groups that enhance their chances of success. This teamwork, known as sperm conjugation, challenges the long-held belief that it's all about individual competition. It's fascinating to think about how evolution has played with this idea over millions of years, allowing sperm cooperation to emerge and disappear repeatedly.
