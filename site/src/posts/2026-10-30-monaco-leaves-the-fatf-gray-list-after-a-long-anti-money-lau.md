So: Monaco has been removed from the FATF’s gray list after making significant progress against money laundering and terrorist financing. The listing in June 2024 had unsettled investors and challenged the principality’s image as a destination for international wealth. Reforms include new laws, more prosecutors and the creation of an independent financial watchdog, which has also brought the yacht sector under anti-money-laundering rules. Now Monaco is setting its sights on leaving the European Commission’s own list, too - a clear next step in its long-running effort to strengthen financial oversight.
