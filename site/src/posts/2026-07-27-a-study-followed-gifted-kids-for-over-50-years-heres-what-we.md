This fascinating study has been tracking gifted kids for over 50 years, revealing some surprising insights about their lives and careers. It turns out that intelligence isn't the same as passion, and these kids thrive in various fields beyond just science and medicine. Plus, the idea that gifted kids will excel on their own without guidance is a myth; they need support just like everyone else. Ultimately, hard work plays a crucial role in their success, reminding us that effort is just as important as natural talent.
