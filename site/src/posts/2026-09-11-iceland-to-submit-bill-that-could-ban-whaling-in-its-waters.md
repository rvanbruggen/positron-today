Iceland is gearing up to submit a bill in February 2027 that could put a permanent end to whaling in its waters, which is fantastic news! This comes after a report highlighted the suffering of harpooned whales, leading to a temporary suspension of the hunt. With strong public and political support, this bill is seen as a significant step towards animal welfare and conservation. It’s exciting to think that if this passes, Iceland could join the ranks of countries that prioritize marine life over outdated practices.
