Scientists at the University at Buffalo have created an innovative injectable hydrogel that keeps osteoarthritis treatments effective for much longer. This clever material transforms from a liquid to a smooth, lubricating semisolid inside the joint, allowing it to release medication gradually over several weeks. This could mean fewer injections for patients and a better chance of addressing the underlying issues of osteoarthritis. It's exciting to think about the potential for improved joint health and reduced pain for so many people!
