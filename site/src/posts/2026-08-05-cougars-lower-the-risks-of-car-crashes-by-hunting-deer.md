So, it turns out that bringing cougars back into the mix could really help reduce car accidents caused by deer! A recent study shows that in areas where these big cats thrive, deer tend to steer clear of roads, leading to a whopping 76% drop in collisions. This fascinating research highlights how nature can play a role in keeping our roads safer. It's amazing to think that a predator like the cougar can help balance the ecosystem and protect drivers at the same time!
