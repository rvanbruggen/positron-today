Isn't it fascinating that people who live beyond 100 years have a unique advantage? Researchers discovered that these supercentenarians possess a remarkable number of killer immune cells, known as CD4 CTLs, which actually increase as they age. These cells not only help fight infections but also target harmful cells like cancer. This suggests that our immune system can adapt even in extreme old age, opening up exciting possibilities for understanding longevity.
