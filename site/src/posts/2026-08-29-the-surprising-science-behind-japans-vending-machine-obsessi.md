Japan is famous for its incredible variety of vending machines, offering everything from hot ramen to fresh flowers. With over 2.6 million machines in the country, there's one for nearly every 50 people! The history of these machines is fascinating, tracing back to ancient Egypt and evolving significantly over the years. Today, they provide a quick and convenient solution for busy Japanese people on the go.
