I find it fascinating that in Edinburgh, bikes have finally outnumbered cars during the morning rush hour on Leith Walk, a historic first! This surge is largely thanks to the popularity of e-bike rentals and new segregated bike lanes making cycling safer and more appealing. Glasgow is also seeing a big boost in bike journeys, with some roads recording record numbers. And here's the thing - people like Kirsty, a health visitor, are saving time and enjoying the calm of cycling instead of driving. It’s clear that Scotland’s investment in cycling infrastructure is really paying off.
