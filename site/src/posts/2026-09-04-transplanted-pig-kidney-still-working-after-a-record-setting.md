So, here's the exciting news! A woman has been living with a pig kidney for over nine months without needing dialysis, which is a remarkable achievement. This breakthrough happened at Massachusetts General Hospital, and it’s all thanks to innovative gene editing by eGenesis. With pig kidneys being a potential solution to the organ shortage crisis, this could change the lives of many waiting for transplants. It's a fascinating development in the world of medical science!
