In the stunning Somiedo natural park, the Cantabrian brown bears are making a remarkable comeback from the brink of extinction. Once dwindling to just 60 bears, their population has now surged to around 500, thanks to dedicated conservation efforts. Nature guide Luis Frechilla shares the thrill of spotting these magnificent creatures, even if it’s just a distant glimpse. As the sun sets over the mountains, the experience is enriched by the local cuisine, making it a perfect blend of nature and culture.
