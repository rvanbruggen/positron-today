It's exciting to see how architects are transforming car-centric spaces into vibrant public areas! The European Prize for Urban Public Space showcases innovative projects that tackle the climate crisis by turning parking lots into gardens and roundabouts into splash pools. One standout is Leopold Banchini’s Round About Baths in Logroño, which cleverly repurposed a neglected space. These initiatives highlight the potential for cities to evolve beyond their automotive past and embrace greener, more accessible environments.
