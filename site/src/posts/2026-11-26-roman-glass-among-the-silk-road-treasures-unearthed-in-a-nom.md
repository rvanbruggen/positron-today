Now, what a fascinating window into the Silk Road: archaeologists in Mongolia have uncovered a remarkable Xiongnu noble’s tomb, with treasures linked to places far beyond the steppe. Among 413 artifacts are shards of a Roman glass cup, jade pieces, precious stones and an entire lacquered chariot. The finds point to a far-reaching exchange network connecting the Xiongnu with Rome, Han China and the Korean Peninsula. Researchers hope DNA analysis will reveal more about the tomb’s still-unknown occupant and the society they lived in.
