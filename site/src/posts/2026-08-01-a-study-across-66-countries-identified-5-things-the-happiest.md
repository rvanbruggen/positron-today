So, a fascinating new study reveals that happiness isn't just about individual pursuits but also about the communities we belong to. Researchers analyzed data from over 97,000 people across 66 countries and found five key factors that contribute to happier societies. These include individual emancipation, support for Enlightenment ideas, meeting basic human needs, effective governance, and community connectedness. It's a reminder that our social environments play a crucial role in our overall well-being!
