I just stumbled upon this wonderful initiative called Papy Hours, and it really warmed my heart. It is all about bringing that cozy, lively bistro atmosphere directly into retirement homes to help residents connect. So: instead of just sitting around, seniors are enjoying social drinks and conversations that feel like a real night out. And here's the thing - it is such a simple idea, but it makes a massive difference in combating loneliness. I think we could all learn a thing or two from this kind of community spirit.
