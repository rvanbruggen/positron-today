So, I just stumbled upon this fascinating discovery about plesiosaurs, those incredible marine reptiles from the Jurassic period. It turns out they had a rather unique way of dealing with indigestible meals, much like modern owls do today. Paleontologists have found fossilized vomit pellets, which suggest these creatures would swim to shallow river deltas to regurgitate shells and other leftovers. And here is the thing, it really shows how much we can still learn about ancient life just by looking at what they left behind. It is truly amazing to think about these massive predators behaving in such a relatable way.
