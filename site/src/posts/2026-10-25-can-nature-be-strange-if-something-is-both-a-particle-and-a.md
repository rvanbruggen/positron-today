In her new book, physicist and cosmologist Chanda Prescod-Weinstein explores the universe through the lively, associative workings of her own mind. She connects ideas in physics with cultural history, music and personal experience, from Maxwell’s discoveries to the meaning of 1865 for Black Americans. These unexpected links, she says, can make science more welcoming to readers who have not always felt at home with physics. And here's the thing: sometimes the universe is strange, but we may simply need a better metaphor to describe it.
