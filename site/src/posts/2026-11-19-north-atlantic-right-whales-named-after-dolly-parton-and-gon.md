Conservation researchers have given 20 North Atlantic right whales new names, including Dolly, Gonzo and Dodo, to help track them and make their stories easier to connect with. Dolly’s distinctive head markings bring the country singer’s famous hairstyle to mind, while Gonzo is named for the beloved Muppet. The whales remain critically endangered, with an estimated population of 382 and ongoing threats from fishing gear and ship strikes. There’s encouraging news, too: 24 calves were born during the 2025–2026 season, the most since 2009.
