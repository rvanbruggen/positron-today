I find it fascinating how Indigenous communities in Peru are turning the tide on wildfires despite worsening climate conditions. Through the Paamari programme, they combine traditional knowledge with modern tech like drones and satellite imagery to spot and stop fires early. The results are impressive: an 81% reduction in burned areas and even zero wildfires in 2025. It’s a powerful reminder that respecting nature and culture can lead to real climate solutions.
