While working at NASA, she realized that the needs of astronauts and those without plumbing on Earth are surprisingly similar. This innovative toilet evaporates 95% of waste, making sanitation safer in low-income areas. Plus, she just won a $100,000 award to expand this incredible technology to even more communities!
