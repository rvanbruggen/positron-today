I love how Toby Kiers’s childhood mushroom hunts grew into a career exploring the hidden life beneath our feet. Her pioneering research shows how mycorrhizal fungi exchange resources with plants, form intricate networks and process information without a brain. Kiers and her team have also revealed how vital these networks are to moving carbon into deep soil, while much of the fungal world remains unexplored. Now, as the youngest-ever recipient of the 2026 Tyler Prize for Environmental Achievement, she is also working to secure stronger legal protections for fungi.
