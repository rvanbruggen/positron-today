Flaco the Eurasian eagle-owl captured hearts after escaping captivity in Central Park and thriving in Manhattan for over a year. Directed by Penny Lane, the documentary 'Wild Inside' showcases his incredible journey, highlighting how he learned to survive in the urban jungle. New Yorkers from all walks of life connected with Flaco's story, seeing him as a symbol of resilience and transformation. Tragically, his tale takes a somber turn, raising questions about freedom and survival in a city that can be both magical and harsh.
