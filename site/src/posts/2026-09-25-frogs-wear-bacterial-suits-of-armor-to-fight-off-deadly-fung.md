So: some tropical frogs have a clever way to protect their delicate skin from a deadly fungus using bacteria as a natural shield. A study showed that the skin microbiome of certain frog species can actually help them survive the chytrid fungus, which has wiped out many amphibians worldwide. The researchers tested two frog species in Brazil and found that one species’ bacterial community offered strong protection, while the other remained vulnerable. And here's the thing - it’s not just the number of bacteria that matters, but how well they defend against the disease.
