So, researchers have found a way to produce important cancer drug precursors using modified baker's yeast instead of relying on an endangered plant. This is exciting because it could help protect the rare Sinopodophyllum hexandrum, which is often used in making these drugs. Millions of people rely on medications like etoposide and teniposide, and this new method could simplify their production. It's a fantastic example of how science can innovate while also being environmentally conscious!
