Nancy Grace Roman, known as the mother of the Hubble Space Telescope, has made a remarkable impact on astronomy. The newly launched Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is the first space telescope named after a woman and aims to explore dark matter, dark energy, and exoplanets. Despite facing budget cuts, Roman's legacy ensures that this groundbreaking project continues. Her efforts in promoting space astronomy have paved the way for future discoveries and inspired countless scientists.
