A cultural-awareness training developed over two years by the Brussels-West police zone in Molenbeek will now be offered to police officers across Belgium. It explores the history of migration in Belgium and shares practical ways to communicate better with young people from migrant backgrounds. Youth worker Ayoub Ben Abdeslam describes how a hostile encounter during a routine check shaped his early view of the police. His message to officers is that a respectful first contact can help build trust and prevent a cycle of mutual suspicion.
