Researchers at the Proefcentrum Fruitteelt in Sint-Truiden have found that clover and herbs can lower temperatures in fruit plantations by up to 20 degrees compared to traditional grass. This year, the difference is striking due to the ongoing drought and heat. While grass strips are completely dried out, the areas with clover and herbs remain lush and green. This discovery could lead to a shift in how fruit growers manage their plantations, especially during extreme weather conditions.
