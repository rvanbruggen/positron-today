So, get this! China is gearing up to launch the Chang’e-7 mission, which will send a unique 'hopping' probe to explore the Moon's polar craters for hidden water ice. This mission is super exciting because it could help us understand how much ice is there and how it got there, which is crucial for future lunar bases. The probe will drill into the frozen soil and analyze the gases released, providing insights into the Moon's history. Plus, it’s a big step in China's lunar exploration journey, showcasing their growing capabilities in space science.
