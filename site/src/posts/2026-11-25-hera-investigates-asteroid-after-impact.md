Now, ESA’s Hera spacecraft has reached Dimorphos to investigate the aftermath of NASA’s deliberate asteroid impact in 2022. The Dart spacecraft struck the 150-metre-wide asteroid, shortening its orbit around Didymos by about 33 minutes and sending a vast trail of debris into space. Over the next year, Hera and its two small companion satellites will examine the impact site and measure Dimorphos’s mass. And here’s the thing: those findings could help scientists work out how to deflect a future asteroid heading towards Earth.
