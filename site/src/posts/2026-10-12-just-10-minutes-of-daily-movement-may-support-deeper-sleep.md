So, here's an encouraging idea: moving every day, even for just 10 minutes, may help you get more restorative deep sleep. Researchers at the University of Texas at Austin studied months of activity and sleep data from students wearing Fitbit devices, and found that exercise frequency seemed to matter. Regular walking and breaks from sitting were also linked to healthier sleep patterns, better mood and more energy the next day. The findings suggest that spreading movement throughout the week may be a simple way to support well-being.
