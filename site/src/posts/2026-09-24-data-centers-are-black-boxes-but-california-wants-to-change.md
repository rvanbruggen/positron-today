So: California just took a big step to shine a light on data centers, those mysterious giants gobbling up electricity and water. Gov. Newsom signed several bills requiring these centers to disclose their energy and water use, which is fantastic for transparency. Now, communities and scientists can finally get a clearer picture of their impact on local resources and bills. And here's the thing - these laws also push for fairer power rates and more renewable energy use, aiming to keep costs down and the environment happier.
