So, if you're in Hawaii around late May or mid-to-late July, you might just notice something incredible—no shadows! This phenomenon, known as Lahaina Noon, happens when the sun is directly overhead, making vertical objects cast no shadow at all. Locals celebrate this unique moment with fun activities, including snapping mind-bending photos that look like optical illusions. It's a fascinating reminder of how nature can create such magical experiences!
