So, the largest T. rex ever discovered, named Scotty, has revealed some fascinating secrets! This 66-million-year-old giant not only stands tall at 13 feet but also has preserved soft tissue in one of its bones, which is super rare in paleontology. Researchers used advanced imaging techniques to explore Scotty's rib, uncovering a network of mineralized blood vessels that provide clues about its death. It seems Scotty suffered a broken rib during a fierce battle, and while it tried to heal, it ultimately succumbed to its injuries in a salty marsh.
