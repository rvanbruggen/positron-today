Now, here's an encouraging result: three Belgian universities feature in the global top 50 for sustainability. KU Leuven ranks 12th worldwide, its best result yet, while Ghent University takes 36th place; UCLouvain ranks 43rd in the environmental impact category. The ranking assesses 2,004 universities on environmental and social impact, as well as governance, and Belgium has ten institutions in total. Belgian universities score above the global average across all indicators, though nine of the ten fell in the health and well-being category.
