The World Bank is making significant strides in enhancing the reproducibility of its research, which is crucial for the social and behavioral sciences. Since 2023, they've introduced reproducibility packages that document how conclusions are drawn from data, ensuring transparency and trust. With over 500 packages released, they aim to speed up this process even further. This initiative not only benefits the bank but sets a standard for other international organizations to follow.
