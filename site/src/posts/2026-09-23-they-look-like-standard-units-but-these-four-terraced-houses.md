I just stumbled upon this fascinating project in Vinge, where four new terraced houses are changing the game. The best part is that they are built entirely from materials you can find at your local hardware store. So: it is actually replicable and scalable for everyone else. And here is the thing - they have set a record for sustainability in Denmark while looking completely unassuming. It really makes me wonder why we do not build like this everywhere.
