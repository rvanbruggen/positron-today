So, researchers at NIT Rourkela have come up with a brilliant solution to a pressing problem by turning coal mining waste into a strong road-building material. This innovative composite combines coal mine parting, fly ash, and a binder, making it perfect for heavy truck traffic in coal-mining regions. It's exciting to think that waste can be repurposed to create better infrastructure while also addressing environmental concerns. This project not only showcases the potential of recycling but also embodies the principles of a circular economy.
