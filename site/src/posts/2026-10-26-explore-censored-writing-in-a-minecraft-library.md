So: Minecraft is home to a remarkable library where readers can explore writing that authoritarian governments have tried to silence. Reporters Without Borders and design collective BlockWorks created the grand virtual space, filling its country-themed wings with books, essays, and articles. The collection includes works from writers in countries such as Iran, Russia, and Egypt, and recently added a section about press freedom in the United States. You can visit the library online in Minecraft or download it to explore offline.
