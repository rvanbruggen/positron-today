This is such an exciting breakthrough! Researchers at UCLA have found a way to guide heat through materials like light, and they did it at room temperature. By studying boron arsenide, they discovered that heat can travel in concentrated paths instead of spreading out randomly. This could revolutionize how we manage heat in electronics and quantum technologies, making devices faster and more efficient.
