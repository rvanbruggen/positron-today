So: Denmark is stepping up to invest heavily in education for developing countries by pledging up to a billion kroner to the Global Partnership for Education by 2030. This commitment kicks off with 250 million kroner in 2027, showing real dedication to the cause. And here's the thing - education is seen as one of the best investments, benefiting both the children receiving support and Denmark itself. This move comes as the global fund faces financial pressure, making Denmark's support even more crucial.
