I am genuinely thrilled to see some positive movement on the international stage today. It looks like Belgium and Rwanda have officially decided to restore their diplomatic relations, which is just fantastic news for everyone involved. Our foreign minister, Mr. Prévot, mentioned that this is the result of some very honest and respectful discussions with his counterpart. And here is the thing - even though they still have some differences to work through, they have agreed to keep talking systematically. It really shows that open dialogue is the best way forward for us all.
