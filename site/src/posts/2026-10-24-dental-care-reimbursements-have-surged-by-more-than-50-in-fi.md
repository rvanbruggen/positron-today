Here’s a striking trend: Belgium’s dental-care reimbursements rose from just over €1 billion in 2021 to €1.6 billion in 2025, a jump of more than 50%. Over the same period, the total number of dental procedures increased by 16%. According to L’Echo, about half of the spending rise comes from indexation of agreed tariffs, while more dentists are also charging supplements above official rates. The growing number of people eligible for increased reimbursement has contributed too.
