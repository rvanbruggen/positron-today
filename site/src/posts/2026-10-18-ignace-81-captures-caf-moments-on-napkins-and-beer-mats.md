I love this wonderfully simple idea: 81-year-old Ignace captures moments from café life on napkins and beer mats. He says that by the time his pint is finished, the words are already on the page. So an ordinary drink becomes a little creative ritual, preserving passing moments before they disappear. It is a charming reminder that inspiration can arrive right at the table, with a pen close at hand.
