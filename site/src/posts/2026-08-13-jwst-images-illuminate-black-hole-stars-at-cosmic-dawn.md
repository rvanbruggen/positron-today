So, the James Webb Space Telescope has revealed some fascinating cosmic phenomena known as 'black hole stars'. These massive entities, which are at least 100,000 times larger than our sun, have been spotted in the early universe, and they’re full of surprises. Researchers believe these stars are actually black holes surrounded by hot gas, giving them a starlike appearance. The implications of this discovery could reshape our understanding of how black holes and galaxies formed in the cosmos.
