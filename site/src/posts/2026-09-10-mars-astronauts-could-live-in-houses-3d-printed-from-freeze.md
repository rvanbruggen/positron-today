Imagine this: astronauts on Mars living in houses made from freeze-dried yeast! Researchers have discovered a way to 3D print a building material that can withstand the harsh Martian conditions, using simple ingredients like gelatin and yeast. This innovative approach not only promises sturdy structures but also requires less energy compared to traditional methods. It's a fascinating step towards creating livable habitats on the Red Planet, and who knows, maybe we'll be growing our homes just like we grow our food!
