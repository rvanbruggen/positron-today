I just love how a simple doorbell camera caught an 81-year-old delivery driver named Don Johnson struggling with heavy luggage, sparking a huge wave of kindness. Erin Howell started a GoFundMe that has raised over $370,000 to help Don cover his wife’s cancer treatments and daily expenses. Don works tirelessly because it’s the only job that hires him, even though he’s exhausted and his car barely runs. And here’s the thing - this generosity is letting him finally rest and care for his wife, which is just beautiful to see.
