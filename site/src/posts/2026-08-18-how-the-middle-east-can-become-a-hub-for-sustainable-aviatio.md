The Middle East has a unique opportunity to transform its energy landscape by becoming a leader in sustainable aviation fuels (SAF). With the aviation sector under pressure to reduce carbon emissions, SAF produced from waste and biomass presents a viable solution. Gulf nations, equipped with world-class infrastructure and connected airports, can leverage their assets to bridge the current production gap. This shift not only diversifies economies but also creates jobs and positions the region as a key player in the global clean energy transition.
