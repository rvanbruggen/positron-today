Wow, the US battery market is really booming! In the second quarter of 2026, installations hit a whopping 20.2 gigawatt-hours, enough to power around 700,000 homes daily. This surge is largely due to cheaper batteries and the growing need for energy storage as renewables take off. While utility-scale systems are leading the charge, behind-the-meter batteries are also making strides, especially in data centers. It's an exciting time for energy storage, and the shift towards US-made technology is something to watch closely!
