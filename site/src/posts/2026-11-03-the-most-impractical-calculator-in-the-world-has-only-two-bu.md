Could a scientific calculator work with just two buttons? Physicist Andrzej Odrzywołek shows that a single number, 1, and one operation, eml(x,y) = exp(x) − ln(y), can be used to build familiar functions such as square roots, tangents and exponentiation. A proof-checking program has verified the mathematical reasoning, turning this curious idea into a rigorously checked result. And here's the thing: although calculators make calculations far easier, the old-fashioned work of consulting logarithm tables once helped astronomers spot the pattern behind Benford’s law.
