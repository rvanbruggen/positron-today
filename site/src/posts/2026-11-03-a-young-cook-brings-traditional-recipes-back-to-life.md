At 20, Visista has documented more than 250 traditional recipes, inspired by a childhood spent learning in the kitchen beside her grandmother. Growing up on a farm in Karnataka, she explored food through hands-on learning and now shares her discoveries through TRUE FOOD, using heritage ingredients and cooking methods. Her recipes bring forgotten dishes within reach of home cooks, showing that wholesome food can be delicious too. And here's the thing: for Visista, every dish is a chance to discover the stories and ideas behind the ingredients.
