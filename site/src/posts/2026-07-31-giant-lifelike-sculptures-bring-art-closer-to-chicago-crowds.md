Carole Feuerman's stunning hyperrealistic sculptures are turning Chicago's Magnificent Mile into an incredible open-air gallery. With her exhibition 'Monuments of Stillness', visitors can engage with 12 detailed sculptures that showcase the beauty of the human form. Feuerman, a true pioneer in the superrealist movement, aims to make art accessible to everyone, especially those who might not visit museums often. This vibrant display runs until November, bringing contemporary art right to the city's streets.
