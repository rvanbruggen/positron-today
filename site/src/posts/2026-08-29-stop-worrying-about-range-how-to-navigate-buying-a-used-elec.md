So, buying a used electric vehicle can feel a bit daunting, but it doesn't have to be! The key is understanding the battery's health, which is often overstated in concerns about degradation. Most modern EV batteries lose capacity slowly, and with a quick diagnostic check, you can ensure you're making a smart purchase. Plus, there are some fantastic deals out there, especially on older models like the Nissan Leaf, making electric driving more accessible than ever.
