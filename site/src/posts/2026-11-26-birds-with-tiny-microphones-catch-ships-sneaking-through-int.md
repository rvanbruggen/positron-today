So, birds carrying tiny microphones could help expose ships moving unnoticed across international waters. Their recordings may reveal vessel activity in places where monitoring and enforcement are difficult. Researchers are exploring how the sounds captured by birds can offer a fresh way to keep an eye on these vast ocean areas. And here's the thing: in waters that can feel like the Far West, listening from above could make a real difference.
