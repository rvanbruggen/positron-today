How exciting is this? Researchers have discovered that moonquakes could help locate hidden water ice beneath the lunar surface! By analyzing seismic waves, they can identify where ice might be buried, which is crucial for future lunar missions. This ice could provide drinking water and even fuel for astronauts, making long-term exploration much more feasible. It’s like finding a treasure map for future explorers on the moon!
