So: nearly 600 meters beneath the Pacific, researchers captured stunning 4K footage of a barreleye fish, one of the deep sea’s most extraordinary residents. This was only the 11th time MBARI scientists had encountered the elusive fish in almost 40 years of exploration. Its transparent head protects its unusual eyes, which can rotate from their usual upward gaze to keep track of prey. What a remarkable reminder of how much wonder is waiting in the ocean depths.
