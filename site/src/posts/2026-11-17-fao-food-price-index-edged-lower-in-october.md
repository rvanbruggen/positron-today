So, the FAO Food Price Index averaged 133.9 points in October, down 1.2% from September as prices for cereals, dairy and meat eased. It remained 5.9% higher than a year earlier, but 16.4% below its March 2022 peak. Wheat and maize prices fell amid subdued demand and improved supply prospects, while vegetable oils edged up and rice prices rose. Sugar stood out with a 6.6% monthly increase, reaching its highest level since December 2024 amid weather disruptions and production concerns.
