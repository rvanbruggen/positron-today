So, what if fallen leaves could help cook meals for 1,200 students? At IIT Bombay, startup Infixen turns nearly two tonnes of garden waste into fuel pellets every day. These pellets power the kitchen at Hostel 16 and help cut its LPG use by 60%. And here's the thing: waste that once cost nearly ₹50 lakh a year to transport is now being put to work as a valuable energy source.
