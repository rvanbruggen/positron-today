At 26, Franco-Senegalese aerospace engineer Allan Petre has fulfilled a childhood dream by joining NASA as a researcher, after growing up in a working-class neighbourhood near Paris. His parents encouraged him to focus on school, and a first glimpse of Jupiter through a telescope at age eight sparked his fascination with space. When a teacher warned that an aerospace career was almost impossible, a classmate’s words reminded him that even one chance in a million is still a chance, and Petre chose the science track. His journey, now told in a newly published manga, is a joyful reminder of how curiosity and persistence can open remarkable doors.
