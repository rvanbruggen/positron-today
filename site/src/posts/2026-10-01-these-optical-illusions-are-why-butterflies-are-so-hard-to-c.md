So: scientists have used high-speed footage to uncover why butterflies are such tricky targets for birds. It turns out their wing markings create optical illusions that confuse the eye during takeoff. This clever visual trickery makes it much harder for predators to predict their flight path. And here's the thing - it’s a brilliant example of nature’s subtle defenses in action. I find it fascinating how something as simple as wing patterns can have such a big impact!
