So, it turns out that the chicken kidney sponge is a real hero for our polluted ports! This amazing creature filters out toxins and heavy metals while recycling nutrients back into the water. Researchers in Spain have found that it thrives in harsh conditions, making it the top choice for cleaning up dirty harbors. With its ability to reproduce and survive in tough environments, this sponge could be a game changer for marine ecosystems.
