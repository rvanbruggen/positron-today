In Ethiopia, researchers are tackling unpredictable rainfall by developing improved seeds and efficient irrigation methods. This initiative aims to protect farmers' harvests while supplying the textile industry with locally grown fiber. The focus is on moving these solutions from research to the fields, ensuring farmers can adapt to climate changes. It's an exciting step towards a more resilient agricultural future!
