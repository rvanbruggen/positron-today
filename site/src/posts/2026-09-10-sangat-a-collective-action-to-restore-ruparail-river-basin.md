For years, families in the Ruparail river basin faced severe water shortages, but their hope never dried up. Communities, local administrations, NGOs, and CSR initiatives came together to create a powerful solution: they built check dams to capture rainwater. Revived ponds now store water for the dry months, and thousands of trees have been planted to help retain moisture in the landscape. This positive cycle not only supports farming and daily needs but also ensures that the river basin thrives, benefiting everyone who lives there.
