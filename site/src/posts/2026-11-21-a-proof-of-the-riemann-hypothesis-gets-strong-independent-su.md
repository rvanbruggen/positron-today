So, an AI-generated proof of the Riemann hypothesis is looking remarkably convincing: independent mathematicians have successfully checked that its Lean code compiles. The hypothesis, first proposed in 1859, concerns a deep pattern in the distribution of prime numbers. Researchers are still checking that the code proves exactly the original claim and contains no tricks or hidden shortcuts. That final comparator check is still pending, but one independent mathematician says the chance that anything is amiss is approximately zero.
