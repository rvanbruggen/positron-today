Here’s a fascinating glimpse into a hidden corner of the gut microbiome: researchers have found large DNA viruses thriving in the digestive systems of people and animals. These viruses are close relatives of poxviruses, while similar viruses were previously known mainly from the environment and rarely reported in gut communities. Their widespread presence brings a long-overlooked part of the microbiome into view. It also supports the idea that poxviruses may have evolved in the digestive systems of animals.
