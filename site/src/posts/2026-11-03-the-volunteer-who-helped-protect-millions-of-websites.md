So, here’s the thing: Czech engineer Ondrej Surý helped protect millions of websites by quickly packaging fixes for serious PHP vulnerabilities, including for older software versions. His volunteer work saved developers precious time and showed just how much the internet relies on people who maintain open-source tools. Surý says software needs ongoing care if we want it to stay secure. And with AI helping uncover vulnerabilities faster, his story is a timely reminder to support the people keeping the digital world running.
