So, archaeologists in Pompeii have uncovered a cabinet that had remained closed for nearly 2,000 years. Inside, they found jewellery, a small perfume bottle and 38 coins. The objects offer a tangible glimpse into life in the ancient city. And here's the thing: after centuries hidden away, this little collection is now helping bring Pompeii's past to light.
