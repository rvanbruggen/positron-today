In Keene, New Hampshire, they're taking a fantastic step to help salamanders safely cross roads by building small underpasses. This initiative follows successful crossings in Vermont that reduced amphibian deaths by 80%. It's all about habitat connectivity, allowing these creatures to find food, mate, and escape harsh weather. With the help of dedicated volunteers, this project aims to ensure that amphibians can thrive without the constant threat of traffic.
