I find it absolutely fascinating how inmates at the Redgranite Correctional Institute are finding a new sense of purpose by raising monarch butterflies. It is such a heartwarming story - they are nurturing these delicate creatures from caterpillars all the way to their migration journey. So, instead of just serving time, these men are actively contributing to conservation efforts by tagging butterflies and maintaining habitats. And here's the thing, the program has grown significantly in just its second year, showing how much the participants truly care. It is just wonderful to see such a positive impact coming from behind prison walls.
