Kinase inhibitors have revolutionized precision cancer treatment, especially for lung cancer, but resistance often develops as the enzymes mutate. Now, a fresh approach is emerging that doesn’t just inhibit these kinases but actually eliminates them entirely. This could be a game-changer for patients whose cancers have become resistant to current drugs. So: targeted protein degraders might open a new door to longer-lasting remissions and better outcomes. And here's the thing - this strategy could reshape how we think about treating cancer at a molecular level.
