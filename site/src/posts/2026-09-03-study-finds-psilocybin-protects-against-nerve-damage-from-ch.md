So, this groundbreaking study has found that psilocybin, the active compound in magic mushrooms, may actually protect against nerve damage caused by chemotherapy. Researchers tested this on mice and discovered that those given psilocybin experienced significantly less nerve pain after treatment. This is a huge deal because nerve damage is a common and painful side effect of chemotherapy that often lasts long after treatment ends. The team is now excited to explore how this could translate to human patients, which could change the game for many undergoing cancer treatment.
