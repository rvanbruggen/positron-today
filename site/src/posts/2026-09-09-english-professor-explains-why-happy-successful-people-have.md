Literacy is more than just the ability to read; it's about understanding and interpreting information. Dr. Shanté Holley highlights the gap between basic reading skills and true literacy, which can significantly impact our lives. Poor literacy is linked to negative mental health outcomes, while strong literacy skills boost self-confidence and critical thinking. It's fascinating to see how the elite prioritize true knowledge over superficial content, and this insight is a powerful reminder of the importance of literacy in achieving happiness and success.
