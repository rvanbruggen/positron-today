So, Polish startup Smartmedics is bringing clinical-grade heart monitoring into patients’ homes with a compact recorder, an electrode patch and a smartphone app. Its system supports consistent electrode placement, making recordings comparable over time and easy to share with doctors, including in real time. The first version reached the European market, and a commercial evaluation with an international partner generated more than 22 million złoty in sales between 2020 and 2022. Now, Smartmedics is seeking EU certification for its second version, with the technology also offering promising data for more personalised, AI-supported care.
