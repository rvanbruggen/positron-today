So, have you ever wondered why ancient gods are often depicted as animals? In the case of Thoth, the Egyptian god of magic, writing, and the moon, researchers have found that both baboons and ibises reflect light similar to moonlight. This fascinating discovery links their colors to the deity, suggesting a deeper meaning behind their representation. It's amazing to think that the ancient Egyptians chose these animals for their divine qualities based on something as ethereal as moonlight!
