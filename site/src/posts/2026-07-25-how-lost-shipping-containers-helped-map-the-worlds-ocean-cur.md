Isn't it fascinating how lost shipping containers can actually help us understand ocean currents? When containers fall overboard, they sometimes break open, releasing their contents into the sea. This unexpected event has turned into a treasure trove of data for oceanographers, who track these items to map currents more accurately. From Nike sneakers to Lego figures, these accidental probes have provided insights that help us navigate and understand our oceans better.
