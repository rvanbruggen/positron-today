So, astronomers have made an exciting discovery of a supermassive black hole that's far away from its galactic center! They spotted it during a spectacular event when a star got too close and was torn apart, creating a bright flash. This black hole, which weighs about a million suns, is located an astonishing 30,000 light-years from its galaxy's core. It's fascinating to think about how these wandering giants can go unnoticed until something dramatic happens, revealing their hidden presence.
