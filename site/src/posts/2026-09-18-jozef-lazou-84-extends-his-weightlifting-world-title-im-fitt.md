At 84 years old, Jozef Lazou from Waregem has just extended his world title in weightlifting - an absolutely remarkable achievement. What I find most striking is his attitude about it - he's clearly proud of his fitness level and compares himself favorably to much younger people. So here's the thing: this isn't just about lifting weights, it's about staying active, engaged, and defying the expectations people have about aging. His comment about the bus seat says everything - he's proving that age is just a number when you've got the determination to keep moving.
