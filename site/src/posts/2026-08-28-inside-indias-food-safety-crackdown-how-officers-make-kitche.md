In India, food-safety officers are on a mission to ensure that what we eat is safe and healthy. With surprise inspections and rigorous enforcement, they’re tackling issues like adulteration and expired products head-on. Tukaram Mundhe in Maharashtra has been particularly active, inspecting thousands of establishments and seizing unsafe food. This crackdown is not just about raids; it's about protecting public health and raising awareness among consumers.
