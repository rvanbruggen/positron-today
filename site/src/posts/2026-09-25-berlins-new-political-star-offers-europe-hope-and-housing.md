I find it fascinating how Berlin’s leftwing party Die Linke has captured the city’s heart by focusing on the housing crisis. Their lead candidate, Elif Eralp, promises lower rents and more public housing, which really speaks to people’s everyday struggles. This victory is a beacon of hope, especially as other parts of Germany see far-right gains. And here’s the thing - Eralp might become Berlin’s first mayor with a migration background, showing real progress. It’s a fresh, positive example of politics listening and acting on what truly matters.
