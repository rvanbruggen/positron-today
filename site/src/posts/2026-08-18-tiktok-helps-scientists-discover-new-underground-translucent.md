Isn't it amazing how TikTok can lead to scientific discoveries? A team of scientists stumbled upon a new colorless eel, Gangaichthys indonepalicus, thanks to a short video showing a peculiar fish emerging from the ground near the India-Nepal border. After some serious effort and thousands of gallons of water pumped, they finally found this unique creature, which is the first of its kind in the Gangetic Basin. This little eel, adapted to darkness and measuring just 1.5 inches, highlights the incredible biodiversity still waiting to be uncovered in India!
