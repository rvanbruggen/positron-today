Isn't it fascinating that a surprising number of bee species have magnetic properties? Researchers found that 74 out of 96 tested bees can detect the Earth's magnetic field, which helps them navigate. This discovery challenges previous assumptions that only social bees had this ability. It turns out both solitary and social bees possess this remarkable internal compass, revealing a deeper evolutionary trait in these incredible insects.
