I find it fascinating that Cato van Schyndel, at just 27, is pushing for citizens to have more say over their health data. So: she’s all about empowering people to control what happens with their personal information. Now, this is crucial because health data is sensitive and should be handled with care and respect. Which brings me to the bigger picture - giving people ownership can boost trust and innovation in healthcare. And here’s the thing, it’s not just about data, it’s about dignity and transparency.
