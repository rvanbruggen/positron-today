Volvo is taking a big step forward with its new hazard alert system that allows cars to communicate about dangers on the road. This means that when one Volvo spots a risk, like an animal or a cyclist, it can alert other Volvos nearby. It's a fantastic way to enhance safety, especially for vulnerable road users. With this connected technology, Volvo is not just looking out for its own drivers but also sharing information with other brands and traffic management centers.
