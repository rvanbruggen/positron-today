I find it absolutely fascinating that scientists have discovered a new species of flowering plant called Thismia daemona in Thailand! This unique plant, known as the 'devil flower,' lacks chlorophyll and gets its nutrients from underground fungi. It was found in a surprising high-altitude environment, which is quite unusual for its genus. With fewer than 50 known individuals, this species is critically endangered, making its conservation crucial for the future.
