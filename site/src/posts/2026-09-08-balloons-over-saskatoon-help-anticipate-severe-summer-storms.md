This summer, Saskatoon has been seeing big white weather balloons floating high in the sky, part of an initiative by Environment Canada. These balloons, filled with helium or hydrogen, carry a device that collects crucial atmospheric data, helping forecasters predict severe weather. It's fascinating how they gather information from different elevations, providing a vertical snapshot of the atmosphere. This pilot program is a great step towards improving weather predictions in Saskatchewan, and I can't wait to see how it develops!
