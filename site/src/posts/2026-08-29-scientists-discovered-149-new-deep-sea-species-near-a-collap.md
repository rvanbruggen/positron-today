I find it absolutely fascinating that an expedition in the Indian Ocean has uncovered 149 new deep-sea species! This area, particularly the volcanic seamounts, has been largely overlooked, and researchers are finally shedding light on its incredible biodiversity. They even named one of the newly discovered calderas the Eye of Sauron, which adds a fun twist to the scientific exploration. It's a reminder of how much we still have to learn about our oceans and the hidden wonders they hold.
