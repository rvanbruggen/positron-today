This article dives into the complex world of long COVID and its triggers, highlighting the journey of Amy Proal, who turned her personal health struggles into a mission for understanding post-viral syndromes. With millions affected globally, the urgency for research has never been greater, especially as funding increases to tackle these chronic conditions. Researchers are making strides in identifying the biological mechanisms behind long COVID, although many questions remain unanswered. The pandemic has undeniably spotlighted these issues, pushing the scientific community to find effective treatments and support for those suffering.
