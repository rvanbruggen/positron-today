So, it turns out that coffee isn't just about that morning brew – it's also about the trees that grow above it! In the Western Ghats, shade-grown coffee plantations create a vibrant ecosystem, providing homes for birds, insects, and even mammals. Research shows that these canopies not only support wildlife but also enhance coffee production by protecting soil and promoting pollination. It's a beautiful reminder of how agriculture can coexist with nature, benefiting both farmers and the environment.
