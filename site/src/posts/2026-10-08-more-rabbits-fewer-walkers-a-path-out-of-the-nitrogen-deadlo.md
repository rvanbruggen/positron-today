So, there may be a way forward for development in Rotterdam’s port: a joint plan aims to restore nature in four nearby dune areas. Better water management, more rabbits and tackling invasive plants could help, while limiting recreation would ease pressure on the dunes. The Dutch government is setting aside 50 million euros, and permits for port projects could start moving again next year if the plan is put into practice. It’s encouraging progress, though areas with intensive livestock farming face a much more complicated challenge.
