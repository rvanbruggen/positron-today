So, Calabria is trying something different from the familiar €1 home offers. The Italian region is renovating houses and making them available free of charge for ten years. It’s a fresh way to give empty homes a new lease on life and attract people to the area. And here’s the thing: a renovated home with a decade of free use makes for a pretty compelling invitation.
