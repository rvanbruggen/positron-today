So, I just read about this incredible fossil discovery in Mongolia that is completely shaking up what we thought we knew about ancient mammals. For nearly 40 years, scientists assumed these creatures called zhelestids were related to modern placental mammals, but this new, nearly complete skeleton tells a very different story. It turns out their teeth were misleading, and they actually belong to a totally separate group of small, shrew-like animals. Which brings me to the beauty of fieldwork - it really shows that you cannot rely on just one piece of the puzzle to understand the whole picture. And here's the thing, it is just so exciting to see how one discovery can rewrite history.
