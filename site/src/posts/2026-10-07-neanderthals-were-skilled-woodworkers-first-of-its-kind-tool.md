So, Neanderthals were not just skilled makers of stone tools - a remarkable trove shows they also worked wood with impressive skill. Wooden objects rarely survive for thousands of years, which makes this first-of-its-kind collection especially revealing. It offers a fresh glimpse of the practical know-how Neanderthals brought to everyday life. And here's the thing: the find adds to a richer picture of their ingenuity and adaptability.
