Here’s the thing: many videos aimed at children on YouTube can be hyperstimulating, hard to vet, and sometimes misleading. Maka, a new subscription streaming app for children aged 0 to 6, offers a curated alternative with no ads, autoplay, or endless scrolling. Child development experts assess every episode using a framework developed with researchers at the Yale Child Study Center. Its launch lineup includes 25 shows, such as Sesame Street and The Wiggles, chosen with children’s development and wellbeing in mind.
