Here’s a fossil that really makes you look twice: a small whale from around 6 million years ago had an astonishingly crooked face. Named Scaphokogia huachafo, it had both nostrils shifted to the left and facial bones that differed dramatically in size and position. Researchers first wondered if the skull had been twisted after burial, but its undamaged bones show that this unusual shape was natural. Its anatomy may also offer clues to a distinctive way of hunting, possibly along the seafloor rather than in the deep ocean.
