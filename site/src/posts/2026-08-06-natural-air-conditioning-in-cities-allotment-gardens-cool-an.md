In Poland, there are around 5,000 family allotment gardens that play a crucial role in cooling urban areas. These gardens can lower surrounding temperatures by several degrees while also storing water and enhancing biodiversity. Experts suggest that with climate change intensifying, these green spaces should be integral to city infrastructure. The natural gardening methods used in these allotments not only support the environment but also promote sustainable practices.
