Ivorian designer Loza Maléombho is expanding her creative vision beyond fashion with her project Pokou, which blends local craftsmanship and artificial intelligence. Inspired by the legendary Queen Abla Pokou, she's creating a universe that includes clothing, film, art, and architecture. This initiative aims to transform African stories into valuable intellectual property while addressing the challenges faced by local fashion businesses. It's an exciting evolution that showcases the richness of African culture and creativity.
