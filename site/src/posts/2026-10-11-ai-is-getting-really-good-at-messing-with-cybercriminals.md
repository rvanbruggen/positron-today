So, AI is helping scammers - but it’s also giving defenders a clever way to fight back. Australian company Apate uses hundreds of thousands of AI bots to keep phone, text, and chat scammers busy while gathering intelligence about their schemes. The bots adopt different personas and can hold convincing conversations for hours, making them frustratingly hard for scammers to spot. They won’t stop every scam, but they could waste criminals’ time and help protect potential victims.
