So: a 2,200-year-old Roman shipwreck off Croatia is revealing two clever ways ancient shipbuilders kept water out. Researchers found one coating made from pine tar and another that mixed pine tar with beeswax. Microscopic pollen trapped in the coatings also offers clues about the plants growing near the ship when it was built or repaired. By combining chemical analysis with pollen studies, the team has uncovered fresh evidence of ancient maritime know-how.
