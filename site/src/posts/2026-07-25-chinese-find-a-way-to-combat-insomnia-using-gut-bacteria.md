So, researchers in China have discovered a fascinating way to tackle insomnia by using gut bacteria! They found that taking capsules containing fecal microbes from healthy donors significantly improved sleep quality in patients suffering from chronic insomnia. After a month, those who took the capsules experienced more efficient sleep and fewer awakenings compared to those on a placebo. This innovative approach highlights the potential of gut-brain axis therapies in addressing sleep disorders.
