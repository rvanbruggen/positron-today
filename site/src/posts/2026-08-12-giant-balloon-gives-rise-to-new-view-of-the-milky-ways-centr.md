So, an ultra-sensitive camera attached to a giant balloon has managed to capture light from a mysterious source at the centre of our Milky Way. This is super exciting because the exact origin of the high-energy γ-rays emitted from this area has puzzled scientists for ages. Now, with this innovative approach, researchers are getting closer to unraveling this cosmic mystery. It's a fantastic example of how creativity in science can lead to groundbreaking discoveries!
