Fánad Lighthouse is not just a stunning landmark; it's the heart of Ireland's most northerly Gaeltacht community. After a pandemic pause, people are flocking back to raise families and enrich this vibrant area. With around 65,000 visitors each year, the lighthouse is buzzing with activity, offering tours, renovated cottages, and a café. It's a social enterprise that reinvests profits into the community, promoting the Irish language and culture, which is so vital for the area's future.
