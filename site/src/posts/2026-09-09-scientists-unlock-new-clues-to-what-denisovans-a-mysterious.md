So, researchers have made exciting discoveries about Denisovans, our ancient relatives, by analyzing bones found in a cave in China. These findings reveal not only what they looked like but also how they thrived over 130,000 years ago. It turns out that Denisovans might have resembled modern humans more than Neanderthals, which is fascinating! This research opens up new avenues for understanding our shared history with these ancient hominins.
