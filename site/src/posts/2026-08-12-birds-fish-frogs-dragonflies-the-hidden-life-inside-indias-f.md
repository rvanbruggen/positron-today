Ponds across India are vibrant ecosystems teeming with life, from fish and frogs to dragonflies and birds. Each region has its own unique pond, like the pukur in Bengal or the johad in Rajasthan, which supports a diverse range of wildlife. These water bodies not only provide food and shelter but also play a crucial role in the local environment. It's fascinating to see how something as simple as a pond can be a hub of activity and biodiversity.
