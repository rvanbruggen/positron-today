In Canada’s Arctic, a new generation of Inuit creatives is drawing inspiration from identity and heritage. Their art offers a vivid way to express Inuit perspectives and experiences. By bringing their cultural roots into contemporary creative work, these artists are helping keep traditions meaningful for a new generation. So, their work also invites audiences to see the Arctic through Inuit eyes.
