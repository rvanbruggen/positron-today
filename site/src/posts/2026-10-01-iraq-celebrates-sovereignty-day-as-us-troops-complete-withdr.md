Iraq is buzzing with joy as it marks Sovereignty Day, celebrating the full withdrawal of US and international forces after more than ten years. The country kicked off a four-day holiday filled with nighttime celebrations, reflecting a new chapter of independence and hope. It’s a moment of pride and relief for many Iraqis who have lived through challenging times. And here’s the thing - this milestone feels like a fresh start, a chance to focus on rebuilding and moving forward with confidence.
