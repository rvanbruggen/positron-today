I’ve been really inspired by the farmers in Ireland who are stepping up to create wildlife havens on their land. The ReFarm project in Wicklow is a fantastic example, helping farmers implement biodiversity actions without the hassle of paperwork. With a focus on creating wildlife ponds, they’re not just enhancing the landscape but also supporting local ecosystems. It’s amazing to see how quickly and effectively these initiatives can take shape, proving that farmers are indeed key players in restoring our natural environment.
