So, Rome has a brand new museum near the Colosseum, and it's showcasing some spectacular ancient artworks! After a long restoration, a stunning fresco and a well-preserved mosaic are finally being revealed to the public. The fresco, called 'The Painted City', offers a bird's-eye view of a walled harbor city, and it's the largest of its kind from ancient Rome. Visitors can currently see these treasures in small groups, making it a unique experience for art lovers.
