How exciting is this? The area around Easter Island is set to become one of the largest indigenous-managed ocean reserves in the world, covering over 270,000 square miles! The indigenous Rapa Nui people will have a majority on the governing council, ensuring their voices are heard in protecting their marine environment. This initiative not only honors their rich Polynesian heritage but also promises to safeguard biodiversity for future generations. It’s a fantastic step towards sustainable ocean management!
