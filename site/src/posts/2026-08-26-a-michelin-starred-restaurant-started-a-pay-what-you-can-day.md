So, here's the exciting news! Masala y Maíz, a Michelin-starred restaurant in Mexico City, kicked off a 'Pay What You Can' day during the pandemic to make fine dining accessible to everyone. This initiative has blossomed, with 100 restaurants from 14 countries joining in for the next event on August 26, 2026. It's all about community, trust, and making hospitality inclusive, which is just fantastic! I love how this movement is reshaping the dining experience and bringing people together across different backgrounds.
