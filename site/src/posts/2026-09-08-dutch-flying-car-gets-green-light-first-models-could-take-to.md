How exciting is this? The Dutch company Pal-V has received the green light from the European aviation authority EASA to produce flying cars! With three wheels and foldable rotor blades, this vehicle is like a mini helicopter. After a year and a half of testing, we could see the first flying cars in the air by 2028. Imagine transforming your vehicle into a flying machine in just two minutes!
