What a heartwarming story! A critically endangered roloway monkey named Masaya underwent a complex surgery to save her foot from amputation, and just months later, she gave birth to a healthy daughter named Lagertha. This operation was groundbreaking, as it was the first of its kind for her species, and it allowed Masaya to care for her baby without any limitations. The success of the surgery is not just a win for Masaya but also a significant boost for the conservation of her species, which is facing serious threats in the wild.
