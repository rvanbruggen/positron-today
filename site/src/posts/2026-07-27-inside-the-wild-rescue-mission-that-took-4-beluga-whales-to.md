So, imagine the incredible journey of four beluga whales from Niagara Falls to Chicago! The team worked tirelessly to ensure the whales were as comfortable as possible during their unprecedented plane ride. With careful planning and a lot of love, they successfully transported these magnificent creatures to their new home at the Shedd Aquarium. Now, the belugas are settling in and adjusting to their surroundings, all while being monitored by dedicated experts.
