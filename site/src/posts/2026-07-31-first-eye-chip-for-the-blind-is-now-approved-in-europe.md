So, here's some exciting news! The PRIMA eye implant has just received its CE marking in Europe, marking a significant milestone in treating age-related macular degeneration. This tiny chip, placed under the retina, could help millions regain part of their lost vision. With around 480,000 people in Germany affected by late-stage AMD, this innovation offers hope where there was none before.
