In India, many forests are seen as sacred spaces, where ancient rules protect nature from exploitation. These sacred groves, often rich in biodiversity, are maintained by communities through cultural traditions passed down through generations. As modern development threatens these areas, the importance of these age-old practices is becoming more relevant than ever. They not only safeguard ecosystems but also provide essential resources for local communities.
