In Karnataka, Dr. P Manohara Upadhya has dedicated his life savings to create a 600-tree gene bank for wild mangoes, preserving the unique flavors and stories of local varieties. These mangoes are not just fruits; they represent a cultural heritage that is fading due to modern agricultural practices. His project, Maavu Mantapa, aims to collect and grow these varieties before they vanish completely. With the help of a dedicated team, he is ensuring that each tree carries a name and a story, keeping Karnataka's mango legacy alive.
