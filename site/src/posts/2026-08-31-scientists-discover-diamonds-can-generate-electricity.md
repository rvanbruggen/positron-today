So, researchers at the University of Hong Kong have made a groundbreaking discovery that diamonds can actually generate electricity! This challenges a century-old belief that diamonds were non-piezoelectric. By creating ultrathin, flexible diamond membranes, they found that bending these membranes produced stable voltage signals. This could lead to exciting applications in medical devices and tiny power systems, making diamonds not just beautiful but also functional!
