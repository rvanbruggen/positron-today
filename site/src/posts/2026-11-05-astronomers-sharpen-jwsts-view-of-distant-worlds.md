Astronomers have found a way to make the James Webb Space Telescope’s view of distant worlds sharper, helping reveal details that were harder to see before. The improvement helps researchers distinguish faint planetary signals from the glare of their host stars. That could offer a clearer look at exoplanets and their atmospheres, and help scientists compare these worlds with planets in our own solar system. It’s an exciting step toward making even more of JWST’s remarkable observations.
