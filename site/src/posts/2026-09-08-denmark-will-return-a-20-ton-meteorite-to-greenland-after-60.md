How exciting is this? After 60 years, the Agpalilik meteorite is finally heading back to Greenland, where it was discovered in 1963! Danish and Greenlandic officials are working together to finalize the transfer, which holds great cultural significance for the Inuit people. This meteorite not only represents a piece of history but also has been used traditionally for making tools. I can't wait to see where it will be displayed in Greenland!
