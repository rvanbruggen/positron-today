Before the September sun rises, seasonal workers hand-pick jasmine in Grasse, when its delicate scent is at its best. Carole Biancalana, a fourth-generation grower, supplies roses, tuberoses and jasmine exclusively to Dior, with every harvest carefully timed and quickly processed. After decades of decline, Dior’s investment has helped revive local cultivation, and the region is now home to dozens of growers, including 15 partner farms for the perfume house. This patient craft keeps French know-how alive and brings a rare, natural character to luxury fragrances.
