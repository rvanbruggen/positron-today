I find it exciting that tiny magnetic structures called skyrmions could one day help computers process information more like the human brain, while using far less electricity. In a study published in Nature Nanotechnology, researchers explored how these magnetic vortices behave and how they can be controlled. For the first time, they filmed a skyrmion lattice melting in real time while changing the shape and size of individual skyrmions. This closer look could help scientists build smaller, more energy-efficient computing systems and advance quantum technologies.
