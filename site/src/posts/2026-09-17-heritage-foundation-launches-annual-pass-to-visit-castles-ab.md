So here's something really exciting - the Heritage Foundation is rolling out an annual pass that gives you unlimited access to explore castles, abbeys and all sorts of historic monuments across the country. I think this is brilliant because it opens up cultural treasures to more people without them having to worry about entry fees adding up. What makes it even better is that this kind of initiative encourages repeat visits and deeper connections with our architectural heritage. It's a genuine step toward making history accessible to everyone who's curious about it.
