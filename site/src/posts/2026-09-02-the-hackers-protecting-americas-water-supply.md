So, it turns out that America's water supply is quite vulnerable to cyberattacks, and that's a big concern! Many water utilities are small and lack the resources to defend against these threats. But here's the exciting part: volunteer hackers are stepping up to help protect our access to clean water. They're working hard to identify vulnerabilities and strengthen security, making a real difference in this critical infrastructure.
