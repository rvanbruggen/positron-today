So: scientists have just spotted the Milky Way’s first microblazar, a tiny version of a blazar that shoots out superfast jets of plasma. This cosmic duo, a black hole and a star orbiting each other, was hiding behind dust and overlooked for decades. Now, with jets traveling at three quarters the speed of light aimed at Earth, astronomers are buzzing with excitement. And here's the thing - this discovery opens a new window to study how such systems evolve and interact with the galaxy around them.
