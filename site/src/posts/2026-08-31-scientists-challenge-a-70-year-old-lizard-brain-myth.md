So, it turns out the brain's evolution is way more complex than just a battle between the 'lizard brain' and our rational side. Researchers from Georgia Tech are suggesting that instead of stacking newer parts on top of older ones, it's all about how these areas are wired together. This new perspective could change how we understand brain evolution and even influence the development of AI systems. It's fascinating to think about how interconnected our brain regions really are!
