Can you believe it? Sixty years ago, NASA's Lunar Orbiter 1 captured the first-ever photo of Earth from the moon! This historic moment paved the way for iconic images like 'Earthrise' taken during the Apollo 8 mission. Recently, astronaut Christina Koch added to this legacy by snapping a stunning 'Earthset' during the Artemis II mission. It's amazing to see how our view of Earth from space continues to inspire wonder and curiosity!
