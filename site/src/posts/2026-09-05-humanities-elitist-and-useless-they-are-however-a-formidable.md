So, the article dives into the importance of humanities in education, arguing that they are far from elitist or useless. Instead, they cultivate critical thinking and creativity, preparing students to be free citizens. The author emphasizes that the value of humanities lies in their ability to teach what is truly important, rather than just what is useful. In a world that often prioritizes practicality, the humanities remain a vital asset for future generations.
