I find it absolutely fascinating that a small area of the Amazon could be home to around 40,000 insect species, many of which are completely new to science! Hundreds of scientists are working together to catalog these tiny creatures, using innovative methods like DNA barcoding to identify them. They’ve set up an impressive array of traps that reach from the forest floor to the treetops, continuously collecting specimens for over a year. This project not only highlights the incredible biodiversity of the Amazon but also the importance of preserving such unique ecosystems.
