Isn't it fascinating that Mohenjo-daro, an ancient city, actually became more equal as it grew? Unlike the usual story of rising inequality, this 4,000-year-old urban center showed a narrowing wealth gap over time. Researchers found that resources were shared widely, with practical infrastructure benefiting everyone rather than just a powerful elite. This challenges our assumptions about economic growth and inequality, highlighting a unique path in history.
