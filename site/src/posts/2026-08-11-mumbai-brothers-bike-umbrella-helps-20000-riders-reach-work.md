The arrival of the monsoon can be tough for two-wheeler riders in India, but brothers Kashyap and Vidhey have come up with a brilliant solution. Their SEPAL Shield is a detachable canopy that protects riders from rain and wind, making commutes much more comfortable. After three years of testing and refining, this innovation is now helping over 20,000 riders stay dry on their way to work or school. It’s amazing to see how a simple idea can transform the daily lives of so many people!
