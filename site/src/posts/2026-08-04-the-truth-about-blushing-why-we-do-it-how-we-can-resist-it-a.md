Blushing is such a fascinating human expression, and it turns out it has deep roots in our social behavior. It’s a signal of embarrassment that’s completely genuine and can even enhance trustworthiness among us. While many might dread blushing, it actually serves a purpose in helping us navigate social situations and express remorse. Interestingly, those who blush frequently often feel more negatively about it than those who don’t, which is a curious twist on perception.
