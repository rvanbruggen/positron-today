In Ladakh, snow leopards have long been seen as a threat by pastoralists who lose livestock to the elusive cats. Now, the Snow Leopard Conservancy – India Trust is helping communities protect their animals with stronger corrals, safer herding practices and better veterinary care. And here’s the thing: it’s also creating new ways for local people to benefit from conservation, including homestays and felted-wool crafts. These efforts are helping people and snow leopards live alongside one another, while supporting a healthy Himalayan ecosystem.
