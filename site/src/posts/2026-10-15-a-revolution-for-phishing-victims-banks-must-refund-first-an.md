So, this proposed change would give phishing victims their money back straight away, instead of making them wait while responsibility is investigated. The order matters: people facing the consequences of fraud could receive immediate financial relief. Banks would still examine what happened and who was at fault, but only after the refund. It could mark a major shift in how victims are protected when digital fraud strikes.
