So, a small piece of Neanderthal DNA carried by some people today is associated with an almost 10 percent lower risk of schizophrenia. In UK Biobank data, the variant was also linked to a slightly smaller occipital lobe, a brain region involved in visual processing. Researchers say the DNA lies near a gene involved in early brain development, though exactly how the two interact remains unclear. The variant was not associated with depression, bipolar disorder, ADHD or intelligence, and scientists are now searching the genome for more clues from our ancient relatives.
