So here's what I find really beautiful - across India, entrepreneurs are looking at temple flowers not as waste, but as a genuine resource. Startups like Phool, HolyWaste, Aaruhi Enterprise and Yuvan are collecting discarded flowers from temples and transforming them into incense, soaps, compost and innovative biomaterials like Fleather, a leather alternative. What makes this even better is that these ventures aren't just solving an environmental problem - they're creating livelihoods, especially for women involved in sorting and processing. And which brings me to the real insight here: when you treat something sacred with respect, you unlock possibilities that benefit both the planet and the people working to protect it.
