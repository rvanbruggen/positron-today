Researchers have discovered a protein called SET that could make glioblastoma, one of the deadliest brain cancers, more vulnerable to existing treatments. Instead of replacing current therapies, this approach aims to enhance their effectiveness. By blocking SET and related proteins, they found that glioblastoma cells became less able to survive radiation. This exciting breakthrough could pave the way for new strategies to tackle this challenging disease.
