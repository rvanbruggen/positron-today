So: two Belgian restaurants have earned places among the world’s ten best plant-based dining spots in the 2026 We’re Smart Awards. Ghent’s Vrijmoed, led by chef Michaël Vrijmoed, came in fifth, while Brussels restaurant Humus x Hortense, run by Nicolas Decloedt and Caroline Baerten, ranked ninth. The awards were presented in Bruges on Monday, putting Belgian talent firmly in the spotlight. Lima’s Central took the top prize, followed by Piazza Duomo in Alba and Flore in Amsterdam.
