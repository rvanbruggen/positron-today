Isn't it fascinating how something as simple as sal leaf plates is making a comeback in Europe? While these traditional plates have faded from Indian streets, they’re now celebrated as eco-friendly alternatives to plastic in restaurants and hotels across the continent. The craftsmanship behind these plates is rooted in generations of tribal knowledge, and they’re not just biodegradable but also unique in their appearance. This shift not only supports sustainable practices but also uplifts the livelihoods of forest-dependent communities in India.
