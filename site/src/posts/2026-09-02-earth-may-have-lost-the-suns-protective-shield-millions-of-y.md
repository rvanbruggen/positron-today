So, it turns out the Sun has played a much bigger role in Earth's climate than we thought! Two recent NASA studies suggest that the heliosphere, the Sun's protective bubble, may have shrunk at times, exposing our planet to harsh interstellar conditions. This could explain some ancient climate shifts and even how the young Earth stayed warm enough for life. Isn't it fascinating to think about how our cosmic environment shapes us?
