Mexico is using AI to speed up breast cancer detection as long waits and a shortage of specialists make screening more difficult. At one hospital, the technology flags suspicious scans for specialists to review. They can assess the images remotely, helping more people access expert attention. It’s a promising way to bring potential cases to specialists sooner.
