I was absolutely fascinated to learn that the indri, a lemur from Madagascar, actually sings just like an opera star! It turns out these primates adjust their mouth shape to hit high notes, which is a technique we usually only associate with trained human vocalists. And here's the thing - they do this to make their calls travel further through the dense rainforest. It is such a brilliant example of convergent evolution, showing how nature finds similar solutions across millions of years. I just love seeing these unexpected connections between us and our animal cousins.
