The United States and Denmark have struck a deal on Greenland after Donald Trump threatened to seize control of the strategic Arctic island. Washington now has an expanded role in Arctic security while Danish and Greenlandic sovereignty remains intact - a diplomatic solution that gives both sides something to claim as a win. The agreement comes just days after Trump's ultimatum, following his aggressive moves in Venezuela, which had alarmed Europe about the stability of transatlantic alliances. So here's the thing - this deal shows how careful diplomacy can defuse even the most provocative territorial threats without completely backing down either party.
