So, a large analysis of nearly 10,000 people found that escitalopram performed best among the antidepressants studied at helping prevent depression from returning. Agomelatine and duloxetine also ranked well, while the differences between medicines were modest. Experts say the findings are useful for conversations between patients and doctors, but they are not a reason for someone doing well on their current treatment to switch. The right option depends on individual needs and preferences, and support can also include talking therapies.
