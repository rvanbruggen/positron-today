A fascinating Finnish study has revealed that more screen time in childhood may actually lead to better cognitive processing in adolescence. Researchers found that the type of screen activity matters, with engaging digital tasks promoting active thinking and creativity. It's a reminder that screen time isn't just harmful; balance with physical activity is key. The study also highlights the complex relationship between physical activity and cognition, showing different impacts for boys and girls.
