Isn't it exciting to think about wearing an exoskeleton in the near future? Robotic exoskeletons have rapidly evolved from experimental designs to consumer-friendly gadgets. They’re not the bulky suits you might imagine from sci-fi movies, but sleek devices that could soon be part of our daily lives. As this technology advances, we might find ourselves walking, running, and jumping with a little extra help sooner than we think!
