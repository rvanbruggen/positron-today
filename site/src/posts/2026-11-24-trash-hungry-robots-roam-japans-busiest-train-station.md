So: Shinjuku Station in Tokyo has completed a roughly 10-week trial of three cheerful, mobile trash bins. They follow programmed routes through the station from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., then head back to the collection area when they’re full. With nearly three million passengers passing through each day, JR East hopes the robots can ease the workload for sanitation workers. Now, the big test is whether they can collect rubbish safely without getting in the way of the crowds.
