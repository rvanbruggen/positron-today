This year, the Ig Nobel Prizes have made their debut in Europe, moving from the US due to safety concerns. The event, organized by the Annals of Improbable Research, awarded ten research teams with a quirky prize and a shower of paper airplanes. Among the winners, Brazilian biologists studied snake bites in a rather unusual way, while Swiss researchers buried underwear to assess soil health. It's a fun reminder of how science can be both serious and delightfully silly!
