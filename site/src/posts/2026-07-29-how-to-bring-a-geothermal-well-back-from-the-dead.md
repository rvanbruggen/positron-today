So, Zanskar has really turned things around with their geothermal power plant in New Mexico! After facing significant heat loss, they drilled a new well and now it's one of the most productive geothermal sites in the US. This success story is not just a win for Zanskar but also a hopeful sign for the geothermal industry, which is seeing renewed interest and investment. It’s fascinating how technology and a bit of luck can breathe new life into what many thought was a failing system.
