So, crows can judge which way moving dots are travelling in an average of just 235 milliseconds, while staying remarkably accurate. In experiments, their accuracy reached nearly 100 percent when the visual signal was clear. Brain recordings showed that neurons began distinguishing between the choices only 70 milliseconds after the dots appeared. And here's the fascinating part: despite having a very different brain structure from humans, crows seem to use similar decision-making processes, though the reason for their speed remains unknown.
