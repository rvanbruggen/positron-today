When Amey Desai's grandfather was diagnosed with Parkinson's, it sparked a journey to create a device that helps patients walk more freely. His invention, called WALK, uses gentle vibrations and mild electrical stimulation to assist those experiencing freezing of gait. This innovative wearable technology has transformed the lives of many, including Anima Guria, who now walks with confidence. It's amazing to see how personal experiences can lead to such impactful solutions!
