In Tamil Nadu, the palmyra tree is not just a tree; it's a lifeline for many communities. Dr. Preethi Hari Babu has created tourism trails that connect visitors with the climbers, who have been harvesting from these trees for generations. This initiative not only educates people about the tree's importance but also helps sustain the livelihoods of the climbers. It's amazing to see how tourism can foster community engagement and environmental conservation at the same time!
