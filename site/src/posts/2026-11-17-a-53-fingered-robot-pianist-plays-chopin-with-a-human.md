In Prato, Italy, robot pianist TeoTronico joined concert pianist Roberto Prosseda for a musical meeting on 12 November. They took turns playing Chopin before performing together, and the robot also tackled works by Mozart and Rimsky-Korsakov with remarkable precision. Created in 2008, TeoTronico uses electromagnets and computer instructions to play, and can even chat thanks to Google's Gemini. And here's the thing: Prosseda says technical perfection is impressive, but music also depends on human emotion and connection.
