Researchers have created a bioengineered chewing gum that significantly reduces harmful microbes linked to head and neck cancer. In tests, it lowered HPV levels by an impressive 93% in saliva samples, showcasing its potential as a new treatment option. This gum not only targets dangerous bacteria but also preserves beneficial oral flora, which is a huge plus. With the need for innovative cancer therapies, this could be a game-changer in how we approach prevention and treatment.
