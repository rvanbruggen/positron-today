In Mukalla, Yemen, a special climate phenomenon called Al-Baldah brings a refreshing break from the scorching summer heat, attracting beachgoers from all over. Young entrepreneurs like Abdulrahman Badr are seizing the moment to sell traditional snacks, helping to support their families. The beaches, usually quiet, come alive with laughter, games, and the joy of swimming. However, amidst the fun, some locals express concerns about the ongoing struggles in their lives, highlighting the need for more substantial support.
