Imagine a satellite the size of a carry-on bag that could help us understand the universe's early days! The CosmoCube is designed to travel to the moon and use its far side to escape Earth's noise, allowing it to detect faint signals from the cosmic dark age. This ambitious project aims to gather crucial data about hydrogen and dark matter, which played a key role in forming the first stars. With a potential launch in five years, the race is on as other countries eye the moon's far side too!
