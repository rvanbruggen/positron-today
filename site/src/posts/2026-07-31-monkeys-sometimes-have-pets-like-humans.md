Isn't it fascinating that monkeys can form friendships with other species, just like we do? A recent study reviewed over 300 cases of these inter-species bonds, revealing that primates are quite socially curious. From tufted gray langurs petting squirrels to capuchin monkeys raising a baby marmoset, these relationships show a depth of empathy and social flexibility. This research not only sheds light on primate behavior but also hints at the evolutionary roots of companionship between humans and animals.
