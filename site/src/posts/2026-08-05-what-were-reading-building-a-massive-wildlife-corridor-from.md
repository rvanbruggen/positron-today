I just love the idea of reconnecting wildlife habitats, especially with the Yellowstone to Yukon Conservation Initiative! This ambitious project aims to create a 2,100-mile corridor across the Rockies, which is just incredible. It’s already protected over 24 million acres and built more than 200 wildlife crossings. Plus, there's exciting tech being developed to help whales navigate safely in busy shipping areas. How amazing is that?
