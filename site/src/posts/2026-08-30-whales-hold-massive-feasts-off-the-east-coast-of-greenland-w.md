It's fascinating to see how humpback and minke whales are now flocking to the east coast of Greenland in droves. They seem to be following their food source, which is moving north due to warming ocean waters. With the melting sea ice, these magnificent creatures are finding it much more comfortable to swim in these waters. Researchers estimate that thousands of these whales are feasting on krill and capelin, with their feeding habits dramatically reshaping the local ecosystem.
