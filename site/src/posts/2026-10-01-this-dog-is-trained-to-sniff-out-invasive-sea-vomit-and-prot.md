I find it fascinating how Uisge, a clever Labrador retriever in Scotland, uses her incredible nose to sniff out invasive sea squirts known as 'sea vomit.' These pesky creatures threaten native oyster reefs and marine biodiversity, but Uisge’s training helps protect these vital ecosystems. Her handler, Dr. Texa Sim, combines marine biology and dog training to make a real difference. And here’s the thing - Uisge is not just smart but driven, with enough energy for three dogs, making her a true hero of the coast.
