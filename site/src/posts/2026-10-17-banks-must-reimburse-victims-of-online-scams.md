So, a new European court ruling brings an important change for people targeted by online scams. Banks must now immediately reimburse victims of unauthorised online payments, even when they have been grossly negligent. The ruling clarifies how existing Belgian and European rules apply to scams such as phishing, where fraudsters pose as trusted organisations to steal account details. It marks a major shift from current practices and gives victims clearer protection.
