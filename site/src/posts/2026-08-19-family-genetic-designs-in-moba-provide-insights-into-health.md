This article highlights the incredible potential of family-based genetic studies, particularly through the Norwegian Mother, Father and Child Cohort Study (MoBa). By analyzing genetic data from families, researchers can better understand how both direct and indirect genetic effects influence health outcomes. It's fascinating to see how parental genetics can shape offspring traits, offering a clearer picture than traditional population-based studies. MoBa's extensive data on over 100,000 children promises to advance our knowledge of health and functioning significantly.
