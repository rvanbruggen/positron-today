There's a fascinating new trend in the search for naturally occurring hydrogen gas, and it's happening underground! Researchers are discovering hydrogen resources in various regions, including the Midwest of the U.S., where geological conditions are just right. Companies like HyTerra and Koloma are leading the charge, exploring how much hydrogen can be produced naturally and how to capture it effectively. With innovative methods like stimulated geologic hydrogen, the future of clean hydrogen production looks promising and could revolutionize the energy landscape.
