A fascinating new study is shaking up our understanding of bonnethead sharks! Researchers found that both male and female sharks develop rounder heads as they age, contradicting earlier beliefs that males only get their pointed snouts upon reaching maturity. This research, conducted in Florida, also revealed that diet differences don't explain the distinct head shapes between the sexes. It's exciting to see how science continues to uncover the mysteries of these unique creatures!
