So, have you heard about the incredible domino chains in kidney donation? It’s a game changer for those waiting for transplants. A man named Mark Scotch, after a chance meeting, donated his kidney and helped save another life through a unique voucher system. This approach not only matches donors and recipients effectively but also raises awareness about the urgent need for living donors. It’s inspiring to see how one conversation can lead to such impactful change!
