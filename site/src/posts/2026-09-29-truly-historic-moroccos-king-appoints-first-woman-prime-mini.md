So: Morocco has just made history with King Mohammed VI appointing Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri as the country’s first female prime minister. She’s not only the first in Morocco but the second woman ever in the Arab world to hold this position. El Mansouri, with her rich background as mayor of Marrakesh and urban planning minister, called her appointment a proud moment for all Moroccan women. Now, her big task is to form a coalition government after her party won the most seats but not a majority. And here’s the thing - this new government will play a key role in shaping investments ahead of the 2030 FIFA World Cup, which Morocco will co-host.
