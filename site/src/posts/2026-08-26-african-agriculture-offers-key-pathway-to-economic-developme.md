Africa has so much potential with its vast arable land and youthful population, making agriculture a crucial sector for economic growth. With about 60% of Africans employed in agriculture, there's a real opportunity to boost food production and create jobs. However, the continent faces challenges like food insecurity and reliance on imports, which need addressing. By empowering each country to develop tailored agricultural programs, we can harness technology and local strengths to tackle these issues head-on.
