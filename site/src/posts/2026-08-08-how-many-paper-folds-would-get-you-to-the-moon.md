Isn't it fascinating how our brains think linearly while the universe is anything but? When you fold a piece of paper, its thickness doubles each time, leading to some mind-boggling exponential growth. To reach the moon's distance, you'd only need to fold a standard sheet of paper 42 times! It’s a wild concept that really shows how quickly numbers can escalate. Just imagine trying to fold that paper that many times—what a challenge!
