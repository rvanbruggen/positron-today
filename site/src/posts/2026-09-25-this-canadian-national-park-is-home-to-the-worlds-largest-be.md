Wood Buffalo National Park in Canada is not only vast but also home to the world’s largest beaver dam, which is so huge it can be seen from space! The dam stretches nearly half a mile and holds back water to protect the land from flooding. What really fascinates me is how beavers are such natural engineers, creating habitats that improve water quality and help the environment. It’s incredible that despite its size, almost no one has visited it on foot, making it a hidden marvel in this wild landscape.
