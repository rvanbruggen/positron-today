So, baby talk has been a hot topic among parents, but guess what? Science has come to the rescue! Research shows that using baby talk, or 'infant-directed speech,' is not only natural but also beneficial for language development. It turns out that this high-pitched, sing-song way of speaking helps babies grasp language patterns and even enhances their vocabulary. Isn't it fascinating how something so instinctive can have such a positive impact?
