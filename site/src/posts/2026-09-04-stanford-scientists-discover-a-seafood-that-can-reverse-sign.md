Aging is something we all experience, but what if we could actually reverse some of its signs? A fascinating study involving sea squirts has shown that dietary supplements containing plasmalogens can improve memory and even restore hair color in aged mice. Researchers found that these compounds not only helped the mice learn better but also enhanced their brain connections. This groundbreaking work opens up exciting possibilities for combating cognitive decline as we age.
