I find it fascinating how sound can shape our experience of cities in ways we rarely notice. Chris Downey, who became blind, discovered that sounds like wind chimes can help define spaces just as much as physical structures. He’s now pioneering designs that use sound to make cities more accessible and enjoyable for everyone. And here’s the thing - this approach is opening new doors for inclusive design, blending architecture and sound to create richer urban experiences.
