So, portraits offer a fascinating window onto how beauty ideals have changed across Europe. A study of images of wealthy women suggests that fuller figures, once proudly displayed, gradually gave way to slimmer silhouettes over the centuries. These painted bodies tell us not only about appearance, but also about the fashions and social values of their time. It’s a vivid reminder that ideas of beauty are always evolving.
