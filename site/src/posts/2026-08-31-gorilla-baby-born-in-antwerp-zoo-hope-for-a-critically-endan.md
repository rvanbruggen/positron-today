A baby gorilla has been born at the Antwerp Zoo, and it’s a significant moment for the critically endangered western lowland gorilla. This little one represents hope for a species that is under severe threat in the wild due to deforestation and poaching. The mother, Mayani, is doing well with her second baby, while the silverback Ayo welcomes his first offspring. The zoo is inviting the public to help name the baby, making September a special month for gorilla enthusiasts.
