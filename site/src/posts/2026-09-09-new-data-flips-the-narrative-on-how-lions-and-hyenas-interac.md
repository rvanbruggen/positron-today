So, it turns out that the relationship between lions and hyenas is a bit more complex than we thought! A recent study shows that male lions are actually the ones tracking hyenas, not the other way around. This flips the long-held belief that hyenas follow lions for food scraps. With GPS collars and extensive observations, researchers like Dr. Nancy Barker have uncovered fascinating movement patterns that reveal how these two species interact in the wild.
