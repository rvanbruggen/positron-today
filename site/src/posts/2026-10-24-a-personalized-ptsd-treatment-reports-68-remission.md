So, here’s an encouraging development for people living with PTSD: the FDA has cleared a personalized treatment that uses brain activity measurements to guide magnetic stimulation. An EEG records each patient’s brain signals, helping an AI-assisted system tailor the stimulation plan. In early clinical trial findings reported by Wave Neuroscience, symptoms fell by 52% and 68% of patients achieved remission. The full results are still being prepared for release, so these promising findings need more context and should not be treated as a direct comparison with results from other studies.
