I find it fascinating how Microsoft Research developed a machine learning system that forecasts space weather risks for nearly 67,000 substations across the US. So: it uses solar wind data combined with local geology and grid details to predict where geomagnetic storms might cause trouble. The system can warn operators 30 to 60 minutes ahead, which is a real game-changer for protecting our power grids. And here's the thing - it detected almost 80% of major space weather events during tests, showing impressive accuracy and promise.
