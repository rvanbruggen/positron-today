At Polytechnique Montréal, researchers are gearing up to tackle future pandemics with innovative biomedicine. Their lab is part of the federal Ramp Up initiative, which aims to enhance Canada's biomanufacturing capabilities. Head researcher Gregory De Crescenzo emphasizes the importance of preparation, stating it's not a question of if, but when the next pandemic will strike. By fostering collaboration across sectors, they hope to avoid past mistakes and build a more resilient healthcare system.
