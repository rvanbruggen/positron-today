So: Hugging Face has released Carbon-X, an open-source AI model that lets people ask conversational questions about DNA and gene activity. Trained on genomic sequences and natural-language examples, the 14-billion-parameter model can run locally on a laptop, keeping queries off company servers. Researchers could use it to explore whether mutations may be linked to disease and inform drug or gene-therapy research, while others can ask questions about small regions of a genome. And here's the thing: Carbon-X has not yet been validated in lab experiments, so its predictions still need careful testing.
