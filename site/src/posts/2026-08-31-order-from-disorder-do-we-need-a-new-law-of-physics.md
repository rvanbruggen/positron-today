So, Hazen and Wong are proposing a fascinating idea that challenges our understanding of physics and evolution. They suggest that just as disorder increases over time, there’s also a natural tendency for order to emerge from chaos. This ‘second arrow of time’ could explain how complex systems evolve, from atoms to intelligent life. Their book, Time’s Second Arrow, invites us to rethink the principles that govern both living and non-living systems, and I can't wait to see how this idea sparks discussions in the scientific community!
