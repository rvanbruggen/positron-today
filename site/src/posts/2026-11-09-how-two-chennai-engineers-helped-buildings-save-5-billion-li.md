So, two engineers from Chennai have turned a simple question about a dripping tap into a clever way to help buildings save water. Their IIT Madras-incubated startup, EarthFokus, uses monitoring tools to spot unusual water use and hidden leaks, while other solutions help reduce everyday consumption and water lost through evaporation. The company says its systems have helped more than 130 commercial buildings save over five billion litres of water. And here's the thing: by giving building managers a clearer, more timely picture of water use, EarthFokus can help them tackle waste before it adds up.
