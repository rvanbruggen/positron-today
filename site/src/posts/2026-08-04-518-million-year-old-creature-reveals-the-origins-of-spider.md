Scientists have made an incredible discovery about the origins of spider fangs, tracing them back to a tiny marine creature called Urokodia that lived 518 million years ago. This fascinating fossil, found in China, shows early forms of chelicerae, the appendages that would evolve into the fangs we recognize today. Using X-ray technology, researchers uncovered preserved soft anatomy, revealing a glimpse into the evolutionary past of chelicerates. It's amazing to think that this small creature was part of a rich ecosystem teeming with life during a pivotal time in animal evolution.
