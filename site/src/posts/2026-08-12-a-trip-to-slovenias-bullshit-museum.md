So, have you ever heard of the Bullshit Museum in Ljubljana? It's a quirky spot that showcases the unique humor of the Balkans, with mannequins and AI-generated images bringing jokes to life. The founder, Borut Kokelj, believes that humor can solve problems, especially in tough times. The museum dives into the cultural history of the former Yugoslavia, reminding us how laughter can connect people across different identities.
