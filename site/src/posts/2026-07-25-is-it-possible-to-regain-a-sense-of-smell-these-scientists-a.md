At Ohio State University, Kai Zhao is on an exciting mission to tackle smell loss, a condition often overlooked in the medical field. Unlike other senses, smell is still a bit of a mystery, and Zhao is exploring innovative solutions like wearable devices that enhance airflow to the olfactory region. His prototypes, including a nasal foam plug and a swimmer's clip, have shown promising results in trials, helping participants regain their sense of smell. It's a fascinating step forward, and while not everyone may benefit, the potential for restoring this vital sense is truly inspiring.
