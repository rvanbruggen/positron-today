So, here’s encouraging news: a study of 7,500 people links healthy eating with slower biological aging and a longer life. Researchers compared different ways of eating, and the surprising takeaway is that no single diet has to be the answer. What matters is choosing nourishing foods as part of a healthy overall pattern. That makes eating well feel refreshingly flexible.
