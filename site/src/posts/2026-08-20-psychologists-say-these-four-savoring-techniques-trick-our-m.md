So, happiness is something we can all work on, right? Arthur C. Brooks shares four savoring techniques that can really shift our mindset towards joy. First, he suggests smiling to trick our brains into feeling happier. Then, being present in the moment and sharing positive experiences with others can enhance our happiness even more. Lastly, he talks about mental time travel, which helps us reminisce about good times or look forward to future joys.
