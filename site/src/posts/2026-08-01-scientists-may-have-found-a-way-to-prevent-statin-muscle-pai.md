It's fantastic news for millions who rely on statins to manage cholesterol! Researchers at McMaster University have discovered a biological pathway that explains the muscle pain some patients experience. This breakthrough could lead to treatments that allow people to benefit from statins without the discomfort. The study highlights a surprising link between the immune system and muscle cell metabolism, paving the way for safer and more effective medications in the future.
