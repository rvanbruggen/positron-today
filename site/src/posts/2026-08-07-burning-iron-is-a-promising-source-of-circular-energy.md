So, researchers at Eindhoven University are exploring the burning of iron as a CO2-free energy source. This innovative approach could potentially replace fossil fuels, which is super exciting! Iron, in its powdered form, is safe to transport and has a higher energy density than hydrogen. Plus, the process produces no greenhouse gases, making it a fantastic circular solution for energy needs.
