A lizard’s tail, preserved in amber for 99 million years, offers a remarkable glimpse into the distant past. The fossil’s extraordinary preservation makes this small fragment a valuable record of ancient life. So, even a single tail can help bring the world of long ago into clearer view. It’s a fascinating reminder of how amber can preserve traces of life across immense stretches of time.
