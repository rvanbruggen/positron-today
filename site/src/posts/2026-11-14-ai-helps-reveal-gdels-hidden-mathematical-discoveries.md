I’m fascinated by how much can still be hiding in a great thinker’s notebooks. Finnish logician Jan von Plato used AI model Claude to help analyse 16 of Kurt Gödel’s densely written notebooks, whose German shorthand had slowed the work to about a page a week. In just days, the AI produced a wide-ranging digest that pointed to hundreds of previously unknown theorems, results anticipated decades early and 14 unexplored themes in logic. Von Plato says every page still needed checking, but the project shows how AI can connect ideas across a thousand pages and open fresh doors into Gödel’s remarkable legacy.
