A Swiss-designed ball-and-socket implant is offering a new option for people with severe thumb arthritis, replacing the damaged joint while leaving the trapezium bone in place. So, unlike traditional surgery that removes the bone, this approach can provide immediate stability and may speed recovery. Trial results show improved range of motion that remained stronger over time, and patients may leave the outpatient procedure with only a light bandage. Used more than 130,000 times in Europe, the implant is now reaching patients in the United States after FDA approval, though doctors still recommend trying nonsurgical care first.
