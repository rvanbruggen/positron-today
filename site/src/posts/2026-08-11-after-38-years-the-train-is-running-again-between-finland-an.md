It's such exciting news that after 38 long years, the train service between Finland and Sweden is finally back on track! This connection is not just about transportation; it symbolizes renewed ties and opportunities for both countries. Travelers can now enjoy the scenic journey across the Baltic Sea, which is bound to attract tourists and boost local economies. I can't help but feel a sense of nostalgia and joy for all the adventures that await!
