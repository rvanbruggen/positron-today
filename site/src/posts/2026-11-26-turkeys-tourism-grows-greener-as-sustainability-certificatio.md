Turkey welcomed nearly 64 million international visitors in 2025, including 1.9 million from Poland, whose visitor numbers have risen by 117 percent since 2019. Now, the country is stepping up a sustainability programme developed with the Global Sustainable Tourism Council and introduced in stages since 2022. Its four pillars cover responsible management, environmental protection, cultural heritage and benefits for local communities and workers. Certification is gaining momentum for hotels, restaurants and entire cities, helping Turkey welcome visitors while caring for the places they come to enjoy.
