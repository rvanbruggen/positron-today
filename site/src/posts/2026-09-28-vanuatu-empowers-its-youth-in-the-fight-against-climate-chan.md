So, I just read some truly inspiring news coming out of the Pacific. Vanuatu has officially signed a major international declaration to ensure that children and young people have a real seat at the table when it comes to climate decision-making. It is fantastic to see them becoming the fifth Pacific nation to join this movement, especially since nearly half of their population is under eighteen. And here is the thing - this is not just about signing a piece of paper, but about genuinely empowering the next generation to protect their own future. I think it is a brilliant step forward for climate policy that actually puts the needs of the youth first.
