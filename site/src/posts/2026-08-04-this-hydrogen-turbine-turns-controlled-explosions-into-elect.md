Researchers at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology have made a groundbreaking achievement with a new hydrogen turbine that ran for over five minutes. This innovative design uses pressure-gain combustion, eliminating the need for a mechanical compressor, which is a game changer for efficiency. By harnessing hydrogen, which can be produced from renewable sources, this technology paves the way for cleaner energy solutions. It's exciting to think about the potential for lighter and more efficient turbines in the future!
