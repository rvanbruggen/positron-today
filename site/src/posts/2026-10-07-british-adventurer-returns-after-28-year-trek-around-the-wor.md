After 28 years and a 50,000-kilometre journey on foot, Karl Bushby is back on British soil. He set out from Punta Arenas, Chile, in November 1988, with the goal of reaching Hull under his own power. Along the way, he even swam across the English Channel. Now, just one final leg remains in this remarkable round-the-world adventure.
