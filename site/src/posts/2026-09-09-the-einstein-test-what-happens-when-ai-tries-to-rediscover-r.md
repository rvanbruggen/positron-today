Imagine this: AI trying to replicate Einstein's groundbreaking theory of relativity! At a recent summit, experts discussed training language models on historical data to see if they could make such a monumental discovery. While the idea is thrilling, current AI models seem to struggle with the creative leaps needed for such breakthroughs. It's a fascinating challenge that could reshape our understanding of both AI and physics.
