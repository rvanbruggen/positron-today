It's incredible to think that Kaziranga National Park went from 27 rhinos poached a year to absolutely zero! I mean, thanks to the efforts of IFS officer Sandeep Kumar and his team, they really stepped up their game with drones and community involvement. This historic achievement in 2022 is a testament to what can happen when people come together for a cause. But, of course, we can't rest on our laurels—there are still challenges ahead regarding the Eco-Sensitive Zone. Can Kaziranga maintain this amazing success? I'd love to hear your thoughts!
