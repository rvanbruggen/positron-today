Gloria Steinem, a pivotal figure in the feminist movement, dedicated her life to fighting for women's rights until her passing at 92. Despite her significant contributions, recent events in the U.S. have cast a shadow on her legacy, with many hard-won rights now under threat. Steinem's journey from a challenging childhood to becoming a leading voice for equality is truly inspiring. Her undercover work at the Playboy Mansion and her fierce advocacy for women's issues have left an indelible mark on American culture.
