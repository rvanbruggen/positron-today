So, researchers have discovered ancient triangular projectile points in Uzbekistan that date back 80,000 years! These tiny arrowheads are similar to those made by Homo sapiens later on, suggesting a much earlier presence in Europe than we thought. This exciting find challenges long-held beliefs about human migration and settlement patterns. It’s fascinating to think about how these discoveries can reshape our understanding of our ancestors' journeys.
