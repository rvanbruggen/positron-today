So: more than 25,000 supporters from 50 countries helped raise £30 million to secure the 9,400-acre Rothbury Estate for conservation. The Wildlife Trusts will make it part of a 40-mile nature corridor linking the coast with inland reserves across Northern England. Curlews, lapwings, red squirrels, merlins and endangered eels are among the wildlife that will benefit. And here's the thing: the vision brings nature-friendly farming and local communities together so people and wildlife can thrive side by side.
