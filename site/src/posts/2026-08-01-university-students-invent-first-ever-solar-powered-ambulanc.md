In an exciting breakthrough, students from Eindhoven University of Technology have created the world's first solar-powered ambulance, named Stella Juva. This innovative vehicle aims to provide medical care in remote areas while harnessing sustainable energy. With solar panels on its roof, it can reach impressive speeds and cover long distances, equipped with essential medical devices. The team is eager to test it in Kenya, hoping to improve healthcare access and inspire others to use technology for social good.
