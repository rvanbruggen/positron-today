A major Nordic study has discovered that a simple, inexpensive aspirin can actually cut the risk of colon cancer recurrence in half for certain patients. It is just amazing how a common medication can be repurposed to save so many lives through precision medicine. Now, doctors are recommending genetic testing for tumors to identify exactly who can benefit from this treatment. And here is the thing - it really shows how sometimes the biggest breakthroughs come from the most unexpected places.
