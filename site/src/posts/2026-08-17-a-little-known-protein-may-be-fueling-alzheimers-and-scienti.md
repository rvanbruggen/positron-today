So, there's this fascinating new development in Alzheimer's research! A team led by Ursula Quitterer has discovered a compound, dubbed 'Compound 10', that shows promise in slowing down the disease's progression in mice. They found that this compound can prevent a harmful protein from clumping together, which helps keep nerve cells functioning properly. The results are not just limited to the brain, as it also positively impacts heart function and even some signs of aging in the mice!
