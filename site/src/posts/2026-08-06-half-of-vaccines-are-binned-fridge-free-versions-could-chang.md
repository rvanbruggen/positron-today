So, here's some exciting news! UK scientists have successfully trialled vaccines that don't need refrigeration, which could drastically reduce waste. Currently, about half of vaccines are discarded due to temperature issues, but this new 'fridge-free' version shows promise. Inspired by resurrection plants, it uses a sugar to stabilize the vaccine, making it safe and effective. This innovation could improve access to vaccines in remote areas, which is just fantastic!
