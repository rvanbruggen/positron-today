So, a bronze plaque in Paris will mark the spot where Louis Daguerre took his 1838 photograph of Boulevard du Temple. Two blurry figures appear in the image, and they are believed to be the first people captured in a documentary photograph. Swedish filmmakers Maximilien Van Aertryck, Axel Danielson and Kalle Boman launched the initiative and used archival research and 3D scans to pinpoint the location. The memorial offers a lovely reminder of how photography changed the way we see and record the world.
