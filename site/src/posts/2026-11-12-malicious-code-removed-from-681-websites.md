So: Denmark’s National Special Crime Unit, working with police in other countries, has removed malicious code from 681 websites. The code, known as Gootloader, had infected hundreds of WordPress sites in Denmark and abroad. The operation aims to stop people from downloading viruses and becoming targets of digital extortion or ransomware attacks. Police say the threat from cybercrime remains very high, and the action focused on a central player.
