A mega satellite factory in Charleroi is intended to help Europe become less dependent on Starlink. But the ambition goes further than creating a European alternative, with its backers saying they see an even bigger opportunity. The project could put Belgian industry at the centre of Europe’s drive for greater independence in space technology. So, Charleroi may be preparing for a role that reaches well beyond the factory floor.
