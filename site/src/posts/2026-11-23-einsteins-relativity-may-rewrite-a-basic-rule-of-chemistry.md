Here’s a fascinating twist: chemists at Brown University found direct evidence that triple bonds involving heavy elements can behave differently from the familiar textbook picture. Using photoelectron spectroscopy, they studied bonds between carbon and bismuth, where relativistic effects can strongly influence how electrons behave. Their results show that the usual distinction between sigma and pi bonds can blur, even though the atoms still form three bonds. It’s a striking example of how Einstein’s relativity can reshape the chemistry of heavy elements.
