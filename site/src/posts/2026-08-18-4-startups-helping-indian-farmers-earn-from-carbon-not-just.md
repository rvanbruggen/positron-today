What if farmers could earn money not just from their crops but also from the carbon they help keep out of the atmosphere? That's exactly what these innovative startups are doing! Varaha, for instance, is making regenerative farming financially viable for small farmers, while Mitti Labs is tackling methane emissions from flooded rice fields. It's exciting to see how these initiatives are creating new income streams for farmers and promoting sustainable practices at the same time.
