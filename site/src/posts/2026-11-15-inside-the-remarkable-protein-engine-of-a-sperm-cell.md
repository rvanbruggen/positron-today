At Utrecht’s Hubrecht Institute, Miguel Leung uses cryo-electron microscopy to map the proteins in egg and sperm cells, work that recently earned him the Heineken Young Scientists Award for Biomedical Sciences. By freezing cells rapidly and combining millions of grainy images, his team builds three-dimensional models of their proteins, with AI helping locate each one. They have identified more than 150 proteins in the sperm tail and are uncovering how these components work together to create a remarkably organized swimming engine. This fundamental insight could eventually help researchers understand what goes wrong in some cases of infertility, while Leung also aims to lead his young team with care and encouragement.
