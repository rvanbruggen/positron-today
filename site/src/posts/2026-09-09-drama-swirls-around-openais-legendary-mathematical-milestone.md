OpenAI has announced a groundbreaking solution to the Navier-Stokes problem, a major mathematical challenge that has stumped experts for nearly 90 years. This achievement, powered by an advanced AI model and thousands of agents, has sparked both excitement and controversy. Just a day prior, a professor raised concerns about whether OpenAI accessed their research data to reach this solution. OpenAI insists that no specific user data was used, but the debate continues, making this a fascinating moment in the world of mathematics.
