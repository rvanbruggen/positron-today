So: a flydubai flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv faced a terrifying attack when one pilot stabbed the other, trying to crash the plane. The injured pilot, Smit Machchhar, showed incredible courage by opening the cockpit door despite his wounds, allowing passengers and crew to overpower the attacker. Among the heroes were a plumber, a dentist, and off-duty pilots who helped stabilize and safely land the plane in Saudi Arabia. It’s amazing how teamwork and quick thinking saved 174 lives in such a dire moment!
