Imagine making pumpkin pie without canned puree or a food processor: in the 18th century, cooks had to cook, mash, and strain fibrous pumpkin by hand, a process that could take more than ten hours. The earliest known American pumpkin pie recipe appeared in Amelia Simmons’s 1796 cookbook, where the pumpkin had to be strained before being mixed with milk, eggs, and spices. As Thanksgiving became a national holiday in 1863, pumpkin pie spread from a New England favorite into a familiar part of the celebration. So: factories eventually took over much of the cooking and processing, while growers developed smoother, less fibrous pumpkins - making this beloved dessert far easier to prepare.
