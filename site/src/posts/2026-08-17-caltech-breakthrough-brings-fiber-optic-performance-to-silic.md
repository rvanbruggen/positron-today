So, researchers at Caltech have made an exciting breakthrough by moving light across silicon wafers with minimal signal loss, similar to optical fiber! This innovation could lead to super-efficient photonic integrated circuits that have a range of applications, from AI data centers to quantum computing. The team has developed a method to print optical circuits directly onto silicon wafers, achieving fiber-like performance that could revolutionize technology. It's amazing to think how this could reduce energy costs and improve communication systems!
