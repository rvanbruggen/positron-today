The so-called “heart attack vaccine” is available at the pharmacy: it is the flu vaccine. It is reported to lower the risk of serious heart problems during flu by 34 percent. That makes vaccination an especially interesting option for people concerned about flu-related complications. So: a familiar preventive measure may offer protection beyond the infection itself.
