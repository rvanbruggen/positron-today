Gael Finlayson-Davis, a Pākehā grandmother in her 70s, has become a beacon of inspiration by sharing her journey of learning te reo Māori. Her heartfelt message during the Matariki celebrations encourages others to embrace the language, emphasizing that it's never too late to start. She reflects on how learning te reo has deepened her understanding of Aotearoa and its culture, highlighting the beauty of Māori traditions. Gael's story is a powerful reminder that curiosity and openness can lead to transformative experiences.
