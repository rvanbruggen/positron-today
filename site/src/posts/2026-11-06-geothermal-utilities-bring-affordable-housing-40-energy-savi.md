So: Vermont Gas is teaming up with Dandelion Energy on a first-of-its-kind project to bring geothermal heating and cooling to 44 affordable homes in Hinesburg. Drilling is expected to begin in early 2027, and residents could move in by the end of that year. There’s already a compelling example in New York, where geothermal heat pumps cut energy costs by 40.7% at a 72-unit apartment complex, saving residents around $24,000 a year. And here's the thing: individual ground loops will give each home its own system, helping make efficient, lower-cost comfort more accessible.
