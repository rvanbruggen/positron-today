Isn't it fascinating that our brains have waves just like the ocean? Researchers at the Salk Institute have discovered that these neural traveling waves help us process what we see and even predict what might happen next. They suggest that these waves are not just random noise but play a crucial role in how we interpret sensory information. This groundbreaking research could change our understanding of perception and memory in the brain.
