This exciting breakthrough in quantum physics could change the game for future quantum computers! Researchers at ISTA have developed a 'quantum bath' that allows distant qubits to synchronize without active control. This means entanglement can be maintained automatically, which is a huge leap forward from previous methods. It's like having a reliable source of entanglement that’s always ready for use, paving the way for practical quantum technologies.
