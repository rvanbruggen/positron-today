So, researchers are suggesting that language actually developed around the campfire rather than during hunting activities. This idea is fascinating because it highlights the social and communal aspects of language formation. It makes you think about how storytelling and sharing experiences could have played a crucial role in our communication evolution. And here's the thing: this perspective opens up new avenues for understanding human interaction in prehistoric times.
