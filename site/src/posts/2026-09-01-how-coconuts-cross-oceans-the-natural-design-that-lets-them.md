Isn't it fascinating how a coconut can float across oceans for thousands of kilometres? It’s all thanks to its thick husk and buoyant structure that protect the seed inside. This amazing fruit can stay viable at sea for up to 110 days, allowing it to travel far before reaching land. Once it washes ashore, it has everything it needs to start growing, making it a true marvel of nature.
