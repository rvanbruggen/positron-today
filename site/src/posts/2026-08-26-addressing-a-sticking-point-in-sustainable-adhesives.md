So, Patty Ferreira is tackling the issue of petroleum-based adhesives that hinder recycling efforts. With her company, Silvis Materials, she's developed fully biodegradable cellulose adhesives that could revolutionize the industry. This innovation not only aims to cut production emissions by up to 80% but also uses half the energy of traditional glues. It's exciting to see how bio-based solutions can make a real difference in sustainability!
