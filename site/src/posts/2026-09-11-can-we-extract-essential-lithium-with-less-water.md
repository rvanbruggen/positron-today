It's quite astonishing how much water is needed to extract lithium, especially in already dry regions like Chile. Jasper Zuidervaart from Ioniqs is on a mission to reduce this water usage with innovative techniques that purify wastewater and boost lithium yield. This is crucial as lithium is essential for sustainable energy, powering everything from electric cars to smartphones. With the demand for lithium expected to double in the next five years, finding sustainable extraction methods is more important than ever.
