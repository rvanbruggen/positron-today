About 50 years ago, red-legged frogs vanished from Yosemite National Park due to habitat loss and invasive species. Now, after years of dedicated conservation efforts, including habitat restoration and breeding programs, 10,000 frogs have been released back into the wild. The San Francisco Zoo plays a key role by raising frogs from eggs until they are ready to thrive in nature. It’s inspiring to see multiple generations of these frogs thriving again, proving that teamwork and persistence really pay off.
