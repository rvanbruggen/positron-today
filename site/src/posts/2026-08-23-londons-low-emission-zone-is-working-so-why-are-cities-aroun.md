It's amazing to see how London's low emission zone has led to healthier lungs for children, showing a significant improvement since its introduction. Alastair Harper's personal story highlights the urgency of tackling air pollution, which has affected countless families. Despite the clear benefits, many cities remain hesitant to adopt similar measures, facing cultural and political opposition. The technology to reduce pollution exists, and cities like Paris and Barcelona are leading the way, proving that change is possible.
