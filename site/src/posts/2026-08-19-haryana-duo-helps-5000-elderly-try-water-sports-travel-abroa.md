Retirement can be a new beginning, and that's exactly what two entrepreneurs from Haryana are proving. They founded 'SeniorWorld', a travel platform designed specifically for seniors over 55, encouraging them to explore the world with confidence. With a focus on comfort and companionship, they've helped thousands of elderly individuals embrace adventures they once thought were behind them. It's all about making memories and realizing that age shouldn't limit your dreams.
